FAQ

Questions, Answered

When does N2 enter volume production?expand_more

N2 is in risk production now; the full volume ramp opens in Q3 2026. Monthly MPW shuttles already run for research customers.

What are the minimum order volumes?expand_more

MPW shuttles accept designs from a single die. Dedicated wafer starts begin at 12 wafers per quarter; reserved capacity starts at 500 wafers per month.

Which certifications does NANOVA hold?expand_more

Our fabs are certified to ISO 9001, IATF 16949 and ISO 14001, with ISO Class 1 cleanroom audit reports available under NDA.

How is customer IP protected?expand_more

Design data is encrypted at rest with customer-held keys, mirrored into an air-gapped fab network, and purged from shuttle clusters after delivery.

What are typical lead times?expand_more

Standard flows run 12 weeks of fabrication plus 2 weeks of validation; fast-turn MPW shuttles compress the same path to 8 weeks.
