Before you ask

Do I need an expensive camera?add

No. Any camera with manual controls works, and several challenge winners shot on phones. We care about timing and framing, not gear lists.

Which editors do the presets support?add

Every preset ships as XMP for Lightroom and Camera Raw, plus DNG profiles for Capture One and mobile editors that accept them.

Can I sell my street shots here?add

Studio members can list prints in the marketplace. You keep 85 percent of every sale and full copyright, always.

How do the weekly challenges work?add

A prompt drops every Monday. You submit one frame by Sunday with EXIF intact, and a rotating jury of working photographers picks the winner.

Can I cancel my membership?add

Yes, in two clicks from your account page. You keep access until the end of the billing period and your uploaded work stays yours.
