From camera roll to keepsake in three steps

No design skills needed. If you can hold a glue stick, you can do this.

  1. 1

    Gather

    Drop in photos, scans, voice memos, and screenshots. Nimbus sorts them by date and place like loose prints on a desk.

  2. 2

    Paste

    Drag pieces onto the page. Tilt them, tape the corners, scribble a caption. Crooked is encouraged.

  3. 3

    Keep

    Print a bound copy, share a private link, or export the whole book as an archive you own forever.

Questions from the margins

Do I own my books if I cancel?expand_more

Yes. Every book exports as a full archive of images and pages at any time, on any plan. Your memories are never held hostage.

Can I print my scrapbook as a real book?expand_more

Keepsake and Heirloom plans include print-ready files. Heirloom includes one professionally bound hardcover per year, shipped to your door.

Who can see my shared albums?expand_more

Only people you invite. Albums are private by default, links can be revoked anytime, and guests paste pages only where you allow.
