The daily practice

Three minutes each morning. The ritual is the product.

01 self_improvement

Sit

Open the blank page. A short breathing pause clears the ground before ink touches paper.

02 gesture

Stroke

Write or draw the day in a single continuous gesture. Hesitation shows, and that is the point.

03 approval

Seal

Stamp your hanko to close the entry. Sealed pages are kept, unedited, in your archive.

Questions, answered plainly

Can I edit or delete a sealed page?expand_more

No. Sealing is final by design. You can archive a page out of view, but the ink itself is never rewritten.

Do I need a stylus or tablet?expand_more

A pressure-sensitive stylus gives the fullest wash range, but a mouse, trackpad, or finger still produces honest strokes.

What happens to my pages if I cancel?expand_more

Your archive stays readable forever on the free tier, and you can export every scroll as print-ready files at any time.
