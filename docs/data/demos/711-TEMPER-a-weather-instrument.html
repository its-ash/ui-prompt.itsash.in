Questions, answered

Does TEMPER need Wi-Fi to work?expand_more

No. All sensing and forecasting run on the device itself. Wi-Fi is only used if you want readings on your phone or history synced across rooms.

How accurate are the sensors?expand_more

Temperature reads within ±0.1°C, humidity within ±1.5%, and pressure within ±0.5 hPa. Each unit is individually calibrated before it ships.

Can I use more than one TEMPER at home?expand_more

Yes. Units pair automatically and build a room-by-room picture of your home. Cirrus supports three rooms; Cumulus covers the whole house.

What data leaves my home?expand_more

Only anonymized outdoor pressure trends, and only if you enable cloud sync. Indoor readings stay on the device unless you export them yourself.

When will my preorder ship?expand_more

First production units ship in spring 2026 in preorder sequence. You can cancel any time before shipping for a full refund.
