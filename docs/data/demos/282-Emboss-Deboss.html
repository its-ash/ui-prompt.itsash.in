Talk to the studio

Before the press runs

The questions we hear most at the counter.

What is the difference between emboss and deboss?expand_more

Emboss raises your mark above the sheet; deboss presses it below the surface. We strike a paper proof of both so you can choose with your hands, not a screenshot.

Do I need print-ready artwork?expand_more

No. Send a logo, a sketch, or a photo of your storefront sign. We clean and proof every file in-house before the die is cut.

How long does a run take?expand_more

Most orders ship 7–10 business days after you approve the proof. Rush impressions can usually hit the press within 72 hours.

Can I reorder with the same mark?expand_more

Yes — every die is catalogued in our library. Reorders skip the die fee entirely and ship in about four days.

Do you ship internationally?expand_more

We ship worldwide from Portland. Kits travel flat in a rigid envelope; full suites ride in a letterpress box with corners you could stand on.
