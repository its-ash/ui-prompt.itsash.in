Before you come out

Is there a dress code?expand_more

Dress for heat and neon. No dress shoes required, no sandals on the main floor. Chrome, linen and anything that catches a laser are encouraged.

What time should I arrive?expand_more

Doors open at midnight. The Grid Room peaks around 2 AM and the rooftop opens at 5. Members skip the line at any hour.

Can I bring guests?expand_more

Yes. Night Owl includes one guest per month, Neon Regular two per week, and Sunrise Society members can request extra codes by email.
