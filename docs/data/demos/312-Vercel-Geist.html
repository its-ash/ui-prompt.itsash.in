Frequently asked questions

How is Nimbus different from traditional cloud hosting?expand_more

Traditional hosting gives you servers to configure. Nimbus is push-to-deploy: connect a repository and every commit is built, isolated, and served from the edge automatically — no VMs, no load balancers, no maintenance windows.

Can I use my own domain and existing framework?expand_more

Yes. Any framework with a build step works out of the box — Next.js, Astro, SvelteKit, plain static sites, and more. Custom domains are one click with managed TLS certificates and automatic renewal.

What happens when I exceed my plan limits?expand_more

Nothing breaks. Limits are soft: we notify you before any overage, your traffic keeps serving, and you can set a hard spend cap. Overage is billed at published per-GB rates — never as a surprise.
