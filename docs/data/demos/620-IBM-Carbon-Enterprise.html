Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions security and finance teams ask first.

Does Nimbus require agents or changes to our workloads?expand_more
No. Nimbus uses read-only IAM roles and cloud-native APIs. Your workloads are never modified, and you can revoke access at any time.
Which compliance frameworks are supported out of the box?expand_more
The policy library ships with mappings for SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and PCI DSS. You can extend any control or write your own in the policy editor.
How does the free trial work?expand_more
You get 30 days on the Business plan with up to 10 accounts. No credit card is required, and your data is deleted if you choose not to continue.
