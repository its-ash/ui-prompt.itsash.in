Questions from the Launch Pad

Does Nimbus Atomic work in older homes?expand_more

Absolutely. The beacon speaks to standard outlets and fixtures, so even a 1948 bungalow joins the space age without rewiring.

What happens if the power goes out?expand_more

A built-in cell keeps your routines safe for up to twelve hours, and everything resumes automatically when the lights return.

Is my household data kept private?expand_more

Yes. All routines live on the beacon itself, not on a faraway mainframe. Nothing leaves your home unless you ask it to.

Can I change plans later?expand_more

Any time. Upgrade or downgrade from your console and the new rate applies from the first of the following month.

What does the warranty cover?expand_more

Two full years of parts, labor, and chrome polishing. If the beacon ever stops humming, we ship a replacement within five days.
