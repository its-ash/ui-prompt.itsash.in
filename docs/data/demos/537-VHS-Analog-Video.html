Tracking adjustments

Answers to the questions we hear at the drop-off counter.

VHS, VHS-C, S-VHS, Hi8, Video8, and MiniDV. If you are unsure of the format, send a photo with your kit request and we will confirm before you ship.

Usually not. Mold-affected tapes go through a cleaning and controlled baking process before playback. Severely damaged reels are quoted before any work begins, and you can decline with no charge.

Standard orders finish in about two weeks from the day your box arrives. Archive-tier orders are prioritized and typically complete within five business days.

Always. Every cassette is returned in its original sleeve with insured, trackable shipping. We keep a backup of your digital files for 90 days in case you need a re-download.

Only the operator assigned to your order. Files live on encrypted storage, download links are private and expiring, and we never use customer footage for marketing or training.

Blank or unrecoverable tapes are not billed. We flag them in your order summary, return them with the rest of your box, and refund that tape's fee automatically.
