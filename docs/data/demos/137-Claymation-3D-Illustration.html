helpFAQ

Squishy questions, solid answers

Do I need any 3D experience?expand_more
None at all. Squishling works like real clay — if you can pinch playdough, you can sculpt here. Every tool has a gentle tutorial that walks you through your first blob.
Can I export my characters elsewhere?expand_more
Yes! Export 3D models as GLB or FBX, grab PNG turntables for illustrations, or render MP4 stop-motion loops straight from the timeline.
Is the free plan really free?expand_more
Really free. The Lump plan stays free forever — paid tiers simply add unlimited projects, the full Claymate library, and higher-resolution exports.
