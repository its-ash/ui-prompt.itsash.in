From gather to anneal

Three movements, one ancient rhythm — you'll run all of them in your first session.

  1. 01

    Gather

    Dip the blowpipe into the crucible and wind molten glass onto its nose, turning steadily so the gather stays centered.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Block, pad, and marver the gather at the bench, swinging through the glory hole for heat between every pass.

  3. 03

    Anneal

    Knock the finished piece into the annealer at 900°F, where a two-day cool locks its strength in for good.

Before you book

The questions we hear at the furnace door.

Do I need any experience?expand_more

None at all. Over half our first-timers have never touched hot glass. A master stays with you from first gather to knock-off — bring only steady curiosity.

Is there an age minimum?expand_more

Bench spots start at 12 with a guardian on the floor. For private events we can host younger makers as helpers at the marver — just tell us when you book.

What should I wear?expand_more

Closed-toe shoes and natural fibers only: cotton, denim, or linen. Synthetics melt near the glory hole, so skip the fleece. We provide cotton aprons and didymium safety glasses.

When can I pick up my piece?expand_more

Glass needs a full 48 hours in the annealer, so pieces blown today are ready the day after tomorrow. We hold finished work for 60 days; members can arrange shipping.

What's the cancellation policy?expand_more

Reschedule free up to 24 hours before your slot. Inside 24 hours we can't refund the furnace time — the melt runs whether or not you make it.
