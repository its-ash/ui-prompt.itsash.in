Questions

Frequently asked

Which devices does Folio support?add

Folio delivers standards-compliant EPUB to Kindle, Kobo, PocketBook, Boox and reMarkable. Each issue is re-typeset for your panel size and margins rather than stretched to fit, so the page you see is the page we approved.

Can I read entirely offline?add

Yes. Editions download in full over any connection and stay on the device — nothing streams, nothing refreshes, nothing is withdrawn mid-issue. Aeroplanes, basements and cabins behave exactly like your desk.

How do billing and cancellation work?add

Plans are billed monthly or annually, VAT included, and can be cancelled in two clicks from your account — no calls, no retention offers. Access runs to the end of the paid term, and your archive exports as EPUB whenever you wish.
