04, FAQ

Questions from careful readers

Can I install apps or browse the web?addremove

No, and that is the point. Margin runs exactly one activity: reading. There is no browser, no app store, and no notification system to disable.

Which file formats does Margin support?addremove

EPUB, PDF, MOBI, and plain text. EPUBs are re-typeset with your chosen font and measure; PDFs render as printed, with margin annotation on the Pen models.

How do my margin notes leave the device?addremove

Notes export as plain text or Markdown over USB-C or Wi-Fi sync. Each note carries the book title, chapter, and the passage it anchors to.

Does the screen work in the dark?addremove

An optional warm frontlight is built in, tuned to a candle-like 2200K. It lights the page from the edge rather than shining into your eyes, and it is off by default.

What happens to my library if Margin the company closes?addremove

Your books are ordinary files in an ordinary folder. Everything syncs over open protocols, and a full local backup is one menu item away. No DRM locks you in.

Is there a subscription of any kind?addremove

No. You pay once for the hardware. Sync, typesetting, and note export are included for the life of the device.
