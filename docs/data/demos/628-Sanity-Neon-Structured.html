05 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is structured content and why do I need it? expand_more

Structured content breaks down information into reusable components with defined relationships. This enables omnichannel delivery, easier maintenance, and scalable content operations compared to unstructured WYSIWYG editors.

How does real-time collaboration work? expand_more

Our operational transforms merge changes instantly without conflicts. See cursors, presence indicators, and live previews as teammates edit the same document simultaneously.

What APIs are available for content delivery? expand_more

We provide REST and GraphQL endpoints with granular permissions, webhook support, and CDN integration. Content is versioned and can be queried with powerful filtering and sorting options.
