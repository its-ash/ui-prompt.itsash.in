Chapter the Sixth

Gentle Inquiries

How is my letter delivered?expand_more

Your parcel is sealed in wax, wrapped in rose paper, and carried by our private courier within three to five days, anywhere in the realm. Tracking arrives by post — discreetly addressed, of course.

May I request a custom wax seal or ribbon?expand_more

Most certainly. Choose from forty seals and a rainbow of silk ribbons, or commission an engraved seal bearing your own cipher — the favorite flourish of our returning patrons.

Are my words kept private?expand_more

Every scrivener swears an oath of confidentiality; your words belong to you and your beloved alone. Unopened parcels returned within fourteen days are refunded in full, no questions posed.
