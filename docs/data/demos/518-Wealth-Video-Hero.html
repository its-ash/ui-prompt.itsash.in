Common questions

Clear answers before you commit a dollar.

What is the minimum to open an account?expand_more
Essential starts at $10,000. Private begins at $100,000, and Family Office relationships typically start at $2 million across linked accounts.
Can I transfer an existing portfolio without selling?expand_more
Yes. We support in-kind transfers for most stocks, ETFs, and mutual funds, so you avoid triggering taxable events. We reimburse transfer fees charged by your current broker.
How is my money protected?expand_more
Securities are held with our custodian and covered by SIPC up to applicable limits. Cash sweeps are FDIC-insured through partner banks, and all data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
