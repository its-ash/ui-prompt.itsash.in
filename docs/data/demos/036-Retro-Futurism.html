05 // user manual

Frequently asked questions

The queries our support channel handles at warp speed.

Will the beacon slow down my site?expand_more

No. The beacon is 2.1 KB, loads asynchronously, and batches events in the background. Median impact on page load is under 4 ms, and it never blocks rendering.

Can I export my data if I leave?expand_more

Always. Export everything to CSV, JSON, or Parquet at any time — including on the free plan. No hostage situations, just files.

Do you ship SOC 2 and GDPR compliance?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II audited, fully GDPR compliant, and offers EU data residency. Our DPA is signable in one click — no lawyers required.
