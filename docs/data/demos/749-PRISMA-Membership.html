Frequently Asked Questions

What is PRISMA and who is it for?
PRISMA is a premium digital membership club designed for professionals, creators, and innovators who want exclusive access to cutting-edge tools, AI-powered insights, and a high-value community. Whether you're building a startup, advancing your career, or exploring new ideas, PRISMA provides the resources and network to accelerate your journey.
How does the free trial work?
Your free trial gives you full access to the Professional tier for 7 days. No credit card is required to start. You'll receive a reminder 24 hours before your trial ends, and you can cancel anytime during the trial period without any charges.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and PayPal. For Enterprise plans, we also support ACH transfers and wire payments. All transactions are secured with PCI-DSS level 1 compliance.
Can I change or cancel my membership?
Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your membership at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. We don't believe in lock-in contracts — your membership should work for you, not the other way around.
Is my data safe with PRISMA?
Your privacy and security are our top priorities. We use end-to-end encryption, zero-knowledge architecture, and regular third-party security audits. We never sell your data, and you retain full ownership and control of your information at all times.
