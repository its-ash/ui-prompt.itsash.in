Questions, answered

Everything about fit, fading, and forever repairs.

How should raw denim fit on day one?expand_more

Snug through the seat and thigh, but never painful — expect roughly one inch of stretch within two weeks. If you can't sit down comfortably, size up; we'll exchange unworn pairs within 30 days.

How do I care for raw denim?expand_more

Wash rarely — most pairs need it two or three times a year. Turn them inside out, soak cold for 45 minutes, hang dry away from sun. Every wash sets the fade, so wear hard and wash less.

Do you really repair jeans for life?expand_more

Yes. Crotch blows, torn pockets, popped rivets, re-hems — bring any Nimbus pair back to the shop (or mail it in) and we repair it free, forever, no receipt needed. The rivets are our signature; we stand behind them.
