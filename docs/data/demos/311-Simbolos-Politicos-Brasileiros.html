FAQ

Straight answers.

How is Símbolo funded?add

Entirely by readers. Subscriptions fund the newsroom, and we accept no money from parties, candidates, government agencies, or politically interested advertisers. Our funding ledger is published every quarter.

What methodology do you follow?add

Every claim must trace to a primary source — the Diário Oficial, TSE records, House and Senate roll calls, or institutional archives. Where evidence is contested or thin, we say so in the text, and every correction is logged publicly.

Where does your data come from?add

Official records only: TSE electoral data, chamber voting records, IBGE statistics, and institutional archives. We never publish a number we cannot trace back to an official record.

How do you guard against bias?add

Two editors from different political backgrounds review each analysis against a published bias checklist, and every piece names its own uncertainties. Criticism targets records and conduct — never identity or affiliation.

Can I cancel anytime?add

Yes. One click in your account, effective immediately, with pro-rata refunds on annual plans. The weekly briefing and the Citizen tier stay free forever.
