Questions from the bench

Everything bakers ask before their first timed proof.

Does DOUGH TIME work offline?expand_more

Yes. Timers run entirely on your device, so a spotty kitchen Wi-Fi signal never ruins a proof. Sync resumes automatically when you are back online.

Can I sync timers across devices?expand_more

Rising Star and Full Oven plans sync live timers between your phone, tablet, and laptop. Start a bulk ferment on your phone and check it from the couch on your tablet.

What happens if my dough overproofs?expand_more

DOUGH TIME sends an early warning at 80 percent of your proof window, plus a final alert. If you miss both, the app suggests rescue steps like reshaping or retarding in the fridge.

Does it handle recipes other than bread?expand_more

Absolutely. Templates cover pizza dough, croissants, cinnamon rolls, and even non-dough kitchen jobs like brining, roasting, and resting meat.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes, cancel in two taps from your account page. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and your saved recipes stay yours forever.
