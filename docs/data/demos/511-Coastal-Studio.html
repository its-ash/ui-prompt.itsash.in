Questions, answered

How long does a typical project take?expand_more

A Shoreline engagement runs three to four weeks. Tidewater projects usually take six to eight weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on content readiness.

How is pricing structured?expand_more

Packages are fixed scope and fixed price, invoiced half at kickoff and half at launch. Open Sea work is quoted after a scoping call and billed monthly.

How many revisions are included?expand_more

Every package includes structured revision rounds at each milestone. Because you review work weekly, major surprises are rare and extra rounds are seldom needed.

Do you work with clients outside your region?expand_more

Yes. Most of our clients are remote. We run kickoffs and reviews over video, share progress in a live workspace, and overlap at least four working hours with your time zone.

Who owns the work when the project ends?expand_more

You do. Final files, source assets, and documentation transfer to you in full upon final payment. We keep only the right to show the work in our portfolio.
