Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our process, pricing, and deliverables.

What is your typical turnaround time for a 30-second animation? expand_more

Turnaround varies by complexity, but standard 30-second cinematic animations typically take 4-6 weeks from concept approval to final delivery. Rush options are available for urgent projects.

How many revision rounds are included in your packages? expand_more

All packages include two rounds of revisions at key milestones (storyboard, animation preview, and pre-final render). Additional revisions are available at hourly rates.

What file formats do you deliver for final renders? expand_more

We deliver in industry-standard formats including ProRes 422 HQ, H.264 MP4, and image sequences (EXR, PNG) upon request. All files are color-graded and ready for broadcast or digital use.

How is pricing determined for custom projects? expand_more

Custom project pricing is based on scope, complexity, duration, and required resources. We provide detailed quotes after our discovery call, with clear milestones and payment schedules.

Who owns the rights to the final animation and assets? expand_more

Upon full payment, clients receive 100% commercial rights to all delivered animations and assets for unlimited use in any medium, worldwide, in perpetuity.
