Common questions

Everything else you might want to know before loading your first tank.

Does it work for color film like C-41 and E-6?expand_more

Yes. The recipe library covers black and white, C-41, E-6, and ECN-2 processes, with tight temperature windows and per-stage timing for color work.

Can I use it in a completely dark room?expand_more

That is the point. Voice prompts announce every stage and agitation cue, and the display uses a deep red safelight theme that will not fog paper or film.

What if my developer is not in the library?expand_more

You can build custom recipes with your own times, dilutions, and agitation schemes, then save and reuse them like any built-in preset.

Does the timer keep running if my phone locks?expand_more

Yes. The timer runs in the background and sends local notifications for stage changes and agitation reminders, even with the screen off.

Can I export my development logs?expand_more

Pro and Studio plans export logs as CSV or PDF, including chemistry, times, temperatures, and your notes for each roll.
