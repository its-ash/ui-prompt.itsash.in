Questions, answered in daylight

Sparkling water, solar-steeped citrus peel and lemongrass, sea-mineral electrolytes, plant-based vitamin D3, and a small amount of green-tea caffeine. Nothing artificial, no added sugar.

Each bottle carries 100% of your daily vitamin D (20 mcg D3) and 35 mg of caffeine, roughly a third of a cup of coffee. Enough to notice, never enough to jitter.

We ship across the US and EU in 2 to 4 business days, carbon-neutral and free on every order. Bottles travel in molded fiber packaging, never plastic foam.
