Straight Answers

Questions from the Trip

Is Kaleido really free, man?expand_more

Totally. The Day Tripper plan is free forever — no hidden karma, no surprise invoices. Upgrade only when your trip needs more room on the bus.

Can I bring my whole commune onboard?expand_more

Far out, yes. Flower Child seats ten crew mates, and Cosmic Voyager is unlimited. Everyone gets their own handle, boards, and swirl of color.

Does it work offline at the festival?expand_more

Cosmic Voyager keeps an offline cache, so you can rerun the light show from a muddy field. Everything syncs the moment you find Wi-Fi again.
