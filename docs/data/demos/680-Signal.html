The Method

Three steps. No ambiguity.

01

Integrate

Connect your archives, subscriptions, and internal corpora through audited connectors. Signal indexes provenance metadata alongside content from day one.

02

Calibrate

Set evidentiary thresholds per research program: source classes, corroboration counts, and recency windows. Your standards become the operating rule.

03

Brief

Each morning, qualifying findings are compiled into a cited briefing with a full audit trail, ready for review boards, leadership, or publication.
