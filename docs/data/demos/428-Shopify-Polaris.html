Common questions

Which platforms does Stockpile connect to?expand_more

Stockpile connects to Shopify, WooCommerce, and Squarespace out of the box. A CSV import covers any other platform that can export orders.

How long does the initial import take?expand_more

Most stores see their first report within an hour. Stores with more than 50,000 historical orders may take up to a day, and we email you when it is ready.

Can I set my own stock thresholds?expand_more

Yes. You can override the automatic sell-through calculation with a fixed minimum for any product, supplier, or category.

What happens when my trial ends?expand_more

Your reports stay readable for 30 days. Pick a plan to keep alerts and reorder suggestions running, or export your data and walk away.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?expand_more

Annual plans include two months free. The discount is applied automatically at checkout when you choose yearly billing.
