Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?expand_more

We combine three publicly maintained records: NOAA GlobalTemp, NASA GISTEMP, and HadCRUT5. Each yearly value is the average anomaly across the sources you select.

Why stripes instead of a line chart?expand_more

Stripes remove axes, units, and scale debates. Color alone carries the trend, which makes the graphic readable by anyone in under five seconds, including audiences with color-vision support palettes.

Can I reuse the visuals in my own work?expand_more

Yes. Every export is licensed CC BY 4.0. Credit Warming Stripes and the underlying data providers, and you may use the images in articles, teaching, and presentations.
