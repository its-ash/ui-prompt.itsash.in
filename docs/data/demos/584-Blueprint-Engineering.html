Common questions

Does Meridian Draft replace our CAD software?expand_more

No. It sits alongside your CAD tools as the planning and verification layer, importing standard formats and exporting shop-ready files back out.

How does revision signing work?expand_more

Any reviewer with signing authority can stamp a revision. The stamp records the signer, timestamp, and sheet hash in the audit trail.

Can we try it before buying seats?expand_more

Yes. Request a demo and we will set up a 30-day pilot workspace loaded with a sample project from your industry.
