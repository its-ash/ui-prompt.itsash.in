Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before your first box ships.

Where do your products come from?expand_more

We work directly with independent makers in coastal towns across the Pacific Northwest, New England, and the Gulf Coast. Each box includes a card introducing the makers behind the items.

Can I skip a box or cancel my subscription?expand_more

Yes. Log into your account to skip any upcoming box or cancel your plan entirely. There are no fees and no minimum commitment.

What is your return policy?expand_more

You have 60 days from delivery to return any item in its original condition for a full refund. Return shipping is on us for damaged or defective goods.
