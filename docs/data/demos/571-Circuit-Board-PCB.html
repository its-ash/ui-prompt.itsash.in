Frequently probed questions.

Which file formats can I import?expand_more

Traceboard imports KiCad 6 and 7 projects, Altium Designer schematics and layouts, and standard IPC-2581 and ODB++ packages. Gerber-only imports open as read-only reference layers.

How fast is same-week fabrication, really?expand_more

Orders locked before Tuesday 14:00 UTC ship Friday of the same week on the Gold Pin tier. Electrical test and inspection reports are attached to your revision before dispatch.

Do you support controlled impedance?expand_more

Yes. Pick a stackup from the fab library, enter target impedances per net class, and the editor computes trace widths and checks them continuously as you route.

Who owns my design data?expand_more

You do, always. Projects export at any time as open formats, and deleting a project purges it from our storage within 30 days. We never use customer designs for training or marketing.

Can I assemble boards as well as fabricate them?expand_more

Turnkey assembly is available on Gold Pin and Production tiers for BOMs sourced from our partnered distributors. You approve the final pick-and-place file before anything runs.
