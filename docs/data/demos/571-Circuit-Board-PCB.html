Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you accept?

We accept Gerber RS‑274X, ODB++, and IPC‑2581. Zip files containing drill files are also supported.

How fast is the turnaround?

Standard service ships in 5‑7 business days. Express options are available for 24‑hour or 48‑hour delivery.

Do you offer assembly services?

Yes. We provide SMT and through‑hole assembly with optional functional testing.
