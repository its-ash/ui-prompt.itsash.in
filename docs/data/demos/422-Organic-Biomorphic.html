Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account settings in under a minute. Paid plans stay active until the end of the billing period, and your data stays yours.

Does Verdara work offline?expand_more

Check-ins and reflections work fully offline on mobile and desktop. Everything syncs quietly the next time you connect.

How is my data handled?expand_more

Your habits and notes are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell personal data, and you can export or delete everything at any time.
