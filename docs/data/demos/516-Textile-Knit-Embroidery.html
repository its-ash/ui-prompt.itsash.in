Common questions

How do I care for hand-knit wool?expand_more

Hand wash cool with a wool-safe soap, press water out in a towel, and dry flat. Never wring or tumble dry. Airing between wears means you'll wash it far less than you think.

How does sizing work?expand_more

Each listing shows flat measurements rather than letter sizes, since hand knitting varies slightly. If you're between sizes, write to us and we'll recommend the right fit or adjust the pattern.

How long does shipping take?expand_more

In-stock pieces ship within two working days. Made-to-order knits take up to ten days before dispatch. UK delivery is two to three days; international is one to two weeks.

What is your returns policy?expand_more

Unworn pieces can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Custom Atelier pieces are final sale, but we'll always adjust the fit free of charge.

Where do your fibers come from?expand_more

Our lambswool comes from two family farms in Yorkshire, our merino from a certified organic mill in Devon, and our plant dyes are mixed in-studio from madder, weld, and indigo.

Do you offer gift wrapping?expand_more

Yes, every order can be wrapped in recycled tissue with a hand-stamped card at no extra cost. Just tick the gift option at checkout and add your note.
