Common questions

What hardware do I need?expand_more

Nimbus runs on Meta Quest 3, Apple Vision Pro, and Magic Leap 2 natively. Teammates without a headset can join the same session from any modern browser, tablet, or phone with full view and annotation access.

Where is my spatial data stored?expand_more

Room scans are processed on-device and stored as encrypted anchor maps, never as raw camera imagery. Models and session data live in SOC 2 compliant storage, with EU or US region pinning on Creator and Studio plans.

Can remote and in-room people collaborate together?expand_more

Yes. Every session is hybrid by default. In-room participants see anchored content in passthrough, while remote participants see a live virtual copy of the same room with everyone's avatars and pointers.

How does billing work for guests?expand_more

Guests join with a link and never need a paid seat. You only pay for workspace members who create and manage rooms. You can change or cancel your plan at any time from the billing dashboard.

What support is included?expand_more

All plans include documentation and community support. Creator adds 24-hour email response, and Studio includes a dedicated success engineer plus onboarding sessions for your team.
