Frequently Asked Questions

What hardware do I need to use Nimbus? expand_more

Nimbus works with all major mixed reality headsets including Meta Quest Pro, Microsoft HoloLens 2, and Apple Vision Pro. A compatible smartphone or tablet can be used for basic spatial scanning.

How is my spatial data stored and secured? expand_more

All spatial mapping data is processed locally on your device by default. When collaboration is enabled, only encrypted anchor points and object positions are transmitted via our zero-knowledge servers. We never store raw environmental meshes.

Can I integrate Nimbus with my existing 3D models and tools? expand_more

Yes! Nimbus supports FBX, GLTF, and USDZ formats. We offer plugins for Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender, and Cinema 4D to stream assets directly into your spatial workspace.
