Questions from the field

Practical answers about data, formats, and how the atlas behaves.

What data sources can DRIFT read? expand_more

Any stream of timestamped positions: CSV uploads, GPS trackers, AIS feeds, or a REST endpoint. DRIFT normalizes everything onto a shared grid automatically.

Do I need cartography experience to use it? expand_more

No. Projections and coordinate transforms are handled for you. If you can read a weather map, you can read a DRIFT chart.

How far back does trajectory history go? expand_more

The free Drift tier keeps 7 days. Currents and Atlas retain full history for as long as your field stays active.

Can I export charts for reports? expand_more

Yes. Paid tiers export layered charts as PDF for print or GeoJSON for use in GIS tools, with every layer preserved.

What happens if I cancel a paid plan? expand_more

Your atlases drop to the free tier limits but are never deleted. Export everything first, or come back and pick up where you left off.
