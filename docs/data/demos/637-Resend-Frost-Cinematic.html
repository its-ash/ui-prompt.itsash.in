Frequently asked questions

How do I verify my domain?
Add the provided DKIM and SPF TXT records to your DNS provider. Once propagated, use the Verify Domain endpoint or the dashboard checker.
Can I send attachments?
Yes. Encode files as base64 and include them in the attachments array of the send payload. Total message size limit is 25 MB.
Is there a rate limit?
Free tier: 100 requests/minute. Paid tiers scale with your plan; contact us for custom limits.
