Frequently asked questions

How long does setup take?expand_more
Most teams connect their first source in under ten minutes. Pre-built dashboard templates mean you see useful charts the same day, not after a consulting engagement.
Is my data stored by Nimbus?expand_more
By default Nimbus queries your warehouse in place and caches only aggregated results. Enterprise plans can run fully inside your own cloud with no data leaving your network.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime?expand_more
Yes. Plans are month to month, upgrades apply instantly, and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Your dashboards stay readable on the free tier forever.
