FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything else, our team answers at support@pulseboard.io.

How long does setup take?expand_more
Most teams connect their first data source in under five minutes and have a live dashboard within fifteen. Guided connectors handle authentication and schema detection for you.
Can I migrate data from my current tool?expand_more
Yes. Pulseboard imports dashboards and historical data from the major BI platforms, and our team assists free of charge on Growth and Scale plans.
Do you offer SSO and SOC 2?expand_more
SOC 2 Type II is certified and SSO via Google, Okta, and Azure AD is available on the Scale plan. Audit logs and role-based access are included.
What happens if I exceed my plan limits?expand_more
Nothing breaks. We notify you at 80% usage and keep everything running while you decide — we never throttle or drop your data mid-cycle.
Can I cancel anytime?expand_more
Yes, plans are month-to-month (annual billing is discounted 20%) and you can cancel from settings in two clicks. Your data stays exportable for 90 days.
