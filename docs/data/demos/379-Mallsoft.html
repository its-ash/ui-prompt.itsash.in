Asked at the info desk

What is mallsoft, exactly?expand_more

A branch of vaporwave that slows, stretches, and re-rooms muzak and field recordings until they sound like a shopping mall at 4 p.m. on a Tuesday. Nimbus builds ours from real dead-mall acoustics.

Do these malls actually exist?expand_more

Most did. Every floor plan is traced from archived directories of malls that closed between 1994 and 2010. A few — like the Fountain Atrium — are loving composites.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes. Leave the mall whenever you like — no exit interviews, no parking validation. Your favorite benches stay warm for 90 days, just in case.
