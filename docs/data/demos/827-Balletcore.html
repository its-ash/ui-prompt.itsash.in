Questions, answered

The practical details, before you take the stage.

How does sizing work?expand_more

We carry XS through 3X with garment measurements listed flat on every piece. Between sizes, size up; our knits are cut close and relax with wear.

How do I care for tulle and satin?expand_more

Cold hand wash or a mesh bag on delicate, then air dry. Each capsule includes a printed care card with piece-specific notes.

When am I billed for membership?expand_more

On the first of each month. Skip a month from your account page before the 25th and you will not be charged.

Where do you ship, and how fast?expand_more

We ship to the US, Canada, and the EU within 3 to 7 business days. Shipping is free on all membership capsules.

What is the exchange policy?expand_more

Thirty days, unworn, tags on. Members exchange free both ways; one-time purchases include a prepaid return label.

I am new to balletcore. Where do I start?expand_more

Start with one anchor piece, like a wrap top or ribbon-tied cardigan, and pair it with what you already own. Our styling notes show three ways to wear each item.
