Why weave with us

Everything in the studio exists to make the craft approachable without flattening what makes it special.

texture

Heritage technique

Learn plain weave, twill and tapestry on restored floor looms, taught the way they were passed down.

eco

Natural materials

Small-batch wool, linen and plant-dyed yarns sourced from regional mills and dyed in-house.

gesture

Guided workshops

Small groups of six, one weaver-instructor per loom, and a pace set by your hands, not a clock.

How a session works

Three unhurried stages, from bare frame to finished cloth.

01

Warp the Loom

Choose your yarn palette and learn to dress the loom, thread by thread, with your instructor beside you.

02

Weave the Pattern

Settle into the rhythm of shuttle and treadle while we guide your tension, edges and colour changes.

03

Finish and Take Home

Cut your piece from the loom, knot the fringe, wet-finish it, and leave with cloth you made yourself.
