Gentle answers to good questions

Do I need a credit card to try Nimbus?expand_more

Nope! Every plan starts with a free 14-day trial — no card, no nagging, just one friendly nudge when it wraps up.

Can I invite my whole team to a board?expand_more

Absolutely. On Bloom and Grove, teammates join with a single link and can comment, edit, and celebrate in real time.

What happens to my work if I cancel?expand_more

Your boards stay read-only for 90 days and export to PDF or PNG anytime, so nothing vanishes into thin air. Pinky promise.
