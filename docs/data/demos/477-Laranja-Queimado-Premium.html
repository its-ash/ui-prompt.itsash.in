Common questions

Are the pieces dishwasher and microwave safe?expand_more

Yes. Every piece is high-fired stoneware, safe for dishwashers, microwaves, and ovens up to 220°C. Hand washing will keep the glaze at its richest for longer.

How long does a made-to-order set take?expand_more

Most orders ship within three weeks. Custom Atelier commissions take six to eight weeks depending on kiln scheduling and glaze testing.

What if a piece arrives damaged?expand_more

Send us a photo within 14 days and we refire and ship a replacement at no cost. No return shipping, no forms.
