helpQuestions

Before you sit down

Do I need any weaving experience?expand_more

None at all. Most students arrive having never touched a loom. Our makers guide you from your first warp thread to a finished piece — patience included.

Are materials included in workshops?expand_more

Yes — every session fee covers yarn, tools, and loom time. Just bring yourself, and maybe a friend.

Do you ship finished goods?expand_more

We ship across the country in three to five days, wrapped in reusable studio cloth. Commission pieces always ship free.

What is your return policy?expand_more

Standard goods may be returned within 30 days in original condition. Bespoke commissions are final sale, though we will always mend or adjust.

Can I gift a workshop?expand_more

Gladly — gift cards apply to any session or membership, never expire, and arrive wrapped in handwoven cloth.
