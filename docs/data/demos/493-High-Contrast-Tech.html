Common questions

Answers to what teams usually ask before their trial.

How long does setup actually take?expand_more
Most teams stream their first metrics within ten minutes using the SDK or an existing OpenTelemetry collector. Prebuilt board templates mean you skip dashboard design entirely on day one.
Does Pulseboard replace my APM tool?expand_more
For latency, error, and saturation monitoring, yes. Pulseboard focuses on live operational signals rather than deep tracing, and it ingests data from the tools you already run if you want to keep them.
What happens if I exceed my event limit?expand_more
Nothing breaks. We keep ingesting your data and notify you before any overage charge. You can set a hard cap in billing settings if you prefer a strict ceiling.
Is my data used to train models?expand_more
No. Anomaly baselines are computed per account and never shared or pooled. Your telemetry stays in your region and is deleted within 30 days of account closure.
Can I export my data?expand_more
Yes. Every plan includes a full export API and scheduled S3 or GCS delivery, so your historical metrics are never locked in.
