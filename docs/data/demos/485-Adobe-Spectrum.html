Common questions

Answers about trials, imports, and data security.

How long is the free trial?expand_more

Paid plans include a 14-day trial with every feature unlocked. No credit card is required, and the Starter plan stays free forever.

Can I import from my current tools?expand_more

Yes. The guided importer brings tasks, docs, and comments from common project tools in under an hour, preserving owners and due dates.

Where is my data stored?expand_more

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in SOC 2 compliant data centers. Enterprise plans can choose their region and retention policy.
