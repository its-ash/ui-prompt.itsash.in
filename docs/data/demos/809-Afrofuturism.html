Questions from the ground

What does Sankofa mean?expand_more

Sankofa is an Akan concept: reach back and carry forward what matters. The platform is built on that principle, honoring origin while making room for invention.

Who owns the work I archive?expand_more

You do, always. Your archive, maps, and exports belong to you and your community. We never claim rights over transmitted work.

Can I move from Dawn to Orbit later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade in one click and your entire archive travels with you. Nothing is re-uploaded, nothing is lost.
