Questions from the trail

Everything teams usually ask before their first ascent.

How long does it take to set up a first expedition?expand_more

Most teams map their first route in under an hour. Pick an objective, add your crew, and SUMMIT drafts camps and milestones you can adjust.

Can we migrate goals from our current tool?expand_more

Yes. Import from spreadsheets or common project tools, and SUMMIT converts existing milestones into camps on your new route automatically.

What happens if a project stalls mid-ascent?expand_more

Altimetry flags any workstream that stops gaining altitude for a week, and acclimatization check-ins help the team rebalance before it becomes a rescue.
