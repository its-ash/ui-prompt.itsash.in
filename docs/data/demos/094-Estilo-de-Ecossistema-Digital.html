Frequently asked questions

How secure is the Instant Wallet?

Funds are held with 256-bit encryption and hardware key signing. Biometric unlock and a one-tap freeze lock everything down in seconds, and payouts only go to accounts you verify yourself — verified prizes settle in under 60 seconds.

How does the AI Crowd Feed work?

On-device ranking models score every clip, match recap and squad invite against your play history and interests. Your feed sharpens as you play, and your data is never sold to third parties.

Which games and platforms are supported?

NEXORA syncs cross-platform: PC, console and mobile titles all feed one profile. XP, stats and wallet balances follow you across every linked account.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes — cancel in two taps from your wallet settings. Access runs to the end of the paid month, and your XP, stats and guild history stay on your profile forever.
