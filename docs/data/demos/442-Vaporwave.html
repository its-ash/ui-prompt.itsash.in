FREQUENTLY AVOIDED QUESTIONS

Answers rendered in good faith and classical serif.

Is Nimbus a real product?expand_more

Nimbus is as real as the mall in your memory of 1994. It installs, it runs, it plays jazz. What more do you require from software?

Why does the installer make a dial-up sound?expand_more

Because progress should be audible. The sound is calibrated to the exact frequency of anticipation felt while waiting for a photo to load one line at a time.

Can I cancel my subscription?expand_more

Of course. Cancellation is a single click, followed by a tasteful animation of a palm tree waving goodbye. Most users cancel the cancellation.

Where is my data stored?expand_more

In a climate-controlled vapor cloud, redundantly backed up to a format we describe only as "romantic." Retrieval times vary with the tide.

Does Nimbus work on modern hardware?expand_more

Yes, reluctantly. It runs fastest on machines that still have a floppy drive, out of respect.
