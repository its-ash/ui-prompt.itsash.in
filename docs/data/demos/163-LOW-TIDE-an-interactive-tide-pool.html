helpFAQ

Questions from the shoreline

The things people ask us while the water pulls back.

Where does Low Tide get its data?expand_more

We blend official government tide stations with community-verified readings, then correct for your exact pin using local bathymetry. Every number shows its source and age.

Which shorelines are covered?expand_more

Every major coast in North America, plus a growing set of community-mapped pools in the UK, Australia and Chile. If your pool isn't listed yet, you can add it yourself.

Does it work without signal?expand_more

Yes. Your last sync caches the day's tide curve, and paid plans include offline pool maps that load without a single bar of service.

Can I cancel any time?expand_more

Any time, from your account in two taps. You keep access until the end of the billing period — no questions asked.

Do you show metres or feet?expand_more

Both. Switch units in settings and we'll remember your choice across alerts, widgets and printed tide tables.
