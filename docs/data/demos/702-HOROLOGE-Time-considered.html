Questions, answered plainly

How accurate is the movement?expand_more

Each calibre is regulated to within four seconds a day across five positions, then observed for seven days before casing. Your certificate records the measured rate.

How often should it be serviced?expand_more

We suggest a full service every five to seven years. It is included for life; you pay only insured shipping, both ways.

What is the warranty and return policy?expand_more

A ten-year movement warranty covers every piece. Unworn watches may be returned within thirty days for a full refund, no questions recorded.
