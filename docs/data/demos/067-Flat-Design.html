FAQ

Questions, answered flatly

How is Ledgerly different from a spreadsheet?expand_more

Spreadsheets record the past. Ledgerly checks every transaction against your policy the moment it posts, and the forecast updates itself.

Is my financial data secure?expand_more

Yes. Connections are read-only, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we are SOC 2 Type II certified. We never store bank credentials.

Which tools do you integrate with?expand_more

QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, and Slack, plus 12,000+ banks through our aggregation partners. API access ships with the Scale plan.

Can I import history from another tool?expand_more

Bring a CSV export from any major expense platform and we map your categories during onboarding — usually done in under a day.

Can I cancel or change plans anytime?expand_more

Yes. Plans are month-to-month, upgrades apply instantly, and downgrades take effect at the end of the billing cycle. No cancellation fees.
