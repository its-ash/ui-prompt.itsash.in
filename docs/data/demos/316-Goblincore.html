Questions from the undergrowth

Is Nimbus right for non-goblins?

Absolutely. Moss does not discriminate, and neither do we. If you have muddy boots and curious pockets, the burrow door is already open.

Can I export my hoard if I leave?

Yes — every trinket leaves in a tidy bundle, labels intact. We only ask that you wave goodbye to the mushrooms on your way out.

What happens if I step on a snail?

Gently relocate the snail to softer ground, then log the encounter under “small friendships”. Nimbus keeps a dedicated category for exactly this.
