Frequently asked questions

Can I migrate from Jira or Asana?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus imports projects, attachments, and comments from Jira, Asana, Trello, and Linear — usually in under an hour. Our team assists on plans above 20 seats.

Is there really a free plan?expand_more

The Free plan is free forever for up to 2 teammates and 3 projects. No credit card required, and no trial countdown.

How does billing work when I add people mid-month?expand_more

Seats are prorated automatically. You only pay for the days each teammate is active, and invoices update in real time.
