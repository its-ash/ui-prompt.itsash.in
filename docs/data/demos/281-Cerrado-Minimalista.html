Questions, answered

What members ask before their first seedling goes in the ground.

Where exactly does my money plant trees?expand_more

In four restoration corridors in Goiás and Tocantins, on degraded pasture surveyed with local agronomists. Your yearly impact receipt lists every plot by coordinates.

Can I visit my hectare?expand_more

Bloom and Canopy plans include a guided visit during the November–March planting season. Coordinates and travel notes arrive with your welcome kit.

What's in the harvest box?expand_more

Seasonal Cerrado goods from restored plots: baru nut bars, pequi oil, wildflower honey and buriti jam — each with a note on who grew it.

How do you verify trees actually survive?expand_more

Every seedling is geo-tagged and monitored for 18 months. Anything lost to the dry season is replanted free; our target — and last year's result — is 85% survival.

Can I pause or cancel?expand_more

Cancel anytime from your account and keep your impact receipts forever. You can also pause up to three months without losing your plot.
