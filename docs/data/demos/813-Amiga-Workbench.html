addremoveDoes Nimbus work on my actual Amiga?
Nimbus is a modern web app wearing Workbench chrome. It runs in any current browser on desktop and mobile. Real 68k hardware is admired but not required.
addremoveCan I import from Trello, Asana, or Notion?
Yes. The importer reads exports from all three and maps boards, tasks, and docs into Nimbus windows. Most teams finish in under an hour.
addremoveWhat happens if I cancel?
Your workspace drops to the free A500 tier and your data stays put. You can export everything as open formats at any time, paid or not.
addremoveIs my data encrypted?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. A4000 adds SSO, audit logs, and regional data residency for regulated teams.
