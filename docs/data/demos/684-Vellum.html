Can I read offline?expand_more

Yes. Scholar and Institution shelves download to every device, and your notes sync the next time you are connected.

Do my notes and drafts export?expand_more

Always. Marginalia export as Markdown with citations intact, and drafts export as .docx or plain text. Your work is never held hostage.

Of course. Cancel in two clicks; your library remains readable on the free Reader tier and your exports stay yours.
