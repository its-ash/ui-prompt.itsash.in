Questions, Answered Softly

How long does a bespoke commission take?expand_more

Six to eight weeks from first conversation to wax-sealed flacon: three trials, then the final rest.

Do you ship internationally?expand_more

We courier worldwide in insulated coffrets; summer shipments travel cold-chain at no extra cost.

Can a fragrance be reformulated later?expand_more

Signature patrons keep their formula on file, and one reformulation each year is included — so the scent can age with you.

Are your materials cruelty-free?expand_more

Entirely. We work with flowers, resins, and ethical synthetics — never animal musks.
