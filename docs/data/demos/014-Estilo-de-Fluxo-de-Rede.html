Frequently asked questions

How does NexaFlow handle security and compliance?expand_more

NexaFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified and HIPAA-eligible, with AES-256 encryption for traffic in transit and at rest. Role-based access control, audit logging, and zero-trust edge policies are included on every plan, while Enterprise adds data-residency controls.

How long does deployment take?expand_more

Most teams connect their first site in under an hour with a lightweight agent or an edge gateway. A full multi-region rollout typically takes days rather than months, with no forklift hardware upgrades.

Does NexaFlow integrate with our existing infrastructure?expand_more

Yes. NexaFlow layers cleanly over your current firewalls, switches, and cloud VPCs without reconfiguration. It interoperates natively with AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes clusters, and any standard IP network.

What uptime and latency guarantees do you offer?expand_more

Professional plans include a 99.99% uptime SLA with service credits, and Enterprise customers can negotiate 99.995%. Real-time status is published on our public network-status page, and global median latency is about 12 ms.

What does migration from our current network look like?expand_more

A named engineer shadows your first cut-over, then traffic shifts gradually by weight so nothing flips at once. You can roll back to your previous topology at any point during the migration.

What support tiers are available?expand_more

Starter includes community support with docs and forums. Professional adds 24/7 priority support with a one-hour response target, and Enterprise includes a named technical account manager with a 15-minute response SLA for critical incidents.
