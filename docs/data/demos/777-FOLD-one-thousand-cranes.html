Questions from the folding table

Each kit uses 15cm square washi-style paper, pre-scored along the crane fold lines. It holds a crisp crease yet forgives refolding, and every tone is dyed with fade-resistant pigment.

First-time folders finish a crane in about fifteen minutes with the crease map. By your twentieth bird, most people fold one in under five minutes without looking at the diagram.

Senbazuru is the Japanese custom of folding one thousand paper cranes and stringing them together. Legend holds that the folder, or the person they gift the flock to, is granted one wish: health, peace, or good fortune.

Yes. Kits ship in a gift-ready paper wrap with a blank wish tag. If you fold a flock for someone, the keepsake box in the Thousand Cranes kit is sized to carry all one thousand birds safely.

The Flock and Thousand kits include a group guide with seating plans, pacing suggestions, and shared cords so many folders can contribute to a single strung flock at weddings, memorials, or classrooms.

Kits ship flat in recycled mailers within two business days. Delivery takes three to seven days in most regions, and every order includes tracking. Paper is packed between stiff boards so no sheet arrives pre-folded.
