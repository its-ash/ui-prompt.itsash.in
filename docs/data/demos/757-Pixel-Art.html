Answers before you spend a single coin.

Do I need to know how to code?add

No. PixelForge uses visual rules for movement, collisions, and scoring. If you can drag a tile, you can ship a game. A scripting lane is there when you want it.

What can I export my game as?add

Every plan exports a shareable browser link. Paid plans add a ROM-style file you can run in common emulators and flash carts.

Who owns the games I make?add

You do, completely. Every sprite, track, and level you create is yours to sell, share, or port anywhere.

Can I collaborate with my team?add

Arcade Pro includes live co-op editing, so one person can paint tiles while another writes the boss music in real time.

Is there a free trial of paid plans?add

The Rookie plan is free forever. Speedrunner and Arcade Pro come with a 14-day trial, no card required.
