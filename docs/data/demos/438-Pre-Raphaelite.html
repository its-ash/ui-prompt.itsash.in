How It Works

Three movements, as deliberate as a studio apprenticeship.

  1. 1

    Prime the canvas

    Choose your ground. Nimbus stretches a fluid, token-tinted base to any screen without a single crack.

  2. 2

    Lay the glaze

    Build sections in translucent layers of colour and shadow, each one revealing the composition beneath.

  3. 3

    Sign the work

    Ship it. Contrast in both light and dark is verified before your panel is hung in the gallery.

Questions, Answered

Inquiries from the gallery floor.

Will Nimbus work beside my current tools?expand_more

Nimbus exports plain, semantic HTML and CSS, so it sits happily beside any framework or content platform you already keep in the studio.

Are the light and dark themes truly accessible?expand_more

Yes. Every pairing of ink and ground is contrast-checked in both themes — gold is darkened to an ink tone on parchment so nothing fades into mere ornament.

Can I cancel my patronage?expand_more

Any time, and without ceremony. Your compositions remain yours to keep, download, and hang wherever you please.
