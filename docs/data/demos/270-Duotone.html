Frequently Asked Questions

What is duotone design? expand_more
Duotone design uses two contrasting colors to create striking visual compositions. This approach enhances brand recognition, improves visual hierarchy, and creates memorable user experiences through intentional color blocking and gradient applications.
How does the theme toggle work? expand_more
Our theme toggle persists your preference using localStorage and respects your system preferences on first visit. The duotone gradient adapts seamlessly between light and dark modes while maintaining visual consistency and accessibility standards.
Is duotone accessible? expand_more
Yes, we've implemented WCAG AA compliant contrast ratios in both themes, ensured focus visibility, and tested with screen readers. The duotone approach actually improves accessibility by creating clear visual distinctions through color and luminance contrast.
