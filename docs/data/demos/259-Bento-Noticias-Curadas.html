FAQ

Questions, answered

How does Bento curate my news?expand_more

Overnight, our models score stories from sources you trust against your interest profile, strip out duplicates and clickbait, and balance the mix across your categories. You wake up to one finite, relevant grid — not an infinite feed.

Can I choose which sources appear?expand_more

Yes. Mark outlets as favorites to prioritize them or mute them entirely. Trusted sources can be set per category, so you can follow tech from specialists and world news from wire services.

What happens to my reading data?expand_more

Your reading history stays private. It is used only to tune your own curation, never sold, and never used for advertising. You can export or delete it at any time from settings.

Can I change my categories later?expand_more

Any time. Add or remove categories, resize tiles and rearrange your grid — the next edition adapts within a few hours, and your likes and hides refine it continuously.

Is the free plan really free? Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

The Reader plan is free forever, with no credit card required. Paid plans are month-to-month or yearly, and you can cancel in two clicks — you keep access until the end of your billing period.
