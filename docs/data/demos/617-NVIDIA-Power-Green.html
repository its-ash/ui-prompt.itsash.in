Common questions

Which GPU types are available?add

We currently offer H100, H200 and B200 accelerators in SXM configurations, with L40S nodes for inference and rendering workloads. New silicon lands within one quarter of general availability.

How does per-minute billing actually work?add

Metering starts when your container first executes on the node and stops the moment the job exits or you tear down. Provisioning time is never billed, and there are no minimum commitments on Dev or Cluster tiers.

Can I bring my own container images and data?add

Yes. Pull from any OCI-compatible registry, mount object storage buckets directly, or stage datasets on our local NVMe tier. Data is encrypted at rest and wiped on cluster teardown.
