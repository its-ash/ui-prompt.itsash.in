P600FREQUENTLY ASKED
P601WHAT EXACTLY IS A BROADCAST PAGE?▶

A broadcast page is a public, numbered status screen. It carries your service states, incident notes and release log, and refreshes for every reader at the same moment.

P602DO SUBSCRIBERS NEED AN ACCOUNT?▶

No. Readers open your broadcast address and enter a page number. Optional email alerts are available for people who want incidents pushed to them.

P603HOW FAST DO INCIDENT ALERTS APPEAR?▶

Within one broadcast cycle, which is under five seconds on all paid plans. The affected page flashes its header until the incident is resolved.

P604CAN I KEEP PAGES PRIVATE?▶

Yes. The Station plan adds private page ranges protected by access keys, so internal teams can broadcast without going public.

P605WHAT HAPPENS IF I CANCEL?▶

Your pages move to the free Relay tier and the archive keeps its final 7 days. You can export the full broadcast record at any time before or after cancelling.
