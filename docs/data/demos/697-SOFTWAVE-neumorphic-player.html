Questions, answered

The essentials before you press play.

Which platforms does Softwave run on?expand_more

Softwave is available on macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android. Your library and settings sync across devices when you sign in, and everything works fully offline.

Does Softwave stream music or play my own files?expand_more

Softwave plays your own files: MP3, FLAC, ALAC, WAV, and OGG. It does not require a streaming subscription, and it never uploads your library.

Can I switch plans or cancel later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account page at any time. Paid features stay active until the end of your current billing period.
