Why it lasts

Materials we’d want in our own homes

We make a small number of things and make them properly — every piece is solid, serviceable, and honest about what it’s made of.

forest

Solid, honest materials

White oak, black walnut, spun brass, and undyed wool. No veneers, no fillers — if it looks like oak all the way through, it is.

handyman

Made by hand, here

Twelve people, one workshop, zero outsourcing. Every piece is signed by the maker who finished it and dated on the base.

build

Repairs for life

Standard parts and joints that can be reglued. If an Alder & Ash piece ever fails you, we mend it or replace the part, free.

The process

From our bench to your room

No warehouses, no mystery supply chains. Here’s exactly how a piece comes to you.

01

Choose your piece

Pick from an edited collection of lamps, boards, mugs, and throws — each sized and finished for daily use, not for a photograph.

02

We make it to order

Your piece is cut, joined, and finished in our Portland workshop. Most ship in two to three weeks, with a note the day it leaves.

03

It arrives, and stays

Delivery is on us, and so is the aftercare: every piece carries a lifetime repair promise and a small spare-parts kit.
