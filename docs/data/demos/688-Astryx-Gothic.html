Questions from the gate

Straight answers, no riddles.

How long does setup take?

expand_more

Most teams connect their first cluster and see live metrics within fifteen minutes. Full policy setup typically takes an afternoon.

Does Astryx work with our existing CI pipeline?

expand_more

Yes. Astryx sits after your CI and accepts builds from GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Buildkite, and any system that can call a webhook.

What happens if a rollback fails?

expand_more

Rollbacks are sealed snapshots, not re-deploys, so they cannot fail the way a rebuild can. If infrastructure itself is down, Astryx pages your on-call with the exact state of every service.
