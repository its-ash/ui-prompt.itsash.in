Common questions

How long does the free trial last?expand_more

The Starter plan is free forever. Team and Business plans include a 14-day trial with full features, no credit card required.

Can I import from my current tool?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus imports from Trello, Asana, Jira, and CSV files. Owners, due dates, and attachments carry over automatically.

Is my data private?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Business plans add SSO, audit logs, and EU data residency options.
