Questions

Frequently posted inquiries.

Everything collectors ask before affixing a single hinge.

What does “archival-grade” actually mean?expand_more

Pages follow conservation standards: no acidic templates, no lossy compression on your scans, and metadata kept in open formats your grandchildren's software will still open.

Can I import an existing catalogue?expand_more

Yes — upload CSV or spreadsheet exports, or photograph stock-book pages. Philately reads Scott, Stanley Gibbons and Michel numbers and pre-fills the issue details for you.

Are first-day covers and postal history supported?expand_more

Fully. First-day covers, postcards, entires and revenues each get dedicated fields for cachet, cancellation type and postal route — first-class citizens, not afterthoughts.

Who can see my album?expand_more

Your album is private by default. Share whole pages or single items via links you control — and set them to expire like a seasonal postmark.

What happens to my pages if I cancel?expand_more

Everything exports — pages, provenance and scans in open formats. Your collection stays yours, mint condition, forever.
