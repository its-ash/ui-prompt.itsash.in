What exactly is a fog panel?expand_more

A translucent pane of interface that blurs whatever sits behind it. The effect is calm, not clutter — depth you can feel, edges you can't.

Will all that blur slow my machine down?expand_more

No. Blur is applied per panel and capped, and it switches itself off whenever your device asks for reduced motion or battery mercy.
