Section E, Questions, Answered

From the letters page

How do bilingual editions actually work?expand_more

Each story carries two language versions that share one grid. You can publish them side by side on the same page or as paired editions; readers switch languages without losing their place.

Do I need a designer to make pages look right?expand_more

No. The layout engine enforces editorial standards automatically: balanced columns, correct leading, and clean rules. Designers can still override everything at the house-style level.

Can I import an existing archive?expand_more

Yes. Broadside imports from WordPress, Ghost, and plain Markdown, preserving headlines, bylines, and dates. Most archives move in under an hour.

What does print-ready PDF mean?expand_more

Every edition exports as a press-quality PDF with crop marks, embedded fonts, and your grid intact, ready for a local printer or a digital press run.

Can I cancel or change plans mid-month?expand_more

Anytime. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the billing cycle, and your editions stay readable forever.
