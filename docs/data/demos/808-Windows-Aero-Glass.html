Questions, answered

The things people ask before they download.

Does the blur hurt performance?expand_more

No. AeroDesk renders blur on the GPU with a cached pipeline, holding 120fps on integrated graphics from the last six years. CPU usage stays under 2% at idle.

Does it work offline?expand_more

Yes. Scenes, layouts, and window switching are fully local. Your account only syncs when you choose to share scenes with a team.

Which platforms are supported?expand_more

Windows 10 and 11 today, with macOS in public beta. Linux builds are on the roadmap for late 2026.
