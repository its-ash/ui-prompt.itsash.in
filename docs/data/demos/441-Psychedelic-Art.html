Questions, Man?

Everything you might want to know before you commit to the colors.

Are these original 1960s posters?expand_more

No. These are new screen prints made in the Fillmore tradition by working artists, printed in small numbered runs on archival paper.

How long does shipping take?expand_more

Prints ship within three business days. US orders arrive in about a week; international orders take two to three weeks with tracking.

Can I return a print?expand_more

Yes. Unframed prints can be returned within 30 days in their original mailer for a full refund, no questions asked.
