Frequently asked questions

Why true black instead of dark grey?expand_more

On OLED panels, black pixels are physically turned off. True black backgrounds reduce power draw and eliminate the grey haze that causes eye fatigue in dark rooms.

Does Nimbus meet accessibility standards?expand_more

Yes. Every default theme pairing is tested against WCAG AA: 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and interface components.

Can I customize the accent colors?expand_more

Pro and Team plans include the accent system editor. You can remap action, link, and highlight colors while the contrast checker keeps every pairing compliant.

Is there a light mode?expand_more

Nimbus is built night-first, but a high-contrast day theme is available for shared screens and bright offices. Your preference syncs across devices.

How does the free plan work?expand_more

The Free plan includes three projects and the core theme engine, forever. No credit card required, and you can upgrade or downgrade at any time.
