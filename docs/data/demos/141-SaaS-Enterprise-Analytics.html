FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does implementation take?expand_more

Most teams connect their first warehouse in under an hour and are fully live within two weeks — the median time to a first dashboard is four days.

Which data sources does Nimbus support?expand_more

Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks, and Postgres, plus 450+ managed connectors for CRMs, billing, and marketing tools.

How do you keep our data secure?expand_more

Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified with encryption in transit and at rest, SSO/SAML, row-level permissions, and complete audit logs. We never store your raw data.

Can we migrate from Looker, Tableau, or Power BI?expand_more

Yes. Our importer converts existing dashboards and semantic models automatically, and our team runs a parallel rollout so there is no reporting gap.
