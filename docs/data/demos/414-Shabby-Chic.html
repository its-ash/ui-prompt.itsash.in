Questions, answered softly

Are the pieces genuinely vintage?expand_more

Yes. Every item is at least thirty years old, sourced from estate sales, auctions, and house clearances across England. We never sell reproductions.

What if a piece arrives damaged?expand_more

Write to us within fourteen days with a photograph and we will repair, replace, or refund, whichever you prefer. Breakage in transit is rare but we always make it right.

Can I request something specific?expand_more

Garden tier members may commission a search, whether a particular china pattern or a pine blanket box. Most requests are fulfilled within two seasons.

Do you ship outside the UK?expand_more

We post smaller pieces across Europe for a modest fee. Furniture travels within mainland UK only, carried by our own careful drivers.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?expand_more

Anytime, with no fuss. Pause for a season or cancel entirely from your account page, and any paid box still on its way will arrive as promised.
