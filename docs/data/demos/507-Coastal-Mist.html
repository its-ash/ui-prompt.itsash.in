Common questions

Everything you might want to know before your first session.

How does guided breathing actually help?expand_more

Slow, paced breathing activates the parasympathetic nervous system, which lowers heart rate and signals the body that it is safe to relax. Tidewell sessions use rhythms of four to six breaths per minute, the range most studied for this effect.

Do I need any experience with meditation?expand_more

None at all. Every session tells you exactly what to do and when to do it. There is no posture to learn and no technique to master; if you can breathe, you can use Tidewell.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?expand_more

Yes. Cancel from your account settings in two taps, with no calls and no retention screens. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and the free Drift plan remains available forever.
