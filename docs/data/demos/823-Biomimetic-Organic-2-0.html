Questions from the greenhouse

Do designers stay in control of the output?expand_more

Yes. Morpho proposes growth, you prune it. Every generated component is a suggestion you can edit, pin, or reject, and your tokens always remain the single source of truth.

How do the nature algorithms actually work?expand_more

Morpho models branching patterns found in leaves and corals. Your tokens act as nutrients, and the algorithm grows component variations along those patterns, keeping spacing and proportion biologically consistent.

What about animation performance and reduced motion?expand_more

All motion runs on transform and opacity only, so it stays on the compositor. When a visitor prefers reduced motion, Morpho swaps every animation for a short opacity fade automatically.
