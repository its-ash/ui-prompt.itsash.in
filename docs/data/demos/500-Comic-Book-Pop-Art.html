Panel 06

Ask the Narrator

Answers to the questions every new reader asks.

How long does setup actually take?expand_more
Most teams create their first board within five minutes of signing up. Import tools pull tasks in from spreadsheets and other trackers, so nothing gets retyped by hand.
Can I switch plans mid-story?expand_more
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing page. Changes apply immediately and we prorate the difference, so you only pay for the power you use.
Is my team's data safe in the Nimbus universe?expand_more
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups and regional hosting options. League plans add SSO, audit logs, and granular permissions.
