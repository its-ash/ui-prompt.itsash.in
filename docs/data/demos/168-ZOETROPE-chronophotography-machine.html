Questions, answered frame by frame

Everything else lives in the docs drum.

How does ZOETROPE make loops seamless?expand_more

The frame engine samples exactly one full motion cycle. It detects where the stride pattern repeats and trims to that boundary, so the last frame meets the first with no visible cut.

Do I need a tripod or a rig?expand_more

No. The drum stabilizes electronically and the burst sensor works handheld, from half a meter out to thirty meters, in most lighting conditions.

Which formats can I export?expand_more

Seamless GIF, WebM, APNG, and Live Photo. Cinema plans add RAW frame stacks for compositing in any NLE.

Is there a free tier?expand_more

Yes. Reel is free forever: 24 fps bursts and 720p GIF exports with no watermark, up to three saved reels.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes — plans are monthly, and every loop you have already exported stays yours forever, even after you cancel.
