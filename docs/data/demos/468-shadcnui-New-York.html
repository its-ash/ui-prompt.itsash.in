Frequently asked questions

Everything else, support@nimbus.dev answers within minutes.

How is Nimbus different from other deployment platforms?expand_more

Nimbus specializes in zero configuration: it detects your framework, cache rules, and build steps for you. There is no YAML to maintain, and every deploy produces an immutable snapshot you can restore in one click.

Can I use my own domain and TLS certificates?expand_more

Yes. Add any domain in the dashboard and Nimbus provisions certificates automatically, or upload your own. Certificates renew 30 days before expiry, so visitors never see a warning.

What happens if I exceed my plan's limits?expand_more

Nothing breaks. We keep serving your traffic and notify you with usage details. You can upgrade in one click, or set a hard spending cap per project at any time.
