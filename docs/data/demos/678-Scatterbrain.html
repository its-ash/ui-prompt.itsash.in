From chaos to plan in three steps

A typical session runs twenty minutes and ends with a decision.

1

Dump everything

Set a timer and let the whole team throw notes on the wall at once. Quantity beats quality here.

2

Sort the pile

Cluster related notes, merge duplicates, and let the quiet patterns surface on their own.

3

Vote and commit

Everyone gets five dots. The top clusters turn into tasks with owners before you leave the room.
