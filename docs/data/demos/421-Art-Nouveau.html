The Craft

Made by hand, grown by heart

Pressed Botanicals

Petals and fronds gathered each season, pressed between linen for six slow weeks until they hold their color forever.

ink_pen

Hand-Drawn Lettering

Every monogram and invitation is lettered with a nib pen, so no two commissions ever share the same line.

spa

Gilded Finishing

Edges are brushed with warm gold leaf and sealed with beeswax, the way ateliers finished work a century ago.

From seed to keepsake

Three unhurried movements carry your commission from first sketch to finished piece.

1

The Consultation

We meet over tea to hear your story, then sketch three botanical directions for you to choose among.

2

The Making

Your piece is lettered, pressed, and gilded in our studio over four to six weeks of careful handwork.

3

The Unveiling

Wrapped in tissue and sealed with wax, your heirloom arrives with a certificate of its making.
