How It Works

From Stem to Keepsake

1

Gather

Choose the stems that speak to you, or let our florists compose from the season's finest cutting.

2

Press

Your blooms rest between linen blotters for six patient weeks, until every drop of moisture yields.

3

Bind

We mount the pressings, emboss your folio in gold leaf, and sign it with the artisan's mark.

Questions

Before You Write to Us

May I send flowers from my own garden?expand_more

With the Heirloom tier, yes — post your blooms to the atelier and we will press, mount and return them inside your folio, with pressing instructions enclosed for every season.

How long does a pressing take?expand_more

Six patient weeks between blotters, then two more for gold tooling. Most folios reach your letterbox within a season of ordering.

Will the colours truly last a lifetime?expand_more

We press only at full bloom, fix with oak-gall, and mount on cotton rag — so we guarantee the colours for one hundred years, in writing.
