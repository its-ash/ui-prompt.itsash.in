From sketch to sealed print

Three stages, one registration mark. The same sequence Hokusai's printers followed.

  1. 一 Step 1

    Draw the Key Line

    Your design is transferred to a cherry block and carved in relief, leaving the bold sumi outline that anchors every color.

  2. 二 Step 2

    Carve the Color Blocks

    One block per color, each aligned by carved kento marks so Prussian blue and vermillion land exactly where they belong.

  3. 三 Step 3

    Press by Hand

    Dampened washi meets pigment under the baren. Each impression is pulled by hand, dried flat, and stamped with our seal.
