Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check real-time arrivals?

expand_more
Our app and station displays show live arrival times. Enable notifications for alerts on your preferred routes.

Are transfers free between lines?

expand_more
Yes! All transfers within 90 minutes of your first tap are free. Just tap again at the connecting station.

Is the system accessible for wheelchair users?

expand_more
All stations feature elevators, ramps, and priority boarding areas. Our fleet includes wheelchair-accessible vehicles.

What happens if I lose my card?

expand_more
Report it immediately in the app or at any station kiosk. We'll block the old card and issue a replacement with your balance transferred.

Can I bring my bike on board?

expand_more
Yes! Bikes are allowed in designated areas during off-peak hours (before 7am, 10am-3pm, after 7pm). Folding bikes are welcome anytime.
