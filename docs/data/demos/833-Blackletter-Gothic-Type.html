The Artisan's Path

How We Create Legacy Type

I

Consultation & Design

We begin with your vision, studying historical references and crafting preliminary sketches that honor tradition while speaking to modernity.

II

Matrix Cutting & Punching

Using century-old techniques, our engravers hand-cut each character into copper matrices, creating the masters from which type is cast.

III

Presswork & Finishing

Each sheet is fed through our restored Columbian press, impression by impression, then inspected for perfection before leaving our workshop.
