From Manuscript to Press

A commission passes through three hands before it reaches yours.

  1. I

    The Brief

    You send us your text and intent. We advise on face, format, and paper stock suited to the work.

  3. II

    The Setting

    Our compositors set the type, pull proofs, and correct until every line stands true and even.

  5. III

    The Pressing

    Sheets are inked and pressed, capitals gilded, and the finished work bound and delivered to your door.

Questions from the Counter

A single sheet is typically pressed within two weeks of an approved proof. Bound editions and bespoke typecasting run from six weeks to a full season, depending on the work.

Yes. Our cases hold full Latin, German, and French sorts, including long s, ligatures, and diacritics. Tell us the tongue of your text and we will confirm the fount.

Patrons on any standing commission are welcome on the first Saturday of each month, when the presses run for demonstration. Write ahead and we shall have ink on the stone for you.

Every parcel is insured and packed between boards. Should harm befall it in transit, we reprint and reship at our own cost, without question or delay.
