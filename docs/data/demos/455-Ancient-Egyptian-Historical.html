Questions from the caravan

Do I need any background in ancient languages?expand_more

None at all. The Apprentice path assumes no prior study and introduces each sign with its sound, meaning, and a memory aid drawn from its shape.

Which stage of Egyptian does Seshat teach?expand_more

We teach Middle Egyptian, the classical literary language of the Middle Kingdom, which is the standard starting point for reading monumental inscriptions.

Are the inscriptions in the archive real?expand_more

Yes. Every text is transcribed from published corpora and museum records, with provenance and bibliography attached to each entry.

Can I use Seshat with a classroom?expand_more

The Pharaoh rank is built for exactly that. It includes shared seats, curated lesson paths, and progress reports for instructors.

What happens when my trial ends?expand_more

Your account returns to the Apprentice rank. Your progress, notes, and drill history are preserved and waiting if you upgrade later.
