Built slow, on purpose

Three things Nimbus does well. It politely refuses to do the rest.

Paper-first editor

A page that behaves like paper. No toolbars shouting at you, no badges, no streaks. Just ink, margins, and room to wander.

ink_pen

Honest search

Find notes by the words you actually wrote, including the misspelled ones. We index your typos because that is how memory works.

cloud_off

Offline by default

Your notes live on your device first. Sync happens when you ask for it, over a connection you choose. Airplane mode is a feature.

Three steps, no ceremony

From download to first sentence in about a minute.

Fair questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

Does Nimbus use AI to write for me?add

No. There is no autocomplete, no summary bot, no generated anything. Nimbus stores your words and finds them later. The thinking is entirely your job.

What happens to my notes if you shut down?add

Every page can be exported to plain text or PDF at any time, on every plan including the free one. Your notes are files, not hostages.

Can I sync between my phone and laptop?add

Yes, on the Bound and Archive plans. Sync is end-to-end encrypted and off by default. You turn it on, choose a passphrase, and we never see your pages.
