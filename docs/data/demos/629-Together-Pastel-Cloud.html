Frequently asked questions

How is my data handled during fine-tuning?expand_more

Your datasets are encrypted at rest and in transit, processed in isolated tenants, and never used to train shared models. Enterprise plans add VPC peering and custom retention policies.

What uptime do you guarantee?expand_more

Scale plans carry a 99.9% monthly SLA on dedicated endpoints. Enterprise plans can contract up to 99.99% with multi-region failover and service credits.

Which models can I deploy?expand_more

Over 200 open-weight models across language, vision, audio, and embeddings, plus any custom checkpoint you fine-tune or upload in a supported format.
