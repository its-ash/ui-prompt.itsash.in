05 / Straight Answers

Before you askask anyway

When do drops land — and do you restock?add

Drops go live every second Friday at 20:00 local time, announced 48 hours ahead by newsletter and socials. Every run is capped and numbered, and retired pieces never come back. If it sold out, it stays sold out — that's the point.

What's your return and sizing policy?add

Unworn pieces come back within 30 days for a refund or a size swap, tags still on. Check the size guide first — our cuts run one size raw, and heavyweight fleece relaxes about half a size after the first wash.

Do you ship worldwide? Can artists collab?add

We ship everywhere in 5–10 business days; duties are on you, tracking is on us. Artists: send three work samples and one wall shot to collabs@rawdistrict.store — we answer every pitch and we pay every collaborator.
