Quiet questions

Everything dreamers usually wonder before their first orbit.

Is wander. a game or a meditation app?expand_more

Neither, quite. There are no scores and no timers. It is a slow place to look at beautiful planets and let your thoughts unclench for a few minutes.

Do I need to know anything about astronomy?expand_more

Not at all. Every planet arrives with a short, gentle story written for curious beginners rather than textbooks.

Can I use wander. offline?expand_more

Yes. Voyager and Deep Field plans let you download your favorite orbits, so the sky travels with you on flights and far-off cabins.

Is it suitable for children?expand_more

Very much so. The stories are calm, the colors are soft, and there is nothing to buy or tap accidentally inside a shared orbit.

Can I cancel my plan whenever I like?expand_more

Of course. Cancel in two taps and keep every keepsake you have gathered. Your constellation stays yours forever.
