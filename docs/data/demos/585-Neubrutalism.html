Questions, answered

The stuff people actually ask before hitting publish.

Do I need to know how to code? add

No. Everything is drag and drop with sensible defaults. If you can write an email, you can ship a Blokkit page.

Can I use my own domain? add

Yes, on Pro and Studio. Point your DNS at our edge network and we handle certificates automatically.

What happens if I cancel? add

Your pages stay live on the free Starter plan. You keep your content, your subdomain and your sanity.

Is there a limit on traffic? add

No bandwidth caps on any plan. Pages are static files served from the edge, so viral moments are free.

Can I export my pages? add

Studio plans can export clean, self-contained HTML and CSS at any time. No lock-in, ever.
