Chapter Five · FAQ

Questions on the scroll

What exactly is a sealed release?expand_more

Every deploy is hashed and signed with a red-seal provenance record — who shipped what, when, and from which commit. Tampering is impossible to hide.

Will Nimbus replace our existing pipeline?expand_more

No — it sits on top. Adapters for GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, and Jenkins import your workflows in minutes; your scripts stay untouched.

Is there a free tier?expand_more

Yes. Apprentice is free forever for one project, with no card required. Upgrade only when your scroll outgrows the brush.
