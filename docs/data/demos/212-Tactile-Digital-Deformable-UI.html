FAQ

Questions, squished flat

Everything teams ask before their first bounce.

Which browsers does Squish support?expand_more

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari (last two versions). Where backdrop-filter is unsupported, glass surfaces fall back to solid panels — every press still deforms and bounces.

Does it respect reduced-motion settings?expand_more

Yes. Under prefers-reduced-motion every wobble, squash, float and reveal is disabled, and haptic vibration is never fired. The interface stays fully usable, just calm.

Does it work with my framework?expand_more

Squish is framework-agnostic CSS at its core — it drops into React, Vue, Svelte or plain HTML. Only the toast and toggle helpers are vanilla JS, and they are optional.

What's the license?expand_more

Starter is free for personal and open-source projects. Pro and Studio cover unlimited commercial products per seat, including the Figma kit and token exports.
