Common questions

Everything about shipping, guarantees and what arrives at your door.

How are the plants shipped?expand_more

Each plant is watered, wrapped at the base and locked into molded pulp packaging inside a ventilated box. Orders ship Monday through Wednesday so plants never sit in a warehouse over the weekend.

What if my plant arrives damaged?expand_more

Send us a photo within seven days of delivery and we will ship a replacement free of charge or refund that plant. No return shipping needed.

What size pots do the plants come in?expand_more

Starter plants arrive in 3 inch nursery pots. Monthly subscription plants come in 4 inch pots, and rare cultivars in the Evergreen tier ship in 6 inch pots.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?expand_more

Yes. You can skip a month, pause for a season or cancel entirely from your account page. Changes made before the 25th apply to the next box.

Do you accept returns on pots and supplies?expand_more

Unopened pots, soil and tools can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Live plants are covered by the arrival guarantee instead of a standard return.

Ready to start growing?

Your first box ships within two business days. Pause or cancel whenever you like.
