Questions, answered

Everything teams usually ask before switching to Lumira.

Does Lumira run in the browser?expand_more

Yes. Lumira runs entirely in modern browsers with hardware acceleration, and desktop apps for macOS and Windows are available for offline work.

Can I import files from other design tools?expand_more

You can import SVG, Figma files, and Sketch documents. Layers, text styles, and vector shapes are preserved during import.

What does the CSS export include?expand_more

Exports generate clean custom properties, gradients, backdrop-filter rules, and reduced-motion fallbacks, ready to paste into any project.

Is there a free plan for students?expand_more

Students and educators get the Studio plan free for one year with a valid school email address, renewable while enrolled.

How does billing work for teams?expand_more

Seats are billed monthly or annually with a 20 percent annual discount. You can add or remove seats at any time and charges adjust automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes. Cancel from your workspace settings in two clicks. Your files remain accessible in read-only mode on the free plan.
