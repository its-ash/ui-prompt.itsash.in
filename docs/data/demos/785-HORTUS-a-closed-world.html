FAQ

Questions from curious keepers

You can, but you should not need to. Opening breaks the sealed balance, so we recommend enjoying it through the glass. If you ever unseal it, treat it like a normal potted plant and water lightly.

Condensation is the water cycle at work. Light mist on one side of the glass is healthy. If the glass is fully fogged for days, move the vessel a little farther from direct sun.

Most HORTUS worlds thrive for five years or more, and well-placed sealed terrariums have been documented living for decades. Slow-growing species are chosen specifically for longevity.

Bright, indirect light is ideal, such as a shelf near a north or east window. Avoid harsh direct sun, radiators, and cold drafts, which can overheat or chill the sealed climate.

Layered stone for drainage, activated charcoal to keep the soil fresh, a custom soil blend, and a planting of mosses, ferns, or tropicals matched to the vessel size. Everything is listed on your care card.
