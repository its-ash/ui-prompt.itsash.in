FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are your fees?expand_more

Maker fees start at 0.16% and taker fees at 0.26%, dropping automatically with your 30-day volume. Crypto deposits are always free, and every fee is shown before you confirm a trade.

How is my crypto kept secure?expand_more

About 95% of client funds are held in air-gapped cold storage across geo-distributed vaults. We are ISO 27001 certified, run SOC 2 audits, and publish PGP-signed proof-of-reserves every month.

Which deposit methods do you support?expand_more

Bank transfers (SEPA, Fedwire, SWIFT), debit cards, and Apple or Google Pay in supported regions — plus free crypto deposits on 40+ networks.

Which assets can I trade?expand_more

More than 200 assets, from BTC and ETH to SOL and major stablecoins, with new listings added weekly and an OTC desk for large block orders.

How can I contact support?expand_more

Live chat agents are available 24/7 in 12 languages with a median first response under two minutes, backed by a searchable help center and a public status page.

Is the exchange regulated?expand_more

Kraken Purple Exchange is registered or licensed in multiple jurisdictions, including FinCEN in the US and AUSTRAC in Australia, and client funds are fully segregated from company assets.
