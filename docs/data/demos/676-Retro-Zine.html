Questions from the mailbag

answered in plain ink

Do I need design software?expand_more

No. Every template works in our free browser editor, and you can also print the blanks and paste up by hand, then scan the pages back in.

How long does printing take?expand_more

Ten business days from file approval to your doorstep, anywhere in the US and EU. Rush runs of five days are available on the Underground tier.

Who owns my zine?expand_more

You do, completely. We never claim rights to your work, and you can pull your issue from shops and reprint it anywhere, any time.
