Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up a process?expand_more

Most teams map their first process in under 30 minutes. Import an SOP from Notion, Word, or Markdown and Nimbus turns it into editable steps automatically.

How is the radial timeline different from a Gantt chart?expand_more

A Gantt tracks time across projects; the arc tracks position within one running flow, showing the current step, its owner, and what remains at a glance.

Does Nimbus integrate with our existing tools?expand_more

Yes — Slack, Gmail, Jira, and Google Workspace work out of the box, and a REST API plus webhooks cover everything else.

What happens when we hit our plan limits?expand_more

Nothing breaks. Existing flows keep running and your admin is invited to review the next tier — your data is never locked away.

Is our data secure?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified, with EU or US data residency on Enterprise plans.
