Questions from the trail

Everything you might want to know before your first box ships.

Where do you ship?expand_more

We ship to over 60 countries with carbon-neutral carriers. Delivery usually takes 7 to 14 days depending on your region.

What is inside a wanderlust box?expand_more

Each box holds a rotating mix of textiles, ceramics, jewelry, and small home goods, plus a card introducing every maker.

Can I return an item?expand_more

Yes. Unused items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund, and we cover return shipping on damaged goods.
