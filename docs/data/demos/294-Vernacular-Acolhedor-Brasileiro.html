Questions, answered

Everything readers ask before pulling up a chair.

What kind of content do you publish?expand_more

Long-form house tours, artisan profiles, and hands-on technique guides — all photographed in real Brazilian homes and studios. We publish twice a week, with no paywalls on the Journal.

Is the Journal really free?expand_more

Yes — the Reader tier is free forever. Memberships exist to pay featured artisans a share and to fund workshops, never to lock the stories away.

Do you host live workshops?expand_more

Every month online, plus seasonal in-person ateliers held in a different region each time. Members get recordings; Atelier Circle patrons book their seats first.

Can I share my own home or craft?expand_more

We'd love that. Send a note through Contact with a few photos — reader houses and maker stories are featured in the Journal each month.

How do I cancel my membership?expand_more

One click from your account page — no email required. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and you can rejoin anytime.

Come sit with us

One slow letter each month: a house tour, a technique, and a maker's hands. No noise — only earth.
