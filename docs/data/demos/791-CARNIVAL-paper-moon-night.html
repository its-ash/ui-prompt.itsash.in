Questions About the Night

What should I wear to the carnival?

Casual attire is recommended. Comfortable shoes for walking the midway paths and light layers for evening breezes.

Are outside food or drinks allowed?

For safety and enjoyment of all guests, outside food and beverages are not permitted. However, we offer a variety of carnival treats and beverages throughout the midway.

Is the event accessible for wheelchairs?

Yes, all pathways are paved and wide enough for wheelchair access. Game booths have accessible options, and lantern release areas are designed for inclusivity.
