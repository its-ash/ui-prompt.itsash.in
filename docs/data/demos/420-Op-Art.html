Common questions

Do the patterns work in every browser?expand_more

Yes. OPTIKA outputs repeating linear and radial gradients, supported by all evergreen browsers. No Houdini, no canvas, no fallback images required.

How do patterns behave in dark mode?expand_more

Each export ships as a pair of custom properties. When your theme switches, the ink inverts from near-black to near-white and the optical effect is preserved.

What about motion sensitivity?expand_more

The Studio plan lints every pattern against photosensitivity guidelines and flags high-frequency fills. Reduced-motion exports swap vibrating bands for solid rules automatically.
