The city as a living canvas

Ninety artists, fourteen venues, one harbour city. The Meridian Biennale returns with three months of exhibitions, performances and public works.

Get Tickets View Programme

Three months, three currents

The programme unfolds across the city in three movements, each anchored in a different district of Rotterdam.

palette

Harbour Paintings

Large-scale canvases and murals respond to the working port, from the Maasvlakte terminals to the old warehouses of Katendrecht.

graphic_eq

Sound in the Open

Weekly open-air performances turn squares, bridges and rooftops into listening rooms, curated with Rotterdam's music venues.

architecture

Temporary Structures

Pavilions built from reclaimed harbour materials host talks, workshops and residencies, then return to the city's material cycle.

Plan your visit

One ticket covers every venue. The rest is a short walk, a tram ride, or a water taxi across the Maas.

01

Choose your pass

Day, season or evening passes are available online and at every venue desk throughout the biennale.

02

Follow the route

The printed programme maps a walking route linking all fourteen venues along the river and the old docks.

03

Stay for the evenings

After 18:00 the pavilions shift to performances, readings and late openings, all included in your pass.
