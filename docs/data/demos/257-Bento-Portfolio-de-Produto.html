FAQ

Practical questions, direct answers

How do we start working together?expand_more

A free 30-minute scoping call, then a fixed-rate proposal with timeline and deliverables within one week. If it fits, we kick off the next Monday.

Do you work with early-stage startups?expand_more

Yes — the Essential tier is built for pre-Series A teams. We prototype one core flow fast and validate it before engineering commits.

What does engineering handoff include?expand_more

Dev-mode Figma files, a documented token set, component specs, and two weeks of design QA after release.

Can you extend our existing design system?expand_more

Yes. I start with an audit, adopt your naming conventions, and contribute inside your library — no forks, no parallel systems.

What happens if we need to pause?expand_more

Pause anytime with 30 days' notice. Unused hours roll over for 30 days and your calendar slot stays warm for a quick restart.
