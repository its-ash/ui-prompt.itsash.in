[ 05 — FAQ ]

Answers, in plain type.

How far can I customize the grid?

expand_more

Columns, measure and baseline are numbers you set directly, not presets you unlock. Every edit propagates site-wide. The one thing MONO refuses is decoration: no gradients, no shadows, no motion for its own sake.

Can I use my own domain?

expand_more

Yes, on every paid plan. Point your DNS records at MONO and the domain resolves in under an hour. SSL certificates are provisioned and renewed automatically.

Can I export or migrate my site?

expand_more

Every site exports as clean HTML and CSS at any time. There are no proprietary formats and no lock-in. If you leave, your work leaves with you.

How hard is it to cancel?

expand_more

One click in settings, effective at the end of the billing period. No retention calls, no exit interviews.

Is MONO accessible out of the box?

expand_more

Default themes ship WCAG 2.1 AA compliant: contrast, focus order, reduced-motion behavior and semantic markup. An accessibility report for your site is regenerated on every publish.
