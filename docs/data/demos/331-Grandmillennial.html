Parlour Questions

Everything the front parlour gets asked, answered in one sitting.

How fresh are the blends when they arrive?

expand_more

We blend in forty-tin lots every Tuesday and post within two days, so your leaves are never more than a fortnight from the workroom table. Every tin is dated in ink, like a good letter.

Can I gift a subscription?

expand_more

Gladly — every parcel ships with a blank provenance card and a wax-seal kit so your handwriting does the gifting. Add a note at checkout and we'll tie it with ribbon.

What if I dislike a blend?

expand_more

Send one polite note and we'll swap next month's blend, no questions and no phone trees. Our blender answers letters personally, usually within a day.

Do you offer caffeine-free options?

expand_more

Our chamomile and rooibos blends are naturally caffeine-free, and the Rose Ruffle is pleasingly low. Each tin card lists caffeine by the teaspoon, in Nana's own format.
