Fine print, big answers.

Will it play in my deck?expand_more

Yes. Model SC-84 follows the JIS C-6432 standard, so it fits any compact cassette deck, boombox, or Walkman ever made.

What tape is inside?expand_more

Genuine Type-II chrome formulation, 60 minutes total. Set your deck to chrome EQ and 70μs for the full sparkle.

Can I choose my shell colors?expand_more

Solo and Twin orders pick from all six colorways at checkout. Full Set includes the complete rainbow, one of each.

How fast is shipping?expand_more

Orders ship within 3 business days in a protective mailer. Full Set orders ship free, worldwide.

What if my tape jams?expand_more

Every cassette carries a lifetime anti-jam warranty. Send it back and we replace it, no questions, no forms.
