Can I migrate existing design files?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus imports Figma and Sketch files directly, preserving layers, styles, and components so nothing gets lost in the move.

What happens when my trial ends?expand_more

Your workspace drops to the free Spark plan. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade again whenever you are ready.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?expand_more

Registered nonprofits and educators receive 50 percent off any paid plan. Write to our team with proof of status to apply.
