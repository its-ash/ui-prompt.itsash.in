05 FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I import boards from other tools?add

Yes. RASTER imports documents and task lists from common formats including CSV, Markdown, and PDF. Structure is mapped onto the grid automatically.

What happens when I cancel a paid plan?add

Your workspace moves to the Draft tier. Boards remain readable, version history is kept for 30 days, and you can export everything at any time.

Is my data stored in Europe?add

All workspace data is hosted in Zurich and Frankfurt data centers. Backups are encrypted and retained for 90 days before deletion.
