Answered directly

How long does a factory build take?add
From locked specification to handover, plan for 10 to 14 weeks depending on paint program and market. Your advisor confirms a dated build slot at order.
What does the warranty cover?add
Four years or 80,000 kilometers, whichever comes first, covering powertrain, chassis, and electronics. Track use within factory guidelines does not void coverage.
Can I service the car at any Nimbus dealer?add
Yes. Every authorized service center holds M Line certification, and your service history is stored centrally so any location sees the full record.
Is the delivery session mandatory?add
It is included, not required. Most owners take it: two hours on a closed course with a factory instructor, calibrated to your experience level.
What happens if my specification changes after ordering?add
Changes are accepted until your build slot locks, typically three weeks before assembly. After that, the specification is final and documented.
