Section 06 — Specifications

Frequently specified questions.

Q.01–Q.05

Q.01Does Nimbus connect to our existing warehouse and orchestrator?expand_more

Nimbus reads metadata — never row data — from Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Postgres, dbt and Airflow over read-only credentials. No agents to install, and sheets stay in sync with the live environment.

Q.02Can we export blueprints for docs and CI?expand_more

Every sheet exports to PDF with a proper title block, plus SVG, PNG and a versioned, machine-readable YAML. Most teams commit the YAML to git and let CI validate sheets on every pull request.

Q.03How does revision control actually work?expand_more

Every save is an immutable revision with author and timestamp. Diff any two revisions, branch a proposal for review, and tag a release — REV 2026.02, say — once it is signed off.

Q.04Is our metadata secure?expand_more

Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II. Metadata is encrypted in transit and at rest, SSO/SAML ships with Firm, and the full service can run inside your VPC. We never execute queries against your data.

Q.05Can the whole team work on one sheet?expand_more

Sheets are multiplayer: analysts draft, architects review, and comments pin to symbols like redlines on a drawing. Role-based permissions keep the sign-off block honest.
