How a Commission Proceeds

From first audience to final burnishing, your project moves through three ceremonial stages.

  1. I

    The Audience

    A private consultation at your residence or our salon, where ambitions are measured and sketches begin.

  2. II

    The Atelier

    Carvers, gilders and ornamentalists labour in concert; you receive weekly illuminated progress folios.

  3. III

    The Unveiling

    Installation by our own hands, concluded with a candlelit reveal and a signed certificate of provenance.

Enquiries, Answered

Do you work outside Europe?

expand_more

Yes. Our ateliers travel with their tools and leaf; recent commissions include Charleston, Kyoto and Buenos Aires. Travel is arranged within your patronage tier.

How long does a full room commission take?

expand_more

A drawing room typically requires four to seven months, depending on carving density and gilding coverage. You receive a dated schedule at the Audience stage.

Can you restore existing giltwork?

expand_more

Conservation is half our practice. We match historical gesso recipes and leaf karat, and document every intervention for your estate records.
