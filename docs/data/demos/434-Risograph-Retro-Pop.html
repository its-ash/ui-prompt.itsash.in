Straight Answers

The questions every first-time riso printer asks, answered plainly.

Do I need to own a risograph machine?add

No. Nimbus simulates the full process digitally. If you want physical copies, our partner print shops run real riso presses and ship directly to you.

Why only three ink colors per project?add

That is how real riso printing works: one drum pass per color. Limiting plates keeps your files honest and your prints affordable, and overprints give you far more than three visible colors.

Can I use my own paper textures?add

Yes. Scan any stock at 300 DPI or higher and import it. Paid plans can save custom textures to a shared library for your whole team.

What file formats can I export?add

Press-ready PDF with separated plates, plus PNG and TIFF for digital use. Color separations are labeled per ink so any print shop can run them.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?add

Yes. Plans are month to month with no lock-in. Your projects stay readable on the free tier after canceling, and exports you already made are yours forever.
