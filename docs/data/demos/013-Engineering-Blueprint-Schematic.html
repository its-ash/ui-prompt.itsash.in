FIG. 06 — CLARIFICATIONS

TECHNICAL INQUIRIES

HOW DOES NIMBUS HANDLE SCHEMA DRIFT?expand_more

Every pipeline declares a schema contract at build time. Drift is detected on ingest, the offending batch is quarantined, and the quality gate alerts your channel of choice before downstream consumers are affected.

CAN NIMBUS DEPLOY INTO OUR OWN VPC?expand_more

Yes. Enterprise plans run Nimbus fully inside your cloud account — no data leaves your perimeter, and the control plane can be air-gapped for regulated environments.

WHAT DOES THE FREE DRAFT TIER INCLUDE?expand_more

Two pipelines, one project, and seven days of telemetry retention — enough to prototype a real production schematic before you commit to a paid spec.

WHICH SOURCES AND SINKS ARE SUPPORTED?expand_more

Postgres, Kafka, S3, Snowflake, BigQuery, and any REST or gRPC endpoint via the connector SDK. New adapters ship every sprint.

HOW FAST IS A ROLLBACK?expand_more

Rollbacks are metadata operations, not reprocessing jobs. Most complete in under 30 seconds and are triggered automatically when a quality gate trips.
