Curious minds ask

A few honest answers before you plant your first task.

Is Daisy Days really free to start?expand_more

Yes. The Sprout plan is free forever with no card required. You can plan one day at a time, grow a weekly garden, and upgrade only if you want week-ahead planning or sync.

What happens when I miss a task?expand_more

Nothing scary. Unfinished tasks quietly roll into tomorrow's sketch. There are no streaks to break, no red badges, and no scolding. Your garden keeps growing at your pace.

Can I use Daisy Days with my family?expand_more

The Meadow plan covers up to six people with shared plans, chore petals, and one big family garden. Everyone keeps their own morning sketch while pitching in on the same patch.
