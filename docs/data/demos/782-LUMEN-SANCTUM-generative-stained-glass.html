Frequently Illuminated Questions

Do I need artistic or technical experience?

None at all. Lumen Sanctum is designed for complete beginners while offering depth for professionals. The algorithm handles the complexity—you guide the vision.

Can I use creations commercially?

Yes! All paid plans include a commercial license. Free trial creations are for personal use only. Check our Terms of Service for full details.

How does the light simulation work?

We simulate sunlight at configurable times of day and angles, calculating how light refracts through virtual glass and lead lines to cast accurate colored shadows and highlights.

What file formats can I export?

PNG (up to 4K), SVG vector, and layered PSD (Artisan and Master plans). All exports maintain transparency for easy overlay on any background.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your creations and prompts are stored encrypted on our servers. We never use your data for training. Read our Privacy Policy for full transparency.
