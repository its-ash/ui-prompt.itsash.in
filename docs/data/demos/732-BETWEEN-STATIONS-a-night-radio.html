Common questions

Asked by listeners, answered by the booth.

Is it really free?expand_more

Yes. The Static tier costs nothing and asks for no card. One rotating frequency, the live stream, and a 30-minute drift-off timer, for as long as you like.

What plays at 3 AM?expand_more

The deepest block of the night: slow records, long ambient pieces, and the quietest host segments. Around 4 AM the dial warms back up toward First Light.

Can I download broadcasts?expand_more

Signal and Shortwave listeners can save any broadcast for offline listening. Downloads stay in the app and travel with you, no connection required.

How does the drift-off timer work?expand_more

Pick a window from 15 minutes to 2 hours. The volume lowers gradually, the music thins to static, and the stream closes itself so you never wake to a dead phone.

Which devices are supported?expand_more

Any modern browser, plus iOS and Android apps. CarPlay and Android Auto are supported, and Shortwave adds a lossless desktop player.
