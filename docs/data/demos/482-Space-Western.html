Asked at every port

Straight answers before you sign the manifest.

How fast is a standard cargo run?expand_more

Inner-system runs land within two days. Rim lanes take four to nine depending on drift and port traffic, and the live chart shows the honest estimate the whole way.

What freight will you not carry?expand_more

Unmarked volatile cargo and anything impounded by port authority. Everything else, from grain to reactor parts, gets a hold and a stamp.

What happens if a run is delayed?expand_more

Dispatch contacts you before the delay hits the chart. Miss the contracted window and that run ships at half rate, no claim forms required.

Can I track my freight in flight?expand_more

Yes. Every manifest includes a live star-chart link with position, lane conditions, and the crew's next check-in time.

Do you fly passenger berths too?expand_more

Marshal and Syndicate tiers include up to four passenger berths per run, with the same insurance and the same honest charts.
