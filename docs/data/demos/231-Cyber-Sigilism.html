FAQ

Questions under oath

Can Nimbus staff read anything I store?expand_more

No. All encryption and decryption happens in your browser. Our servers hold ciphertext, and the keys are sharded on your devices. There is no master key, no backdoor, and no way for us to open a seal even under legal pressure.

What happens if I lose my key?expand_more

At setup you appoint guardians — teammates or offline copies — who each hold a shard. Any quorum you define can re-mint your key. If you lose the key and the quorum, the workspace stays sealed forever. That is the trade for true secrecy.

Does vanishing ink really delete everything?expand_more

Yes. Expired content is cryptographically erased: ciphertext is overwritten, key shards are destroyed, and share links return a dead seal. Our logs never contained plaintext to begin with, so there is nothing left to leak.
