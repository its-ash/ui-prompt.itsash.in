Common questions

Quick answers before you dive in.

Do I need to install anything?expand_more

No. Vectordelia runs entirely in your browser on any modern device. Your canvases sync to your account, so you can pick up where you left off anywhere.

Can I import my existing SVG files?expand_more

Yes. Drag any SVG onto the canvas and it becomes fully editable, including paths, groups, and gradients. AI and PDF imports are available on Studio and Team plans.

What happens if I cancel my plan?expand_more

You keep every file you created. Paid features lock, but your canvases remain viewable and exportable as SVG and PNG forever.
