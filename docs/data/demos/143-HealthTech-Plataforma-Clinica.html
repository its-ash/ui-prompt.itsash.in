Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything teams usually ask before switching.

How does Nimbus keep patient data secure?expand_more

All records are encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by role-based access controls and full audit logs. Nimbus runs on HIPAA-ready infrastructure and undergoes independent penetration testing twice a year.

Can patients reschedule or cancel on their own?expand_more

Yes. Patients can move or cancel any appointment from the portal up to 24 hours in advance, and Nimbus automatically offers freed slots to your waitlist — no phone tag required.

What do I need for a telemedicine visit?expand_more

Just a smartphone or computer with a camera and a stable connection. Visits run in the browser — no app downloads for patients, one-click session links for clinicians.
