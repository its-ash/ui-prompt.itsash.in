Questions about the questions

Everything people ask before their first reading.

How accurate are the readings?chevron_down
ORACLE is a mirror, not a map. Its answers are crafted to sharpen your own judgment, and most readers tell us the right answer is the one that feels true on first reading.
Is my data private?chevron_down
Yes. Questions are processed on your device and never uploaded, sold, or shared. Your reading history is encrypted and visible only to you.
What can I ask the orb?chevron_down
Anything you would ask a trusted friend: work, love, timing, change. We suggest one honest question at a time rather than a list.
Which devices are supported?chevron_down
ORACLE runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet, and desktop. The physical marble orb pairs over Bluetooth with our iOS and Android apps.
Can I cancel my subscription?chevron_down
Anytime, in two taps, from your account page. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period and your history remains yours.
Is this serious or just for fun?chevron_down
Both, honestly. ORACLE is an entertainment product built with unusual care. Treat it as a ritual for reflection and it will reward you either way.
