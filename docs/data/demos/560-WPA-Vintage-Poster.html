Questions, Answered Plainly

Do I need an account to use the maps?expand_more

No account is needed for the free Rambler maps. An account is only required to save itineraries or download offline packets.

Are the routes kept up to date?expand_more

Yes. Traveler reports are reviewed weekly during the season, and closures or washouts are flagged within days of being confirmed.

Can I use Parkways without cell service?expand_more

Explorer and Ranger plans include offline trip packets you can download before you leave home and carry the whole journey.

What if I need to cancel?expand_more

Cancel any time from your account page. Your plan stays active through the paid month, and your saved trips remain yours.

Is Parkways a government service?expand_more

No. We are an independent company of park lovers. Our artwork honors the WPA poster tradition, but we are not affiliated with any agency.
