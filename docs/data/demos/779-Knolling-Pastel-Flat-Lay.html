Common questions

Straight answers, kept in order.

Do I need photography experience to use Knoll & Co.?chevron_right

No. The board, palettes, and shot guides are built for beginners. If you can arrange objects on a table, you can plan a flat lay here.

Can I export a plan to use at a real shoot?chevron_right

Yes. Every board exports as a placement map with measurements, so you can recreate the layout on your table exactly.

What is included in the free Desk plan?chevron_right

Three active boards, the core pastel library, and placement map exports. It stays free with no time limit.

Can I share boards with clients?chevron_right

Studio and Atelier plans include sharing links. Clients can view and comment on a board without an account.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?chevron_right

Yes. Cancel from your account settings in two clicks. Your boards remain available on the free Desk plan.
