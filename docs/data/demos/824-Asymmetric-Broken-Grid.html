05, FAQ

Straight answers, crooked layout

Q1Does OFFSET work for beginners?expand_more

Yes. The canvas starts from a normal grid and suggests safe offsets, so you learn asymmetry with guardrails instead of guesswork.

Q2Will rotated elements break on mobile?expand_more

No. Tilts and overlaps are reduced automatically below tablet widths, and every export is checked for horizontal overflow down to 320px.

Q3Can I use my own fonts and tokens?expand_more

Studio and Team plans let you upload brand fonts and define color, spacing, and shadow tokens that apply across every project.

Q4How does the legibility guard work?expand_more

It flags rotations past 1.5 degrees on body text, overlaps covering interactive elements, and contrast below WCAG AA, before you publish.

Q5Can I export to code?expand_more

Yes. Export clean HTML and CSS with custom properties, or hand off specs to developers with exact offset and rotation values.

Q6What is the cancellation policy?expand_more

Cancel anytime from your billing page. Paid plans stay active until the end of the cycle, and your projects remain accessible on the Draft plan.
