Questions, answered

How realistic do the voices actually sound?expand_more

In blind listener tests, most people cannot reliably tell Auralis narration from a human read. Prosody, breath, and pacing are modeled per sentence, not stitched from clips.

Who owns a cloned voice?expand_more

You do. Clones are private to your account, require recorded consent, and can be deleted at any time. We never train shared models on your voice.

Can I use the audio commercially?expand_more

Yes. Paid plans include a full commercial license for ads, audiobooks, courses, and products. The Free plan is for personal and evaluation use.

What formats can I export?expand_more

MP3 at up to 320 kbps and uncompressed WAV at 44.1 kHz. Stems per paragraph are available on Creator and Pro.

Do you offer an API?expand_more

Pro plans include a REST API with streaming synthesis, usage-based overage pricing, and per-key rate limits.
