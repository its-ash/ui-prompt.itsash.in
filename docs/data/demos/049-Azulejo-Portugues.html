From blank biscuit to glazed tile

Three unhurried hours in our sunlit Alfama studio.

  1. 01

    Choose Your Pattern

    Browse our archive of 400 historical motifs — pombais, caravels, garlands — or sketch your own with our artists.

  2. 02

    Paint with a Master

    Grind the cobalt pigment, load the brush, and follow stroke by stroke as an artisan demonstrates each wash.

  3. 03

    Kiln & Collect

    We fire and glaze your work overnight. Collect it in 48 hours, or have it shipped home in museum-grade packing.

Questions, answered

None at all. Around 80% of our guests have never held a brush. The master demonstrates each wash before you try it, and the cobalt pigment is remarkably forgiving on biscuit tile.

Firing and glazing take about 24 hours. Collect your tile at the studio after 48 hours, or we ship anywhere in the EU within 5 working days.

Yes — from age 8 upwards, accompanied by an adult. We set dedicated family tables with simplified floral patterns and non-toxic pigments.

We ship worldwide in museum-grade cork and foam packing, fully insured and tracked. Overseas delivery typically arrives within two weeks.
