The Enquiries

Questions of the Salon

How long does a commission take?expand_more
A single gilded object demands six to twelve weeks of handwork; full chamber campaigns unfold across several months, with each movement announced in writing.
Do you deliver beyond the continent?expand_more
Yes. Every commission travels in a purpose-built crate with a white-glove courier, insured to full value and installed by our own hands.
Will you restore pieces not born in the Maison?expand_more
Gladly. Our conservators revive ornament of any provenance, documenting every intervention so the piece's history remains honest.
Is the gold leaf truly gold?expand_more
Always 24 karats, laid over clay bole and burnished with an agate stone — exactly as the seventeenth century demanded.
What happens at a private audience?expand_more
An evening at the atelier: candlelight, gilding demonstrations, and a unhurried conversation about what your chamber deserves.
