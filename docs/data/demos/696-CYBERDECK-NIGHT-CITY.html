Frequently asked

Straight answers, no fine print.

Does the deck need an internet account to activate?expand_more
No. Activation is fully offline. Your identity keys live on a removable chip that never touches our servers, and firmware updates verify by signature, not login.
What happens when I pull the kill switch?expand_more
The secure element erases every key in under two seconds and the storage controller wipes its encryption headers. The hardware survives; the data does not.
Can I repair or upgrade the deck myself?expand_more
Yes. The chassis opens with one tool, the cell and memory are socketed, and we publish full schematics. Repairs never void your warranty.
