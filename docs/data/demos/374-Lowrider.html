Questions From the Cruise

Where does lowriding come from?expand_more

Southern California, late 1940s. Mexican-American builders dropped their cars on chopped springs — and by the mid-'60s, hydraulic pumps — to cruise low and slow down Whittier Boulevard. We build in that tradition, and we name the pioneers who paved it.

How long does a candy paint job take?expand_more

Eight to twelve weeks. Every coat must cure and get cut before the next goes on; rush a candy lay and it telegraphs every shortcut under show lighting. Good candy takes patience — we bring ours.

How do I care for fresh candy paint?expand_more

Hand wash only, pH-neutral soap, two buckets, and a mitt that never touches the wheels. Park in shade, wax by hand each season, and keep it far from drive-through brushes. Treat it like the heirloom it is.
