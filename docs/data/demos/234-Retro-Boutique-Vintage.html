Frequently Asked

The fine print, in plain English.

How do you grade a garment’s condition?expand_more

Every piece is inspected stitch by stitch and graded from “Museum” (near-original) to “Well-Loved” (honest wear). The grade, true measurements, and any repairs are listed on its authenticity card.

How does vintage sizing work?expand_more

Vintage sizes rarely match modern labels. We list true measurements for every garment, and your stylist pulls pieces cut for your exact figure — alterations close any remaining gap.

What is your returns policy?expand_more

Unworn pieces return within 14 days for a full refund, care tags still attached. Altered and final-sale estate pieces are always noted clearly before you buy.
