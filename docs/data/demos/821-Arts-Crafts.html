Common Inquiries

How are the materials sourced? expand_more
We partner exclusively with sustainable forests and local foragers to ensure no harm comes to the landscape.
What is the typical lead time? expand_more
True craft cannot be rushed. Depending on complexity, pieces take between 4 to 12 weeks.
Do you offer custom commissions? expand_more
Yes, Guild Master members receive priority for bespoke commissions tailored to their space.
Is the ink truly organic? expand_more
We use traditional oak gall and iron-based inks, exactly as they were in the 19th century.
How do I care for my pieces? expand_more
A simple application of beeswax every six months is all that is required to maintain the luster.
