FAQ

How does the zero-lag engine work?

add
Our proprietary cycle-exact processor synchronizes with your display's refresh rate, eliminating input buffering through direct hardware-level interrupt handling. Each button press registers within 1ms of physical actuation.

Can I use my existing game controllers?

add
Absolutely! We support all classic 9-pin Atari-style joysticks via USB adapter, plus modern Bluetooth controllers through our universal input mapper. Configure up to 4 simultaneous players.

Is there a limit to how many games I can create?

add
No limits whatsoever. Our procedural generation system combined with your creativity creates infinite possibilities. Share your creations via our community hub with one-click publishing.

What platforms are supported?

add
We run on any device with a modern web browser - Windows, macOS, Linux, Raspberry Pi, and even retrofitted arcade cabinets. No installation required; instant play through HTML5.

How do I access my creations offline?

add
Export your projects as standalone .html files that run anywhere. Includes all assets and the zero-lag engine - perfect for preserving your work or sharing with friends without internet dependency.
