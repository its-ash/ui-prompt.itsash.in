Common questions

The short answers, before you ask support.

What can MONOLITH monitor?expand_more

HTTP and HTTPS endpoints, TCP ports, DNS records, TLS certificate expiry, and multi-step synthetic flows such as login or checkout. Each monitor can run from any subset of our 40+ regions.

How is uptime calculated?expand_more

Uptime is the share of successful checks over total checks per period, weighted by region. Degraded responses count as partial availability, so your numbers match what users actually experienced.

Which alert channels are supported?expand_more

Slack, PagerDuty, Opsgenie, email, SMS and generic webhooks. Escalation policies let you page the primary on-call first, then fall back to a secondary after a configurable delay.
