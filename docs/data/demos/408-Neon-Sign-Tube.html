Questions we hear at the bench

How long does a custom sign take?expand_more

Desk Glow signs ship in two weeks. Wall Statement pieces take three, and venue-scale work runs four to six depending on complexity. Rush slots open up most months; ask when you order.

Can my sign live outdoors?expand_more

Yes. We seal the electrodes and spec outdoor-rated transformers for exterior installs. Outdoor builds add a modest weatherproofing fee, quoted up front in your rendering.

How much power does neon actually draw?expand_more

Less than most people expect. A typical 36-inch sign draws about as much as a 60-watt bulb, and the included dimmer lets you run it softer at night.

Can you bend my business logo?expand_more

Almost always. Fine serifs and tight corners sometimes need small adjustments for the glass to flow, and we flag those in your proof before any bending starts.

What does the warranty cover?expand_more

Five years on tubes and transformers against defects, plus lifetime repair at cost. If a sign arrives broken, we rebuild and reship it free, no claim forms.
