Straight Answers

Do I need a fancy tablet?expand_more

Nope. Nimbus paints happily with a finger, mouse or trackpad. A tablet just makes the fat caps feel extra buttery.

Who owns my pieces?expand_more

You do — one hundred percent. We never sell, train on, or buff your work without your say-so.

Can I sell my art?expand_more

Always. Every export is print-ready, and Legends can open a commission shop right on their wall.

Is it classroom-safe?expand_more

Yes. Private walls and a moderated feed mean kids can paint big without the mess or the fumes.

What happens if I cancel?expand_more

Nothing dramatic. Your walls stay viewable for 90 days, and everything you exported is yours forever.
