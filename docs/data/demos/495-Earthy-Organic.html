Questions, answered plainly

The details most brands ask about before their first run.

Are your materials certified compostable?expand_more

Yes. Every board, film, and adhesive we use carries EN 13432 or ASTM D6400 certification, and most lines break down in a home compost bin within 180 days.

What is the minimum order quantity?expand_more

Seedling runs start at 250 units, which suits most small batch launches. Custom dielines begin at 1,000 units so we can amortize the hand tooling fairly.

Can you match our existing brand colors?expand_more

Our botanical ink library covers most Pantone references within a close delta. We mail printed drawdowns for your approval before any full run begins.
