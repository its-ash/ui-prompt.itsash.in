Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions traders ask us most.

How does Kraken keep my assets safe?expand_more

95% of client assets are held in geographically distributed cold storage. Accounts require two-factor authentication, and our security operations center monitors the platform around the clock. Kraken is ISO 27001 certified.

What are the trading fees?expand_more

Spot fees start at 0.25% on Starter and drop to 0.02% on Pro, with further volume-based discounts. Crypto deposits are always free, and fiat deposits via bank transfer carry no Kraken fee.

How long do withdrawals take?expand_more

Crypto withdrawals are processed within minutes after security checks. Fiat withdrawals arrive in one to three business days depending on your bank and region.

What do I need for identity verification?expand_more

A government-issued photo ID and a short liveness check. Most verifications complete in under five minutes, and higher account tiers may ask for proof of address.

Which regions does Kraken support?expand_more

Kraken serves clients in more than 190 countries. A small number of jurisdictions are restricted due to local regulation; check our Help Center for the current list.

Is support really available 24/7?expand_more

Yes. Live chat and email support run 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with median first response times under two minutes for Trader and Pro plans.
