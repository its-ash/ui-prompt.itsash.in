How the year turns

Three simple motions carry the tree from spring sap to winter sleep.

1

Wake the sap

Press the root base and the trunk warms to spring, pushing tiny felt buds through the branches.

2

Turn the canopy

Rotate the crown a quarter turn. Blossoms fold away and wooden fruit swell into place for picking.

3

Let it rest

Tip the tree gently back and the leaves drop into the base tray, ready to begin the year again.

Questions from the fence line

Everything families usually ask before bringing a tree home.

The turning mechanism suits hands from about age three, and the seasonal storytelling grows with children up to eight. Younger toddlers enjoy it with a grown-up turning the canopy.

Trunks are cut from responsibly harvested beech, canopies from birch ply, and buds from undyed wool felt. Every surface is finished with food-safe linseed oil and beeswax.

Each wooden fruit is larger than standard small-parts test cylinders and locks magnetically into the canopy. The set meets EN71 and ASTM F963 toy safety standards.

The first pressing leaves the workshop in early autumn, timed so your tree arrives as real orchards begin their harvest. We send a postcard when your tree is grafted.
