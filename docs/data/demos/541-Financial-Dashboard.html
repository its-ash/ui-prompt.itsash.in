Common questions

All data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. LedgerPulse is SOC 2 Type II certified, and we never sell or share your financial records.
Which systems does LedgerPulse integrate with?expand_more
We connect natively to QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, SAP, Stripe, and over 12,000 banks. CSV and spreadsheet imports work for everything else.
Can I export the audit trail for my auditor?expand_more
Yes. Export the complete immutable trail, including timestamps, users, and amounts, as CSV or PDF at any time. Enterprise plans also offer a dedicated export API.

Dashboards are unlimited on every plan. Starter includes 5 seats, Growth includes 25, and Enterprise seats are tailored to your organization.
