01, Features

Built for close reading

F.01 tune

Live variable axes

Drag weight, width, and optical size while the specimen redraws in real time. Every intermediate instance renders at full fidelity, no interpolation lag.

F.02 format_letter_spacing

Kerning inspection

Flag tight pairs with vermilion overlays. Step through every kern in the font, compare against your own adjustments, and measure side bearings numerically.

Instant proofs

Waterfalls, pangrams, and full character sets on demand. Export print-ready PDFs, PNG sheets, or CSS tokens with your axis values baked in.

05, FAQ

Fine print

Which font formats are supported?add

TTF, OTF, WOFF, and WOFF2. Variable fonts load with every registered and custom axis exposed as a slider.

Do my font files leave my machine?add

No. Files are parsed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly share a proof with your team.

Can I proof fonts I have licensed elsewhere?add

Yes, provided your license permits desktop or web preview use. GRAVITY does not modify or redistribute the font file.

What can I export?add

Print-ready PDF specimen sheets, PNG waterfalls and pangrams, and CSS custom properties carrying your exact axis settings.

What is the refund policy?add

Cancel any time. Paid plans carry a 14-day full refund window, no questions and no retention calls.
