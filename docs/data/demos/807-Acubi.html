05 / FAQ

Common queries

Pieces run true to a slim Korean block. If you sit between sizes, take the larger one. Full measurements are published per garment, not per letter size.

We ship to 42 countries from Seoul. Duties are calculated at checkout, so the price you see is the price you pay. Delivery runs two to six business days.

Thirty days, unworn, tags attached. Chrome and Archive members get sixty days and a prepaid label. Refunds land within five business days of receipt.

Cold wash, inside out, no softener, hang dry. The brushed finish is durable but heat is its one enemy. Never tumble dry, never iron directly.

Every piece carries a woven serial tag linked to our registry. Enter the code on the archive page to confirm provenance and production run.
