What we do

Three disciplines, one cloth

Every commission moves through design, audit, and research, so the finished flag carries meaning instead of decoration.

Custom Flag Design

From first brief to final artwork, we draw flags that a child can sketch from memory. You receive refined vector proposals, each with written rationale you can defend in a council meeting.

Vexillological Audit

We review existing or proposed flags against the classic principles: simple, meaningful, few colors, no lettering, legible at a distance. You get a scored report with concrete fixes, not vague taste.

history_edu

Symbol & Heritage Research

Charges and colors carry stories. We dig through archives, heraldic rolls, and local history so every element on your flag earns its place and survives public scrutiny.

How a commission unfolds

A clear three-stage path, typically eight to twelve weeks from first call to final files.

01

Brief & Field Research

We interview stakeholders and study your history, geography, and existing symbols. The brief ends with agreed criteria everyone signs off on.

02

Design Iterations

Sketches become refined vector proposals, each tested at distance and in grayscale. Every round arrives with written rationale, so decisions stay objective.

03

Production-Ready Handover

You receive master vectors, ratio variants, and a color and usage guide. Any flag maker in the world can sew from our files without calling us.

Questions from the workbench

Most projects run eight to twelve weeks from signed brief to final handover. Research-heavy civic commissions can stretch to sixteen weeks when archives are involved. We agree on a calendar before any drawing begins, and we have never missed a handover date.

You receive master vectors in SVG, PDF, and EPS, plus ratio variants for 1:2, 2:3, and 3:5 proportions. The usage guide specifies Pantone, CMYK, RGB, and hex values, along with minimum sizes and clear-space rules. Everything is organized so any flag maker or printer can work from it unaided.

You do, fully and forever. On final payment we transfer all rights, including reproduction and modification. We retain only the right to show the work in our portfolio, and we waive even that for clients with confidentiality needs.

Roughly two thirds of our commissions are remote, across eleven time zones so far. Interviews and presentations happen over video calls, and all deliverables ship digitally. For heritage research we partner with local archivists in your region rather than flying ourselves in.

Each tier includes a set number of revision rounds, and a round means a full pass on one concept, not a single tweak. If we exhaust the rounds and the flag still is not right, additional rounds are billed at a fixed rate quoted in advance. We would rather redraw than let a weak flag leave the studio.
