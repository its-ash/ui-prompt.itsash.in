Common questions

How long does setup take?expand_more
Most teams connect their first data source and see live metrics within a few hours. Pre-built templates for revenue, churn, and pipeline mean you start from a working dashboard, not a blank page.
Is my data secure?expand_more
Nimbus Insights uses read-only credentials, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and never stores raw row-level data longer than needed to compute your metrics. Enterprise plans add SSO, audit logs, and regional data residency.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime?expand_more
Yes. Plans are month to month with no lock-in. Upgrades apply immediately, and downgrades or cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
