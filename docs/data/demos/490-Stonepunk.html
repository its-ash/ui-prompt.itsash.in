Questions from the fire circle

Everything tribes ask before trading their shells. Still curious? Send a raven to our support cave.

Do I need to invent the wheel first?expand_more

No. RockWorks ships with two wheels included, pre-rounded. You may admire them, roll them ceremonially, or use them for actual transport.

What happens if my stone slab cracks?expand_more

Every board is backed up to a second, secret slab stored in a dry cave. Cracks affect only the aesthetic, never your data.

Can I pay in something other than shells?expand_more

We accept shells, polished bones, and all major credit cards. Livestock is considered on a case-by-case basis at the Mountain tier.

Is there a free trial of the paid tiers?expand_more

Yes. Boulder and Mountain include a full moon cycle of free use. If you are not moving more boulders by the next full moon, pay nothing.

How do smoke signals work in the rain?expand_more

Tribe Chat automatically reroutes messages through our waterproof drum network during storms. Delivery times may vary with drum enthusiasm.
