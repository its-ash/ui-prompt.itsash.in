05 / FAQ

Ask the grid.

How does the grid score work?expand_more

Your brand rules are weighted into a single 0–100 score. Spacing, contrast, type scale, and alignment each pull their weight, and every deduction links to the exact rule that fired.

Do I have to change my design tools?expand_more

No. GRIDPULSE reads your staging URL or Figma frames directly. No plugins, no migrations, no new workflow to learn.

Is the free plan really free?expand_more

Yes. Solo is free forever with one project and live grid scores. We only charge when your team and your screen count grow.

Can I export reports for stakeholders?expand_more

On Team and Scale, yes. One click produces audit-ready PDFs and share links that turn review meetings into score reviews.

Do you store our screens?expand_more

We store encrypted renders only, never source files. Delete everything with one click, any time, and it is gone for good.
