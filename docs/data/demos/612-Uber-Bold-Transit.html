Common questions

How is the upfront fare calculated?expand_more
We price the route at booking using distance, expected travel time, and current demand. Once confirmed, the fare is locked and will not change, even if the trip takes longer than expected.
Can I schedule a ride in advance?expand_more
Yes. You can book a pickup up to seven days ahead. We confirm your driver 30 minutes before the scheduled time and send a reminder with the pickup point.
What happens if my driver cancels?expand_more
A new driver is matched automatically at the same locked fare. If no driver is available within ten minutes, the trip is free of charge and you are notified right away.
