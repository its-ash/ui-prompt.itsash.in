How It Works

Three simple steps to see emergent order from microscopic randomness.

01

Heat it up

Raise the temperature past the critical point to dissolve magnetic domains and maximize entropy.

02

Nudge the spins

Flip individual spins; nearest‑neighbor coupling causes clusters to grow or shrink.

03

Cool it down

Lower the temperature to lock in aligned domains, observing spontaneous symmetry breaking.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common curiosities.

The Ising model is a mathematical model of ferromagnetism in statistical mechanics. It consists of discrete variables called spins that can be in one of two states (+1 or −1). The spins are arranged on a lattice, allowing each spin to interact with its nearest neighbors. The model captures phase transitions and emergent magnetic order.
