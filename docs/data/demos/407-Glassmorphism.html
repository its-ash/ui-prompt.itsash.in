Questions, answered

The details teams usually ask about before switching.

How does the free trial work?expand_more

Every plan starts with 14 days of full Team features. No card is required, and you can downgrade to Starter at any point without losing your data.

Can I import from Trello, Asana, or Jira?expand_more

Yes. The guided importer maps boards, assignees, and due dates in a few clicks. Most workspaces finish migrating in under ten minutes.

Is my data private and secure?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified, and Enterprise plans add SSO, audit logs, and regional data residency.

What happens if I cancel my plan?expand_more

Your workspace moves to the free Starter tier with everything intact. You can export a full archive of your data at any time, no questions asked.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?expand_more

Registered nonprofits and educational institutions receive 50% off any paid plan. Reach out to our team with proof of status to apply.
