Matters of Etiquette

Frequently Asked

Must I dress in period costume?expand_more

Full costume is admired, never required. Most members arrive in something romantic and comfortable; a few arrive in breeches, and we love them for it.

Are the letters genuinely handwritten?expand_more

Every one. Our secretaries take dictation by candlelight and post by the morrow. You may keep a nom de plume if you wish your admirers discreet.

Where are the gatherings held?expand_more

In letted townhouses, conservatories, and gardens in each city we serve. Venues are announced to members a fortnight before each engagement.

May I withdraw from the club?expand_more

Any member may take leave of the Season at month's end — no questions asked, no scandal incurred, and a warm welcome should you return.

Is my identity kept discreet?expand_more

Titles and pseudonyms are equally welcome. We guard our members' secrets as fiercely as our own, which is to say, fiercely indeed.
