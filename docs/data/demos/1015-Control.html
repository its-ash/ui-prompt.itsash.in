How it works

Three steps to a working grid.

Step 01

Set your columns

Pick a column count, gutter, and outer margin. AXIS previews the grid live as you adjust.

Step 02

Snap your type

Choose a baseline and line-height. Headings and body align to the same vertical rhythm.

Step 03

Export and ship

Copy the CSS or download tokens. Drop them into your project and move on.
