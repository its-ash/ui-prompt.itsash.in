How to Prepare Your Matcha

Three mindful steps to transform powder into a moment of calm.

01

Measure & Sift

Use 1g (½ tsp) of matcha powder. Sift into bowl to prevent clumps for smooth texture.

02

Whisk Vigorously

Add 60ml hot (not boiling) water. Whisk in 'W' motion until frothy (15-20 seconds).

03

Sip Mindfully

Enjoy immediately. Notice the umami sweetness and gradual lift in focus and clarity.

We partner with certified organic farms in Uji, Japan. Each batch undergoes third-party testing for heavy metals, pesticides, and radiation. Our matcha is stone-ground in small batches to preserve nutrients and vibrant color.

Matcha contains about 20-30mg caffeine per gram (vs. 80-100mg in coffee). Combined with L-theanine, it provides calm alertness without jitters or crash — ideal for sustained focus.

Traditionally, a bamboo whisk (chasen) and bowl (chawan) are used. For convenience, a small whisk or frother works well. We include a bamboo whisk with our Ritual and Connoisseur plans.

High-grade ceremonial matcha offers a rich umami flavor with natural sweetness and minimal bitterness. Notes of seaweed, spinach, and a smooth, creamy finish characterize quality matcha.
