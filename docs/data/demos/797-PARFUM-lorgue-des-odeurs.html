The Three Notes

A perfume unfolds in movements. Each note has its own voice, its own moment, its own role in the score.

graphic_eq

Top Notes

The opening chord. Bright citrus, green aromatics, and sparkling aldehydes strike first, shaping the impression you leave in a room before a word is spoken.

favorite

Heart Notes

The melody. Florals, spices, and ripe fruit emerge as the top fades, carrying the character of the composition for the hours that matter most.

layers

Base Notes

The resonance. Woods, musks, and amber settle into the skin and hold the final chord, a trail that lingers long after you have gone.

From Keyboard to Flacon

Three movements take your composition from first idea to the bottle on your shelf.

1

Choose Your Notes

Browse the organ's register of over sixty raw materials and select the voices that sound like you.

2

Play the Composition

Our perfumers balance your selection, then macerate the blend for four weeks until every note sits in tune.

3

Bottle & Receive

Your scent is sealed in a hand-finished 50 ml flacon, engraved with its score, and shipped to your door.

Questions, Answered

Our eaux de parfum carry a 20% concentration. Expect eight to ten hours of wear, with base notes detectable on fabric the following day.

We blend natural absolutes and essential oils with safe modern aroma molecules. Every formula is fully disclosed on your scent score, with no hidden musks of animal origin.

We ship to over forty countries with tracked, insured delivery. After the four-week maceration, parcels arrive within three to seven business days depending on your region.

Signature and Maestro orders include a revision. If the first composition misses the mark, our perfumers rebalance it once at no charge. Discovery sets are refundable within thirty days, unopened.

Every formula complies with IFRA standards, and known allergens are flagged during note selection. We recommend a patch test, and our team can build around specific sensitivities on request.
