Frequently asked questions

Does AntOps support SSO and SAML?expand_more
Yes. Business and Enterprise plans include SAML 2.0 SSO with Okta, Entra ID, and Google Workspace, plus SCIM user provisioning. Role mappings sync automatically from your identity provider groups.
Where is our data hosted?expand_more
You choose US or EU residency at workspace creation. Enterprise plans can pin storage to a specific region, and all data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.
How do we migrate from our current tooling?expand_more
AntOps imports dashboards, alert rules, and runbooks from common monitoring and ticketing tools through a guided wizard. Most teams complete migration in under a week, and our team reviews the plan with you at no charge.
What support response times do you guarantee?expand_more
Business plans include a 4-business-hour first response. Enterprise plans add a named support engineer with a 1-hour response for severity-one incidents, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
What uptime do you commit to?expand_more
Business carries a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA and Enterprise 99.99%, both backed by service credits. Our live status page publishes 90 days of history and post-incident reports.
How does billing work as we grow?expand_more
You pay per active user per month, prorated to the day. Annual agreements receive two months free, and you can move between plans at any time without losing data or configuration.
