asked & answered

Curious clutter, answered

Is Nimbus just a place to hoard stuff?add

Proudly yes — but an organized one. Think of it as a junk drawer that went to finishing school: everything you keep, none of the chaos, all of the charm.

Will you ever force a minimal redesign on us?add

Over our sticker collection's dead body. Light mode or dark mode, both come fully wallpapered and warmly lit. What you see is what we ship.

Can I bring my collection over from another app?add

Bring everything. Import links, files, and half-finished ideas in one glorious dump — we'll hand you the shelf map and a welcome mug.
