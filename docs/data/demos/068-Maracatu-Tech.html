Questions from the courtyard

What is Maracatu Tech?expand_more

A collaboration platform inspired by maracatu — the African-Brazilian percussion tradition of Pernambuco. We apply its discipline, rhythm, and collective energy to the way teams work.

Do I need a credit card to start?expand_more

No. The Baque plan is free forever, and every paid plan begins with a 14-day trial — no card required.

Do you support community organizations?expand_more

Yes. Registered maracatu nações, carnival groups, and nonprofits receive 50% off any paid plan — write to us from the footer.
