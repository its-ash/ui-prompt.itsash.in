Honest Questions, Honest Answers

About authorship, consent, and money.

What is Nimbus?expand_more

Nimbus is a digital platform where indigenous Brazilian communities publish their own stories, songs, and knowledge. We provide recording, translation, and hosting; the communities decide what is shared — and hold the authorship.

Who writes and approves the stories?expand_more

The communities themselves. Every piece is recorded with informed consent, reviewed by its tellers before publication, and remains their intellectual property. Nothing is published about a community without them.

How does my subscription help?expand_more

Seventy percent of every paid plan goes directly to storytellers and the language-preservation projects they lead. The remainder funds hosting, field-recording equipment, and free classroom access.
