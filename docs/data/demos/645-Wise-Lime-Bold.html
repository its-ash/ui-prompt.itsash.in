Frequently asked questions

How are the exchange rates determined? add

We use the mid‑market rate sourced from independent financial data providers, updated in real time.

How long does a transfer take? add

Most transfers arrive within seconds; some currency‑corridor payments may take up to one business day.

Is my money safe with Nimbus? add

Yes. We are regulated as an electronic money institution and keep customer funds in segregated accounts.

Can I cancel a transfer after sending? add

Cancellation is possible only if the funds haven’t been deposited yet. Contact support immediately.

What currencies do you support? add

Over 50 currencies, including GBP, EUR, USD, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, SGD, and many more.

How do I get help? add

Our support team is available via email and in‑app chat 24/7.
