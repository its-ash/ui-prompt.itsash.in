Questions, answered

How much does a transfer cost?add
One flat fee, calculated from the amount and route, starting at 0.12% for Business volume and 0.35% on Pay as you go. You see the exact figure before you confirm, and it never changes after.
How fast will my money arrive?add
Most transfers on major routes arrive in under twenty seconds. Routes that rely on local banking hours can take up to one business day, and we show the estimate upfront.
Is my money safe with zest.?add
Customer funds are held in segregated accounts at tier-one banks, separate from company money. We are licensed as an electronic money institution in every region we operate.
Which currencies can I hold and send?add
You can hold balances in over 50 currencies and send to more than 80 countries, with local account details in USD, GBP, EUR, AUD, and five more regions.
Can I cancel a transfer?add
If the transfer has not been paid out yet, you can cancel from your activity feed for a full refund, fee included. Completed transfers can be recalled through support in most cases.
How do I get help?add
In-app chat runs 24/7 in six languages, and Personal and Business plans get priority routing. Most questions are resolved in under ten minutes.
