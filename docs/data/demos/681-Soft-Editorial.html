Questions from the reading room

Do I need to finish a book before starting another?expand_more

Not at all. Marginalia assumes most readers keep several books in rotation. Each one holds its place, its notes, and its passages until you wander back.

Can I bring in notes I have already written elsewhere?expand_more

Yes. Paste plain text or import a Markdown file and Marginalia will sort entries under the right book. Highlights from most e-readers import cleanly as well.

What happens to my journal if I cancel?expand_more

Your writing is yours. Export everything as a print-ready PDF or plain text at any time, and your account returns to the free Reader shelf rather than disappearing.

Is there a mobile app for reading on the train?expand_more

Marginalia runs beautifully in any mobile browser, and dedicated iOS and Android editions are in the works. Your journal syncs the moment you are back online.
