FAQ

Technical questions, direct answers

How does Nimbus ingest events without sampling?expand_more

The edge collector buffers micro-batches, validates each payload against your tracking plan and writes full-fidelity rows to columnar storage. Dashboards always query 100% of events.

Which warehouses does Nimbus sync to?expand_more

Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift and Postgres. Models are materialized as dbt-compatible tables and refreshed on the schedule you define, from every 15 minutes to nightly.

What does the SDK do to page performance?expand_more

The browser snippet is 11 kB gzipped, sends asynchronously via sendBeacon and never blocks the main thread. Our p75 impact on LCP across monitored sites is under 20 ms.

Is data retention configurable?expand_more

Yes — from 30 days on Starter to custom hot/cold tiers on Enterprise, with automatic Parquet export to your own bucket when data ages out of the hot layer.

How is billing metered?expand_more

By ingested events, computed monthly. Events that fail tracking-plan validation are never billed, and the first 1M events each month are free on every tier.
