Questions from orbit

Is the physics accurate?expand_more

Yes. THREE BODY integrates Newtonian gravitation with an energy-conserving solver, the same class of algorithm used in introductory computational physics courses.

Does it work on tablets and phones?expand_more

The canvas is fully touch-enabled. Drag to place bodies, pinch to zoom the star field, and tap to fling a body along a velocity vector.

Can I use it in a classroom for free?expand_more

The Drifter tier is free forever and needs no account, so students can open a link and start simulating immediately. Observatory adds class management tools.
