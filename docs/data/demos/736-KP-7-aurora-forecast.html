Questions, answered

The short version of everything people ask before their first alert.

The KP index measures global geomagnetic activity on a scale from 0 to 9. Higher values mean the auroral oval pushes farther from the poles. KP 7 is a strong storm, often visible as far south as Germany or the northern United States.

Short-range alerts driven by the DSCOVR satellite give roughly 30 to 60 minutes of warning with high confidence. The 27-day outlook is probabilistic, based on solar rotation, and improves as an active region approaches the Earth-facing side of the Sun.

Not total, but darker is better. Our visibility score factors in moon phase, cloud cover, and local light pollution, then tells you whether tonight is worth the drive and which direction offers the darkest horizon.
