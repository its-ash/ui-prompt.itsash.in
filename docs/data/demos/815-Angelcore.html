Questions, answered softly

Is my writing private?expand_more

Yes. Every entry is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers. We cannot read your pages, and we never will.

What happens after the thirty free days?expand_more

You choose a plan, or you drift gently onto Novena, our free tier. Your entries remain safe and readable either way.

Can I take my entries with me?expand_more

Always. Export everything as plain text or PDF at any time, on any plan, including the free one. Your words belong to you.

Do I have to write every day?expand_more

No. Aurelia keeps no streaks and sends no guilt. The page simply waits for you, as long as you need.

Which devices does Aurelia support?expand_more

Aurelia runs in any modern browser and as an app on iOS and Android, with your journal quietly synced between them.
