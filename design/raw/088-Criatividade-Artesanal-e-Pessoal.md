## Historical Context

Before Canva templates and brand-in-a-box kits flattened everything into the same geometric sans-serif grid, freelancers owned their visual identity through craft. Hand-lettered logos, risograph textures, ink splatter borders — these weren't aesthetic choices, they were survival tactics. When you're competing against agencies with six-figure brand budgets, the one thing they cannot replicate is your hand. The artisanal branding movement traces back to the early 2010s letterpress revival and Etsy-era maker culture, but its roots run deeper — into zine communities, sign painting traditions, and the DIY punk ethic that said 'rough edges are the point.' Freelance illustrators and creative consultants adopted these codes not out of nostalgia but necessity. A wobbly baseline or a scanned paper texture signals something corporate design cannot: that a single human made this, deliberately, with intent. Today the tension is sharper than ever. AI-generated assets push visual homogeneity further. The handmade mark — imperfect, personal, unrepeatable — becomes the clearest signal of authorship a small creative practice can deploy.

## When to Use

When the brand IS the person. Solo consultants, illustrators selling their eye, small studios where the founder's taste is the product. Deploy when differentiation depends on visible craft rather than scalable systems — portfolio sites, proposal decks, social presence for creatives who need to feel singular, not templated. Avoid for anything requiring multi-designer consistency at scale; this approach trades repeatability for personality on purpose.

## Design Principles

- Visible hand — preserve evidence of human process: scanned textures, brush strokes, tool marks, irregular edges that prove a person was here
- Constraint as signature — limit your palette, your tools, your grid deliberately; the restrictions you choose become recognizable faster than any logo
- Warmth over precision — favor optical alignment over mathematical alignment, organic rhythm over rigid repetition, breath over density
- Material honesty — if it references print, ink, or paper, ground it in real material behavior; fake textures read as decoration, real ones read as craft
- Personal mythology — build a visual language from your actual references, obsessions, and working methods rather than borrowing trending aesthetics wholesale

- **Physics:** Ease-out curves, 200-300ms duration. Smooth and predictable.
- **Entry animations:** Fade + translate-Y (16px → 0) over 420ms ease-out. Staggered cascades for lists: 80ms between items.
- **Hover states:** Subtle color shift + shadow adjustment over 200ms.
- **Page transitions:** Fade only (200ms).
- **Performance:** Only transform and opacity animated. No layout-triggering properties.

## Components

- **Primary Button:** Subtly rounded (0.5rem) shape. Accent color fill. Hover: 8% darken + subtle lift shadow. Active: -1px translate tactile press. Font weight 600. No outer glows.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Outline variant. 1.5px border in muted color. Text in primary color. Hover: subtle background fill.
- **Cards:** Subtly rounded (0.5rem) corners. Surface background. Subtle shadow (0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)). 1px border stroke.
- **Inputs:** Label above input. 1px border stroke. Focus ring: 2px accent color offset 2px. Error text below in semantic red. No floating labels.
- **Navigation:** Primary surface background. Active item: accent color indicator. Font weight 500 when active.
- **Skeletons:** Shimmer animation matching component dimensions. No circular spinners.
- **Empty States:** Icon-based composition with descriptive text and action button.

## Do's and Don'ts

- No emojis in UI — use icon system only (Lucide, Heroicons)
- No pure black (#000000) — use off-black or charcoal variants
- No oversaturated accent colors (saturation cap: 80%)
- No 3-column equal-width feature layouts — use zig-zag or asymmetric grid
- No `h-screen` — use `min-h-[100dvh]`
- No AI copywriting clichés: "Elevate", "Seamless", "Unleash", "Next-Gen"
- No broken external image links — use picsum.photos or inline SVG
- No generic lorem ipsum in demos
