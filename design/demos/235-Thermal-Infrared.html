Frequently Asked Questions

What is thermal infrared imaging?

Thermal infrared imaging captures infrared radiation emitted by objects and converts it into a visible heatmap, revealing temperature variations invisible to the naked eye.

How does the AI anomaly detection work?

Our models are trained on millions of thermal frames to learn normal patterns. Deviations trigger alerts with confidence scores.

Is the hardware weather‑proof?

Yes. Sensors are IP66 rated and operate reliably from ‑40°C to 85°C, suitable for outdoor and industrial environments.
