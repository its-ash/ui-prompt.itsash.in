Questions on the record

Is my data actually private?expand_more

Yes. Files are encrypted at rest and in transit, access is role-based, and nothing is sold, shared, or handed over without your say-so. Your secrets stay yours.

Can I change plans later?expand_more

Any time, no interrogation. Upgrades apply instantly; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing cycle. Your files come with you either way.

What happens to my files if I cancel?expand_more

You get a full export — every record, every version — before the drawer locks. After 30 days, our copies are destroyed for good.

Do you offer nonprofit rates?expand_more

We do. Registered nonprofits and small newsrooms get 40% off any tier. Show us the paperwork and the badge is yours.
