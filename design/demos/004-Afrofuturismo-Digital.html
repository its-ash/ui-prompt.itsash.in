Frequently asked

The questions every new keeper brings.

What is a living archive?expand_more

A living archive is versioned like code and voiced like family. Every recording keeps its full history, so updates never erase what an elder first said — they add new light to it.

Do I own the stories I record?expand_more

Completely. Ancestra claims no rights to your memory. You can export audio masters and transcriptions at any time, in open formats your grandchildren will still be able to open.

Can I export my constellation?expand_more

Yes — constellations export as JSON graphs plus standard audio files. Your star charts should outlive any single platform, including this one.
