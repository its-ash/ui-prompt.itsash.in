Questions asked at the threshold.

Is Nimbus real, or did I dream it?expand_more

Both answers are correct. Nimbus exists exactly as much as you need it to — though invoices are issued only in the waking state.

Will my files melt?expand_more

Only their edges, and only for aesthetic purposes. Every file is stored twice: once on servers that never sleep, and once in a place we do not discuss.

Can I cancel while sleepwalking?expand_more

Yes. Cancellation takes one click, asks no questions, and involves no walking into walls. We leave the door open in case you change your mind.
