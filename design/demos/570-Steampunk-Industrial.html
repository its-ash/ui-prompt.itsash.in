Questions from the workshop floor

Straight answers, no fine print etched in six-point type.

How much coal does the engine consume?expand_more

The Foreman unit burns roughly twelve pounds per hour at full load. With the condenser engaged, most owners report closer to eight.

Can one engineer operate it alone?expand_more

Yes. Once the boiler is at pressure, a single operator manages the governor, clutch and telegraph panel from one station.

What does the warranty cover?expand_more

Every gear, bearing, rivet and boiler plate. If a part fails under normal operation, we ship a replacement by the next outbound train, free of charge.
