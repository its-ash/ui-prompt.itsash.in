FAQ

Questions we hear at the counter

Does Nimbus really work offline?expand_more

Completely. Files live on your machine first; every edit is journaled locally and merged conflict-free when a connection returns. You could work for weeks offline and never lose a keystroke.

Where is my data stored?expand_more

On your devices, encrypted at rest. Synced copies live on EU servers, and locked files are end-to-end encrypted — the keys never leave your devices, so neither we nor our hosts can read them.

Can I cancel or downgrade anytime?expand_more

Yes. One click in Billing, no phone call, no dark pattern. Your plan runs to the end of the cycle, and your local data stays yours forever — export everything with two clicks.
