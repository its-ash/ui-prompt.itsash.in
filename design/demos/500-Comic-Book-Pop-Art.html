Is the free plan really free forever?expand_more
Yes. Sidekick stays free with no credit card required. You only pay if you upgrade to Hero or League for bigger teams and automation.
Can I import tasks from my current tool?expand_more
Nimbus imports boards from Trello, Asana, and plain CSV files. Most teams finish the move in under fifteen minutes.
What happens if I cancel a paid plan?expand_more
Your workspace drops back to Sidekick at the end of the billing cycle. Your boards and history stay intact and readable.
