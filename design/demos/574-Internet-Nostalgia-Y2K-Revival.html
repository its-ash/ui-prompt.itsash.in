Your page works in every modern browser, but it will look authentically vintage. We even include a "Best viewed at 800x600" badge for historical accuracy.

Yes. Upload any MIDI and set it to autoplay on load, with a polite mute button for visitors browsing at 2 AM. We are nostalgic, not cruel.
