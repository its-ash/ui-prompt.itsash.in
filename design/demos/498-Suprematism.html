Everything Is a Shape

Squares, circles, crosses, and bars in a locked four-color palette. Drag a form onto the canvas and it snaps to the grid.

Tilt Engine

Rotate any plane in precise degree steps. Suprema keeps every tilt harmonious so compositions float instead of fall.

Process

Three Moves to a Composition

A deliberate workflow that keeps you focused on form, not menus.

1

Place

Drop a black square or a red plane onto the empty ground. The canvas starts silent; you add the first voice.

2

Tilt

Rotate forms until the composition lifts off. Tension comes from angle and spacing, never from decoration.

3

Publish

Export your work or share a live link. Posters, covers, and identities ship straight from the canvas.
