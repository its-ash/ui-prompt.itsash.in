Questions

Asked quietly, answered plainly

Is there a minimum relationship size?expand_more

We generally begin conversations at two million pounds of custodied assets, though we have made exceptions for families whose affairs suit our manner of working.

How is my privacy protected?expand_more

We hold no marketing list, attend no conferences, and confirm no client relationship to any third party. Your name will never appear in anything we publish.

May I withdraw from a mandate?expand_more

At any time, with ninety days notice and without penalty. We believe a relationship kept by obligation is no relationship at all.
