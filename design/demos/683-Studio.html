02, Process

How it works

A tight three-phase run. No bloated timelines, no mystery weeks.

01

Brief it

A working session to pin down goals, audience, and the one thing the work must say. You leave with a written brief we both sign.

02

Build it

Weekly reviews with real work on the table, not status slides. Two structured revision rounds keep momentum high.

03

Launch it

Final files, guidelines, and a handoff call with your team. We stay on for two weeks post-launch to fix anything that rattles.

05, FAQ

Straight answers

The questions every client asks before signing, answered the way we would over coffee.

How long does a typical project take?

expand_more

Spark sprints run two weeks. Studio engagements run six. Scale partnerships are quarterly. We commit to dates in the statement of work and we hit them.

How does pricing work?

expand_more

Fixed price per engagement, 50% to start and 50% at launch. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices. Scope changes are quoted before any extra work begins.

How many revision rounds are included?

expand_more

Spark includes one round, Studio includes two, and Scale is unlimited within the quarter. Weekly reviews mean revisions stay small and focused.

What files do we get at handoff?

expand_more

Everything: source Figma files, production front-end code, exported assets, font licenses we purchased on your behalf, and written guidelines your team can follow without us.

Who will we actually work with?

expand_more

The two founders, directly. No account managers, no handoffs to juniors. The people in your first call are the people doing the work.
