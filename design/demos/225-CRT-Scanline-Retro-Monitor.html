Does PHOSPHOR run on modern hardware?expand_more

Yes. It runs anywhere a modern browser does — the CRT look is pure CSS, no emulators required.

Can I turn off the scanlines and glow?expand_more

Every effect is a toggle in Settings, and the interface honors your system's reduced-motion preference automatically.

Is my data really local?expand_more

By default, yes. Nothing leaves your machine unless you enable Pro sync, which is end-to-end encrypted before upload.
