Questions, answered

Is my banking data secure?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus uses 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest, connects to banks with read-only credentials, and undergoes independent security audits every year. We never sell your data.

Can I export my data if I leave?expand_more

Anytime. Download full CSV exports or PDF statements from Settings — your data is yours, with zero lock-in.

How do I cancel my subscription?expand_more

One click in Settings, no phone calls. You keep Pro features until the end of your billing period, and we delete your data within 30 days of a request.
