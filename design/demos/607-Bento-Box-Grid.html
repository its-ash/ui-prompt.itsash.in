Common questions

Answers to the things teams ask before moving their work into Nimbus.

Can I import boards from other tools?expand_more
Yes. Nimbus imports tasks and files from Trello, Asana, and Notion with a guided two-step importer. Most teams finish in under ten minutes.
What happens when my trial ends?expand_more
Your workspace moves to the free Solo plan automatically. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade again at any time to restore paid features.
Is my data private?expand_more
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your content, and you can export or delete everything from settings.
