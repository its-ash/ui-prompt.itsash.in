Frequently Asked Questions

Get the answers you need to move forward.

What file formats do you support? expand_more

We support GLB and glTF 2.0 formats. All models are automatically optimized for web delivery with automatic LOD generation and texture compression.

How does the AR feature work? expand_more

Our viewer uses WebXR for Android and iOS, Scene Viewer for Android, and Quick Look for iOS, all from a single embed. No app installation required.

Is there a limit to model complexity? expand_more

We recommend keeping models under 5MB for optimal performance. Our optimization pipeline handles decimation and texture baking automatically.

Can I customize the viewer UI? expand_more

Yes! You can hide the AR button, change the background environment, or add your own branding through our dashboard settings.

Do you offer analytics on viewer engagement? expand_more

Our Pro and Enterprise plans include interaction analytics: rotation time, zoom depth, material switches, and AR placements.
