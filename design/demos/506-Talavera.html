From Puebla clay to your table

Three stages, about six weeks, and no shortcuts.

1

Throw and cure

Local black and rose clays are blended, thrown on the wheel, and cured in open air for up to four weeks.

2

Glaze and paint

The first firing hardens the form. Then comes the tin glaze and the freehand painting, guided by chalk outlines.

3

Final firing and signing

A second firing vitrifies the glaze. Each finished piece is inspected, signed, and dated on the base.

Common questions

Is every piece really painted by hand?expand_more

Yes. We use no decals or screen printing. Small variations in line weight and color density are the mark of the brush, and each base carries the painter's signature and date.

Can the pieces go in the dishwasher and microwave?expand_more

Dishwasher yes, on a standard cycle. Microwave use is fine for short reheating, though we recommend hand washing commissioned pieces with raised gold or copper detail.

How long does shipping take, and what if something breaks?expand_more

In-stock sets leave Puebla within 5 business days and arrive in 7 to 14 days internationally. Every shipment is fully insured; send us a photo of any damage and we replace the piece at no cost.
