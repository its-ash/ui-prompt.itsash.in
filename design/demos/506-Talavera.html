Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first firing.

Is every piece really painted by hand?expand_more

Yes. Certified Talavera from Puebla must be hand-thrown or molded and hand-painted. You will see slight brush variations that prove it, and each order ships with its Denominación de Origen certificate.

How long does a made-to-order piece take?expand_more

Plan for four to six weeks. Drying, first firing, painting, glazing, and second firing cannot be rushed without risking cracks. We send photo updates at each stage.

What if something arrives broken?expand_more

Send us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we remake or refund the piece, your choice. All shipments are fully insured and packed in custom molded inserts.
