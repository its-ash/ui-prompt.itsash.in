Questions from the lobby

Asked often. Answered plainly. The lights flicker regardless.

Is my data really safe in the archive?add

Yes. Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can export or erase your entire archive at any time. The signal remembers only what you allow.

Can I change tiers later?add

Any time. Upgrades apply instantly, downgrades apply at the end of your billing cycle, and nothing in your archive is ever deleted for switching rooms.

What happens if I cancel?add

Your archive stays readable for 90 days, then it is erased completely. We keep nothing. The hallway goes quiet, as it should.
