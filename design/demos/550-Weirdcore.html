Questions from the hallway

Is my data really private?chevron_right

Yes. Everything is encrypted at rest and in transit, and only you hold the keys. Nimbus cannot read your archive, and neither can anyone else.

What happens if I cancel?chevron_right

You keep read access for 90 days and can export everything in open formats. Nothing is held hostage. The signal lets go when you ask it to.

Does it work offline?chevron_right

Capture and rewind work fully offline. Sync resumes quietly the moment a connection returns, as if nothing happened.
