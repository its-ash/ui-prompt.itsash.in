Common Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

What is the typical turnaround time? expand_more
Most projects take between 3 to 6 weeks depending on complexity and the number of revisions.
How many revisions are included? expand_more
Depending on your package, we include 2 to 5 rounds of revisions to ensure the final result is perfect.
What file formats do you deliver? expand_more
We provide high-resolution MP4, MOV (with alpha channel), and ProRes files tailored for your platform.
Do you handle sound design? expand_more
Yes, our Studio and Enterprise plans include custom sound design and cinematic audio mixing.
Who owns the final copyrights? expand_more
Upon final payment, full usage rights for the rendered video are transferred to the client.
