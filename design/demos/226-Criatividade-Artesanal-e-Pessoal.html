Questions I hear a lot

Is this only for brand-new businesses?expand_more

Not at all. Some of my favorite projects were twenty-year-old shops that needed a fresh coat of story. If you're tired of sounding like everyone else, we'll get along fine.

How long does a project take?expand_more

The Sketch takes about two weeks. Partnerships unfold over a month or two, at whatever pace real life allows. No deadline is worth your sanity.

How do payments work?expand_more

Half holds your spot, the rest is due when you're happy. Monthly plans cancel anytime — no tiny-print contracts, just a handshake and an invoice.
