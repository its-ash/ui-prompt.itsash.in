Questions, answered.

Which Macs does Nimbus support?expand_more

Any Mac running macOS Sonoma 14 or later. Nimbus is a universal binary, native on both Apple silicon and Intel.

Does Nimbus work offline?expand_more

Yes. Everything is stored locally first, and encrypted sync quietly catches up whenever you're back online.

Can I try Nimbus Pro before paying?expand_more

Every download includes a 14-day Pro trial — no credit card, no signup. When it ends, you keep the Free plan forever.
