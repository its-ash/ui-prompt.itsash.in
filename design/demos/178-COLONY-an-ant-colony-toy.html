Ant questions? Answered.

Are the ants included?expand_more

Classic and Mega kits ship with live harvester ants in 1–2 days. Starter includes a mail-in voucher, since a smaller crew travels best in mild weather. Refill packs of 25 are always in stock.

Is it safe for kids?expand_more

COLONY is built for ages 6 and up: rounded edges, shatter-resistant walls, and an escape-proof double lid. Our worker ants are a gentle species, and the kit includes soft feather tweezers for safe moves.

How long do the ants live?expand_more

Workers live one to three months in the nest, and refills arrive in days. Queens can't be shipped in most states, but a steady crew of twenty keeps the tunnels growing all year long.
