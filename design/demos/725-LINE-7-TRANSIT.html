Before you board

Answers to the questions we hear most at the ticket gates.

How often do trains run?expand_more
Every 4 minutes during peak hours (7–9am and 4–7pm), every 7 minutes off-peak, and every 12 minutes after 11pm. The full timetable is live in the app.
Can I get a refund on an unused pass?expand_more
Yes. Monthly and Annual passes can be refunded pro-rata within the app, no phone call needed. Single rides are refundable within 24 hours if unused.
Is Line 7 accessible for wheelchairs and strollers?expand_more
All 42 stations have step-free access via lifts or ramps, level boarding platforms, and tactile guidance paths. Audio announcements run on every train.
Can I bring my bike on the train?expand_more
Folding bikes are welcome anytime. Full-size bikes are allowed outside peak hours in the marked carriages at both ends of each train.
What happens if a train is delayed?expand_more
You get a push alert with the new arrival time and an alternative route. Delays over 20 minutes automatically credit a free ride to your account.
