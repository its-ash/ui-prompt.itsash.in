The letters page

Asked, answered, typeset.

Is this really just a landing page?expand_more

A very well-dressed one. Every section you've scrolled is finished copy, not a promise of copy. What you see is what ships.

Can I make it mine?expand_more

Entirely. Swap the wordmark, palette, and fonts — the structure won't sulk. We only ask that you keep the borders bold. It's a lifestyle.

Does dark mode come standard?expand_more

It does, and it persists. Choose espresso once and the page greets you in it forever — stored politely on your own device, nowhere else.

What if I hate serif fonts?expand_more

We'll pretend we didn't hear that, quietly point you toward the sans-serif option, and speak no more of it.

How do I cancel?expand_more

One click. No retention maze, no guilt maze, no surprise maze. Exits should be as elegant as entrances.
