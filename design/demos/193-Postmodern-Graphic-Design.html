05 / FAQ

Frequently Questioned Answers

Is Overprint actually useful for professional design work? expand_more

Absolutely. Many award-winning campaigns have used deliberate "mistakes" and anti-grid layouts to stand out in crowded markets. Overprint gives you the tools to do this intentionally, not accidentally.

Do I need to know design theory to use this? expand_more

Not at all. Overprint includes guided tutorials that teach you when to break rules (and when to pretend you meant to do it).

Can I export to print and digital formats? expand_more

Yes. Export as PDF for print, PNG/JPEG for web, and SVG for scalable assets. All with built-in bleed and overprint simulation.
