Questions Whispered in the Dark

Who may petition the Abbey?expand_more

Any keeper of a legacy may petition. We welcome families, collectors, and institutions alike. Every petition is reviewed with equal solemnity.

Is my sanctuary truly private?expand_more

Yes. Your hall and vault are sealed to all but you and those you name. Our keepers swear an oath of silence that outlasts their service.

May I visit before committing?expand_more

Every petitioner is invited to a twilight tour of the grounds before any covenant is signed. You will walk the nave, the cloister, and the vaults.
