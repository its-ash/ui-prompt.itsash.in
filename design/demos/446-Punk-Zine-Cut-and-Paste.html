Do I need design skills to use Nimbus?add
No. The whole tool is built around scissors-and-glue logic. If you can tape a flyer to a lamppost, you can publish an issue here.
Can I cancel whenever I want?add
Yes. One click, no retention call, no guilt-trip modal. Your exported issues stay yours forever either way.
Who owns what I publish?add
You do, completely. We claim no license over your work. Print it, sell it, wheat-paste it to a wall. It is yours.
