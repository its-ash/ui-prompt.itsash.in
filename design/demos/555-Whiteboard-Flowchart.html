Quick answers

The questions we hear most, answered without the fine print.

Do I need to be good at drawing?expand_more

Not at all. Scribble to Shape cleans up wobbly lines automatically, and every shape snaps into place. If you can draw a rough box, you can build a flowchart.

Can people view my boards without paying?expand_more

Yes. Viewers and commenters are always free on every plan. You only pay for teammates who create and edit boards.

What happens if I cancel my plan?expand_more

Your boards stay readable forever. You drop to the free Doodler plan, keep your three most recent boards editable, and can export everything anytime.
