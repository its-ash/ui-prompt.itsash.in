Whispers from the circle

Gentle answers to curious questions

Do I need a green thumb?expand_more

Not at all. Every kit includes an illustrated care scroll, and our mosses forgive a missed watering far more graciously than most houseplants.

What if my plants arrive sleepy?expand_more

Moss travels well, but if anything arrives less than lively, send us a photo within 14 days and we'll reship that treasure free of charge.

Can I gift a Nimbus subscription?expand_more

Choose any plan at checkout and add a handwritten note. Recipients can pause, skip a season, or swap their grove whenever the mood strikes.
