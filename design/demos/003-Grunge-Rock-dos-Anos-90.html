Frequently screamed questions

Is this just another glossy SaaS in a band shirt?

No. Gloss doesn't survive our build process — if a pixel looks too clean, it gets photocopied until it learns some manners.

Can I cancel when the tour ends?

Yes. Two clicks, no guilt trip, and your boards export as plain files. We'd rather earn the encore than trap you in the venue.

Does dark mode exist?

It does — a photocopied night-zine palette. Hit the toggle up top and the practice-room lights go out.
