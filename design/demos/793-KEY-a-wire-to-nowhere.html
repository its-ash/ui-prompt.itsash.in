Questions from the switchboard

Answers, transmitted plainly.

Do I need to know morse code?expand_more
No. Type normally and KEY translates your words into dots and dashes as they travel. If you want to learn, the hand-tap mode teaches you one letter at a time.
How fast does a wire actually arrive?expand_more
Delivery is technically instant, but the sounder plays your message at telegraph pace. A short note takes about a minute to be fully received, which is rather the point.
Can I send wires to someone who does not use KEY?expand_more
Yes. They receive an ordinary email with a link to a listening station where your message is tapped out for them, no account required.
What happens to my old messages?expand_more
Every wire is archived as a paper-style slip you can export or delete at any time. Nothing is scanned, sold, or fed to a machine.
Is there a mobile station?expand_more
KEY runs in any modern browser, and the key itself works beautifully on a touchscreen. A dedicated pocket sounder app is on the wire for later this year.
