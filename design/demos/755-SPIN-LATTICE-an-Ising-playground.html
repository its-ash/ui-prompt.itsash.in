Three moves to a phase transition

The same protocol condensed-matter students run, minus the Fortran.

01

Heat it up

Raise the temperature past the critical point and the lattice dissolves into flickering disorder. Thermal noise wins every local argument.

02

Nudge the spins

Flip a few cells by hand and watch the perturbation spread or die out, depending on how hot the system is.

03

Cool it down

Lower the temperature slowly and domains nucleate, grow, and lock in. The readout settles near plus or minus one.
