Patch Matrix

Route any oscillator to any filter or effect. 24-point normalized patchbay with half-normaling for complex signal flows.

Analog Engine

Oscillators, filters, and VCA’s modeled after classic 1970s semi-modular architecture. Warm, organic timbre.
