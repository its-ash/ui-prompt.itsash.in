Quick answers

Is my data private?add

Yes. Notes are encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never use your content to train models. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Can I import from other apps?add

Nimbus imports Markdown files, plain text, and exports from most major notes apps. Imports land in your inbox and get auto-sorted like everything else.

What happens if I cancel?add

You drop to the free Scribble plan and keep every note. Nothing is locked or deleted, and you can export your full archive whenever you want.
