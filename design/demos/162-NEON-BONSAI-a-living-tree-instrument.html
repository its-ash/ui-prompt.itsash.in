Root questions

Everything you might ask before planting.

Do I need to know music theory?expand_more

No. Seeds set the mood and the engine handles harmony. If you can prune a plant, you can compose with NEON BONSAI.

What does pruning do to the sound?expand_more

Each branch is a voice. Cutting one mutes it and redistributes its notes to its neighbours, reshaping the score in real time.

Can I export to my DAW?expand_more

Yes. Harvest stems as WAV or MIDI on paid plans, and the Ancient tier ships a VST3 plugin for direct DAW use.

What happens if I stop watering it?expand_more

The tree goes dormant and its score is safely saved. Water it again and growth picks up exactly where it left off.

Does it work offline?expand_more

Yes. All synthesis and growth run locally in your browser — no server round-trips, no cloud lock-in.
