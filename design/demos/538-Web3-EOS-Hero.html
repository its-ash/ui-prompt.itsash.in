Questions, answered

Which networks does Nimbus support?expand_more

Twelve networks at launch, including Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Solana, and Polygon. New networks are added quarterly based on customer demand and security review.

Who holds the keys to our vaults?expand_more

You do. Nimbus uses MPC key sharding across independent hardware modules, and no single party, including Nimbus, can sign alone. Policies you set are enforced at the signing layer.

Can we test before moving real funds?expand_more

Yes. Every plan includes a sandbox that mirrors mainnet routing and fee behavior. Most teams run their full transfer flow in the sandbox for a week before going live.
