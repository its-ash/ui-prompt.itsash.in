Live in three steps

From first install to a verified on-chain identity in under five minutes.

  1. Create your passkey wallet

    One tap with Face ID or a fingerprint provisions a self-custody wallet. Nothing to write down, nothing to lose.

  2. Verify once

    Complete a single identity check. Nimbus issues a reusable attestation anchored on-chain, owned entirely by you.

  3. Connect anywhere

    Apps request proofs through the Nimbus SDK. You approve with one tap and share only what each app requires.

Common questions

Everything teams ask before integrating Nimbus.

Do my users need crypto experience?expand_more

No. Nimbus wallets are created with biometrics and behave like any modern login. Users never see gas fees, seed phrases, or chain selectors unless you expose them.

Who controls the identity data?expand_more

The user, always. Attestations are anchored on-chain under the user's keys. Nimbus cannot read, move, or revoke them, and neither can the apps that request proofs.

How long does integration take?expand_more

Most teams ship a working login flow in an afternoon using the SDK. Full attestation flows with custom proof requirements typically take one to two weeks.
