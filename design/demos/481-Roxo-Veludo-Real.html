Questions, Answered

Everything prospective members ask before joining the circle.

What does membership include?expand_more
Every tier includes the seasonal curated collection, access to the members-only salon, and concierge support. Higher tiers add a dedicated concierge line, priority reservations, and invitations to private gatherings.
How does billing work?expand_more
Membership is billed monthly to your chosen card. You will receive an itemized statement each month, and annual billing with a courtesy reduction is available on request.
Can I cancel at any time?expand_more
Yes. Cancel from your member portal in under a minute. Your privileges remain active until the end of the current billing period, with no penalties or exit interviews.
May I gift a membership?expand_more
Certainly. Gift memberships arrive in a presentation case with a hand-written note from our atelier, and the recipient chooses their own start date.
How do I reach support?expand_more
Members reach us by message, phone, or the concierge line depending on tier. Every inquiry receives a reply within four hours, day or night.
