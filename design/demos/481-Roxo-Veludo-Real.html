Frequently Asked

Clear answers to help you decide if Velvet Royale is right for you.

What's included in my membership? expand_more
All tiers include access to our member portal, concierge services, lifestyle credits, and exclusive offers. Higher tiers add dedicated managers, event access, and premium experiences.
How is billing handled? expand_more
Membership is billed monthly to your preferred payment method. You'll receive an invoice via email before each charge. Flexible billing allows you to change or cancel with 30 days notice.
Can I gift a membership? expand_more
Absolutely. Gift memberships are available for all tiers and can be purchased through our concierge team. They include a personalized welcome package and activation assistance.
What is your cancellation policy? expand_more
You may cancel at any time with 30 days notice. Access to benefits continues through the end of your billing period. No prorated refunds for partial months.
How do I contact support? expand_more
Support is available 24/7 via email at support@velvetroyale.com or through the member portal. Urgent requests can be directed to your dedicated lifestyle manager (Signature and Imperial tiers).
