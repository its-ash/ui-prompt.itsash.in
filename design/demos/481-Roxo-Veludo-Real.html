Questions

Everything You Wish to Know

What does membership include?expand_more

Every tier includes curated seasonal selections, access to the members-only salon, and concierge support. Higher tiers add priority invitations, bespoke gifts, and a dedicated personal concierge.

How does billing work?expand_more

Membership is billed monthly to your chosen payment method. There are no setup fees, and your renewal date is always visible in your account.

Can I cancel at any time?expand_more

Yes. You may cancel from your account in moments, and your privileges remain active until the end of the current billing period.
