Loose ends

Freshly stapled questions

Do I need a real risograph printer?expand_more

Nope. Nimbus fakes the chemistry in the browser — multiply blends, spot inks, grain and drum dust included. If you do own a riso, export the separations and feed it real paper.

What happens to my inks in dark mode?expand_more

They stay fluoro-bright. Only the paper turns black and the overprint blend flips from multiply to screen — exactly like printing on black stock.

Can I print my zine on actual paper?expand_more

Yes — every issue exports as press-ready PDFs with one separation per ink. Crop marks on, apologies off.
