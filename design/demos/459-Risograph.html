Why riso

Every pass through the drum leaves a fingerprint. That is the point.

palette

Spot ink library

Pick from fourteen drums, from fluorescent pink to hunter green. Inks sit on the paper, never in it.

Honest overprint

Overlapping colors blend on the sheet, not in software. Expect drift, celebrate it, edition after edition.

How it works

From file to drum in three steps

01

Upload separations

Send one grayscale file per ink layer. Our checker flags trapping issues before anything burns.

02

Approve a proof

We run a test sheet and send photos under daylight lamps so you can judge the real ink.

03

We run the edition

The drum spins, the paper feeds, the registration wanders. Your edition ships numbered and stamped.
