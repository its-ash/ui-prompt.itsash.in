Questions, answered gently

Kintsugi is the Japanese art of mending broken pottery with gold-dusted lacquer. We don't hide the break — we honor it, and the repaired seam becomes the piece's most beautiful line.

Yes. Every vessel is thrown, trimmed, and glazed by hand, so no two curves or glaze pools repeat. We photograph the exact piece you'll receive.

Our mineral glazes are fired to stoneware temperatures and tested food-safe. Mended seams carry a food-safe sealant, so gold lines are welcome at the table.

Hand-wash with warm water and a soft cloth, and keep matte pieces away from dishwashers and sudden heat. Matte surfaces deepen beautifully with use — tea and oil simply join the story.

We do — double-boxed in recycled packing and fully insured. If a piece arrives cracked, we mend it in gold or replace it, and the return is on us.
