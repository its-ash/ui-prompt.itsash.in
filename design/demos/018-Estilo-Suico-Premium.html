Features · The instrument

Engineered to disappear on the wrist

precision_manufacturing

Hand-finished movement

Each calibre is bevelled, black-polished and regulated in five positions by one watchmaker, whose initials are engraved beneath the balance.

Tolerance: ±2 seconds per day, certified at delivery.

diamond

Ninety-hour reserve

A twin-barrel architecture stores ninety hours of autonomy behind a micro-rotor of solid gold — silent, and entirely visible.

Winding measures under 30 decibels at the ear.

shield

Lifetime stewardship

Every Nimbus is archived, serviced and traceable for life — a ledger that follows the piece, never the owner.

Complimentary service every fifth year, indefinitely.

Process · The atelier

From ingot to instrument

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Certified recycled gold and grand feu enamel arrive from two family suppliers we have relied on for a decade.

  2. 02

    Assembly

    One watchmaker assembles, regulates and signs each calibre — roughly six unhurried weeks per instrument.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    The piece travels in a lacquered case with its archive folio, service schedule and the maker’s signature.

FAQ · Common questions

Before you write to us

How long is the waiting list?expand_more

Current lead time is four to six months. A refundable deposit of five percent secures your position in the atelier’s queue.

What does lifetime stewardship include?expand_more

Complimentary servicing every five years, permanent movement archiving and transferable provenance — for as long as the piece exists.

May I visit the atelier?expand_more

Certainly. Collectors may book a private viewing in Geneva; we ask for two weeks’ notice and happily open the workshop floor.
