Gentle answers

The questions that surface most often.

Is Nimbus only for dreams?expand_more

Not at all. It's for any fleeting thought — ideas, lists, sketches, overheard phrases. Dreams simply inspired the softness of the design.

What happens if I lose my connection?expand_more

Notes cache locally and resurface the moment you're back online. Nothing you write ever drifts away.

Can I export or permanently delete my notes?expand_more

Yes — one-click export to Markdown, and deletion is immediate and irreversible. Exactly as it should be.
