Craft in every impression

Each print passes through carved cherry blocks, natural pigments, and the printer's bare hand.

brush

Hand-carved key blocks

Every line begins as a knife cut in mountain cherry wood, registered by the traditional kento marks.

landscape

Natural mineral pigments

Prussian blue, vermillion, and dayflower yellow are ground and brushed by hand for flat, luminous color.

print

Limited numbered editions

Editions close forever at one hundred impressions. Each print is numbered, sealed, and archived.

From block to your wall

Three unhurried stages, exactly as practiced in Edo workshops.

一
Step 1

Choose your print or course

Browse open editions or enroll in a guided mokuhanga course, beginner to advanced.

二
Step 2

We carve and press

Our printers dampen the washi, brush each color block, and rub the impression with a baren.

三
Step 3

Sealed and shipped flat

Your print is stamped with the atelier hanko, dried, and shipped flat with care notes.
