Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries.

What is glassmorphism and why use it?

Glassmorphism is a design style that uses background blur, translucency, and subtle borders to create a frosted‑glass effect. It adds depth and a modern feel to interfaces.

Do I need design experience to use Nimbus?

No. Nimbus provides guided templates and intuitive controls that help beginners create professional‑looking designs quickly.

Can I export my work for print?

Yes. You can export high‑resolution PNG, SVG, or PDF files suitable for both digital and print media.

Is there a mobile app?

Currently Nimbus is a web‑based platform that works on any modern browser, including tablets and smartphones.

Sign up for the Pro plan and get 14 days of full access. No credit card required to start.
