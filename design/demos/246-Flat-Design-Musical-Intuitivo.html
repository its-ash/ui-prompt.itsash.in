FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the free plan really free?expand_more
Yes — $0, forever. It’s ad-supported, plays in shuffle mode and caps audio at 160 kbps. No credit card, no trial clock.
Can I listen offline?expand_more
Premium and Family plans let you download tracks, albums and mixes for offline playback. Downloads stay fresh with a quick sync every 30 days, on up to five devices.
How do I cancel?expand_more
One click in Account → Subscription. Your plan ends with the billing cycle — no phone calls, no retention maze — and your playlists stay in your account forever.
Which devices are supported?expand_more
iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, the web player, and every major smart speaker, TV and car platform — with seamless handoff between them.
Is there a student plan?expand_more
Yes — Premium Student is $4.99/month with full Premium features. Verify once a year with your university email and you’re set.
