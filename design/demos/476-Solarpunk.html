Common questions

Everything else, our community team is happy to answer.

Do we need to own our homes to join?expand_more

No. Renters can join garden and energy-buying pools, and landlords can sponsor rooftop installations through our partnership agreements.

How does cost splitting work?expand_more

Each household pledges what it can, and Nimbus divides equipment and maintenance costs proportionally to usage, reviewed by the group each season.

What happens on cloudy weeks?expand_more

Your microgrid stays connected to the municipal supply as backup. Stored capacity and shared batteries smooth out most short gaps automatically.
