Enquiries, Answered

Who may request an invitation? expand_more

Any person of agreeable disposition. We ask only that you bring good humour, a tolerable opinion of dancing, and respect for the confidences of the house.

What does one wear to a ball? expand_more

Evening dress with a nod to the Regency is encouraged but never enforced. Pearls, gloves, and empire silhouettes are always admired; comfortable shoes are quietly forgiven.

May I end my subscription? expand_more

At any moment, with a single note. Your standing remains until the quarter's end, and your place in the book is kept should you ever wish to return.
