The Ritual

Three Steps to the Ballroom

Entry into society has always followed a certain order. We simply keep it charming.

1

Petition the Patronesses

Send your request for an invitation. A short note on your pleasures and pursuits is quite sufficient.

2

Receive Your Card

Accepted members receive a calling card by post, along with the season's programme of assemblies.

3

Take the Floor

Attend your first salon, be properly introduced, and let the season unfold at a waltz's pace.

Enquiries

Questions from the Ton

Everything a prospective member might wish to know before petitioning.

Who may request an invitation?expand_more
Any person of good humour and genuine interest in society. Our patronesses favour warmth and wit over pedigree, though a fine signature never hurts.
Is there a dress code at the assemblies?expand_more
Evening dress is encouraged but empire waists are not required. We ask only that you dress as though the evening matters, because it does.
May I bring a guest?expand_more
Diamond and Duchess members receive guest privileges each season. Guests are announced at the door and introduced with full ceremony.
What if I cannot dance?expand_more
Then you shall learn. We hold waltz instruction before every ball, and our dance masters are as patient as they are exacting.
May I cancel my membership?expand_more
Of course. A short note to the patronesses ends your membership at the close of the month. No dramatic farewell tour is required.

The Season Awaits

Leave your direction and our patronesses shall send a card. Do check the post; we are terribly traditional.
