Details

Questions, answered.

How do you choose specimens for my space?expand_more

We measure light through the day, note sightlines and architecture, and match plants whose temperament suits the room — never a default list, always a shortlist.

Do you work outside your city?expand_more

Yes. Design and the bound lookbook are fully remote, and installation travels with our horticulturists within Europe. Beyond that, we partner with trusted local growers and supervise remotely.

What happens if a plant fails?expand_more

Every specimen carries our 60-day guarantee and is replaced at no cost. After that, quarterly visits catch decline long before it shows — failures are rare, and never ignored.
