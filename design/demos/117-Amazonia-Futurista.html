FAQ

Asked beneath the canopy

How do bioluminescent sensors work?expand_more

Each sensor is a symbiotic fungal spore engineered to glow in rhythm with its host tree. Light pulses carry encrypted climate data to canopy relays — no wires, no harm, fully biodegradable within five years.

Is the mesh safe for wildlife?expand_more

Yes. The mesh is passive and soft-powered, drawing microvolts from root chemistry. It is certified by the 2050 Amazonia Bio-Ethics Council and audited by 300 independent ecologists.

Can I access canopy data offline?expand_more

Canopia caches your regions locally and syncs through firefly relay drones the moment you re-enter signal — built for deep-forest expeditions.

How is the energy shared back?expand_more

Root-cell groves feed river microgrids directly. As a steward you receive monthly dividends in Canopia credits, redeemable across the Amazonia Flowgrid.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?expand_more

Anytime, in two taps. Your data remains yours — full exports included — and your monitored hectares transfer to the community trust.
