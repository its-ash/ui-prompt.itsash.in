Before the bell rings

Do students need an account to join a board?expand_more

No — anyone with your board link joins as a guest in seconds. Names are optional, and no personal data is stored.

Can I export boards as handouts?expand_more

Yes. Every board exports as a print-ready PDF or PNG, with your title, date, and class name written on the chalk tray.

Is Nimbus accessible for keyboard-only users?expand_more

Every tool is fully keyboard operable, works with screen readers, and replays include captions for spoken annotations.
