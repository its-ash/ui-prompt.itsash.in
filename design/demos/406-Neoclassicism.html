Questions, Answered in Stone

How long does the survey of an existing archive take?expand_more

Most collections are measured within one working week. Vast estates of a million records or more receive a written timetable before work begins.

Can records be exported if we ever depart?expand_more

Always. Your archive is yours; a complete, standards-compliant export is available at any hour, at no charge, without condition.

Is the revision ledger truly immutable?expand_more

Yes. Entries are sealed cryptographically the moment they are written. Not even Portico's own stewards can alter what has been inscribed.

What support accompanies each tier?expand_more

Doric receives written counsel within one day. Ionic adds priority response within four hours. Corinthian appoints a named steward to your house.

Do you offer terms for institutions of learning?expand_more

Universities, museums, and public libraries receive the Ionic tier at half tribute, in keeping with our charter of public record.
