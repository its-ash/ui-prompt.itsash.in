Questions

Addressed in the Assembly

Is Pallas suited to small studios?expand_more

Symmetry does not require scale. The Basis tariff seats a single scholar comfortably, and every plan is composed upon the same central axis — a two-page portfolio receives the care of a cathedral.

Why offer a dark mode at all?expand_more

Ink & Marble is our answer to candlelight: the same architecture rendered in deep ink and pale stone. Contrast is measured against both grounds, so inscriptions remain legible from noon to midnight.

May I leave whenever I wish?expand_more

Certainly — a well-proportioned door invites exit as readily as entry. Your ledger and compositions export entire, in open formats, and no clause binds you to the columns.
