FAQ

Questions, answered loudly

Do you ship internationally, and how fast?expand_more

Yes — every order leaves our Lisbon studio within 48 hours, rolled in a crush-proof tube with end-to-end tracking to 40+ countries. Most deliveries land in 3–8 business days, and the tube doubles as an excellent pretend telescope.

How long does a commission take?expand_more

Concept sketches arrive in five days; finished inkwork takes two to four weeks depending on scale. We lock the timeline and the price before you pay a cent, and you approve every stage.

What if my print arrives damaged?expand_more

Send us a photo within seven days and a replacement ships free — no forms, no interrogation, no restocking fee. Reprints are guaranteed for 60 days after delivery, because accidents are louder with witnesses.
