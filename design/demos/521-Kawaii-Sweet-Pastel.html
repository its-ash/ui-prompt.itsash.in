Curious? We have answers

Everything you might wonder before planting your first habit.

Is Mochi really free to start?expand_more

Yes. The Sprout plan is free forever with three habits, streaks, and a starter sticker pack. No card required.

What happens if I miss a day?expand_more

Nothing scary. Your garden pauses, and Mochi offers a gentle restart so one rough day never erases your progress.

Can I use Mochi on my phone and computer?expand_more

Absolutely. Mochi syncs across iOS, Android, and the web, so your streak garden is always with you.

Can I share habits with friends or family?expand_more

Yes, on the Parfait plan you can open a shared garden, cheer each other on, and trade sticker gifts.

How do I cancel a paid plan?expand_more

Cancel anytime in two taps from settings. You keep premium treats until the end of your billing month.
