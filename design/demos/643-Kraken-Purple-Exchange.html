Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is Kraken Purple Exchange? expand_more

We employ institutional-grade security measures including 95% cold storage for assets, multi-signature wallets, SOC 2 Type II compliance, and continuous 24/7 monitoring by our security team. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption.

What are the trading fees? expand_more

Fees vary by account tier and trading volume. Starter accounts start at 0.25% maker/taker fees, Trader tier at 0.15%, and Pro tier offers custom pricing. Fee discounts are available for high-volume traders and KNC token holders.

How long do withdrawals take? expand_more

Crypto withdrawals are typically processed within 5-15 minutes after network confirmation. Fiat withdrawals to bank accounts take 1-3 business days depending on your bank and country. We provide real-time status updates for all withdrawal requests.
