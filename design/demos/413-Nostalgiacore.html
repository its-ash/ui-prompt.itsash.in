BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions from the attic

Are my original photos ever altered?expand_more

Never. Nimbus stores your untouched originals and applies the vintage look as a separate layer. You can download the pristine file at any time.

Can I scan physical prints with my phone?expand_more

Yes. The mobile app straightens, crops, and de-glares prints automatically. A stack of fifty photos takes about ten minutes on the couch.

What happens if I cancel my plan?expand_more

Your albums stay readable for a full year, and you can export everything in one click. Memories should never be held hostage.
