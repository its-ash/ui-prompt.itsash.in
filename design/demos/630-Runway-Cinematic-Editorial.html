05, FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

How long can a generated clip be?expand_more
A single generation runs up to ten seconds. You can extend any clip in five-second increments up to thirty seconds while keeping characters and lighting consistent.
Who owns the footage I create?expand_more
You do. Paid plans include a full commercial license for everything you render, with no attribution required.
Can I use my own footage as a starting point?expand_more
Yes. Upload a still frame or a short clip and Nimbus will restyle it, extend it, or generate matching shots in the same look.
What resolution can I export?expand_more
Free exports are 720p. Pro and Unlimited render natively at 1080p and upscale to 4K, in H.264 or ProRes.
Do unused credits roll over?expand_more
On Pro, up to one month of unused credits carries forward. Unlimited plans have no credit balance to manage.
