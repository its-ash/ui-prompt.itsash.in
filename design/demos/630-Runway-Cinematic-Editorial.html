04 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum video length I can create?

Free tier allows 10 seconds per month. Pro tier offers 60 seconds monthly. Unlimited tier provides unrestricted generation time with fair usage policy applied.

Can I use the generated videos commercially?

Yes, all videos created with Nimbus come with full commercial rights. You can use them in advertisements, films, social media, and any other commercial project without additional licensing.

What file formats are supported for export?

We support MP4 (H.264), MOV (ProRes), and WebM formats. You can also export image sequences for professional color grading workflows.
