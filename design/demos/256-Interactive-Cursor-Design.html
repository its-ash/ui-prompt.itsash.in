FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the 14-day trial work?

Every plan is free for 14 days with full features — no credit card required. When the trial ends, pick a plan or keep your flows as read-only templates. Nothing is deleted.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and offers SSO, role-based access, and full audit logs on the Scale plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month, you can cancel in two clicks from billing settings, and we prorate refunds on unused annual time.
