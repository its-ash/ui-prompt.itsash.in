Questions from the Hall

Asked and answered

Do I need any background in ancient languages?expand_more

None at all. The course assumes no prior study. The first rank teaches the alphabet from zero, and every inscription includes transliteration so you always know what you are reading.

Which stage of Egyptian does Cartouche teach?expand_more

We teach Middle Egyptian, the classical literary language of roughly 2000 to 1300 BCE. It is the standard starting point in university programs and unlocks the largest body of texts.

How much time should I set aside each week?expand_more

Two to three hours is enough for steady progress. Lessons are divided into twenty-minute drills, and the reading circle meets once a week for one hour.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?expand_more

Yes. Cancel from your account page in two clicks. You keep archive access until the end of the paid period, and your lesson progress is preserved if you return.

Are the translations academically reliable?expand_more

Every text cites its published edition, and our annotations are reviewed by credentialed Egyptologists. Where translations are disputed, we present the alternatives rather than hiding them.
