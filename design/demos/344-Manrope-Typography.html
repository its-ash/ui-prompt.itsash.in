Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to host my own fonts?expand_more

No. Kern works with any webfont — Google-hosted, self-hosted, or licensed foundry files. Just paste a URL or upload the files and we handle the rest.

Which frameworks do exports support?expand_more

Every scale exports as plain CSS variables, JSON, or a ready-to-drop Tailwind config. Figma variable libraries sync automatically on paid plans.

Can I change plans or cancel later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your dashboard — changes take effect at the end of your billing period, with no lock-in.
