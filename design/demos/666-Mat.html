Common questions

Most pieces ship within three weeks of your order. Larger items like shelving systems can take up to five, and we email you at each stage.

Every design uses standard mechanical fixings, so replacement parts stay available for at least fifteen years. Care plan members get them free.

We currently ship across the US and Canada. Flat-packed designs keep freight costs low, and duties are calculated at checkout.
