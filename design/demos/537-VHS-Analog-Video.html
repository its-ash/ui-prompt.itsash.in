Before you ship

Straight answers about formats, safety and turnaround.

We handle VHS, VHS-C, S-VHS, Hi8, Video8 and MiniDV. If your format is not listed, contact us and we will confirm before you ship.

Yes. Every cassette is barcoded on arrival, stored in a climate-controlled room, and returned with your finished order. Playback is done once, gently, in real time.

We clean mold and repair broken shells or snapped ribbon at no extra charge. If a tape is truly unplayable, you do not pay for that tape.

Most orders finish within two weeks of arrival at our studio. Studio Vault orders get priority handling and typically complete faster.

You receive MP4 files at the highest resolution the tape can deliver, plus a private streaming link. Files are named by tape and labeled event where possible.

Only your assigned technician. Files are stored on encrypted drives, never used for training or marketing, and deleted 60 days after you confirm delivery.
