FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Do GridLearn certificates expire?expand_more

No. Certificates are verifiable for life via a public credential URL and never require a renewal fee.

Do I need prior experience to start a track?expand_more

Beginner tracks require none. Intermediate and advanced tracks list prerequisites on the course page, and a free placement check confirms you are ready.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your account settings; changes take effect at the end of the current cycle.
