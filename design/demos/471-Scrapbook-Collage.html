From camera roll to keepsake in about five minutes.

    Gather your pieces

    Drop in photos, voice notes, and little text clippings from anywhere on your device.

  2. 2

    Arrange the page

    Share the album

    Send a link to the people in the photos so they can add the moments you missed.

Questions from the margins

The things people ask before they start pasting.

Do my albums stay private?expand_more

Yes. Every album is private by default and only visible to people you invite by link. You can revoke access at any time.

Can I print my pages as a real book?expand_more

Keepsake and Heirloom plans include print-ready exports. Heirloom adds a yearly credit toward a bound printed book.

What happens if I cancel my plan?expand_more

Your albums stay readable forever on the free tier. You keep every page; you just lose the ability to add beyond free limits.
