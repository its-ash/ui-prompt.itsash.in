PANEL 06
How do I import my existing comics?

Drag and drop CBZ/CBIP files. Our engine auto-detects panels, layers, and speech bubble placements. No manual rework needed.

Can I use Nimbus offline?

Yes. The core engine works offline. Sync your progress when you reconnect. Changes are saved locally indexedDB.

What file formats does Nimbus export?

Export to CBZ, PDF, PNG sequence, or HTML5 canvas. All exports preserve ink outlines and Ben-Day halftone fidelity.
