Common questions

Everything teams ask before adding sound to their interface.

Will sounds autoplay without user consent?expand_more
No. The AudioContext initializes only after the first user gesture, and every sound has a visual equivalent. Users can mute everything with one toggle, and the choice persists.
How does Nimbus handle reduced-motion users?expand_more
Shake, ripple, and glow animations switch off automatically under prefers-reduced-motion. Audio cues remain available but are always paired with static visual states.
Can I use my own audio samples?expand_more
Yes. Resonance and Symphony plans let you upload custom samples or synthesize tones from frequency, envelope, and waveform parameters in the chime designer.
What is the performance cost?expand_more
The runtime is under 6KB gzipped. Oscillator-based tones are generated on the fly, so there are no audio files to download and no impact on initial page load.
Does it work with React, Vue, or plain HTML?expand_more
All three. Use data attributes for declarative wiring in plain HTML, or call the small JavaScript API directly from any framework lifecycle hook.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime?expand_more
Yes. Plans are month to month with prorated upgrades. Downgrading keeps your sound maps intact, just within the lower event allowance.
