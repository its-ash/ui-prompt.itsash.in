The Record

3 questions on record

How is my capital protected?expand_more

Your assets are held in your own name at an independent, regulated custodian — never on our balance sheet. We hold authority to advise; you hold title to everything.

Can the whole family share one account?expand_more

Yes. Essential includes two seats and Signature is unlimited, with role-based permissions so elders, executors, and heirs each see exactly what they should.

How do fees change as the portfolio grows?expand_more

They shrink. Our commission steps down at each milestone of assets under counsel, so our incentive is your compounding — not our billing.
