How It Works

A fixed three-phase structure. No drifting scope, no surprise revisions, no ambiguity about what ships and when.

01

Blueprint

Two weeks of discovery and system design. You receive a technical blueprint with architecture diagrams, data contracts, and a fixed build plan.

02

Structure

The core build. Weekly staged releases let you walk through real progress instead of reviewing static mockups or slide decks.

03

Finish

Performance tuning, accessibility audit, and handover documentation. You own the full system, not a black box.
