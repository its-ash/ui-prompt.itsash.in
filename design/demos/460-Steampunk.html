How long does installation take?expand_more

Most workshops are under full steam within one afternoon. Our fittings connect to the tools you already own, and a journeyman guides your first calibration by telegraph or video call.

Is my workshop data safe aboard your engines?expand_more

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the keys remain in your possession. We comply with the International Aether-Safety Charter of 1901, and then some.

What is the Foundry Guarantee?expand_more

If your machinery fails to hold pressure within the first sixty days, we refund every penny and send an engineer to collect the unit at our expense. No forms, no fuss.
