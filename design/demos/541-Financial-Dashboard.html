Questions, answered

Everything finance and security teams usually ask before connecting a ledger.

How is our financial data secured?expand_more
All data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. LedgerPulse is SOC 2 Type II certified, and connections to banks and ERPs are read-only by default.
Which integrations are supported?expand_more
We connect natively to QuickBooks, NetSuite, Xero, SAP, and 12,000+ banks via open banking. CSV and spreadsheet imports cover everything else.
Can we export the audit trail?expand_more
Yes. Export the full immutable trail as CSV or PDF at any time, filtered by user, period, or account. Auditors can also get read-only guest access.
What are the limits on the Starter plan?expand_more
Starter includes 3 connections, monthly budget tracking, and a 12-month audit trail. Dashboards and users within your seat count are unlimited.
Can we cancel anytime?expand_more
Monthly plans cancel anytime with no fees. Annual plans can be cancelled at renewal, and your data remains exportable for 90 days after cancellation.
