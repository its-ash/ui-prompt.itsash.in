Frequently asked questions

expand_more Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES‑256). We never store raw bank credentials; OAuth 2.0 flows handle connections.

expand_more Which integrations do you support?

QuickBooks, Xero, SAP, Oracle, Snowflake, AWS S3, and CSV import. Custom API connectors available on Enterprise plans.

expand_more Can I export audit trails for external auditors?

Yes. Export immutable CSV, JSON, or PDF reports with digital‑signature options. Auditors can verify timestamps without accessing the live platform.

expand_more What are the plan limits?

Starter: 3 connectors, 2 users, 10K transactions/month. Growth: 10 connectors, 5 users, 50K transactions/month. Enterprise: unlimited.

expand_more How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account settings. Billing stops at the end of the current period; no refunds for partial months.
