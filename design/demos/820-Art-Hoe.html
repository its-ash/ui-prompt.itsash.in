Three steps, zero pressure

A spread takes about ten minutes. Messy is the house style.

  1. 01 photo_camera

    Snap and tape

    Drop in film photos, ticket stubs or scans. Everything lands on the page with a strip of tape.

  2. 02 brush

    Scribble and layer

    Paint over the photos, write in the margins, tear and rearrange until the page says what you meant.

  3. 03 push_pin

    Pin and share

    Keep it private in your journal, or pin it to a shared studio wall for the people you choose.
