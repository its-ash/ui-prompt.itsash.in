Questions, answered plainly

The short version of what people ask before they commit.

Do I need to know how to code?expand_more
No. The visual editor handles layout and tokens. When you are ready, export clean HTML and CSS that any developer can read and extend.
Can I use my own brand colors?expand_more
Yes. Swap the token palette and Nimbus regenerates light and dark themes with contrast-checked pairings automatically.
What happens when I cancel?expand_more
You keep everything you exported. Your projects stay readable for 90 days, and you can export again any time before that window closes.
