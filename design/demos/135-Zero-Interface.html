Questions, answered

Does Aura listen to everything I say?remove

Core audio is processed on your device. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere unless you enable cloud features — and you can wipe your context with one sentence.

Can it work offline?add

Voice commands and gestures run locally. Predictive features use a connection but degrade gracefully — the basics never go dark.

What gestures are supported?add

Wave to pause, tap to silence, a held hand to hold a reminder. Every motion can be remapped, or switched off entirely.

Which devices does it run on?add

Any phone or laptop from the last five years, plus most popular smart-home hubs. No new hardware required.

How do I cancel?add

One sentence — literally, say “cancel my plan.” No forms, no retention maze, no hold music.
