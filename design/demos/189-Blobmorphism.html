Questions, answered

Everything about sourcing, delivery, and our returnable crates.

Where does my produce actually come from?expand_more

Every item is grown on one of 40+ partner farms within 100 miles of your city. Your box lists each farm, what was picked, and when — full traceability, no middlemen.

What if I'm not home for delivery?expand_more

Drivers leave your crate in a shaded spot you choose and text you a photo. Crates are insulated, so produce stays fresh for up to six hours on the doorstep.

How does the returnable crate work?expand_more

Leave the empty crate out on your next delivery day. We collect, sanitize, and refill it — and for every crate returned we fund one new tree with our reforestation partners.
