Everything studios ask before they make the cut.

Do I need to be a print studio to use this?expand_more

No. Letterpress shops, risograph collectives, and solo artists all use Nimbus Press. If you produce work in editions, the tools fit.

Can my clients approve proofs without an account?expand_more

Yes. Sign-off links open in any browser with no login. Clients annotate, approve, or request changes, and every action is recorded.

What happens to my data if I cancel?expand_more

You keep a full export of editions, proofs, and approvals for 12 months after cancellation. Nothing is held hostage.
