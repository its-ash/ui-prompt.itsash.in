Frequently asked questions

How does Parity compute deltas?

You pick a baseline and a comparison period, and Parity aligns the two and shows absolute and percentage change for every metric, with green and red indicators.

Can I compare more than two periods?

Yes. Stack up to four periods or segments on the same board and toggle between them.

Does Parity connect to my data warehouse?

Native connectors exist for Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake, and popular CRMs. CSV import works for everything else.
