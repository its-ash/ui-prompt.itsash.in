The House Rules

Questions, Answered

Everything one politely wonders before the first visit.

The charter caps membership at five hundred names worldwide. We review requests weekly and extend roughly one invitation in forty — not from cruelty, but to keep the room worth being in.

Members may escort two guests per evening. Guests of exceptional charm have been known to receive invitations of their own.

Evening dress after seven o’clock. We ask that denim remain a daytime affair, and that sequins never apologize for themselves.

Your cipher reaches the house concierge by day or night — theatre tickets, a quiet table in another city, or a car waiting in the rain. Consider it a wish, granted in gold.

Either is arranged with thirty days’ written notice and without penalty. Departed members are always welcomed back at the door, though their reserved table may not wait.
