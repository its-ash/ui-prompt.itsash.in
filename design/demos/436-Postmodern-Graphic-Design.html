02 / Method

How to misbehave in three steps

  1. 1

    Step 01, Irony

    Import your tidy layout. We immediately rotate it and pretend it was always like that.

  2. 2

    Step 02, Pastiche

    Borrow from 1974, 1989, and a fax machine. Historical accuracy is optional, attitude is not.

  3. 3

    Step 03, Overprint

    Export with solid offset shadows and clashing ink. If it reads too easily, start over.

05 / Questions

Asked frequently, answered obliquely

Is the grid really dead?expand_more

The grid is a wonderful servant and a tedious master. We keep it in the basement and feed it occasionally.

Can I use Overprint for a serious corporate rebrand?expand_more

Yes, and we admire your courage. Legal has asked us to add that courage is not a defense.

Why only three typefaces?expand_more

Restraint is the last refuge of the maximalist. Also, four fonts in one headline crashed our renderer in 1993 and we never forgave it.
