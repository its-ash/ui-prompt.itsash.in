05 / OBJECTIONS

Questions, answered badly

Is this just ugliness with a pricing page?expand_more

No. Ugliness is accidental. Overprint is deliberate, referenced, and footnoted. There is a difference, and we have a manifesto to prove it.

Will my brand team approve?expand_more

The Institution tier includes brand-safe chaos presets, which keep your logo intact while everything around it has a small, tasteful crisis.

Can I make something readable?expand_more

Technically yes. The tool allows restraint. We simply choose not to ship a button for it, as a matter of principle.
