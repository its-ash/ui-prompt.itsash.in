Frequently Asked Questions

How do I care for my flea performer? expand_more
Fleas require minimal care: a drop of water daily, weekly flea-sized meals (provided), and a dark, cozy habitat. We include a care guide with every adoption.
Is the show suitable for children? expand_more
Absolutely! LA PUCE is designed for all ages. Children particularly enjoy the interactive training mode where they can teach fleas simple tricks.
What equipment do I need? expand_more
Just a smartphone or computer with internet access. Our web app works in any modern browser. No special hardware required.
How long do flea performers live? expand_more
With proper care, our fleas live 2-3 months. We offer adoption renewal programs and can provide successors from the same bloodline.
Can I request a custom act? expand_more
Yes! Ringmaster and Big Top tiers include custom choreography services. Submit your vision and our flea trainers will bring it to life.
