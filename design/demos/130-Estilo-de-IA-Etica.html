FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

How does Nimbus prevent harmful or inaccurate outputs?expand_more

Every request passes intent checks and safety guardrails before generation, and every answer is grounded in verified sources with a confidence score. Low-confidence or sensitive replies are routed to a human reviewer instead of guessing.

Is my data used to train your models?expand_more

No. Conversations are end-to-end encrypted, never used for training, and fully deletable on request. Enterprise plans can pin all data to a single region for added control.

What safety standards does Nimbus comply with?expand_more

We're independently audited against the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO/IEC 42001, with SOC 2 Type II reports available under NDA. Audit results are summarized publicly each quarter.
