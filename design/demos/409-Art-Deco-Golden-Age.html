Enquiries

Questions, Answered

Everything a discerning patron wishes to know before signing the register.

How quickly can I begin?expand_more

Immediately upon joining. Your suite is prepared within minutes, and our guided tour walks you through every gilded corridor.

May I change my tier later?expand_more

Of course. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account; adjustments are prorated to the day, with no penalties.

Is there a trial period?expand_more

Every tier includes fourteen days of full access, complimentary. No card is required until you decide to stay.

What support may I expect?expand_more

Patrons receive replies within one business day. Salon and Proprietor members enjoy priority concierge responses within hours.

Can I cancel at any time?expand_more

Yes, with a single click and no farewell interrogation. Your work remains exportable for thirty days after departure.
