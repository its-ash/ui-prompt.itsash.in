Frequently asked

Do I need a studio or a fancy camera?expand_more

No. Nimbus works with any phone camera and any clean surface — a desk, a sheet of matte paper, or a bright patch of floor by a window.

What exactly is knolling?expand_more

Knolling means arranging objects at right angles, edge to edge, so everything reads as one calm, orderly grid — the signature look of great flat-lay photography.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes. Plans are monthly, you can upgrade or downgrade from the billing page at any time, and cancelling takes one click — no exit interviews.
