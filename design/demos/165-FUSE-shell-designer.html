Questions, answered

Which shells does FUSE support?

zsh, bash, fish, PowerShell (5.1 and 7+) and any starship-compatible terminal. Exports are plain text, so exotic setups work too.

How do I install an exported theme?

Paste the one-line command from the export panel into your terminal. FUSE writes your config file and reloads the prompt immediately.

Who owns the themes I create?

You do. Themes are yours to sell, share or keep private. FUSE claims no rights over your work.

Does FUSE work with PowerShell?

Yes. The studio renders a faithful PowerShell preview and exports a profile script compatible with Windows Terminal, pwsh 7+ and PSReadLine.

What are the free plan limits?

Spark includes one saved theme and community exports. There is no time limit and no credit card required.
