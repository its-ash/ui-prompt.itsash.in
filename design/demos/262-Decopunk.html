Chapter Five

Questions from the Lobby

Must I rewire my entire estate?expand_more

Not a socket. The Halcyon receiver draws ordinary power and tunes itself; our installers leave the plaster untouched and the chrome gleaming.

Will the signal survive a storm at sea?expand_more

Eleven art-deco relay spires carry the broadcast. Should one falter, another takes up the melody within seconds — patrons rarely notice the hand-off.

May I cancel my membership?expand_more

Any evening, by a single click or a polite telegram. The receiver remains yours, and your final month is on the house.

What exactly is “Deco definition”?expand_more

Each frame is restored by hand, then polished until the chrome sings. It is sharper than the future ever promised in 1928.

Do you serve hotels and grand ballrooms?expand_more

Gladly. The Ziggurat tier installs four receivers, wires a private line to our programming office, and sends an architect to oversee it all.
