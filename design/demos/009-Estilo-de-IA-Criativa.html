FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I own the art I create?expand_more

Yes. You own every artwork you generate on any plan, including commercial use on Pro and Studio. We never resell or relicense your creations.

What can I import and export?expand_more

Import PNG, JPG, WEBP and SVG sketches or reference images. Export PNG, JPG and SVG on Free and Pro; Studio adds layered PSD and up to 8K resolution.

Is my work used to train your models?expand_more

No. Private projects stay private by default. Opting in to the community gallery is the only way your art influences shared styles — and you can revoke it anytime.

Can I use Chromaflow commercially?expand_more

Yes — Pro and Studio include a full commercial license covering ads, merch, print and client work. The Free plan is for personal, non-commercial projects.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Anytime, in two clicks, no email required. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and your artwork library stays downloadable forever.
