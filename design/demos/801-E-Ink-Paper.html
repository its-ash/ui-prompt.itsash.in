Frequently asked questions

Does Nimbus support color?add

No. The display is monochrome to maximize contrast and battery life.

Can I load my own PDFs?add

Yes. Drag‑and‑drop via USB or send to your personal @nimbus.read address.

Is there a subscription fee?add

No. Nimbus is a one‑time purchase; all firmware updates are free.

How long does the battery last?add

Up to 8 weeks of regular reading with the front light off.

Does it have a touchscreen?add

Yes, a capacitive touch layer for page turns and menu navigation.

Can I annotate books?add

Yes. Use the optional stylus to highlight, note, and draw.
