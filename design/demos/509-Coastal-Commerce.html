Common Questions

How sustainable are your materials? expand_more
We partner exclusively with certified sustainable sources. 80% of our plastics are reclaimed from ocean waste, and our wood is FSC-certified driftwood.
Do you ship internationally? expand_more
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. We use carbon-offset logistics to ensure our global footprint remains minimal.
What is your return policy? expand_more
We offer a 30-day "Tide-Turn" guarantee. If you aren't satisfied with your purchase, we'll take it back and plant a mangrove tree in your name.
