Questions

Before the first fitting

How long does a bespoke typeface take?expand_more

A single-weight Couture commission typically spans ten to fourteen weeks, including consultations, inked specimens, and refinement rounds.

Do we own the final letterforms?expand_more

Couture commissions include exclusive rights within your industry. Maison commissions include full global licensing in perpetuity.

Can you work from our archive materials?expand_more

Gladly. Historical monograms, packaging, and correspondence often become the seed of the most distinctive commissions.

Are the fonts delivered for web use?expand_more

Yes. Every commission ships with hinted desktop fonts, optimized webfonts, and press-ready specimen sheets.

What if we only need a wordmark?expand_more

The Essai tier is designed for exactly that: a hand-lettered wordmark refined through two rounds, without a full typeface.
