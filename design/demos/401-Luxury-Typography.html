The Enquiries

Fitting Notes

Do the families include a web licence?expand_more

Every collection leaves the atelier with desktop and web entitlements included. Broadcast and app licences are fitted on request, measured to the scale of your production.

May I request a custom ligature?expand_more

Certainly. Bespoke joins are our specialty — bring the letter pair and our cutters will compose a flourish that flatters both. Couture clients receive two custom ligatures with every fitting.

What is the return policy?expand_more

Unworn typefaces may be returned within fourteen days for a full refund. Once a family has appeared in print or on screen, we consider it fitted — and fittings, like couture, are final.
