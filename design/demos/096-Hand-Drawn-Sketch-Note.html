Scribbled questions, straight answers

Still curious? Write to hello@sketchnote.studio — we reply with actual human hands.

Do I need to be able to draw?expand_more

Not even slightly. The doodle library draws for you — if you can type a bullet point, you can make a sketch-note. If you can draw, your flourishes stay entirely yours.

Can I export and print my sketch-notes?expand_more

Yep. Every sketch-note exports as PNG, PDF, or a cleaned-up SVG vector, so it stays sharp on slides, posters, and (we are told) at least one tattoo.

What happens to my notes and doodles?expand_more

They stay yours. Notes are private by default, and you can export or permanently delete everything anytime. No strings, no rulers attached.
