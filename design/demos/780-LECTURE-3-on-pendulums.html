Three moves and you are swinging

The whole lab fits between the bell and the end of class.

  1. 1

    Grab a pendulum

    Pick a rod length: short, medium, or long. The rig adjusts instantly, no string knots required.

  2. 2

    Set it swinging

    Release from a small angle and watch the bob trace its arc. Mass does not matter; length does.

  3. 3

    Predict and check

    Estimate the period from the formula, then run the stopwatch. Close guesses earn chalk stars.
