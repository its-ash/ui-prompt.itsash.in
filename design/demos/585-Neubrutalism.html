Is the free plan actually free?add

Yes. Side Quest costs nothing forever, no card required. We only ask for money when your team needs the big features.

Can I import from Trello, Asana, or Notion?add

One click imports boards, cards, comments, and attachments from all three. Most teams finish migrating in under ten minutes.

Does Hypeboard work for solo projects?add

Absolutely. Plenty of users run personal boards for side hustles, moving houses, or planning a very organized road trip.

What happens if I cancel a paid plan?add

You drop back to Side Quest with all your boards intact. Nothing gets deleted, and you can export everything anytime.

Is my data private?add

Your boards are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell data, and Stadium adds SSO, audit logs, and regional hosting.
