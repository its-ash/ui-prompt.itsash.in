Common questions

Does Nimbus IR record identifiable video?expand_more

No. Sensors capture radiometric heat data, not photographic images. People appear as anonymous heat signatures, and no facial or visual detail is ever stored.

How accurate are the temperature readings?expand_more

Each node reads within plus or minus 0.5 degrees Celsius across its rated range of -20 to 120 degrees, with automatic drift correction every 24 hours.

What happens if a node goes offline?expand_more

The node buffers readings locally for up to 72 hours and syncs when connectivity returns. Your dashboard marks the zone as stale so gaps are never silent.
