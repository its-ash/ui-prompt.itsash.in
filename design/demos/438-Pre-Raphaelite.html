Talk to Us

Questions, answered plainly

How long does it take to move a team in?expand_more

Most teams import existing work and are planning within a day. Our guided setup walks you through projects, people, and permissions in order.

Can I change plans mid-season?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time and we prorate the difference. Your work and history stay intact either way.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?expand_more

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in your choice of regions. Only the people you invite can see your workspace, and you can export everything at any time.
