Questions from the margins

Where do my volumes live?expand_more

On your device first, with optional sync through your own storage. Nimbus never reads or sells the contents of your pages.

Can I leave and take my notes with me?expand_more

Yes. Every volume exports to plain text and standard image formats at any time, on any tier, including the free one.

Does Nimbus work without a connection?expand_more

Fully. Write, annotate, and bind offline; everything syncs the next time you are connected.
