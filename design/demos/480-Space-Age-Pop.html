How it works

Three steps to liftoff.

No rewiring, no renovation. Most homes are fully orbiting within a single afternoon.

1

Place the pods

Set a sensor pod in each room. They pair with the console on their own, like satellites finding a signal.

2

Turn the dial

Choose a scene on the chrome console: Sunrise, Deep Focus, or Lounge. Every room responds together.

3

Let it learn

Orbit OS notes your preferences for a week, then starts adjusting before you reach for the dial.

FAQ

Ground control answers.

The questions we hear most before liftoff, answered plainly.

Do the pods need professional installation?expand_more
No. Each pod sits on a shelf or mounts with the included adhesive ring. Pairing happens automatically when you power on the console.
What devices does the console control?expand_more
Any Matter, Zigbee, or Wi-Fi light, thermostat, purifier, or speaker. The console bridges older protocols, so most existing gear works out of the box.
Can I cancel my plan at any time?expand_more
Yes. Plans are month to month. If you cancel, the console and pods keep working in manual mode forever, with no subscription required.
