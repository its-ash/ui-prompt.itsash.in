Ground control answers

Does the pod work with my existing fixtures?add

Yes. Nimbus Orbit speaks to standard smart bulbs, thermostats, and vents over Wi-Fi and Thread. A compatibility checker in the app confirms your setup before you buy.

What happens if my internet goes down?add

The pod runs every routine locally on the device. Your flight plans keep executing offline; the app simply reconnects when service returns.

Can I move it to a new home?add

Unplug it, pack it, plug it back in. The pod re-maps the new space in about ninety seconds and keeps your preferences intact.

Is there a subscription fee?add

No. You pay once for the hardware and every software update is included for the life of the product.
