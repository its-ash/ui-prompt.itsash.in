FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

How are listings verified on Coral Stays?expand_more

Every host completes ID verification, and every listing is reviewed by our trust team before it goes live. Guests can also filter for stays with 50+ completed verified trips.

What does hosting cost?expand_more

Listing is free. We charge hosts a 3% fee on completed bookings (1.5% on Pro). Guests pay a separate service fee at checkout — never a surprise on arrival.

Can guests cancel for free?expand_more

Yes — every booking includes free cancellation within 48 hours of booking, and each stay's cancellation policy is labelled clearly before you pay.
