Manifesto

Three tenets, cut into the colophon.

design_services

Typography is plot

Grid, margin and fold carry as much story as the sentences do — so our designers read the manuscript before the typesetter does.

Every edition begins with the author over a blank grid: serif for the narrator, sans for the interruptions. The typeface is cast like a character — with motives, an alibi, and a habit of breaking the rules it set for itself.

edit_note

Marginalia, welcomed

Our editions leave deliberate white space — and Colophon Club members fill it before the book goes to press.

The sharpest member annotations are set into the margins of the final edition, credited by name. Your pencil, legally bound into the canon — with an ISBN to prove it.

menu_book

The object remains

Sewn signatures, foil stamps, and ink you can smell from across the room. Ephemeral texts; permanent artifacts.

Print runs are numbered and never reprinted. When a Red Galley edition sells out it becomes a rumor with an ISBN — and, once in a while, a small scandal at auction.

Process

From unruly manuscript to numbered object.

  1. 01

    Submit without a genre

    Send the manuscript your other publisher asked you to flatten. No agent, no fee, no form — the slush hatch opens every March and September.

  2. 02

    The editorial forge

    Two editors argue in your margins for six weeks. Structure wins the fight; darlings are re-homed, not murdered.

  3. 03

    Built as an object

    Typeset, hand-proofed, and printed in a numbered run. You receive fifty author copies and one very loud launch.

Answered

Questions, resolved in the margin.

Do you accept unsolicited manuscripts?expand_more

Yes. The slush hatch opens every March and September. No agent, no reading fee, no genre field — send the manuscript other publishers asked you to flatten, and expect a real reply within eight weeks.

What counts as “experimental” here?expand_more

If the form is doing narrative work, it counts. Constraint-driven prose, documentary collage, a novel shaped like an index — we care less about shock than about structure: does the shape carry the story?

Can I buy single titles without a membership?expand_more

Always. Every book is sold outright through our catalog and independent bookshops. Memberships exist to deliver limited runs early and keep the presses solvent — the books themselves are never locked away.

Add your name to the colophon.

One letter a month: new galleys, open calls, and a single beautifully typeset argument. No spam — we find it typographically offensive.
