FAQ

Before You Pack

The questions every explorer asks before boarding.

How are your tours actually sustainable?expand_more

We cap groups at eight, route around sensitive habitats with ICMBio partners, offset every kilometer traveled, and replant native trees with 5% of each booking. Our lodges run on solar power and buy food from surrounding communities.

What group sizes and difficulty levels should I expect?expand_more

Departures max out at 8 travelers. Difficulty spans easy — 2–4 hour walks and Pantanal safaris — to challenging cave treks in Chapada Diamantina. Every itinerary lists a fitness level, and your guide adapts the daily pacing to the group.

What is your cancellation policy?expand_more

Cancel up to 30 days before departure for a full refund minus a 5% conservation contribution. Inside 30 days, reschedule free of charge within 12 months. Extreme-weather cancellations are always refunded in full.
