Questions From the Bar

Everything else, ask the bartender.

Do I need to be 21 to subscribe?expand_more

Yes. Every crate contains spirits, so an adult signature is required at delivery. The mugs and records, however, are all-ages friendly.

Can I skip a month or cancel?expand_more

Anytime, from your account page, in two clicks. Skip a month when the bar cart is full; cancel when you move to an actual island.

Where do you ship?expand_more

We ship to most US states where direct spirits delivery is legal. At checkout we confirm your address before charging a cent.
