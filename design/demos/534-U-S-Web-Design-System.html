Common questions

Answers in plain language. If you need more help, our support team replies within one business day.

Is Nimbus a government agency?expand_more
No. Nimbus is a private service that helps you prepare federal forms. We follow government design and accessibility standards, but we are not affiliated with any agency. You can always file directly with the agency for free.
How is my personal information protected?expand_more
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to systems that process your filing. We never sell or share your information for marketing, and you can permanently delete your records from your account settings.
What if my form is rejected?expand_more
If an agency rejects a form because of an error Nimbus missed, we will help you correct and resubmit it at no charge. If the rejection is due to eligibility, we explain the agency's notice in plain language so you know your options.
Can I use Nimbus on a screen reader?expand_more
Yes. Every page is built to WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 standards, with semantic structure, labeled controls, and full keyboard support. We test regularly with common screen readers.
Can I cancel my plan at any time?expand_more
Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled from your account page in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the paid period, and your completed forms remain available for download for 90 days.
