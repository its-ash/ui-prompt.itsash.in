Questions

Can I use a different grid size?

No, and that is the point. Every board is 3 by 3. You can link boards together for bigger stories.

What files can I add?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, short MP4 clips, text notes and color swatches.

Do I keep rights to my images?

Always. Ninefold only stores and displays them for you and never trains on your work.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your boards stay readable on the free plan after you cancel.
