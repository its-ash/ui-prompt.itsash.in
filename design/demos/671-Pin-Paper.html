Questions, answered by hand

Is the paper fountain-pen friendly?expand_more

Yes. Every sheet is 120gsm cotton-blend stock, tested with fine, medium, and broad nibs. Ink sits on the page with no feathering and no bleed-through.

When will my first kit arrive?expand_more

Your first kit ships within 5 business days of your order, worldwide. After that, kits leave the studio on the first Monday of each month.

Can I pause or cancel?expand_more

Anytime, from your account page, in two clicks. Pausing never costs anything, and your archive stays exactly as you left it.

Do I need the companion app?expand_more

No. The app is optional, free, and ad-free. It exists only to scan and pin your pages; the paper ritual works entirely without it.

Can I give a subscription as a gift?expand_more

Yes. Gift plans include a handwritten note from the studio tucked into the first envelope, and the recipient chooses their own start month.

Do you ship internationally?expand_more

We ship worldwide at a flat rate. Kits travel in sturdy paper envelopes, and most international orders arrive within two weeks.
