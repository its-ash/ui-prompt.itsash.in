Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about performance, support, and licensing.

Will Glide hurt my page performance?expand_more

No. All motion runs on the compositor thread using transforms and opacity only. Glide adds roughly 12 kB gzipped and idles at zero cost when nothing is animating.

How does it handle accessibility?expand_more

When a visitor prefers reduced motion, Glide disables smoothing, parallax, and reveals automatically. Content is always fully visible without JavaScript.

Does it work with React, Vue, or Svelte?expand_more

Yes. Pro and Team plans include thin framework adapters, and the core library works with any setup that renders real DOM elements.
