Frequently asked questions

Can I use my existing database?

Yes. Aether connects to your current Postgres instance without migration.

Is branching safe?

Branches are isolated and can be discarded without affecting production.

Do you support rollbacks?

Yes. Every migration includes a reversible plan and audit history.

What about uptime?

Deployments avoid downtime by routing traffic gracefully.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long term contracts on Starter or Pro.
