Frequently asked questions

Everything else lives in the docs.

Where does Nimbus run?expand_more

Nimbus connects to your warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, or Postgres) over read-only credentials and executes queries inside your account. Your data never leaves your VPC.

How fresh are the dashboards?expand_more

CDC-connected sources land in under 60 seconds; batch sources sync on the schedule you define. Every widget shows its own freshness timestamp.

Does Nimbus work with dbt?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus reads your dbt models and exposures, and turns tested models into governed metrics your whole company can query and alert on.
