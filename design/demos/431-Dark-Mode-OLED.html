Common questions

Everything else, answered plainly.

Does true black really save battery?expand_more

Yes. On OLED and AMOLED panels, black pixels are physically turned off and draw no power. Our interface maximizes true black surface area.

Can I import from my current tools?expand_more

Nocturne imports docs, tasks, and boards from the most common workspace tools. Most imports finish in under five minutes.

Is there a free plan limit?expand_more

The Free plan includes one workspace with unlimited docs and tasks. It stays free forever, no trial clock.

How does Night Shift Sync work?expand_more

Using your local sunset time, Nocturne gradually warms accent colors and reduces glow intensity as the evening progresses.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes. Cancel from your billing page in two clicks. Your data stays exportable for 30 days after cancellation.
