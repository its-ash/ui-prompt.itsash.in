Frequently Asked Questions

Does this app work on LCD screens?
Yes. While optimized for OLED, the app functions perfectly on LCD displays with the same dark theme.
Is there a light mode toggle?
No. This release is dark‑only by design to maximize OLED benefits.
How does the battery saver work?
The UI uses true black (#000000) which turns off OLED pixels, reducing power draw.
Can I customize the accent colors?
Absolutely. The Pro and Team tiers let you pick from neon green, electric blue, gold, or plasma purple.
Is my data stored on your servers?
We use end‑to‑end encryption and never store personal data on our servers.
