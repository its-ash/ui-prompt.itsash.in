Questions from the meadow

Everything new pilots ask before their first launch.

Do I need to download anything?expand_more

No. Paper Winds runs entirely in your browser on desktop and mobile. Open the page, build a kite, and fly.

How realistic is the wind simulation?expand_more

We model lift, drag, line tension, and gust patterns. It is tuned for fun first, but experienced kite flyers tell us the handling feels familiar.

Can I play with friends?expand_more

Yes. Share a race link and up to twelve pilots can fly the same sky. Wind Guild members can also run private guild races.

What happens if I cancel my pass?expand_more

You keep every kite you built and drop back to the free Groundling pass. Your race history and ribbons stay on your profile.

Does it work on a phone?expand_more

Yes. Touch controls are built for small screens, and the workbench collapses into simple swipes for paper, spar, and tail choices.
