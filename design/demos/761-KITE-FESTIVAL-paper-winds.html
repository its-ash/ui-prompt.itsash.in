How does the wind simulation work?

Our physics engine models real air density, gust patterns, and kite aerodynamics. You can tweak spars, surface area, and tail length to see instant feedback.

Can I play offline?

Yes. All core flight mechanics run locally. Leaderboards and multiplayer features require an internet connection.

What if I have accessibility needs?

Full keyboard navigation, screen‑reader friendly labels, and a reduced‑motion mode are built in. Every interaction is operable without a mouse.
