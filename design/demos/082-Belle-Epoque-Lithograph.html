The Method

I

The First Sitting

Bring us your subject — a portrait, a château, a steaming café — and our master draughtsman composes it in the flowing line of the era.

II

Upon the Stone

The composition is drawn on limestone with greasy crayon, etched with gum arabic, and pulled for its first proof beneath your eye.

III

The Presentation

Your edition arrives in a cedar case, framed in gilded timber, with its numbered certificate of provenance enclosed.

Enquiries

Are the impressions truly printed by hand?expand_more

Yes. Every lithograph is drawn upon Bavarian limestone, etched, and inked by hand. Editions are strictly limited to one hundred and fifty impressions, each numbered and signed by the house.

May I commission a bespoke subject?expand_more

Most gladly. Sittings are held by appointment at the atelier, or from photographs sent by post. Patrons of The Maecenas receive one bespoke commission with each year of patronage.

How are editions dispatched?expand_more

In cedar cases by insured courier. Gilded timber framing is offered at a member's reduction, and every parcel travels accompanied by its certificate of provenance.

Will members receive new editions?expand_more

Patrons of The Connoisseur and above receive a hand-numbered impression each season, selected by the master printer and accompanied by a letter of the house.

May my patronage be paused?expand_more

Certainly — a note before the quarter's close suffices, and any edition already upon the stone shall be honoured in full. Should you wish to resign entirely, the ledger is amended upon written notice.

Request Your Private Audience

Leave your address, and our secretary shall write within two days with a private invitation to the atelier.

We honour your privacy as we honour our editions — never shared, never sold.
