Before You Battle

Is Nimbus Arena free to play?expand_more

Yes — the Rookie tier is free forever. Ranked ladders and tournaments unlock with Champion, which is cancelable anytime.

Can I play with friends?expand_more

Squad up in 3v3 clan battles, host private dojo matches, or study your friends' ranked replays frame by frame.

Which platforms are supported?expand_more

PC, Mac, and mobile via cloud streaming, with full cross-play — your fighter and rank follow you everywhere.

How does ranked matchmaking work?expand_more

A power-rating system pairs you within seconds with fighters of similar skill, from Bronze to Champion.

Can I get a refund?expand_more

Cancel Champion or Legend within 14 days and we refund you in full — no questions asked.
