STRAIGHT ANSWERS

FREQUENT CHALLENGES

WHO FUNDS YOU?

Readers, mostly. About two thirds of our revenue comes from memberships; the rest from print sales and one grant with zero editorial strings. No ads, no party money, no "strategic partners." Ever.

WHY NO ADS?

Ads put someone else's priorities between you and the argument. We would rather ask you for five dollars than ask a brand for permission.

WHAT IS YOUR CORRECTIONS POLICY?

Mistakes get a dated public correction at the top of the article, in the same size type as the error. Write to corrections@therawledger.example and we answer within 48 hours.
