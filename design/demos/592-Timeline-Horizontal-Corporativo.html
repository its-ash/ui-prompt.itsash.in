Common questions

How is Nimbus different from a Gantt chart?expand_more

Gantt charts track tasks. Nimbus tracks outcomes. Milestones sit on a single horizontal line with category colors, so executives read the plan in seconds while teams still get dates, owners, and status detail.

Can I import an existing roadmap?expand_more

Yes. CSV import maps your dates, owners, and categories onto the timeline automatically. Most teams are fully migrated in under an hour.

Do the category colors meet accessibility standards?expand_more

Every category pairs its color with an icon and a text label, so meaning never depends on color alone. Contrast is verified in both light and dark themes.

What happens after the free trial ends?expand_more

Your timelines stay readable forever. Editing pauses until you pick a plan, and nothing is deleted or locked behind a paywall retroactively.
