Frequently Asked Questions

How does the timeline work?

Our horizontal timeline connects milestones with a thin line, using color‑coded markers and slide‑in modules to show each phase.

Can I customize the colors?

Yes – each category uses a distinct accent (pink, cyan, purple) that you can replace with your brand colors.

Is the page mobile‑friendly?

Absolutely. The timeline converts to a vertical layout on small screens, preserving readability and interaction.
