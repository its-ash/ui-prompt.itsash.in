The Craft of the Square

Three disciplines anchor every commission, each executed with the rigor of the Wiener Werkstätte.

grid_on

Geometric Ornament

Borders, grids, and repeating square motifs composed for facades, packaging, and print. Every line is measured, every repeat intentional.

format_paint

Gold Leaf Work

Genuine leaf applied by hand to signage, monograms, and ceremonial objects. Gold is used as an accent of honor, never as decoration for its own sake.

text_fields

Bespoke Lettering

Custom inscriptional alphabets drawn for your house alone, cut for stone, cast for metal, and set for the printed page.

The Atelier Journey

From first audience to final unveiling, each commission moves through three deliberate stages.

01

The Audience

A private consultation where we study your house, its history, and the surfaces the work will inhabit.

02

The Drawing Room

Our masters draft motifs, letterforms, and gilding plans. You review full-scale proofs before a single leaf is laid.

03

The Unveiling

Execution by our own hands, installed and inspected on site, then entered into the atelier registry.
