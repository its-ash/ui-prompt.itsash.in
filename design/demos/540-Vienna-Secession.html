The Craft of the Square

Three disciplines, one geometry. Every commission passes through the same rigorous bench work.

grid_on

Geometric Ornament

Checkerboards, square bands, and concentric frames drawn to the millimetre. Ornament with the discipline of architecture, in the manner of Hoffmann.

format_paint

Gold Leaf Work

Genuine leaf laid by hand over gesso, burnished to a quiet lustre. Gold used as an accent and a vow, never as a flood.

text_fields

Bespoke Lettering

Inscriptional capitals drawn for a single client and a single purpose, spaced with the wide breath of the Secessionist poster.

From Commission to Leaf

Three stages, each signed off at the bench before the next begins.

01

The Sitting

We meet, we listen, we measure the problem. You leave with a drawn proposal and a fixed price.

02

The Drawing

Full-scale cartoons in pencil and gouache. You approve the geometry before a single leaf is cut.

03

The Gilding

Leaf laid, edges burnished, work sealed and delivered with a certificate of the atelier.

Questions at the Bench

Apprentice pieces ship within four weeks. Craftsman suites take six to eight weeks, and Master works are scheduled individually, usually a full season.
