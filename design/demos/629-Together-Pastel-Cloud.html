Frequently asked questions

Do you support enterprise security standards?
Yes. We are SOC 2‑Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR‑compliant. All data at rest is encrypted with customer‑managed keys.
What are the model context‑window limits?
Context windows range from 8K to 1M tokens, depending on the model tier. Contact sales for custom limits.
Can I bring my own model?
Yes. You can upload custom model artifacts and run inference or fine‑tuning through our platform.
What is your uptime SLA?
99.9 % monthly uptime SLA for Scale and Enterprise tiers. Developer tier best‑effort.
How do you handle billing and upgrades?
Billing is monthly or annual. Upgrades take effect instantly; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
