Frequently sketched questions

Do I need any drawing experience?expand_more

None at all. Courses are labeled from Beginner to Confident, and every project opens with a five-minute warm-up so your first strokes always land softly.

What art supplies will I need?expand_more

A pencil, an eraser, and paper to start. Medium-specific courses include a short shopping list built around affordable, everyday materials — nothing rare or pricey.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?expand_more

Yes — cancel in two clicks from your account. You keep full access until the end of your billing period, and everything you've made stays yours forever.
