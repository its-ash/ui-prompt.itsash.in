Inquiries

Frequently Posed Questions

Are the works faithful to the originals?expand_more

Entirely. Every piece is scanned in partnership with the holding institution at archival resolution and verified by our curatorial board before it enters the salon.

May I resign my patronage at any time?expand_more

Certainly. Patronage is held month to month; depart whenever you wish, without fee or ceremony. Your private cabinet remains open to you for ninety days thereafter.

Do you welcome schools and academies?expand_more

We do. Registered institutions receive guided virtual tours led by a resident historian, with lesson folios drawn from the collection. Write to our curators to arrange a viewing.
