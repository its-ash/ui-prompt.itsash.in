Questions from the dock

Straight answers before you sign the manifest.

How fast can you move cargo to the Outer Rim?expand_more

Standard runs land within five days. Marshal tier guarantees a 48-hour window, and Syndicate clients can book same-cycle express runs on most corridors.

Is my cargo insured during transit?expand_more

Every manifest includes full-value coverage at no extra cost. Claims are settled within ten working days, stamped and signed.

What happens if weather closes a corridor?expand_more

Navigators reroute around ion storms and debris fields in real time. If a delay is unavoidable, you are notified before we are, and priority rebooking is automatic.

Can I track my shipment in flight?expand_more

Yes. Every run carries a live beacon, and Marshal tier and above get the full star-chart feed with position, velocity, and corridor status.

Do you haul restricted or hazardous freight?expand_more

We are licensed for Class B hazardous materials in sealed holds. Restricted manifests are reviewed by dispatch before quoting, usually within one working day.
