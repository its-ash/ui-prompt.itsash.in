helpSquishy questions

Squishy questions, squishy answers

Do I need to know how to draw?add_circle

Not at all! Nimbus redraws every stroke with a soft, wobbly outline, so even a hurried scribble comes out looking charmingly hand-made. Squiggles are basically a feature here.

Can I sell the stickers I make?add_circle

Yes! On Cloudlet and Studio plans you own your art and get a full commercial license. Sprout doodles are for personal joy and gifting only.

Does Nimbus work offline?add_circle

Your ten most recent doodle sheets stay cached on your device, so you can keep drawing on the train. Everything syncs to the cloud the moment you reconnect.
