Common questions

Anything else, our engineers answer support directly.

How long does domain verification take?expand_more

Usually under ten minutes. Once you add the DNS records, we poll for propagation and enable sending as soon as DKIM and SPF both pass.

Can I migrate from my current provider without downtime?expand_more

Yes. Run both providers in parallel, shift traffic by percentage with our routing rules, and cut over fully once delivery metrics match.

What happens if I exceed my monthly volume?expand_more

Nothing breaks. Overage is billed per thousand emails at your plan rate, and we notify you at 80% and 100% of your quota.
