Sysop help screen

Do I need a real modem?

No. The web terminal works in any browser. A real modem is supported for the full experience on 1-555-NEON-BBS.

What is a ratio?

Members have no ratio. Guests can download five files per day, then upload one to unlock five more.

Can I submit my own ANSI art?

Yes. Upload to the NEW ART area and the curation crew reviews it within a week.

Is the board family friendly?

Message bases are moderated nightly and adult-only areas require age verification.
