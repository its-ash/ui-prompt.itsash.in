──[ FAQ ]──

DO I NEED A MODEM?

Yes, a dial-up modem is required to connect to Nimbus BBS at 300–1200 baud.

CAN I UPLOAD FILES?

Upload privileges depend on your tier. LURKER accounts have read‑only access; USER and SYSOP tiers allow file uploads.

WHAT TIME ZONE IS THE SYSTEM IN?

The system clock is set to UTC. All message timestamps display in UTC offset.
