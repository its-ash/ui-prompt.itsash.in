FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I import work from other design tools?expand_more
Yes. Lumira imports Figma and Sketch files natively, preserving layers, components, and text styles so you can pick up where you left off.
Does the free plan expire?expand_more
No. The Sketch plan is free forever with three active projects. Upgrade only when you need unlimited projects or team features.
How does real-time collaboration work?expand_more
Every canvas is multiplayer by default. Teammates see cursors, comments, and edits live, and a full version history lets you restore any earlier state.
What formats can I export?expand_more
Export CSS variables, design tokens as JSON, SVG and PNG assets, and shareable live preview links. Atelier plans add automated style-guide PDFs.
Is my work private?expand_more
All projects are private by default and encrypted in transit and at rest. You control exactly who can view, comment, or edit each canvas.
Can I cancel anytime?expand_more
Yes. Cancel from your billing settings in two clicks. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, then returns to the free tier.
