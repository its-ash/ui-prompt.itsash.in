Fair questions

Who owns our member data?expand_more

You do, full stop. Export your entire list at any time in one click. We never sell, share, or mine your contacts.

Can volunteers use it without training?expand_more

Yes. Volunteers see only their shifts and call lists. Most people are knocking doors within ten minutes of their first login.

What if our group cannot afford a plan?expand_more

Grassroots groups with budgets under $5,000 a year get the Movement plan free. Write to us from the contact page and we will set you up.
