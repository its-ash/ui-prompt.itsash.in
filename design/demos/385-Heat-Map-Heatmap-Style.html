Frequently asked questions

Which data sources can I connect?

HeatLens connects to major warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Postgres), CSV and Excel uploads, and REST or streaming APIs. Dashboards can mix sources, and paid plans sync as often as every 60 seconds.

Is it usable for colorblind teammates?

Yes. Every value is available as text via tooltips and labels, and the colorblind-friendly mode replaces hue with signed numbers on each cell. Palettes are tested against deuteranopia and protanopia by default.

What are the cell, export and security limits?

Explorer maps hold 10K cells; Analyst scales to 5M with client-side rendering. Paid plans export PNG, SVG and CSV. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with on-prem deployment and audit logs available on Scale.
