Common questions

Everything you need to know before you pre-order.

How does MAGMA turn wax motion into sound? expand_more

Thermal sensors inside the chamber track the wax as it rises and falls. That motion is mapped to pitch, amplitude, and texture in real time. The result is a continuous drone that follows the flow of the wax.

Do I need to know how to play an instrument? expand_more

No. MAGMA is designed to be intuitive. You control heat and flow; the wax does the rest. The sound engine handles the translation.

Can I connect MAGMA to my DAW? expand_more

Yes. MAGMA Classic and Inferno Studio include MIDI out and USB-C. Inferno Studio also includes CV/Gate for modular rigs.

Is the glass chamber replaceable? expand_more

Yes. The chamber is removable and replaceable. Spare chambers will be available on our store.

What is the warranty? expand_more

Every MAGMA includes a 2-year warranty covering the heating element, sensors, and electronics. The glass chamber is covered against manufacturing defects.

When will pre-orders ship? expand_more

Pre-orders placed now are expected to ship in Q3 2026. You will receive an email confirmation with a tracking number when your unit leaves the studio.
