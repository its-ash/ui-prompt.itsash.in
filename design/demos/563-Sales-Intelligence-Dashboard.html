Common questions

Which CRMs does PipelineIQ integrate with?expand_more

Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive sync two-way out of the box. A REST API and CSV import cover everything else, and most teams finish setup in under an hour.

How is our sales data secured?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance, offer SSO/SAML on Enterprise, and never train models on your data.

How does forecast accuracy scoring work?expand_more

We compare each rep's commit against historical close patterns by stage, deal size, and cycle time. The meter updates nightly so you can see which commits to trust.

What happens after the free trial ends?expand_more

Your dashboards stay intact and read-only. Pick a plan to keep syncing, or export your data any time. We never require a card to start.
