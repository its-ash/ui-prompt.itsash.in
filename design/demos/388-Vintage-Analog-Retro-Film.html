Questions from the projection booth

Which film stocks can I shoot with?expand_more

Every era is in the fridge: Super 8, VHS-C, Polaroid 600, Hi8 camcorder — plus expired “mystery rolls” with delightfully unpredictable fading. A new stock is voted in by the community almost every month.

What quality are the exports?expand_more

Up to crisp 4K — the grain is simulated, never the resolution. Paid plans remove the watermark and can export print-ready Polaroid sheets at 300 DPI.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Anytime, in two taps — no exit interview. You keep every roll you’ve developed, and anything inside 14 days is refunded, no questions asked.
