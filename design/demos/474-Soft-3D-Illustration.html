FAQ

Questions, answered

The things teams ask before their first scene.

Do I need 3D experience to use Clayform?expand_more

No. Clayform is built for designers and PMs, not 3D artists. Strokes inflate into forms, lighting is automatic, and every template starts from a sensible default.

Which file formats can I export?expand_more

Paid plans export GLB for game engines and AR, PNG renders for decks, and an embeddable web viewer you can drop into any page with one script tag.

Can viewers comment without an account?expand_more

Yes. Anyone with a share link can orbit the scene and leave pinned comments. Only editors need a Clayform seat.

What happens to my scenes if I cancel?expand_more

Your scenes stay readable forever. Export everything to GLB before you go, or keep the free Studio plan and retain your three most recent scenes.
