Good to know

Questions, answered

What exactly comes in each kit?expand_more

Every kit includes metal straws with a cleaning brush, oversized scrunchies, and a sticker sheet. Golden Hour adds the insulated flask and puka shell necklace, and Full Send adds a second flask, an anklet, and a canvas tote.

How long does shipping take?expand_more

Orders ship within two business days and usually arrive in five to seven. Full Send kits get priority shipping at no extra cost, and every order over thirty dollars ships free.

Are the materials actually eco friendly?expand_more

Yes. Straws are stainless steel, packaging is recycled and plastic free, and one dollar from every kit funds beach cleanups along the California coast. Save the turtles, for real.
