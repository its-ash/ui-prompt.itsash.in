Questions

Frequently asked

Why Merriweather?expand_more
It is an open-licensed serif drawn specifically for screens — warm on the page, crisp at small sizes, and free forever under the SIL Open Font License.
Can I use Nimbus with my framework?expand_more
Yes. The system is plain CSS variables and semantic class names, so it drops into React, Vue, Rails, or static HTML without a build step.
Does dark mode cost extra?expand_more
No. Every token ships as a matched pair tuned for contrast in both directions — cream on cocoa, cocoa on cream.
How does it affect performance?expand_more
Merriweather loads as subsetted woff2 files with font-display: swap. The full token system adds under 40KB of CSS.
What if it is not for us?expand_more
Paid plans carry a 30-day, no-questions refund. Cancel from the dashboard in two clicks.
Can I self-host the fonts?expand_more
Please do. Download the family from Google Fonts or npm and serve it from your own origin.
