Straight answers

The questions every writer asks before their first reservation.

Are the walls actually legal?expand_more

Yes. Every wall on the map carries a signed agreement with the property owner. We verify each listing in person before it goes live, and your reservation confirmation works as your proof of permission on the day.

Do I need experience to join?expand_more

No. The free Toy pass is built for writers starting out. You can book beginner-friendly walls, study pieces in the archive, and join a crew page to learn can control from people who have been painting for decades.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?expand_more

Anytime, from your profile, in two taps. You keep your wall history and profile forever, and any confirmed reservations stay valid through the end of your paid month.
