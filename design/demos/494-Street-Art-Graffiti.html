Straight Answers

No fine print, no filler.

Are the walls actually legal?expand_more

Yes. Every wall on the map has written permission from the property owner or the city. When you claim a slot, the permit paperwork is generated in the app and stored on your profile, so you can show it on the spot if anyone asks.

Do I need to be good already?expand_more

No. Walls are tagged by difficulty, from chill practice spots to high-visibility halls of fame. Start on practice walls, log your progress, and work up to the walls where the heavy hitters paint.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?expand_more

Anytime, in two taps, no emails and no phone calls. Your claims stay valid through the end of the billing month and your wall history stays on your profile forever, even on the free plan.
