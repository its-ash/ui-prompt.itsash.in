Questions from the quarry

Quick answers before you start stacking.

Which file formats can I export?expand_more

Every plan exports .vox. Builder and Studio add .obj and .gltf, plus a sprite-sheet PNG for 2D previews of your model.

Which browsers are supported?expand_more

VoxelForge runs in current Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. Anything with WebGL 2 works; no plugins or installs required.

Is there a limit on cubes per model?expand_more

The free canvas holds about 260,000 cubes. Builder raises that to 16 million, which is more than most GPUs want to think about.

Can I get a refund?expand_more

Yes. Paid plans come with a 14-day, no-questions refund. Cancel any time and keep your exports forever.

Do I keep the rights to my builds?expand_more

Completely. Everything you make is yours. Public gallery posts grant a remix license only if you opt in.
