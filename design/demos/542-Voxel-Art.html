Common questions

Which browsers does VoxelForge support?expand_more

Any current version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. Everything renders with WebGL, and projects autosave to your account so you can switch devices mid-build.

What file formats can I export?expand_more

Free plans export .vox for MagicaVoxel. Paid plans add .obj and .gltf with full material and palette data, ready for game engines and 3D printing pipelines.

Is there a limit on cubes per model?expand_more

Canvases go up to 256 cubes per side on Builder and Studio plans, which is over 16 million possible voxels. Most detailed characters use fewer than 20,000.

Can I get a refund?expand_more

Yes. Every paid plan comes with a 30-day refund window, no questions asked. Cancel from your account page and keep anything you exported.
