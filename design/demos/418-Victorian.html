The Process

From Consultation to Sealing Wax

filter_vintage
I

Private Consultation

We discuss your occasion, papers, and pigments over tea in our Mayfair rooms, or by correspondence abroad.

II

Proof & Approval

A hand-set proof is dispatched for your inspection. Nothing proceeds to press without your written consent.

III

Press & Delivery

Your suite is printed on our 1892 Albion press, sealed by hand, and delivered in a linen-bound presentation box.

Enquiries

Questions, Answered

filter_vintage
How long does a commission require?add

Most suites are completed within four to six weeks of proof approval. Illuminated and Heritage commissions may require eight weeks, as each border is painted by hand.

Do you accept international commissions?add

Certainly. We correspond with patrons across the Continent and the Americas. Consultations are conducted by letter or telegraph-style exchange, and proofs travel by insured post.

May I supply my own family crest?add

With pleasure. Send a rubbing or clear photograph of your crest and our lapidary will engrave it in brass for use on seals and dies. The matrix remains your property thereafter.
