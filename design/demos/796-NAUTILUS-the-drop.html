Questions from the surface

Everything divers usually ask before their first descent.

What exactly happens during a dive?expand_more

When you start a dive, Nautilus pauses notifications, collapses distracting tabs, and opens a timed focus view with your single objective. When the timer ends, everything resurfaces and you get a short debrief prompt.

Which platforms does Nautilus support?expand_more

Nautilus runs on macOS, Windows, and as a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. Mobile companions for iOS and Android sync your dive schedule and hold notifications.

Can my team dive together?expand_more

Yes. On the Abyss plan, teams can schedule synchronized dives where every member's status flips to diving at once, with shared debriefs afterward. Many teams use it to replace daily standups.

What if something urgent comes up mid-dive?expand_more

You can designate up to three emergency contacts whose messages always break through. You can also abort a dive at any time with one keystroke; Nautilus logs the partial session so your streak stays honest.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?expand_more

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day trial, no card required. The Shallows tier is free forever, so you can keep basic dives even if you decide not to subscribe.
