Questions

FAQ

How we grow with nature’s intelligence

How much control do I retain over the generative process?

You define the DNA: constraints, goals, and inspiration. Morpho explores within those boundaries—you guide evolution through selection and feedback, not direct pixel manipulation.

How do the nature-inspired algorithms actually work?

We use genetic algorithms with mutation, crossover, and selection inspired by biological evolution. Fitness functions evaluate designs against your goals, while environmental factors introduce adaptive pressures for real-world resilience.

Will animations impact performance or accessibility?

All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Core animations use CSS with GPU acceleration. Interfaces adapt complexity based on device capability—simple on low-end, rich on high-end—while maintaining WCAG compliance.

Is Morpho accessible and compliant with reduced motion preferences?

Yes. We honor system preferences, provide non-animated alternatives, ensure keyboard operability, maintain contrast ratios, and use semantic structure. Motion enhances but never conveys essential information.

Which browsers and devices are supported?

Modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (last 2 versions). Mobile: iOS Safari, Android Chrome. Degrades gracefully to static interfaces in older browsers while preserving functionality and accessibility.
