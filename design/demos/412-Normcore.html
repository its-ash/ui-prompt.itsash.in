How it works

There are three steps. None of them are hard.

01

Open it

Go to the page or install the app. There is no setup wizard.

02

Write something

Type a note or add a task. It saves on its own, quietly.

03

Find it later

Search or scroll. Your stuff is where you left it.

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Do I need an account?

expand_more

No. You can use Ledger without one. An account is only needed if you want syncing across devices.

Where is my data stored?

expand_more

On your device first, and on our servers if you turn on sync. You can export everything at any time.

Can I cancel whenever?

expand_more

Yes. Cancel from the settings page in two clicks. Your notes stay readable on the free plan.

Is there a mobile app?

expand_more

Yes, for iOS and Android. It looks the same as the website, on purpose.

What happens if you shut down?

expand_more

You can export your notes as plain text files today, so you would keep everything either way.
