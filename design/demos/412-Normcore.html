What it does

Three things, done adequately.

edit_note

Writing

A text box. It saves as you type. There is no toolbar to learn because there is barely a toolbar.

search

Finding

Search looks through your notes and shows the ones that match. Results appear as you type.

sync

Syncing

Your notes show up on your other devices. It happens quietly in the background, like it should.

How it works

About as complicated as it sounds.

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Do I need an account?

expand_more

No. You can use Plain without one. An account is only needed if you want sync across devices.

Can I get my notes out?

expand_more

Yes. Export everything as plain text files at any time, on any plan, including the free one.

What happens if I stop paying?

expand_more

Your notes stay readable and exportable. You drop to the Basic plan. Nothing gets deleted.
