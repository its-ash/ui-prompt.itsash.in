FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How does the 14-day trial work?expand_more

Every paid feature is unlocked from day one — no credit card, no sales call. If you do nothing at the end of day 14, your workspace simply drops to the free Starter plan and keeps all of its data.

Can I migrate from Trello, Asana, or Jira?expand_more

Yes. Our importer maps lists, labels, attachments, and comments automatically, and most teams finish in under an hour. If anything looks off, one click undoes the entire import.

What happens when my team grows past the free plan?expand_more

Nothing breaks. The sixth teammate triggers an upgrade prompt, billing is prorated to the day, and every board, automation, and file stays exactly where it was.
