Frequently asked questions

What is Nimbus-2?expand_more

Nimbus-2 is our frontier generative model: a 1M-token, tool-using reasoner tuned for coding, analysis, and long-horizon planning tasks.

How does pricing work?expand_more

You pay per token, never per seat. The Developer tier includes 1M free tokens each month; Scale adds priority routing and an SLA.

Is my data used for training?expand_more

No. API inputs and outputs are never used to train our models, and a zero-retention mode is available on every plan.
