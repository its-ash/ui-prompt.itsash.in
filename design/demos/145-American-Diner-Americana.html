Burning Questions

Everything folks ask Hank while he pours the coffee.

Do you take reservations?expand_more

Nope — every booth is first come, first served. The counter almost always has a free stool, and the pie wait is worth it.

What are your hours?expand_more

We're slinging hash 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., seven days a week. Pie usually sells out by 8 — come early.

Got anything vegetarian?expand_more

Veggie burgers, grilled cheese, big chopped salads — and our buttermilk pancakes are famous among the meat-free crowd.

Can I book the diner for a party?expand_more

Saturdays after close we host birthdays, sock hops and rehearsal dinners. Give Hank at least two weeks' notice.

Our Story

movieThe 1955 projector warms up on Fridays — catch the reel at the counter.

Rosie opened this joint in 1955 with a secondhand griddle, a jukebox full of Elvis, and a hand-painted sign. Seventy years on, the chrome's still polished, the shakes are still thick enough to fight, and every first-timer gets the booth by the window. That's not marketing — that's just how we were raised.
