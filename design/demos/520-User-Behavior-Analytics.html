Common questions

How long does setup take?expand_more

Most teams send their first event within 15 minutes. The snippet is under 12KB and loads asynchronously, so it never blocks your page.

Do you collect personal data by default?expand_more

No. IP addresses are anonymized on ingestion, and you control exactly which properties are captured. GDPR and CCPA deletion requests are handled from the dashboard.

Can I export my data?expand_more

Yes. Every plan includes CSV export, and Growth and Enterprise plans add a full API plus scheduled warehouse sync to BigQuery, Snowflake, or Redshift.
