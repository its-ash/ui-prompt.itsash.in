Ask the mestre

Questions from the crowd

What exactly is Maracatu Tech?add

A CI/CD platform for teams that ship fast without losing the plot. Connect a repo, get build pipelines, preview stages, and progressive deployments — all with an interface inspired by the color and rhythm of maracatu from Recife and Olinda.

Do I need to know anything about Maracatu?add

No. The culture shapes the design language — bold reds, royal blues, imperial gold, and rhythm — but the tool behaves like any modern pipeline, just faster and considerably louder in spirit.

Can I switch tiers or cancel anytime?add

Yes. Move between Baque, Nação, and Império whenever you like, and cancel in two clicks from settings. No lock-in, no exit fees — the goodbye parade is entirely optional.
