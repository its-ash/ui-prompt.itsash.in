FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there really a free plan? expand_more
Yes — Drift is free forever for up to 3 teammates, with 2 live dashboards and 30 days of history. No credit card required.
How does the free trial work? expand_more
Every workspace gets 14 days of Nimbus Pro with no limits. If you do nothing at the end, you simply glide back to the free plan — we never auto-charge.
Can I import historical data? expand_more
Absolutely. Connect your warehouse (BigQuery, Snowflake, Postgres and more) and Nimbus backfills years of events so your trends start deep, not shallow.
