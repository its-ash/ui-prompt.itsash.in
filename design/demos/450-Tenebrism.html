Questions, answered plainly

Can I change plans later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect on your next billing date, and we never lock your data behind a plan.

Does Nimbus work offline?expand_more

Capture and review work fully offline on every device. Everything syncs quietly the moment you reconnect, with conflicts resolved in your favor.

How is my data protected?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your information, and you can export or delete everything with one click.
