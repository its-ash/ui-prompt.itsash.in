How it works

Three steps from scattered to focused. No setup marathons, no configuration tax.

1

Gather

Import tasks from your existing tools or jot them directly into Nimbus. Everything lands in one dim lobby.

2

Light one thing

Choose the single project that gets the spotlight. Nimbus mutes the rest for the length of your session.

3

Review the ledger

Close the session with a short note. Over weeks, the ledger shows your real patterns of attention.

Questions

The short answers. Write to us if you need the long ones.

Does Nimbus replace my task manager?expand_more
No. Nimbus sits on top of the tools you already use. Import tasks from your tracker, then use the spotlight to decide what gets your attention right now.
What happens when a focus session ends?expand_more
Muted notifications resurface in a single digest, and you are prompted to leave a one-line note in the ledger. Nothing is lost while you work.
Can I cancel Studio at any time?expand_more
Yes. Cancel from your billing page in two clicks. You keep Studio features until the end of the paid period, then drop back to Solo with all your data intact.
