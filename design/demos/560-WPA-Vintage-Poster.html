Questions from the Road

Answers to what travelers ask us most.

Do I need an internet connection on the trail?expand_more

No. Every itinerary can be printed or saved for offline use before you leave home, so a dead zone never spoils the day.

How current are the road and trail reports?expand_more

Our community of travelers files reports through the season, and our editors review them weekly against ranger station bulletins.

Can I share one plan with my whole party?expand_more

Yes. Any itinerary can be shared by link, and Ranger accounts let five family members edit the same plan together.

What if I only visit one park a year?expand_more

The Rambler pass is free forever and covers three parks with a full itinerary. It suits the once-a-year traveler handsomely.

May I cancel a paid pass at any time?expand_more

Certainly. Cancel from your account page in two clicks. Your plans remain readable for the rest of the paid month.
