01, The Craft

Three decisions, made slowly

Every box follows the same quiet discipline our kitchen has kept for a decade.

kebab_dining

The Cut

Bone-in chuck short rib, dry-aged fourteen days and trimmed by hand. Marbling you can read like a map.

soup_kitchen

The Braise

Seared hard, then sunk into red wine, bone stock, and charred mirepoix at a bare tremble for six hours.

bedtime

The Rest

An overnight rest in its own jus lets the collagen settle back into the meat. Reheated, it eats even better.

Sealed and refrigerated, the ribs hold beautifully for five days. The flavor actually deepens by day two as the jus settles back into the meat.

Cover the ribs with their jus in a baking dish, seal with foil, and warm at 300 degrees for twenty minutes. Never microwave; the collagen deserves better.
