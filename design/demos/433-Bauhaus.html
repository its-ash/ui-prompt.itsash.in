How it works

Three steps from first login to a running cycle.

1

Define the cycle

Set a fixed timebox and name the outcome. Nimbus blocks new scope once the cycle starts.

2

Shape the work

Break the outcome into tasks on the canvas, assign owners, and flag dependencies before work begins.

3

Ship and review

Close the cycle with a review board that shows what shipped, what slipped, and why.

Questions, answered

Can we change plans mid-cycle?add

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades apply at the next billing date. Your data and boards stay intact either way.

Does Nimbus replace our issue tracker?add

For most teams, yes. If you keep an existing tracker, Nimbus imports tasks and syncs status both ways on Workshop and Factory plans.

What happens when a cycle ends with unfinished work?add

Unfinished tasks return to the shaping board with their history attached. The review report shows what slipped so the next cycle can be scoped honestly.

Is there a free trial?add

Every plan starts with a 14-day trial on the Workshop tier. No card required, and you can export everything if you decide not to continue.

How is our data handled?add

Data is hosted in EU and US regions, encrypted in transit and at rest. Factory plans add SSO, audit logs, and a custom retention policy.
