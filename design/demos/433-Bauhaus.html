Questions

Answers about tokens, themes, and licensing.

Does Nimbus work with my existing CSS?add

Yes. Tokens are plain CSS variables, so you can adopt the grid and palette incrementally without rewriting your stylesheets.

How does the dark theme stay accessible?add

Every background token ships with a paired text token tested to WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark modes.

Can I change the accent colors?add

Atelier plans include custom token themes. Red, blue, and yellow are defaults, not requirements.
