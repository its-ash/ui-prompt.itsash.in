FAQ

Questions, answered gently

Is Evergrow really free to start?expand_more

Yes — Seedling is free forever. You only upgrade when your neighborhood is ready for bigger gardens and richer dashboards.

Do I need panels, land, or gardening skills?expand_more

Nope. Most members start with a windowsill herb and a library card. Evergrow matches contributions to whatever sunlight — or enthusiasm — you have.

How is my energy data handled?expand_more

Readings stay on your local hub by default. Only anonymized neighborhood totals ever reach the map, and you can export or erase everything anytime.
