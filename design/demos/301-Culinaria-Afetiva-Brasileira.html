Do I need to be an experienced cook?

expand_more
No — burned-rice stories welcome. We celebrate the journey, not perfection. Your first attempt at brigadeiro is just as valuable as your hundredth.

What is 'affective cuisine'?

expand_more
Food made with memory and affection. It's the taste of childhood, the smell of a family gathering, the feeling of being loved through a shared meal.

Can I keep family recipes private?

expand_more
Yes, private groups let you share recipes only with chosen family members. Your culinary heritage stays protected while still being accessible to those who matter most.

Can I print or export my recipes?

expand_more
Yes! The Family Table plan and above include printable notebook exports. The Community Chef tier adds a beautifully bound hardcover cookbook of your collected recipes.

Is it free to join?

expand_more
Absolutely. The Kitchen Helper plan is free forever, giving you access to core features and community recipes. Upgrade only when you're ready for more.
