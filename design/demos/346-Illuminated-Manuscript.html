The Method

How a Folio Comes to Light

Three quiet labors, in the manner of the old workshops — from the camera to the reader's hand.

  1. I

    Digitize the Folio

    Conservationists photograph each quire in raking and ultraviolet light, capturing what the unaided eye alone cannot hold.

  2. II

    Curate the Scholarship

    Medievalists transcribe, translate, and annotate, so every gloss in the margin carries its provenance with it.

  3. III

    Illuminate the Reading

    Readers open the finished facsimile beside the scholarship, side by side — a scriptorium in every pocket.

Questions from the Reading Room

Frequently Illuminated Questions

Are the colors faithful to the original pigments?expand_more

Yes. Every facsimile is calibrated against spectrophotometer readings taken from the manuscript itself, so ultramarine reads as ultramarine — never as a printer's blue.

Can I cite Scriptorium in academic work?expand_more

Certainly. Each folio carries a stable URI and a ready-made citation in Chicago and MLA forms, one click from the reader's margin.

What happens to my annotations if I cancel?expand_more

Your marginalia remain yours. Export them at any time as plain text or JSON; cancelled accounts keep read-only access to every note they have made.
