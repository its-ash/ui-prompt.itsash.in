FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Culture in Motion cover?expand_more

Visual arts, literature, music, theatre, film and heritage across every Brazilian region — from the São Paulo Biennial to neighbourhood ateliê openings, reported in English by our correspondents.

Can I pitch a story or list an event?expand_more

Yes. Readers can pitch features to our editorial desk, and verified venues list cultural events in the agenda for free — send a note and our team reviews it within a week.

How do I cancel my subscription?expand_more

One click in your account settings — no phone calls, no forms. You keep full access until the end of the billing period, and your saved articles remain yours forever.
