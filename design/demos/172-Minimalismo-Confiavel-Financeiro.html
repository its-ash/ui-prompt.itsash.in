Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, in writing, before you ever sign.

How are you compensated?expand_more

We are fee-only: a flat monthly retainer or 0.75% of assets under advisory, billed quarterly. We accept no commissions, referral fees, or fund incentives of any kind.

Are you held to a fiduciary standard?expand_more

Yes — always, for every client and every account, in writing. Our Form ADV and engagement letter both commit us to act in your best interest at all times.

What are your account minimums?expand_more

Ongoing advisory begins at $500,000 in investable assets. Below that, our Essentials tier provides planning-only engagements with no asset minimum.
