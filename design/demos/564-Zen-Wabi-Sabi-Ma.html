Common Questions

What is wabi-sabi?expand_more

Wabi-sabi is a Japanese aesthetic philosophy finding beauty in imperfection, impermanence, and simplicity. It values authenticity over perfection.

Do I need prior experience?expand_more

No experience required. Our approach meets you exactly where you are, honoring your unique journey.

How much time is needed?expand_more

Practices range from 2-minute mindful pauses to 45-minute sessions. Consistency matters more than duration.
