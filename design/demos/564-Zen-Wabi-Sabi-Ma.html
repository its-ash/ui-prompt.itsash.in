What the practice holds

Three simple things, made slowly and sent with care.

emoji_food_beverage

Seasonal tea

Leaves chosen at their moment, not on a schedule. Each parcel follows the season it was picked in.

air

Guided stillness

Short audio sittings recorded in our Kyoto room. Ten quiet minutes, a kettle, and nothing else asked of you.

edit_note

A paper journal

One unlined notebook a year. Rough edges, honest paper, and no prompts telling you what to feel.

How it works

No streaks, no scores. Only a rhythm you can keep.

  1. 一

    Choose your season

    Tell us when your days feel heaviest. We time the first parcel to arrive just before.

  2. 二

    Receive and unwrap

    Tea, a sitting, and a quiet note. Everything fits in one small cloth bundle.

  3. 三

    Keep ten minutes

    Sit with the cup while it cools. That is the whole practice. The rest is yours.

A few questions

Answered plainly.

Do I need experience with meditation or tea?expand_more

None at all. The sittings assume nothing, and the tea comes with one small card on water and time. Beginners are most of our members.

Can I pause or cancel?expand_more

Yes, from your account page, in one step. Pausing simply skips a season. Nothing is charged while you rest.

Where do you ship?expand_more

We ship to most countries within ten days of each season's turn. Parcels travel slowly by design, in paper and cloth only.
