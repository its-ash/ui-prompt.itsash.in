Frequently asked questions

What does the 14-day trial include?expand_more

The trial unlocks every Premium feature for your whole team, with no credit card required. When it ends, you can pick a paid plan or drop to the Free tier without losing your boards.

How does per-user billing work?expand_more

You only pay for active members. Deactivated users are removed from the next invoice automatically, and annual plans are prorated when you add seats mid-cycle.

Which tools does Flowboard integrate with?expand_more

Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Figma, Google Drive, and over sixty more through our open API and Zapier connector. Integrations are included on every paid plan.

How is our data protected?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers. Premium adds SSO, audit logs, and regional data residency options.

Can we migrate from Trello or Jira?expand_more

Yes. The built-in importer moves boards, cards, members, comments, and attachments in one pass. Most teams finish a full migration in under an hour.

Can we cancel, and what support do we get?expand_more

Cancel anytime from workspace settings; your data stays exportable for 90 days. Every plan includes help-center access, and paid plans get email support with a one-business-day response time.
