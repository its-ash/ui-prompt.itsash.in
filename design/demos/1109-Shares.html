Common questions

Everything you need to know

How does Vellum handle 409A valuations?chevron_right
We partner with accredited valuation firms to deliver audit-ready 409A reports. The process is initiated from your dashboard and typically completes in 5-7 business days.
Can I migrate from Carta or Pulley?chevron_right
Yes. Our import wizard supports CSV, Excel, and direct API connections from major cap table providers. We reconcile discrepancies and flag issues before finalizing.
What compliance standards do you meet?chevron_right
SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA compliant. All securities transactions follow Rule 701, Rule 144, and applicable state blue sky laws. Audit logs are immutable and exportable.
