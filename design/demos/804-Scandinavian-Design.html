How it works

Three small habits, repeated daily, keep your work moving without overhead.

01

Capture quickly

Open a blank note from anywhere with one shortcut. Ideas land in your inbox until you sort them.

02

Sort once a day

Move each note into a project or let it go. A two-minute review keeps the inbox from piling up.

03

Focus on three things

Each morning, pick three priorities. Nordlys shows only those until you choose to look further.
