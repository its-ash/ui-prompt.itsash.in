Questions, answered

Everything else lives in our docs — searchable, of course.

How is Nimbus different from other workspace tools?expand_more

Most tools add features to win demos. Nimbus removes them to win your attention. Every screen is audited against one rule: if it doesn’t help you finish work, it’s gone.

Can I import my existing data?expand_more

Yes. One-click importers cover Notion, Evernote, Todoist, Trello and plain Markdown or CSV. Nothing in your source tools is ever modified.

What happens to my data if I leave?expand_more

Everything exports in open formats — Markdown, JSON and CSV — in a single click. Delete your account and your data is erased from our servers immediately and permanently.
