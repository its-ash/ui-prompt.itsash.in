Questions, Assembled

Yes — full refunds until 14 February 2026 and 50% until 1 March. After that, tickets transfer freely to another person.

Kraftwerk is step-free with accessible restrooms. Both stages offer induction loops and live captioning, and a quiet room is open all day. Tell us what you need at access@construct26.berlin.

Ticket holders receive a booking link on 20 January 2026. Studios are capped at 24 seats and fill in minutes — set an alarm.

All keynotes and most track talks are recorded and published within two weeks, included with every ticket.

Students and nonprofits pay $79 with proof of status, and community tickets are distributed through our outreach partners.

Write to hello@construct26.berlin — a human replies within two working days.
