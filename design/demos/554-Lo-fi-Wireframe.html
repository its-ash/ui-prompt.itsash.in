[ 05, faq ]

Questions, answered plainly

Can I make things look polished in Sketchframe?expand_more

No, and that is the point. Everything stays dashed, grey, and honest so feedback focuses on structure. When you are ready for polish, export and hand off.

Do my teammates need an account to comment?expand_more

No. Anyone with a share link can view and leave comments. Only editors need a seat, which keeps your Team plan small and your feedback wide.

What happens to my sketches if I cancel?expand_more

Your boards drop to read-only and stay exportable for 90 days. We will not hold your grey boxes hostage.
