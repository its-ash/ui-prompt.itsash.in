[ 01, FEATURES ]

Everything a rough draft needs

Three things, done properly. No feature grid the size of a spreadsheet.

drag_pan

Drag, drop, done

Grey boxes, placeholder lines, and X'd-out images. Pull them onto the canvas and your layout exists. Fidelity sold separately.

forum

Honest feedback loops

Share a sketch link and collect comments on the structure, not the shade of blue. Stakeholders argue about what matters.

ios_share

Ship the sketch

Export to PNG, PDF, or a live link. Your wireframe leaves the building looking exactly as unfinished as it should.

[ 02, HOW IT WORKS ]

Three steps, zero polish

From blank canvas to shared sketch in less time than a status meeting.

01

Open a canvas

Pick a frame size. It is a rectangle. You have seen rectangles before.

02

Drop in blocks

Headers, images, buttons, text bars. Everything snaps to a grid that forgives you.

03

Share the sketch

Send a link, gather notes, iterate. Nobody asks about fonts. That is the point.
