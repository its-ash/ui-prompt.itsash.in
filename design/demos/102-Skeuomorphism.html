Questions, Answered

What customers ask before their first commission.

How long does a custom piece take?expand_more

Small leather goods ship in about two weeks. Furniture takes six to eight weeks, mostly waiting on finishes to cure. Your quote always includes a firm date.

Do you ship internationally?expand_more

Yes — we ship to more than 30 countries, fully insured. Duties and taxes are calculated up front, so there are no surprises at your door.

What if my piece needs repair?expand_more

Send it back. Repairs are free for the life of the piece — restitching, re-oiling, hardware, all of it. We keep your pattern on file forever.

Can I visit the workshop?expand_more

Yes, by appointment. Come watch your piece being made, meet your artisan, and leave with sawdust on your sleeves.

What materials do you refuse to use?expand_more

Anything pretending to be something else: bonded leather, veneer, plated zinc. If a material can't be repaired, we won't build with it.
