Questions from the garden gate

Can you restore a piece I already own?add

Yes, gladly. Send us a photograph and we will write back with an honest assessment and a quote. Heirlooms with sentimental wear are our favourite kind of project.

How long does a commission take?add

Most pieces take two to four weeks from sourcing to delivery. We never rush a finish, and we will send you photographs at each stage so you can watch the transformation.

Do you deliver outside the Cotswolds?add

We deliver nationwide in our own van, blanket-wrapped and carried into the room of your choice. Deliveries beyond 100 miles carry a small additional charge.
