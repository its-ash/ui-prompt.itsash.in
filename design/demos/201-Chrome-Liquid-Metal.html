FAQ

Questions, answered

Does MOLTEN run in the browser or on my desktop?expand_more

Both. The editor is a native desktop app; exported players run anywhere WebGL does, including phones.

Can I use MOLTEN renders in commercial work?expand_more

Yes. Every paid tier includes a full commercial license with no attribution required. Free-tier exports carry a watermark.

What hardware do I need for real-time reflections?expand_more

Any GPU from the last six years handles 1080p chrome at 60 fps. Ray-traced presets want a modern discrete card.
