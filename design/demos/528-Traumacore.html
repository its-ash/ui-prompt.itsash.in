How it works

No accounts to perform for, no streaks to keep. Just three small steps, at your own pace.

  1. 1

    Open the box

    Create your private space in under a minute. Everything is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves it.

  2. 2

    Say it raw

    Type, whisper, or scribble. Messy is welcome. Nimbus never grades your grammar or your feelings.

  3. 3

    Look back gently

    When you are ready, revisit old entries with kinder eyes and see how far you have actually come.

Quiet questions

The things people usually ask before they let themselves try.

Is what I write really private?expand_more

Yes. Entries are encrypted on your device with a key only you hold. We cannot read your journal, and neither can anyone else.

Is Nimbus a replacement for therapy?expand_more

No. Nimbus is a companion for between sessions, or a first step when you are not ready to talk to someone. If you are in crisis, please reach out to a local helpline first.

Can I delete everything, forever?expand_more

Anytime, in two taps. Deletion is permanent and complete, including backups. Your heaviness belongs to you, and so does letting it go.
