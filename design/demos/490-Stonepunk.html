Questions from the cave

Answers etched clearly, so nobody has to grunt in confusion.

What if my mammoth eats the shipment?expand_more

Mammoths are contractually forbidden from eating rocks. If one develops a taste for granite, we replace both the mammoth and the shipment within one moon.

Do you deliver during the rainy season?expand_more

Yes. Our mammoths enjoy mud. Deliveries may arrive slightly muddier and significantly happier.

Can I pay with something other than shells?expand_more

We accept shiny pebbles, quality flint, dried fish, and compelling stories. Stories are valued by length and number of wolves involved.

What happens if a sabertooth blocks the route?expand_more

Terrain Scout detects predators three grunts ahead. We reroute around the tar pits and bill the sabertooth for the delay.

Is the wheel really included?expand_more

One wheel per plan, reinvented fresh each morning. The Boulder and Mountain tiers include a spare wheel for ambitious days.
