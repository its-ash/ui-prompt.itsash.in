FAQ

IS THERE AN AGE LIMIT?

18+. ID and wristband are scanned at every gate. No wristband, no entry — no exceptions, no refunds at the door.

CAN I BRING MY OWN SYNTH?

Yes. The modular lab runs open CV and MIDI patch bays for all pass holders. Bring your own cables and label your gear — the crew is not your lost-and-found.

WHAT IS THE REFUND POLICY?

Passes are transferable up to 48h before doors. Refunds only if the festival is cancelled — otherwise the signal never drops.
