Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical lead time for a piece? expand_more

Lead times range from 6 to 12 weeks depending on complexity and material availability. We provide a detailed timeline after the design consultation.

Which wood species do you use? expand_more

We work with sustainably sourced Brazilian hardwoods such as Jequitibá, Rosa Imbuia, and Peroba Rosa, each selected for grain and durability.

How should I care for the leather upholstery? expand_more

Use a dry, soft cloth for dusting. Apply a leather conditioner every 6–12 months. Avoid direct sunlight and harsh chemicals.

Do you deliver and assemble internationally? expand_more

Yes. We offer white‑glove delivery and professional assembly worldwide, with customs handling included in the quote.

What warranty do you provide? expand_more

All structural components carry a lifetime warranty. Finish and upholstery are covered for 2 years against manufacturing defects.
