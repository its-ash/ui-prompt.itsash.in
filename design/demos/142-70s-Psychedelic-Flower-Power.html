Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment do I need to start?

Just a device with internet access! For art courses, basic supplies like paper and paints help. Music courses suggest a keyboard or guitar, but we offer alternatives.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! All plans include a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid charges.

How do I access course materials?

Materials are available instantly after enrollment via our website or mobile app. Downloadable resources are yours to keep.
