Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on safety, feedstock, and integration.

How do you prevent uncontrolled replication?expand_more
Every assembler lacks the instructions to copy itself and carries a hardware kill-switch plus a 72-hour chemical decay timer. Replication is physically impossible, not just policy-blocked.
What feedstock does the fab chamber use?expand_more
Standard cartridges contain purified carbon, silicon, or titanium suspensions. One cartridge yields roughly 1.4 kg of finished lattice material, and empties are returned for refining credit.
Can I import designs from my existing CAD tools?expand_more
Yes. Molecular CAD imports STL, STEP, and OBJ files, then runs a bond-level structural pass to flag geometry that cannot survive assembly at nanoscale tolerances.
