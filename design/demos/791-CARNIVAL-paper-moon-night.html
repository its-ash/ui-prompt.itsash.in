Questions from the queue

Answers before you reach the ticket window.

How fast can I launch an event page?expand_more

Most organizers publish within ten minutes. Pick a theme, set your date, and share the link. Tickets and the midway map switch on together.

Do guests need an account to buy passes?expand_more

No. Guests check out with an email address and receive a glowing QR stub they can save to their phone or print at home.

What happens if it rains on opening night?expand_more

Move the date in one click. Every pass holder is notified automatically and their stub stays valid for the new evening.
