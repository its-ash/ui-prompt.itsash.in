Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first burn.

How long does each candle burn?expand_more

Our classic vessels burn for roughly 45 to 60 hours depending on size. Trim the wick to a quarter inch before each lighting for the cleanest, longest burn.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?expand_more

Yes. Manage your plan from your account page at any time. Pauses take effect immediately and you will never be charged for a skipped season.

Are your ingredients sustainably sourced?expand_more

Our wax is a coconut and soy blend from certified farms, and every fragrance oil is phthalate-free. Vessels are made by partner studios using lead-free glazes.
