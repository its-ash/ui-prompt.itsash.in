Questions, answered simply

Can I import my existing tasks?expand_more
Yes. Nimbus imports from Todoist, Notion, and plain text files. Most imports finish in under a minute.
Does Nimbus work offline?expand_more
Fully. Everything lives on your device first and syncs quietly when a connection returns.
Can I cancel at any time?expand_more
Of course. Cancel in two clicks. Your data stays exportable forever, even on the free plan.
Is my data private?expand_more
Your notes are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never read them, and we never sell data.
Do you offer discounts?expand_more
Students, educators, and nonprofits receive 50% off any paid plan. Write to us from your account page.
