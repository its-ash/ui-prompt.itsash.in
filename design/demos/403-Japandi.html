Common Questions

What exactly is Japandi style? expand_more
It is a hybrid style that combines the rustic warmth of Scandinavian design with the refined minimalism of Japanese aesthetics.
Do I need to buy all new furniture? expand_more
Not at all. We focus on curation, which often means repurposing your favorite pieces and removing the clutter.
How long does a typical project take? expand_more
Depending on the tier, a project usually takes between 2 to 6 weeks from initial consultation to final layout.
