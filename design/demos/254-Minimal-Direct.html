05 — FAQ

Frequently asked

How is Nimbus different from other work tools?expand_more

Most tools make you configure everything before you start. Nimbus makes the decisions for you: one list, one priority order, one view. You can't set it up wrong.

Can I import my existing tasks?expand_more

Yes. Import from CSV, Trello, Asana, or Jira in under two minutes. Your source data is never modified.

Can I cancel at any time?expand_more

Yes — one click in Settings. You keep read-only access and full export of your data for 90 days after cancellation.
