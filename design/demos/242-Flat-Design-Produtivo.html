Frequently asked questions

Is Nimbus really free to start?

expand_more

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever — no trial clock, no credit card. You get three active lists, the focus timer, and seven days of activity history. Upgrade only when your workflow outgrows it.

Can I import tasks from another tool?

expand_more

One click. Nimbus ships importers for Todoist, Trello, Asana, and plain CSV files, mapping projects, due dates, and assignees automatically. Most teams migrate in under ten minutes.

Does Nimbus work offline?

expand_more

Yes. Every list is cached on your device, so you can check tasks off in a tunnel or on a plane. Changes sync automatically the moment you're back online — no conflicts, no duplicates.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

expand_more

Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from Settings in two clicks. Yearly plans stay active until the end of the paid period, and we refund unused months within the first 30 days.
