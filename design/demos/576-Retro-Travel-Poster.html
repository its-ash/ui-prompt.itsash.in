Questions From the Platform

Answers to what travelers ask us most before booking.

Sleeper berths are limited, so we recommend reserving three to six months ahead for summer routes. Winter departures often have space up to six weeks out.

Full refund up to 60 days before departure, 50 percent up to 30 days, and a transferable credit after that. Your deposit is never lost, only moved.

Never more than twelve travelers, and eight on Grand Tour departures. Most trips run with six to ten, plus your route guide.

We run routes across Europe, North Africa, Japan, and the Andes, with new lines scouted each winter. Every route is traveled by our team before it is sold.

Most days involve gentle walking between stations, cafés, and lodgings. Optional hikes are marked in your folio, and porterage covers your main bag.
