Good to Know

Questions From the Platform

Everything travelers ask before they climb aboard.

Most routes fill three to four months out, and summer departures can go even earlier. We recommend reserving as soon as your dates are set.

Cancel up to sixty days before departure for a full refund of your deposit. Inside sixty days, we offer credit toward any future route within two years.

Never more than twelve travelers plus one guide. Many departures run with eight to ten, which keeps meals, lodging, and conversations intimate.

We run rail and road routes across the mountain west, the Pacific coast, and the old eastern corridor, with seasonal routes announced each spring and autumn.
