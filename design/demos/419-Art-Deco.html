Inquiries

Frequently Posed Questions

May I gift a membership to another?expand_more

With pleasure. The House will engrave a cipher in your name and dispatch it sealed in champagne paper, by courier or by hand, to any address in the civilised world.

Is my membership transferable between cities?expand_more

Your cipher travels with you. Every House — from Manhattan to Vienna — honours the same key, the same ledger, and the same golden hour.

What happens if I wish to resign?expand_more

A member may resign by written notice at any moment. We close the ledger quietly, keep your preferences in the archive, and leave the door on the latch should you return.
