Enquiries

How far in advance should I reserve?expand_more

We recommend eight weeks for the Ballroom and Penthouse, and three weeks for the Salon. Our concierge occasionally holds a few dates for shorter notice, so it is always worth asking.

May I bring my own caterer or band?expand_more

Our house chef and musicians are part of the Aurelia experience, but we welcome outside talent for cultural or religious occasions. A modest coordination fee applies.

Is there a dress code for guests?expand_more

Evening attire is encouraged in the Ballroom and Penthouse after six o'clock. The Salon is more relaxed, though we find most guests rise to the occasion on their own.
