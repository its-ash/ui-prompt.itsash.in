helpFAQ

Straight answers

Do I need a designer on the team?expand_more

No. Nimbus ships art-directed by default: the ramp places color for you and every component is pre-tuned for both themes. Designers speed you up; they are never a blocker.

How do photos survive a two-color brand?expand_more

Every image is mapped through the ramp in pure CSS — grayscale, then blend modes for shadow and highlight. Any photo joins your palette with no pre-processing or asset pipeline.

Will visitors keep their theme choice?expand_more

Yes. Dark is the default, and each visitor’s toggle is remembered in localStorage, with a prefers-color-scheme fallback for first visits.
