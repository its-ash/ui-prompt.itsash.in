Common questions

Everything you need to know before your first box ships.

When do boxes ship?expand_more

Boxes leave our kitchen every Tuesday during the harvest window. You receive tracking the moment your box is sealed.

Can I skip a season?expand_more

Yes. Pause, skip, or cancel from your account page at any time with no fees and no questions asked.

Are the ingredients organic?expand_more

Over 90 percent of our fruit and botanicals are certified organic, and the rest come from orchards in transition to certification.
