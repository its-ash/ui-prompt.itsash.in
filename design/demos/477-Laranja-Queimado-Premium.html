Frequently Asked Questions

What is Laranja Queimado?

Laranja Queimado is a premium coffee blend infused with burnt orange notes, delivering a warm, earthy flavor profile.

How should I store the coffee?

Store in an airtight container away from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture to preserve freshness.

Is the coffee suitable for espresso machines?

Yes, our reserve roast is optimized for espresso, providing a rich crema and balanced acidity.
