Containment questions, answered

Are the strains safe to culture outside a lab?expand_more

Yes. Every Mycel strain is non-pathogenic, non-sporulating, and growth-locked to its nutrient substrate. Remove the substrate and the colony stops within hours.

How does the console integrate with our facility?expand_more

Vessels connect over standard Wi-Fi and report telemetry to the Mycel console. An open API lets you pipe growth data into your existing monitoring stack.

What happens to harvested material at end of life?expand_more

All three strains are fully compostable. Cured sheets break down in industrial compost within six weeks, leaving no synthetic residue.

Do you offer ethics and biosafety documentation?expand_more

Every shipment includes full sequencing records, containment certificates, and an ethics dossier reviewed by our independent biosafety board.

What support is included during incubation?expand_more

Colony and Ecosystem plans include live support from our cultivation team, with remote vessel diagnostics and replacement cultures if a batch fails.
