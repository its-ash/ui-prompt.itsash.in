Questions? Scribbled answers.

Do I need to know how to draw?expand_more

Not even a little. Stick figures, lumpy circles and spaghetti arrows are practically our house style.

Can I use Doodlio offline?expand_more

Yes — boards live in your browser and sync when you're back online. Sketch on the subway, guilt-free.

How is this different from other whiteboard apps?expand_more

Most tools push you to tidy. Doodlio keeps the hand-drawn warmth — loose edges, marker highlights, arrows that look drawn, not rendered.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Anytime, in two clicks — and your boards stay exportable forever. Consider it a very gentle eraser.
