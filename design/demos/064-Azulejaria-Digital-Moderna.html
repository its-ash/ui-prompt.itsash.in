FAQ

Questions, answered

How does real-time collaboration work?expand_more

Every canvas is a live room. Invite up to 12 co-artists, watch cursors sweep the wall, and see each tile repaint instantly — with full version history for every stroke.

Can I use heritage patterns in commercial work?expand_more

Yes — library patterns are cleared for digital reuse on every plan, and Collective or Institution seats add print and exhibition rights for physical façades.

How do I export or exhibit finished artwork?expand_more

Export panels as PNG, SVG, or print-resolution files anytime, or hang your wall in a curated virtual gallery with a shareable link and visitor analytics.
