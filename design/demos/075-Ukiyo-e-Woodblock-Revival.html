Questions From the Studio Door

Are your prints authentic woodblock impressions?expand_more
Yes — every reproduction is carved, inked, and pressed by hand from cherry blocks onto kōzo washi. Each is a numbered, sealed edition, never a digital inkjet.
What paper and pigments do you use?expand_more
Hand-made kōzo washi and traditional mineral and vegetable pigments — Prussian blue, vermilion, and indigo — mixed in our Tokyo studio.
How long does shipping take?expand_more
Unframed impressions ship worldwide within five working days; framed editions need two weeks for museum glazing. Every print travels flat in archival packaging.
Can I license imagery for commercial use?expand_more
Artisan and Master members may license archive imagery for editorial and commercial projects. Public-domain works carry simple, clearly stated terms.
Can I cancel or gift a membership?expand_more
Cancel any time from your account — no seals to break. Gift a year of the atelier from any tier’s checkout, delivered as a letterpress card.
