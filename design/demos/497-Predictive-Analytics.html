Questions, answered

How accurate are the forecasts?expand_more

Accuracy depends on your data history, but most customers see 90 to 95 percent accuracy on 30-day horizons. Every model is backtested against your own history before it goes live, and accuracy metrics are visible on every forecast.

What does a confidence interval mean?expand_more

The shaded band around a forecast shows the range where the true value is likely to fall. An 87 percent confidence interval means the outcome lands inside that band in roughly 87 out of 100 comparable situations.

How is my data secured?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified, supports SSO and role-based access, and never trains shared models on your private data.

Which tools does Nimbus integrate with?expand_more

Over 40 native connectors including Snowflake, BigQuery, Postgres, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Google Sheets, plus a REST API for custom pipelines.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes. Monthly plans cancel with one click and you keep access until the end of the billing period. Annual plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee.
