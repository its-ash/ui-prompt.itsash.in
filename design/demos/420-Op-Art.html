FAQ

Straight answers, sharp edges

Are the patterns really pure CSS?expand_more

Yes — every stripe, ring, and grid is a repeating gradient. No images, no canvas, no JavaScript paint calls.

Won’t high-contrast stripes risk photosensitivity?expand_more

All animation is gated behind prefers-reduced-motion, and tight vibrating fills swap to solid bands. Patterns are static by default.

How does dark mode invert the art?expand_more

Ink is one CSS variable. Flip it once and every stripe, ring, border, and hard shadow inverts together.
