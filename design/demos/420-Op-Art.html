Questions

Straight Answers

Do the patterns use any images?expand_more

No. Every export is a pure CSS repeating gradient. There are no raster assets, no SVG files, and no runtime JavaScript.

How does dark mode inversion work?expand_more

Each pattern exports as a variable with two ink values. When your theme switches, the ink flips with it, so vibration and contrast hold in both modes.

What about motion sensitivity?expand_more

OPTIKA flags high-frequency fills that can trigger photosensitivity and offers a solid-band fallback for users who prefer reduced motion.
