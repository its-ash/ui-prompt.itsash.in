FAQ

Before you flip the switch

How long does a custom sign take?expand_more

Mock-ups land within 48 hours; bending, gas filling, and a 24-hour test burn take about two weeks after approval. Most signs are hanging three weeks after your first email — ten days on rush builds.

Can I hang my sign outdoors?expand_more

Standard signs are built for indoor use, but we offer weather-sealed outdoor housings for patios and façades. Mention it in the quote form and we'll spec the right enclosure for your climate.

How much power does a sign draw?expand_more

Most signs sip 10–60 watts — less than a laptop charger — from any standard outlet. They stay cool to the touch and cost roughly a dollar a month glowing all night.

Can you recreate my exact logo?expand_more

Send your cleanest vector file and we'll redraw it for glass tube routing at no charge. Very tight angles get an honest simplification, shown in your mock-up before anything is lit.

What's the warranty?expand_more

Two years on transformers, wiring, and workmanship, plus free repair or replacement of transit-damaged glass in year one. After that, our bench repairs any Glowhaus sign at cost, forever.
