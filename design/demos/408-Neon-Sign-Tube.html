Questions

Before you plug in

How long does a custom sign take to make?expand_more

Most signs ship two to three weeks after you approve the proof. Marquee tier orders move to the front of the bending queue and ship in about ten days. Large Landmark installations are scheduled individually.

Can my sign live outdoors?expand_more

Yes. Choose the outdoor-rated option on the Marquee tier or above and we weather-seal the tubing, backing, and transformer. Outdoor signs carry an IP65 rating and handle rain, snow, and direct sun.

How much power does a neon sign draw?expand_more

LED flex signs draw roughly the same as a laptop charger, about 15 to 30 watts for a typical piece. Real glass signs draw more but still less than a standard light bulb per foot of tube. Every sign includes a dimmer so you control brightness and draw.

Can you recreate my business logo?expand_more

Almost always. Send a vector file if you have one, or a clear photo if you do not. Fine detail under about half an inch gets simplified in the bending plan, and we flag any changes on your proof before production starts.

What does the warranty cover?expand_more

Every sign carries a two-year warranty on tubing, wiring, and power supplies, extended to five years on Landmark installations. If a section stops glowing under normal use, we repair or replace it and cover shipping both ways.
