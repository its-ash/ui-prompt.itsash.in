SYS.06 // QUERY LOG

Frequently asked questions

How is photonic routing different from standard networking?expand_more

Standard networks convert data between electrons and light at every hop. Our mesh keeps traffic optical end to end, removing conversion overhead and cutting latency by an order of magnitude on long routes.

Can I migrate existing containers without rewriting them?expand_more

Yes. Any OCI-compliant container runs unchanged. The CLI imports your registry, maps environment variables, and preserves your existing CI triggers.

What happens if a node fails mid-workload?expand_more

The fabric checkpoints running workloads every few seconds and re-instantiates them on a healthy node automatically. Most failovers complete before your monitoring notices.
