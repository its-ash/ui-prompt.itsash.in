Questions, answered

How long does migration take?expand_more

Most teams deploy their first service within an hour. Full migrations from AWS or GCP typically finish in under a week with our import tooling.

What happens if a node fails?expand_more

The mesh detects failure in under 200 milliseconds and reroutes traffic to healthy nodes. Your workloads keep running with no manual failover.

Can I bring my own hardware?expand_more

Yes. Enterprise plans support on-premises nodes that join the same mesh, so hybrid deployments behave like a single region.
