Frequently asked questions

Do I need design experience to use Vectordelia?

No. The Swoosh Engine and preset library handle the hard parts, so anyone can produce polished vector art from a rough sketch.

Can I export assets for commercial projects?

Yes. Every plan, including the free tier, includes a full commercial license for anything you create and export.

How does team collaboration work?

Invite teammates to any canvas. Edits, comments, and version history sync live, and you can roll back to any earlier state.
