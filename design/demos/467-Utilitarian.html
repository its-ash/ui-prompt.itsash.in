SEC/06 // FAQ
Q-01 How does rollback work?

Every release is immutable and stored. Rolling back redeploys the previous release artifact directly, typically in under thirty seconds, with no rebuild step.

Q-02 Can I self-host Nimbus?

Yes. The Division tier includes a self-hosted control plane option with the same manifest format and CLI. Your data never leaves your network.

Q-03 What happens when a deploy fails?

The failing step is flagged in the console with its log output. Traffic stays on the previous healthy release until you promote the fix. No partial states.
