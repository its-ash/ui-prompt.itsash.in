CONTINUE?

Answers from the service counter, before you ask the cashier.

Do I need to download or install anything?

Nope — every game streams straight to your browser with no plug-ins or launchers. A steady 10 Mbps connection is enough for lag-free play on any screen.

Can I really cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in two clicks from your profile — no phone calls, no guilt screens. Your save states stay stored for 90 days in case you change your mind.

Which devices and controllers are supported?

Any laptop, desktop, tablet or phone with a modern browser works. Most USB and Bluetooth gamepads are plug-and-play, and we remap the keyboard per game.
