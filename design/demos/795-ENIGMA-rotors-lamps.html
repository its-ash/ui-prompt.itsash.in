Frequently asked questions

Is ENIGMA actually unbreakable?expand_more

No system is provably unbreakable, but ENIGMA uses layered rotating ciphers with zero‑knowledge key handling, making practical attacks infeasible without the rotor state.

What happens if I lose my keys?expand_more

Because ENIGMA is zero‑knowledge, keys never leave your device. If they are lost, there is no recovery — you must generate a new rotor set.

Does ENIGMA work offline?expand_more

Yes. All encryption happens locally; the lampboard UI works without network. Queued messages sync when you reconnect.
