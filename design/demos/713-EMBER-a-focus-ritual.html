Questions, answered

Everything people usually ask before their first session.

Does EMBER work offline?expand_more
Yes. Sessions, streaks, and breaks run fully on your device. Data syncs to your account the next time you are online, so a cabin weekend costs you nothing.
What happens to my focus data?expand_more
Your session history belongs to you. It is stored encrypted, never sold, and you can export or delete everything from settings at any time.
Can I customize session and break lengths?expand_more
Absolutely. Set any focus length from 5 to 120 minutes and pair it with fixed breaks or let adaptive mode scale rest time to session intensity.
Is there a refund policy?expand_more
Every paid plan comes with a 30-day, no-questions refund. If the ritual does not stick, email support and we return the full amount.
Do you offer discounts for students or nonprofits?expand_more
Yes. Students, educators, and registered nonprofits get 50% off Ember and Blaze plans. Verify once with a school or organization email.
