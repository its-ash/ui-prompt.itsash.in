FAQ

Straight Answers

How we work, priced and scheduled without riddles.

How long does a typical project take?add

Residential commissions run 8–14 months from brief to handover; civic and commercial work averages 18–24 months. The parametric stage compresses concept decisions to weeks — where most studios lose time, we keep it.

How is the budget determined and controlled?add

We fix the fee at the brief stage and report against it monthly. Because design changes re-price in hours rather than weeks, every variation is costed before it is drawn — no change-order ambushes.

Do you warranty your structures?add

Every Formline building carries a 10-year structural warranty. Load-bearing systems are specified one full safety factor beyond code, and post-occupancy audits feed directly into the next generation of details.
