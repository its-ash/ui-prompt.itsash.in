Common Enquiries

Before you commission

What do I receive with a finished plate?

A print-ready engraved file at full press resolution, a vector line master, and a numbered certificate of edition. Larger commissions also include the physical proof sheet.

How long does a commission take?

A single plate typically runs ten working days from approved brief to delivered edition. Multi-plate suites are scheduled sequentially so stroke density stays consistent across the set.

Can you match a historical engraving style?

Yes. Send reference plates — Bewick, Dürer, or banknote guilloche — and we will match stroke direction, line weight, and cross-hatch density within one proof round.

Final Proof Call

Reserve your place on the press.

Edition slots open quarterly and fill in order of request. Leave your address and we will send the commission brief.
