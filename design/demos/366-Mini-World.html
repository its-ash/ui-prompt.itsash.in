Curious? Peek inside the box

The questions every new builder asks, answered toy-box style.

Do I need to download anything?expand_more

Nope! Mini World runs entirely in your browser. Open a tab, claim a plot, and you're stacking blocks in seconds.

Can I really build with friends?expand_more

Yes! On Villager and Architect you get a share link — up to 8 builders can wander and stack on the same island in real time.

What's the deal with Night World mode?expand_more

Flip the switch and your island settles into a cozy night: glowing windows, fireflies, and a whole new starry palette. Same world, just tucked in.

Is Mini World kid-friendly?expand_more

Very. No ads, no loot boxes, no surprise purchases — just blocks, grass, and friendly tiny citizens. Teachers absolutely love it.

Can I cancel my plan?expand_more

Anytime, in two clicks, zero guilt trips. Your islands stay yours forever — even on the free Sprout plan.
