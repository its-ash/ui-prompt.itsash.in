FAQ

Fine print (ours, not yours)

How fast can you print?expand_more

Digital proofs within 24 hours; zines posted in five working days. Full press runs take three days after proof approval — the press is loud, not slow.

Which ink colors do you stock?expand_more

All 18 standard riso inks plus fluorescent pink and blue. Overlap two drums and the paper invents a third color for free — that’s the whole point.

How should I prep my files?expand_more

Grayscale 300dpi PDF, one layer per ink, 3mm bleed. Register it perfectly if you like — we’ll still nudge it a few microns askew. Kindly.
