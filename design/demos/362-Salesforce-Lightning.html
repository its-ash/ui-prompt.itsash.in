Frequently asked questions

Security, migration, and everything in between.

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 aligned, with SAML 2.0 SSO, SCIM provisioning, and complete audit logging on Enterprise plans.

Over 200 prebuilt connectors cover Outlook, Gmail, Slack, Teams, SAP, NetSuite, Marketo, and Zendesk. Anything else can be wired through our REST API, webhooks, or a low-code integration builder.

Most teams go live in under two weeks. A guided import wizard maps objects from Salesforce, Dynamics, HubSpot, or CSV, and a dedicated migration architect validates data before cutover.

You pay a flat rate per user, per month, billed annually. Adding seats is prorated and removing them takes effect at renewal. Volume discounts start at 250 seats, and there is no implementation fee on Professional or Enterprise.

Essentials includes business-hours email support. Professional adds 24/5 chat with a four-hour first-response target. Enterprise includes 24/7 support, a named success manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Yes. We support GDPR and CCPA data-processing agreements, EU, US, and APAC data-residency options, configurable retention policies, and HIPAA-ready deployments under a signed BAA.
