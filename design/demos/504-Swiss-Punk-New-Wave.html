FAQ

Straight answers, crooked type

Does the chaos break my mobile layout?add

No. Every tilt and overlap is clamped and guarded. Below 640px all rotations flatten and stacks collapse to a single column with zero horizontal scroll.

Do exported pages support dark mode?add

Yes. Every export ships with a full dark token set, a persisted toggle, and a prefers-color-scheme fallback. Hard offset shadows stay visible in both themes.

What about accessibility and reduced motion?add

Contrast pairs are validated to WCAG AA in both themes, all states pair color with icons or text, and prefers-reduced-motion removes every rotation and marquee.
