05, Questions

Frequently asked

Anything else, write to hello@gridconf.ch and we reply within one working day.

Where exactly does the conference take place?add

All sessions are held at Kunsthalle Zürich, Limmatstrasse 270, ten minutes on foot from Zürich Hauptbahnhof. Badge collection opens at 08:00 each day at the main entrance.

Can I transfer my ticket to a colleague?add

Yes. Tickets can be reassigned free of charge until 7 October 2026 through the link in your confirmation email. After that date, bring a signed transfer note to the check-in desk.

Are the talks recorded?add

Main-stage talks are recorded and shared with Standard and Team pass holders within two weeks of the event. Workshops are not recorded, as they rely on live critique.

Is there a student discount?add

We hold 40 student passes at CHF 190. Apply with a valid student ID through the link on the tickets page. Applications close 1 September 2026.

What is the refund policy?add

Full refunds are available until 14 September 2026. After that, we offer a 50 percent refund or a free transfer to another attendee up to one week before the event.
