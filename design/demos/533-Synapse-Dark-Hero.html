Common questions

Does Synapse write the updates for me?expand_more

It produces a structured first draft from your merged pull requests and release tags. You always review and approve before anything is published.

Which repositories are supported?expand_more

GitHub and GitLab are supported today, including self-hosted GitLab instances. Bitbucket support is in beta for Growth and Scale plans.

Can I keep a private internal changelog?expand_more

Yes. You can mark any release as internal-only, which publishes it to a private page behind your team login instead of the public changelog.

What happens if I cancel?expand_more

Your published changelog stays online in read-only mode, and you can export every release as Markdown or JSON at any time.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?expand_more

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day trial, no credit card required. The Starter plan is free forever with no time limit.
