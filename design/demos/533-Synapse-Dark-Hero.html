Frequently Asked Questions

What is Synapse?
Synapse is a unified data platform that combines ingestion, transformation, and orchestration in a single UI.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and we are SOC 2 Type II compliant.
Can I run Synapse on my own infrastructure?
Absolutely. We offer Kubernetes‑based deployments for private clouds and on‑premise data centers.
What support options are available?
Free tier includes community forums. Paid plans add email and phone support with SLA‑backed response times.
How do I migrate from my existing ETL tool?
We provide import connectors and a migration assistant to move jobs, schemas, and history with zero downtime.
