FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Which networks does Nimbus support?expand_more

Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and Solana at launch, with new chains added monthly. Historical depth goes back to genesis on supported networks.

Do you ever hold my keys or assets?expand_more

Never. Nimbus is read-only by default — we query public data and never custody funds, sign transactions or request spending approvals.

Can I change or cancel my plan?expand_more

Yes. Plans are month-to-month, upgrades apply instantly with prorated billing, and you can cancel in settings anytime — dashboards stay exportable for 90 days.
