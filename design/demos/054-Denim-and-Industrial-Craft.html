Handcut Tailoring

The Process

From Loom to Your Waistband

01

Fitted & Cut

Send three measurements or visit the workshop. Your pattern is drafted once, then kept on file for life.

02

Sewn & Rested

One tailor sews your pair start to finish, then we sanforize the cloth and hang it to rest for 48 hours.

03

Riveted & Released

Rivets are set, your initials are hot-stamped into the leather patch, and the pair ships in raw canvas.

Questions

Straight Answers, No Fray

How should raw denim fit on day one?expand_more

Snug, not tight. Raw selvedge relaxes about one full size over the first month of honest wear.

Do you really repair every pair forever?expand_more

Yes. Send any pair back and we mend hems, rivets and knees free of charge — you only cover postage.

How long does a made-to-order pair take?expand_more

Three to five weeks: drafting, sewing, a 48-hour rest, and a final press before it ships.

Can I visit the workshop?expand_more

Saturdays, 10 to 4. We pour the coffee; you watch the Union Specials run.

What if the fit isn’t right?expand_more

Your first alteration is free within 60 days — we adjust or re-cut until it’s right.
