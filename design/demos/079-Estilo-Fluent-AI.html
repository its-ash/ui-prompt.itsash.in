Is my data used to train models?expand_more

Never. Your content is encrypted in transit and at rest, region-pinned where required, and excluded from all model training. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and offer a signed DPA.

What counts as an assistant action?expand_more

Any completed task — a drafted email, a scheduled follow-up, a summarized thread. The Free plan includes 50 actions per month; Pro is unlimited.

Can I cancel or change plans anytime?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from settings in two clicks. Your workflows stay available in read-only mode for 90 days afterward.
