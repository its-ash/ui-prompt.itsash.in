Common Questions

How does Aether handle security?
Aether operates in a sandboxed environment. It never stores your secrets and only accesses the parts of the codebase you explicitly authorize.
Does it replace my engineers?
No. Aether is a force multiplier. It handles the tedious implementation and bug-fixing, allowing your engineers to focus on high-level design.
Which languages are supported?
Aether is language-agnostic but works best with TypeScript, Python, Rust, and Go.
