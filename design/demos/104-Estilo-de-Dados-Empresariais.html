Frequently asked questions

Everything enterprise buyers usually ask before switching.

How does the autonomous optimizer work?expand_more

Nimbus models your workload from live query patterns, then continuously tests index, memory, and query-plan changes in a shadow environment — promoting only proven winners, with zero impact on production traffic.

Is my data encrypted?expand_more

Always. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Enterprise plans add customer-managed keys (BYOK) and full audit logging.

Can we migrate from our existing database?expand_more

Yes. The migration toolkit ingests dumps, CDC streams, or live replicas from PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Oracle — with automatic schema mapping and scheduled cutover windows.
