FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Nimbus Health HIPAA-compliant?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and every chart action is written to an immutable audit trail.

How long does implementation take?

Most clinics go live within two weeks. Onboarding includes patient-record migration and staff training at no extra cost.

Do patients need training to use the portal?

No — the patient view is fully guided. Booking takes three taps, and reminders arrive automatically by SMS or email.
