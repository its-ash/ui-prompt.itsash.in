Features

Built for attention, not engagement

Three principles shape every surface of Stillpoint. Nothing else made the cut.

edit_note

A plain page

One font, one column, one margin. The editor disappears so the sentence can appear. Formatting waits until you ask for it.

notifications_paused

A daily bell

A single gentle reminder at the hour you choose. No streaks, no badges, no guilt. Miss a day and nothing happens.

lock

A sealed archive

Entries are encrypted on your device before they ever leave it. We could not read your journal even if asked.

Method

Three movements, once a day

The practice is deliberately small. Small enough to keep for years.

  1. 01

    Sit

    Open Stillpoint at your chosen hour. The page is already blank and waiting. There is nothing to configure and nothing to catch up on.

  2. 02

    Write

    Twenty minutes, one prompt if you want it. The timer is silent and the word count is hidden until you finish.

  3. 03

    Seal

    Close the entry and it joins your archive, encrypted and searchable only by you. Tomorrow the page is blank again.

Questions, answered plainly

Is my writing really private?expand_more

Yes. Entries are encrypted on your device with a key only you hold. Our servers store ciphertext. We have no way to read, mine, or train on your words.

What happens if I miss a day?expand_more

Nothing. There are no streaks to break and no notifications scolding you. The page is simply blank again when you return.

Can I leave and take my archive with me?expand_more

Anytime. Export everything as plain text or Markdown in two clicks. Your writing was never ours to hold hostage.

Does Stillpoint work offline?expand_more

Fully. Write anywhere, and entries sync the next time you are connected. Silence travels well.

Why no free trial of the Monk plan?expand_more

The Novice plan is the trial, without a clock. Use it as long as you like; upgrade only if the practice takes root.
