Questions, answered

Everything artists usually ask before their first render.

Do I need illustration experience to use Chromawave?expand_more

No. Scene presets handle composition and lighting, and the airbrush engine keeps every stroke soft and blended. Experienced artists can still paint fully freehand.

Can I use the artwork commercially?expand_more

Yes. Every plan includes a full commercial license for covers, posters, merchandise and client work, with no attribution required.

What export formats are supported?expand_more

PNG and TIFF up to 6K on the Starship plan, plus layered project files so you can revisit glow, type and grid elements at any time.
