Frequently asked questions

How does data get into Pulsegrid?expand_more

Install our lightweight agent, or forward metrics from Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, StatsD, or cloud providers. Most teams see their first live charts within five minutes.

How fast are alerts delivered?expand_more

On Pro and Enterprise plans, the pipeline from metric ingestion to notification is under two seconds at p99, including evaluation and routing.

Can I self-host Pulsegrid?expand_more

Enterprise plans include a dedicated-region deployment and a fully self-hosted option with the same streaming engine and feature set.

What happens to my data if I cancel?expand_more

You can export all metrics and dashboards at any time. After cancellation we retain data for 30 days, then delete it permanently.

Is there a free trial of Pro?expand_more

Yes, every workspace gets a 14-day Pro trial with no credit card required. It reverts to the free Starter plan automatically.
