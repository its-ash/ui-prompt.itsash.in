Questions from the Temple Steps

Do I need any prior knowledge of the script?expand_more

None. We begin with the uniliteral alphabet — the two dozen signs on which every Egyptian word is built.

How soon will I read real inscriptions?expand_more

Most students parse their first authentic cartouche within six weeks, and a complete stele by the third season.

What materials are provided?expand_more

Workbooks, sign lists, museum-grade photographs of monuments, and recordings of every live session.

Is the scribe certificate recognized?expand_more

It is issued by the House of Life and accepted by museum education programs across four continents.

Can I withdraw my enrollment?expand_more

Any oath may be unbound before its renewal date — no penalties, and no questions asked.
