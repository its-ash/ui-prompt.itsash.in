help

Answers by moonrise

Does Nimbus work in cloudy or light-polluted skies?

Yes. Our harvesters gather ambient glow, scattered starlight, and heat drift, so even a cloudy or city-lit night feeds the battery grove — just more gently than a clear one.

What happens during a new moon?

Full Moon tiers keep up to five nights of reserve in the battery grove, so the darkest stretch of the lunar cycle passes without a flicker.

Is the machinery truly silent?

Every device hums below 18 decibels — quieter than moth wings on glass. If you ever hear yours, a steward retunes it for free.
