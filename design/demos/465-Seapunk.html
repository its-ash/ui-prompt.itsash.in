Is Nimbus actually underwater?expand_more

Legally, no. Spiritually, it has never left the ocean. Our servers are in a normal data center, but the dashboards are extremely moist in feeling.

Can I turn off the holographic gradients?expand_more

You can reduce them in settings. We will know, and we will respect your choice, quietly, from behind a coral reef.

What happens to my data if I cancel?expand_more

You can export everything before you go. After 30 days your data is released back into the sea, meaning securely deleted, forever.
