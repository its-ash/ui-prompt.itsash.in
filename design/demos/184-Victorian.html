Enquiries

Frequently Posed Questions

filter_vintage
Are your artisans truly trained in period methods?add

Indeed. Every craftsman serves a seven-year apprenticeship in chasing, engraving, and finishing before earning the Nimbus hallmark.

Might I commission an entirely bespoke piece?add

Most gladly. Bespoke work begins with a consultation and a firm quotation; the bench typically delivers within eight to twelve weeks.

What is your policy on repairs and refinishing?add

All repairs are executed in-house and warranted for five years. Apprentice subscribers enjoy a reduction; Journeyman patrons receive one restoration gratis each year.
