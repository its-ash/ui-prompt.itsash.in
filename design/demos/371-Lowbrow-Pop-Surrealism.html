Asked at every séance

Questions from the dark

How are the prints made?expand_more

Every artwork is drawn by hand, then printed as a giclée on 310gsm cotton rag with archival pigment inks. Each print is inspected, hand-numbered, embossed with the crypt seal, and logged before it ships.

Do you ship internationally?expand_more

Yes — we ship worldwide with tracking from the studio. Prints travel in crush-proof crates; any customs duties are the recipient's funeral to plan.

Can I license or commission artwork?expand_more

Commissions open a few times a year and are announced to the Crypt Club first. For licensing — album covers, decks, brands — write to licensing@candycrypt.art with your pitch.
