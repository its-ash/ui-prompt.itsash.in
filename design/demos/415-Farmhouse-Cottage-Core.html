Only to sync. Nimbus Hollow works offline in the barn, the field, or the back forty, and catches up the moment you're back on Wi-Fi.
Can I keep my paper ledgers?expand_more
Of course. Photograph or type in old entries and we'll file them beside your digital ones — no smudges, no grease-stained elbows either.
What if I need to cancel?expand_more
No hoops, no phone calls. One click pauses your plan and keeps your records safe for a year. Ready to leave for good? The Close account button sits right in settings.
