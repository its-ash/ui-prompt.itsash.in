good to know

Frequently asked questions

When do crates ship?expand_more
Crates leave the farm during the first week of each season: March, June, September, and December. You will receive a note with tracking as soon as yours is on its way.
Are your preserves shelf stable?expand_more
Yes. Every jar is water-bath sealed and keeps for up to a year unopened in a cool cupboard. Once opened, refrigerate and enjoy within a month.
Can I pause or cancel my subscription?expand_more
Anytime, with no fees and no fuss. Pause a season or cancel entirely from your account page, and it takes effect before the next crate ships.
