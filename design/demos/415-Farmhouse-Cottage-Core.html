Questions from the porch

When do crates ship?expand_more

Crates leave the farm every Tuesday. Seasonal subscribers receive theirs in the first week of March, June, September, and December.

Can I skip a season or cancel?expand_more

Yes. Skip, pause, or cancel from your account page any time before the packing week. No fees, no fuss, and we will hold your spot for two seasons.

Do you accommodate allergies?expand_more

Tell us your household’s allergies at checkout and we pack around them. Our kitchen handles nuts and wheat, so we label every jar with full ingredients.
