Frequently Avoided Questions

Answers rendered at 60Hz for maximum sincerity.

Is this a real product?expand_more

It is as real as the mall of your childhood, which is to say: emotionally real, commercially demolished. The billing, however, is extremely real.

Can I cancel my subscription?expand_more

Yes. Cancellation is a single click, followed by a short film about a sunset, followed by your freedom. We process refunds at dial-up speed out of respect for the era.

Why does the progress bar stop at 87%?expand_more

Because that is where hope lives. Our engineers tested 100% and users reported an unsettling sense of closure. We removed it for your comfort.

Does the marble bust actually judge me?expand_more

The bust contains no sensors, no camera, and no opinions. The judgment you feel is generated locally, by you, free of charge.

Is my data safe in the VHS cloud?expand_more

Your data is encrypted, redundant, and stored on modern hardware. The tape hiss is cosmetic. The security is not. Please be kind, rewind.
