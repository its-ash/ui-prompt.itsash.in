We use FSC-certified plant-based fibers including sugarcane bagasse, bamboo, and wheat straw. All materials are certified home-compostable (OK Compost HOME) and break down in 90-180 days in a healthy compost environment, leaving no microplastics or toxins.
Our packaging holds multiple certifications: OK Compost HOME (TÜV Austria), FSC Mix Credit, FDA food contact compliance, and BPI industrial compostability. We provide full documentation with every order.
Our Seedling plan starts at just 50 units. The Grove plan has a 100-unit minimum, and Forest plan is tailored for high-volume needs with no effective minimum due to volume pricing. We work with businesses of all sizes.
