Questions, answered calmly

Do I need any experience with meditation?expand_more

None at all. Every session begins with a one-line cue, and the pacing adjusts as you go. Most members feel comfortable within their first two sessions.

Is there real science behind paced breathing?expand_more

Yes. Slow, extended exhales are well studied for activating the parasympathetic nervous system. Our rhythms are built around those published findings.
