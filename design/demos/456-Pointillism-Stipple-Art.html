FAQ

Questions, answered dot by dot

What exactly is stippling?expand_more

Stippling is a drawing technique that builds tone and texture from thousands of individual dots. Pointillism is its painted cousin, where dots of pure color blend in the viewer's eye. Stipple the tool automates both, placing each dot based on the tones in your source photo.

Which file formats can I upload and export?expand_more

You can upload JPG, PNG, and WebP images up to 40 megapixels. Exports include PNG and TIFF at print resolution on every paid plan, plus infinitely scalable SVG on the Atelier plan.

Can I use the artwork commercially?expand_more

Yes. Studio and Atelier plans include a full commercial license for client work, merchandise, and print runs. The free Sketch plan is for personal projects and portfolio pieces.

Will it slow down my computer?expand_more

No. The dot engine runs on your GPU through WebGL, so a two-million-dot render typically finishes in a few seconds on a modern laptop. Everything happens locally, with nothing uploaded to a server.

What is your refund policy?expand_more

Every paid plan comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If Stipple is not right for your workflow, write to support and we will refund the full amount.

Does Stipple support dark mode?expand_more

It does, both on this site and in the app. The editor follows your system preference automatically, and you can flip it anytime with the toggle in the header. Your choice is remembered between visits.
