Questions

Fine print, in plain dots

What exactly is stipple art?expand_more

Stippling builds images from dots alone — dense in shadow, sparse in light. Stipple automates the discipline that took the pointillists years; you keep the art direction, it keeps ten thousand points in place.

Which file formats does Stipple support?expand_more

Import JPG, PNG, WebP, or HEIC up to 8,000 pixels wide. Export vector SVG, PNG at up to 300 DPI, or layered files for print — every dot stays crisp from business card to storefront.

Can I sell the artwork I make here?expand_more

Yes. On Studio and Atelier, everything you render is yours — prints, client work, licensing, all of it. Sketch renders are free for personal and study use.

Will it run on my machine?expand_more

The dot engine runs on GPU-backed WebAssembly inside your browser: a typical 100,000-dot render finishes in about a second on any laptop from the last five years. No installs, no upload queues.

What about dark mode and refunds?expand_more

Stipple ships with a warm-ink dark theme — this page runs on the same engine. Paid plans carry a 14-day, no-questions refund, right down to the last dot.
