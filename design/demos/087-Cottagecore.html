questions

Asked over the garden fence

Everything folk usually wonder before their first box arrives.

Every box follows the season: two baking kits with pre-measured heritage grains, a clutch of wildflower seed packets, one handcrafted good from a village maker, and a recipe card written like a letter. Exact contents shift with the harvest.

Boxes leave our barn four times a year, just after each solstice and equinox. UK orders arrive within 3–5 days; international within 2–3 weeks. We'll send a note when yours is on its way.

Gladly. Choose any tier as a gift at checkout and we'll include a card with your message — the price is never mentioned, and you'll get a little note when each box is delivered.

Any time before a box is packed. Pause for a season or stop altogether from your account — no phone calls, no fuss, no wilted questions.

Every scrap of it — shredded straw, paper tape, and boxes that break down in a compost heap within a season. We ask only that you give them back to the garden.
