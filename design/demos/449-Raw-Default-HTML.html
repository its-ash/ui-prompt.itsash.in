Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can write an email, you can build a Plainpage site. The editor is a text box with a publish button.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on the Writer and Studio plans. Point your DNS at us and we handle the certificate automatically.

What happens if I cancel?

Your site stays online for 30 days, and you keep a full export of every file forever. We do not hold content hostage.

Why are there no animations or sliders?

Because your readers came for the words. Every feature we skip is a feature that cannot slow your site down.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

The Reader plan is free forever and needs no card. It is the trial, except it never expires.

One message, no newsletter, no drip campaign. Unsubscribe by ignoring us.
