Questions from the back seat

Pull up a lawn chair — the answers are short and the fire’s still going.

Are these real 1970s travel logs?expand_more

Part time capsule, part living journal. The classic routes are drawn from genuine ’70s field notes, updated with modern road conditions so your VW doesn’t end up in a ditch.

Can I contribute my own road stories?expand_more

Absolutely — the Postcard Club is our open mic. Send 500 words and a photo; if it makes the editors honk the horn, it runs in the journal with your byline.

What if I need to cancel my membership?expand_more

No fine print, no hard feelings. Cancel any time and keep every route map you’ve already downloaded. The road stays open.
