Quick answers from the wiki

The essentials before you place your first block.

Do I need to know how to code?expand_more
No. Logic blocks snap together visually, so doors, timers, and quests are built by stacking rules instead of writing scripts.
Can my friends play my worlds for free?expand_more
Yes. Anything you publish to the arcade is free to play in the browser. Only world creation is tied to your plan.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime?expand_more
Anytime. Upgrades apply instantly, and if you downgrade your worlds stay safe in read-only mode until you return.
