Before You Ask

How fast can my team actually get started?expand_more

Most crews are up and running in under ten minutes. Create a workspace, share one invite link, and start dropping tasks on the board. There is nothing to install.

Can I switch plans or cancel later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your billing page at any time. Changes apply instantly and we prorate the difference automatically.

Is my data safe with Nimbus?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups and regional hosting options. Stadium plans add SSO, audit logs, and custom retention policies.
