Frequently Asked

What is a daguerrean-style portrait?

It is a likeness made upon a polished silver plate in the manner of the 1840s and 1850s — a single exposure, developed in mercury vapour, unique and unreproducible.

How long does a sitting take?

From pose to finished plate, plan on forty-five to ninety minutes, including preparation and a short rest between exposures. Stillness is the greater part of the art.

How should a plate be cared for?

Keep the case closed and away from damp and strong sun; touch the plate only by its edges, and let our conservators attend to any tarnish that may appear.
