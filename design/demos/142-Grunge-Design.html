Questions

Is this actually print-grade?expand_more

Yes. Exports are CMYK at 300 DPI with bleed and registration marks. Your copy shop will stop yelling at you.

Can I import my InDesign files?expand_more

No. SMEAR is a recovery program for template victims. Paste your text, bring your images, start over. It's healthier.

Dark mode or light mode?expand_more

Both, and neither is the default. The app follows your system; this page follows the toggle up top. Try it.
