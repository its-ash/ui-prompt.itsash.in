Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Recipes and notes stay yours. Nothing is sold, shared, or used to train models.

Can I print a recipe?

Every page exports to a clean, print-ready layout with your margin notes intact.

Does it work offline?

The notebook is available offline once loaded. Sync resumes when you reconnect.
