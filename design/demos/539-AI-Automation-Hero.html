Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do I need to know how to code?expand_more
No. The builder is fully visual, and you can describe workflows in plain English. A code step is available if your team wants one.
What happens when a run fails?expand_more
Nimbus retries the failed step up to three times, then pauses the workflow and alerts you in Slack or email with the full error context.
How is my data handled?expand_more
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. AI steps process data in isolated environments and never train shared models on your content.
Can I migrate from Zapier or Make?expand_more
Yes. The importer reads your existing zaps and scenarios and recreates them as Nimbus workflows, which you can review before going live.
