Common questions

Everything teams ask before their first workflow.

Do I need to write code?expand_more
No. The visual builder covers triggers, conditions, and AI steps. A code step is available if you want custom logic.
How does the free tier work?expand_more
You get 5 active workflows and 1,000 runs each month, forever. No credit card required to sign up.
Is my data used to train models?expand_more
Never. Your data stays in your workspace and is only used to execute your workflows. Enterprise plans add region pinning.
Can I migrate from Zapier or Make?expand_more
Yes. Our importer reads exported scenarios and rebuilds them as Nimbus flows, usually in a few minutes.
