Frequently asked questions

What does WCAG AAA mean in practice?expand_more

AAA is the strictest WCAG level: at least 7:1 text contrast, enhanced keyboard support, and extended alternatives for time-based media. Nimbus tests your components against these success criteria automatically.

Do I need to change how my team writes code?expand_more

No. Nimbus works with your existing stack and ships fixes as reviewable patches. Most teams see their first AAA-passing page within a day of onboarding.

Can I use Nimbus with an existing accessibility audit?expand_more

Yes. Import any audit report and Nimbus tracks each finding to closure, re-verifying every fix in CI so resolved issues never resurface.
