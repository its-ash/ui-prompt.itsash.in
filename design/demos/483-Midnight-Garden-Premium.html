Common questions

Everything new growers ask before their first box.

Do night-blooming plants need special lighting?expand_more
No. They thrive in normal daylight and simply open their flowers after sunset. A bright windowsill or balcony is enough.
How are plants protected during shipping?expand_more
Each plant travels in an insulated, light-sealed sleeve with a hydrated root wrap, and arrives within 48 hours of leaving the nursery.
What if a plant arrives damaged?expand_more
Send us a photo within seven days and we ship a replacement free of charge, no return required.
Can I pause my subscription?expand_more
Yes. You can skip a season or pause indefinitely from your account page, and your care plan picks up where it left off.
Are the plants safe around pets?expand_more
Every collection page lists pet safety clearly, and you can filter the catalogue to pet-safe species only.
