Chrome, clarified

Does Nimbus really run entirely in the browser?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus renders through WebGL 2 and computes every material on your GPU — nothing to install, no render farm. Cloud sync only kicks in when you want version history.

Can I export materials into my design system?expand_more

Every material exports as CSS custom properties, Lottie or glTF. Light and dark token pairs are generated and contrast-checked for you automatically.

Do exports work with dark themes?expand_more

Yes — each export ships paired token sets for light and dark, both validated to WCAG AA, so your chrome reads as dark liquid metal, never washed-out white.

What hardware do I need?expand_more

Any machine with WebGL 2 and hardware acceleration — most laptops from the last six years qualify. The Mercury engine falls back gracefully to 60 fps on integrated graphics.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Monthly plans cancel in one click from settings. You keep every export you have already shipped, forever.
