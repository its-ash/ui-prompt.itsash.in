// SPEC SHEET — Q&A

Frequently asked questions

How does the X9 compare to the previous generation?add

On HPL and Llama-class inference benchmarks the X9 delivers 2.1× the performance per watt of the X7. Per-core thermal telemetry re-allocates the heat budget every 40 ns, so sustained clocks stay within 3% of peak across 72-hour runs.

Which socket, OS, and software stacks are supported?add

The X9 uses the LGA-N9 socket. Linux kernels 6.x and newer are supported day one, with first-class integration in Kubernetes and Slurm schedulers. GPU and TPU accelerators attach over the 5.0 GT/s fabric without host-bus bottlenecks.

What are the power and cooling requirements?add

TDP is 350 W per socket. Standard sleds are air-cooled up to a 35 °C inlet; liquid-cooled sleds unlock full boost in dense 1U racks. Per-core power telemetry streams to the orchestration controller in real time.

What support and warranty coverage is included?add

All tiers include next-business-day replacement for the first year. Cluster and Data Center plans add a 24/7 priority SLA, signed firmware delta channels, and a named deployment engineer for the life of the fleet.

How quickly can we deploy a pilot cluster?add

Sleds ship in 2–4 weeks. A 16-node pilot — racked, orchestrated, and running your first workload — is typically online in under a single business day.
