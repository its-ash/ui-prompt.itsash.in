Letters desk

SMALL PRINT, BIG ANSWERS

What is the Halftone engine, exactly?expand_more

Our CSS-first texture system. Every dot grid, offset shadow and misprint effect is drawn with gradients and borders — no images, no JavaScript, no layout shift. It prints beautifully even on a slow connection.

Can I bring my own fonts and colors?expand_more

Yes. Every token is a CSS custom property, so you can re-ink the whole site — palette, dots, radii and shadows — from a single stylesheet. Bangers and Archivo Black ship as defaults.

Can I cancel my press pass anytime?expand_more

Anytime, in two clicks, no call to the circulation desk. You keep every edition you've published, and we refund the current month if you ask within 14 days.
