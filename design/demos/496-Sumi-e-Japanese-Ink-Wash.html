Frequently Asked Questions

What is sumi‑e journaling? expand_more

It combines traditional ink‑wash painting with reflective writing, helping you capture thoughts in a meditative flow.

Do I need any special equipment? expand_more

No. The web app works on any modern browser; a stylus or mouse is enough to simulate brush strokes.

Is my data private? expand_more

Yes. All entries are stored locally in your browser unless you opt‑in to sync.
