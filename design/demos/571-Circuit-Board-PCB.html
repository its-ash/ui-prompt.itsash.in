// DATASHEET

Frequently asked questions

What file formats do you accept?expand_more

We accept Gerber RS-274X, ODB++, and IPC-2581. Files are validated on upload, and any parse errors are reported with the exact layer and line reference.

How fast is the fastest turnaround?expand_more

Production tier orders of two to four layer boards can ship in 72 hours from file approval. Larger layer counts and special finishes add one to three days.

Do you assemble components as well?expand_more

Yes. Upload your BOM and pick-and-place files with your board order, and we source parts, assemble, and functionally test before shipping.
