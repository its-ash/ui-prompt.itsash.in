Questions from the waking world

Answers, as plainly as a dream can manage.

Is my data safe inside a dream?expand_more

Yes. Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, and your workspaces belong to you alone. Export or delete them at any time, no questions asked.

Can I use Nimbus with my team?expand_more

The Lucid and Deep Sleep tiers include shared dreamscapes where teammates can enter the same workspace, leave notes, and pass tasks through doorways between projects.

What happens if I cancel?expand_more

Your account gently returns to the Daydream tier. Nothing is deleted for 90 days, and you can export everything before you drift away.
