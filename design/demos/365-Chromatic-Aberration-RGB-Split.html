// 06 Troubleshoot

Frequently asked frequencies

Will Nimbus work with our existing tools?expand_more

Yes — Nimbus auto-detects over 40 carriers, including Slack, Teams, Discord and email. Connect once and every channel tunes in.

What happens when we hit the rewind limit?expand_more

Nothing breaks. Older broadcasts stay archived on Static; you just lose scrub-and-recover beyond your window. Upgrade any time, retroactively.

Is the signal really private?expand_more

Every transmission is encrypted end-to-end on a zero-knowledge carrier. Not even our engineers can tune into your channel.
