Questions From the Living

Answers, before you ask them in the dark.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?expand_more

Yes. Cancel from your account page in two clicks. Your access continues until the end of the current billing moon, and we never ask why you are leaving.

Is there a refund if the dead do not deliver?expand_more

If anything goes wrong in your first fourteen nights, write to us and we will refund the full amount. No forms, no interrogation, no curses attached.

Which devices can enter the gate?expand_more

Hollowgate runs in every modern browser, on iOS and Android, and on most smart televisions. If your screen glows, we can probably haunt it.

Is the content suitable for younger souls?expand_more

Our catalog is curated for adults and carries clear age ratings on every title. The Necromancer tier includes separate profiles with content locks.

How do I reach support after midnight?expand_more

Our keepers answer email around the clock, usually within a few hours. Acolyte and Necromancer members receive priority in the queue.
