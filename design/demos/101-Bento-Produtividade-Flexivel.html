Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you build.

How customizable is my workspace, really?expand_more

Every board, widget, and view in BentoFlow is a module. Resize cards, switch between kanban, calendar, and table views, and save unlimited custom layouts. Free plans get 3 boards; Pro removes all limits.

Does BentoFlow integrate with the tools I already use?expand_more

Yes — native integrations include Google Calendar, Slack, and GitHub, plus one-click CSV import and an open REST API on Pro and Team plans.

Can I take my data with me if I leave?expand_more

Always. Export any workspace to JSON or CSV in one click — no lock-in, no export fees, no waiting period.
