Yes. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypt data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), and support role-based access with regional data residency.

Which tools do you integrate with?expand_more

Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Postgres, dbt, and Kafka, plus 60+ connectors for Stripe, Segment, Salesforce, and more — all configurable in minutes.

Can I switch or cancel my plan at any time?expand_more

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your billing cycle. Export everything as CSV or Parquet anytime.
