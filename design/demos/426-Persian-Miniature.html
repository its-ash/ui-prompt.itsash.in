Pigments of the craft

Three disciplines, drawn from the ateliers of Herat and Tabriz, shape every page we produce.

ink_pen

Fine Linework

Our artists render each scene with reed-pen precision, layering contour and detail the way court painters built a pavilion line by line.

palette

Mineral Palette

Lapis, vermillion, turquoise, and gold leaf are mixed to historical recipes, then graded for screens so the jewel tones stay true.

auto_stories

Bound Volumes

Finished tales are gathered into navigable digital manuscripts with islimi borders, chapter frontispieces, and a colophon page.

From telling to binding

A commission moves through three stages, each reviewed with you before the next begins.

  1. 1

    Share the Tale

    Send your story, recordings, or letters. Our scribes transcribe and shape a scene-by-scene plan.

  2. 2

    Watch It Painted

    Review pencil compositions, approve the palette, and follow each panel as pigment and gold are laid down.

  3. 3

    Receive the Volume

    Your illuminated manuscript arrives as an interactive volume, ready to read aloud or gift.

Questions from the court

How long does a commission take?expand_more

A Single Folio takes about three weeks. Chapter Volumes run six to eight weeks, and a Royal Epic is scheduled across a full season with monthly reviews.

Can you work from photographs or recordings?expand_more

Yes. Our scribes transcribe audio, and our artists use photographs as likeness references for court figures, dress, and settings before stylizing them into miniature form.

Do I own the finished manuscript?expand_more

You receive full personal and display rights, including print reproduction for family and institutional use. Gulistan retains only the right to show the work in our portfolio with your consent.
