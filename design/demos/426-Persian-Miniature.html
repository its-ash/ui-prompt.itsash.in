Questions from the Caravanserai

Answers for travelers considering the illuminated path.

Do I need painting experience to use Naqqash?expand_more

None at all. The atelier guides composition, pigment and linework for you, while leaving every decision in your hands if you want finer control.

Can I print my illuminations as physical books?expand_more

Yes. Court Painter and Atelier tiers export press-ready folios with proper bleed and color profiles, suitable for fine art printing and binding.

What happens to my work if I cancel?expand_more

Your manuscripts remain yours. You can export every folio before leaving, and we keep your archive readable for twelve months after cancellation.
