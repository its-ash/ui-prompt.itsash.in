Frequently asked questions

Everything else you need to know before your first scene.

Will Nimbus slow my site down?expand_more
No. Nimbus only animates GPU-accelerated properties like transform and opacity, and every scene ships with a frame budget. Products using Nimbus average a 98+ Lighthouse performance score.
How does Nimbus handle accessibility?expand_more
Every generated scene includes a reduced-motion variant, preserves tab order, and passes automated contrast checks. Nimbus targets WCAG 2.2 AA from the first commit — not as an afterthought.
Does Nimbus work with my framework?expand_more
Yes. Nimbus ships adapters for React, Vue, Svelte, and vanilla HTML/CSS, and scenes export as dependency-free code. The runtime stays under 12KB gzipped.
