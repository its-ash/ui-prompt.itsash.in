questions, answered

what exactly is blaze?expand_more

blaze is a hosted platform for running ai models through one simple api. the community publishes models, we run the infrastructure, and you call everything with a single request.

how does usage pricing work?expand_more

you pay per second of gpu time while your request is running, billed by the exact hardware each model needs. when nothing is running, you pay nothing.

can i use model outputs commercially?expand_more

in most cases yes. each model page lists its license clearly, and the majority of popular models on blaze allow commercial use of their outputs.
