Inquiries

Questions, Answered in the Round

Are the motifs historically accurate?expand_more

Every pattern is redrawn from measured surveys, plaster casts, and surviving carved surfaces. Each plate cites the building, the date, and the survey method — authenticity is a matter of record, not resemblance.

Can I use the files in commercial work?expand_more

The Atelier and Master Craftsman tiers carry full commercial licenses for print, product, and architectural use. The Apprentice tier is reserved for study and portfolio pieces.

What formats do you deliver?expand_more

SVG for the screen, DXF for the machine, and print-ready PDF plates at full scale — plus carving guides with tool-path notes for hand work.
