Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up Nimbus?expand_more

Most teams run their first board within ten minutes. Connect your tools, pick a workflow template, and invite your team — no implementation project required.

Can we migrate data from our current tools?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus imports projects, tasks, and attachments from the major trackers via CSV or our API. Enterprise plans include a guided migration by our onboarding team.

What happens to our data if we cancel?expand_more

You keep full ownership. Export boards, logs, and files as CSV or PDF at any time — including after cancellation.
