How an Expedition Runs

Three movements, from charting hall to christening toast.

  1. 1

    Calibrate the Orrery

    Deliver your destination to the charting hall. Our geometers set the great orrery turning and lock your route to the prevailing aether currents.

  2. 2

    Board the Skyship

    Step aboard at Guildhall Pier 9. Stow your trunk, take your berth, and watch the mooring chains released at first dusk.

  3. 3

    Chart the Nebula

    Through the observation dome you sketch, name, and log your nebula — then toast its christening in the officers’ mess.

Questions Before the Void

Answers from the Guild’s purser office.

Is aether travel safe?expand_more

Remarkably so. Every skyship sails with triple-redundant pressure shells and two licensed aether engineers. The Guild has lost no vessel since the incident of 1901, which we do not discuss.

What should I pack?expand_more

Sturdy boots, a warm coat, and no more than one trunk. Goggles, logbook, and brass spectacles are issued at the gangway; ink is provided.

How long is a typical expedition?expand_more

Most charting runs last twelve days. Deep-drift expeditions to the Orion Spur run six weeks, with letters ferried home weekly by dispatch skiff.

Can I cancel my passage?expand_more

Full refund up to seven days before departure; half refund thereafter. Expeditions cancelled for void weather are rebooked or refunded in full.

Are the skyships accessible?expand_more

Guildhall Pier 9 and all berths are step-free. Speaking-tube service reaches the bridge from every deck, and the observation dome offers tactile chart rails.
