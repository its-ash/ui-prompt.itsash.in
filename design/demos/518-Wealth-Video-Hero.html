Common questions

Straight answers before you commit a single dollar.

Is my money protected if Aurum fails?expand_more
Yes. Client assets are held with an independent custodian in your name, covered by investor protection schemes up to statutory limits. Aurum never holds your cash directly.
What is the minimum to open an account?expand_more
Essential starts at $5,000, Signature at $100,000 and Private at $1,000,000. You can begin with a smaller deposit and a monthly contribution plan.
Can I withdraw my money at any time?expand_more
Yes. Portfolios hold liquid funds, so withdrawals typically settle within three business days. There are no exit fees or lock-in periods on any tier.
