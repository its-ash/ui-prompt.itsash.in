Does Pulse work with my stack?expand_more
Yes. Pulse ships as framework-agnostic web components plus optional wrappers for React, Vue and Svelte. If it runs in a browser, it runs Pulse.
Will micro-animations slow my app down?expand_more
No. Every interaction runs on GPU-friendly transforms inside a strict 100ms budget, and our CLI audits your bundle for timing violations before you ship.
