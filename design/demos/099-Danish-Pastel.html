Soft questions, honest answers

Is the free plan actually free forever?expand_more

Yep, zero dollars. Cozy Starter gives you three moodboards and the core pastel palette for as long as you like — we only charge for the extra-fancy stuff.

Can I share a moodboard with my roommate?expand_more

On Pastel Pro and Maximalist you can invite up to five people to a shared board, so you can argue about cushions in real time.

Can I cancel Pastel Pro whenever?expand_more

Anytime, in two taps, no guilt-trip screens. You keep Pro until the end of the month you already paid for.
