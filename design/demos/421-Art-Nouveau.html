Questions

From the potting shed

How long does a commission take?expand_more

Most pieces take four to eight weeks, since we work with live specimens and seasonal pressings. Conservatory commissions follow a full growing season by design.

Can I choose the plants in my piece?expand_more

Absolutely. During the consultation we walk the greenhouses together and select flora with meaning for you, whether a birth flower, a wedding bloom, or a beloved herb.

Do you ship internationally?expand_more

We ship paper works worldwide in archival packaging. Living companions can travel within the UK and EU due to plant health regulations.
