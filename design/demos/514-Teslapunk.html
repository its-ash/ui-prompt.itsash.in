Common Inquiries

Answers from the demonstration ledger.

Is wireless current safe around children and pets?expand_more

Yes. The Arc Governor holds the field well below the threshold of a parlor static spark, and every installation passes a supervised discharge test before we leave the premises.

How far does the field reach?expand_more

A single Manor Array coil fills roughly four thousand square feet. Larger estates and exhibition halls chain additional coils, tuned to the same resonant frequency.

What happens during a thunderstorm?expand_more

The governor grounds itself automatically when it senses atmospheric charge, then resumes transmission once the air clears. Your lamps barely flicker.
