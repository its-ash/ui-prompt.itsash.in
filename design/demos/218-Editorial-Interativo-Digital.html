Questions From the Margin

What exactly is Nimbus?add

Nimbus is a digital book: twelve historically curated journeys, typeset with editorial care and annotated so that sources, maps, and portraits sit a tap away from the prose.

Do I need an internet connection?add

No. Volumes download complete — margins, maps and all — so flights, trains, and dead zones read exactly like the study.

Is the history reliable?add

Each chapter is drafted by narrative historians, reviewed by an independent academic board, and footnoted to primary sources you can open yourself.

Which devices are supported?add

Any modern browser on phone, tablet, laptop, or e-reader. Your library, notes, and bookmarks sync across them all.

Can I gift a plan or cancel later?add

Both. Collector plans can be gifted with a personal bookplate, and cancellations keep your notes — they export cleanly, always.

The Harbor

The harbor at dawn kept two ledgers: one written in ink for the customs house, one written in salt for the gulls. Miriam had learned to read both — and to trust only the second.

Every Nimbus chapter opens this way — parchment, provenance, and a question waiting in the margin.
