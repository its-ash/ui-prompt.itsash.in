Words From the Table

Questions, Answered

Is the festival family friendly?

Absolutely. Children under 8 enter free, the Little Chefs pavilion runs supervised cooking classes all day, and the forró stage welcomes dancers of every age.

Are there vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes — more than half of our fifty stalls offer plant-based dishes, from grilled hearts of palm to a vegan bobó made with banana blossom.

What happens if it rains?

The festival sits under the covered Copacabana Pavilion, so the party continues rain or shine. Free ponchos are handed out at every entrance.
