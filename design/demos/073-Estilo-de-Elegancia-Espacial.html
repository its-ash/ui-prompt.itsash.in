When does Nimbus Halo ship?expand_more

Pre-orders ship in three waves beginning March 2026, allocated strictly by reservation order. We will confirm your exact delivery window by email before charging anything.

Does Halo work with my existing devices?expand_more

Yes. Halo mirrors macOS, Windows, iOS and Android over Wi-Fi Direct, and any Miracast-capable display appears as a spatial panel without extra hardware.

Can I return it if spatial isn't for me?expand_more

Every Nimbus comes with a 30-day, no-questions return window and free return shipping across the US, UK and EU. Refunds are issued within five business days of pickup.
