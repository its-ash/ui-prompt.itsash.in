FAQ

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Simply point your domain’s DNS to our hosting and we’ll handle SSL automatically.

Is there a limit on traffic?

No. All plans include unlimited bandwidth and scalable infrastructure.

How do I update the gradient colors?

Edit the CSS variables --primary and --secondary in :root or the .dark class.
