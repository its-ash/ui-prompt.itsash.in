How a project runs

A tight three-phase process. You always know what happens next and what it costs.

01

Listen

A working session about your audience, your market, and what you want people to feel. No brief theater.

02

Build

Two distinct creative directions, presented with real mockups. You pick one, we refine it together.

03

Launch

Final files, usage guidelines, and thirty days of support while your team rolls the work out.

Questions, answered

The three things every prospective client asks before signing.

How far ahead should I book?expand_more
I take on two projects at a time and typically book four to six weeks out. Rush slots open occasionally; ask and I will tell you honestly.
Who owns the final artwork?expand_more
You do, in full, once the final invoice is paid. I keep the right to show the work in my portfolio unless we agree otherwise in writing.
Do you work with startups on a budget?expand_more
The Spark package exists exactly for that. If even that is a stretch, I will point you to templates and designers who fit your stage.
