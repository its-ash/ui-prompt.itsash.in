Do I need a drawing tablet to use Nimbus?expand_more

Nope — Nimbus works great with mouse and keyboard, but pressure-sensitive tablets unlock the full Live Ink Engine.

Yes. Nimbus imports layered PSD and CBZ files up to 500 MB and keeps every layer intact.
