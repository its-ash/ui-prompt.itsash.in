Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? expand_more
Enter your email on the login page and we’ll send a reset link within minutes. If you don’t see it, check your spam folder.
Can I use Nimbus offline? expand_more
Yes! Sketch locally and changes sync when you’re back online. Your work is never lost.
What file formats can I export? expand_more
Export as PNG, SVG, or PDF. You can also copy any sketch to the clipboard.
