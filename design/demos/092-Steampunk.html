Inquiries & Particulars

The questions most often wired to our office.

Do you truly ship by airship?expand_more

Every automaton travels crated in straw aboard our freight dirigibles, insured against piracy and inclement weather alike. Landlubber couriers are available on request.

What does the warranty cover?expand_more

All clockwork, rivets, and aether seals are covered against defect for the term of your tier. Accidental hat-related damage is repaired at half price.

Is the aether motor safe?expand_more

Certified by the Royal Aether Board. Our dynamo emits less stray aether than a summer thunderstorm and considerably less than your uncle’s moustache wax.

May I return or customize my automaton?expand_more

Bespoke commissions are yours to tailor utterly. Stock chassis may be returned within thirty days — crated, wound down, and politely apologized to.
