FAQ

P601 What is teletext and why use it for team status?
Teletext is a vintage broadcast information system. We adapt its focused, distraction-free interface for modern team communication—no ads, no algorithms, just clear status updates.
P602 Is my data secure with VIEWDATA?
All transmissions use end-to-end encryption. We store minimal metadata and never sell your data. Our infrastructure is SOC 2 Type II certified.
P603 Can I integrate with our existing tools?
Yes! We offer webhooks for Slack, Microsoft Teams, and custom APIs. View our developer docs for full integration guides.
