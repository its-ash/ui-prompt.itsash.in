Frequently Asked Questions

How do I care for my knitwear? expand_more
Hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting. Store folded to maintain shape.
What is your sizing like? expand_more
Our pieces are designed with a relaxed, comfortable fit. Refer to the size chart on each product page for exact measurements. When in doubt, size up for a cozier drape.
How long does shipping take? expand_more
Orders are processed within 2-3 business days. Standard shipping takes 3-5 days within the US. International shipping varies by location.
What is your return policy? expand_more
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unworn items with original tags. Please contact us to initiate a return.
Do you offer gift wrapping? expand_more
Yes! Add gift wrapping at checkout for $5. Includes a handwritten note and reusable fabric bag.
