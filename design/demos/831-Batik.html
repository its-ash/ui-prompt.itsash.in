Hand‑drawn in wax · Dyed in indigo

Timeless Batik, Hand‑drawn with Wax

Each piece is created using the traditional tulis technique, where artisans apply hot wax by hand before immersing the cloth in natural indigo vats. The result is a unique textile that carries centuries of Javanese heritage.

Craftsmanship

gesture

Hand‑drawn tulis

Artisans use a copper stamp canting to draw intricate patterns directly onto the cloth, preserving the authentic batik process.

water_drop

Natural indigo dye

Fabrics are dyed in small batches using sustainably harvested indigo, producing deep, luminous blues that age gracefully.

Court heritage patterns

Motifs inspired by Javanese palace designs convey status, philosophy, and protection, making each cloth a story.

The Process

01
gesture

Draw the Wax

Artisans apply hot wax in freehand patterns using a canting tool.

02
water_drop

Crackle & Dye

The wax resists dye, creating the characteristic crackle; the cloth is then bathed in indigo.

03
flash_on

Boil & Reveal

Wax is removed with boiling water, revealing the final pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions

chevron_right How should I care for my batik cloth?
Hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Avoid direct sunlight when drying to preserve the indigo depth.
chevron_right Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide. Shipping costs and customs duties are calculated at checkout.
chevron_right Can I request a custom motif?
Absolutely. Our commission process lets you work directly with an artisan to create a unique design.
chevron_right What is the lead time for a commission?
Typically 6–8 weeks, depending on complexity and artisan availability.
