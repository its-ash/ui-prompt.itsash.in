Questions

From the Enquiry Desk

How long does a commission take? expand_more

Most pieces spend four to eight weeks at the bench. Wax approvals add time only if a revision is requested — we give a firm date at the drawing stage, and we keep it.

Which metals and stones do you work in? expand_more

Sterling silver, eighteen-karat gold, rose gold, and responsibly sourced diamonds and pearls. If you dream in another material, ask — our refiners are remarkably accommodating.

Can I restore an inherited piece instead? expand_more

Gladly. Send photographs or bring the piece to the atelier. We assess it free of charge and return a written restoration plan within one week.
