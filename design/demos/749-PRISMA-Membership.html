Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before joining.

How does the digital card work?expand_more

Your card is issued instantly after signup and lives in your phone wallet and member dashboard. Show it or tap it at partner checkouts to apply member pricing automatically.

Can I switch or cancel my plan?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle and there are no cancellation fees.

Which partners are included?expand_more

The network covers productivity apps, travel booking, fitness, food delivery, and retail. The full directory is available in the member dashboard and grows every quarter.

Is there a free trial?expand_more

Every plan starts with a 14-day trial with full access. We send a reminder two days before the first charge, and you can cancel in one click.

What does the concierge actually do?expand_more

Plus and Founders members get a direct line for booking help, refund disputes, event planning, and partner recommendations. Weekday responses are guaranteed within four hours.
