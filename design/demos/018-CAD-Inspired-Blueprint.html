Frequently asked questions

Which CAD formats does DraftLine support?
We natively read SolidWorks, Onshape, Fusion 360, and STEP/AP242 files, plus DWG drawing sets. New formats ship monthly.
Can I control who sees which revisions?
Yes. Role-based permissions govern every workspace, and released drawings can be watermarked and locked against export.
How does DraftLine handle large assemblies?
Models stream as lightweight tessellations for review, so 10,000-part assemblies open in seconds without a workstation GPU.
Do you offer an API?
A REST and webhook API ships on every paid tier, covering releases, BOM sync, and automation triggers for your PLM.
