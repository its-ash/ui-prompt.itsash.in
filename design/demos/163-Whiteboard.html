Squiggly questions, straight answers

Everything people ask before they pick up a marker.

Do I need an account to scribble?+

Nope. Board owners share a link and guests can draw right away — you only sign up when you want to save or own a board.

Can I bring my existing boards over?+

Yes. Paste images, import sticky notes from a CSV, or drag in old exports. Full project migration lands this spring.

Is Nimbus free for classrooms?+

It is. Verify with a school email and your whole class gets Studio features free, forever — markers included.
