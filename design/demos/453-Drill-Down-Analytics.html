Frequently asked questions

How deep can a drill path go?expand_more

Scout supports two levels. Analyst and Enterprise have no depth limit, and the breadcrumb trail grows with each step so you always know where you are.

Do filters survive when I drill down?expand_more

Yes. Date ranges, segments, and custom filters persist at every level and are restored when you navigate back.

Which data sources are supported?expand_more

Postgres, Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, MySQL, and any REST API. CSV upload is available on every plan.

Can I share a drilled-down view?expand_more

Every view has a shareable link that encodes the full drill path, so recipients land on exactly the level you were viewing.

Is there a free trial?expand_more

Yes, 14 days on the Analyst plan with no credit card required. You can downgrade to Scout at any time.
