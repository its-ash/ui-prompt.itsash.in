05 / Briefing

Pre-flight questions

How far in advance should I reserve a window?expand_more

Dedicated windows open on the public manifest up to nine months out. A standard slot holds for 14 days, then releases to the rideshare queue.

Do you fly rideshare payloads?expand_more

Yes. Every vehicle flies a secondary deck. Payloads from 25 kg can book a rideshare slot with standard separation services included.

What mass and orbit can each vehicle carry?expand_more

Up to 500 kg to low Earth orbit and 1,200 kg on escape trajectories. Mid-inclination, polar, and sun-synchronous insertions are all standard.

What happens if weather scrubs the launch?expand_more

Scrubs are free. Your payload stays integrated and your window slides to the next available slot at no additional cost.

Is licensing and range safety included?expand_more

Yes. Every flight price covers range coordination, licensing, baseline insurance, and downrange telemetry.
