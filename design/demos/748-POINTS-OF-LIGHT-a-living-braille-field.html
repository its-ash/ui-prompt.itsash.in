FAQ

How does the braille field work?

The system converts visual interfaces into tactile point grids that can be read by fingertip.

Is it compatible with screen readers?

Yes; the point grid is announced as structured text, and the UI is fully keyboard operable.

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari on desktop and mobile.
