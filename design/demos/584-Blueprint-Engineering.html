Reference Notes

Common questions

Does Datum replace our existing CAD tools?expand_more

No. Datum sits between concept and fabrication. Import geometry from any major CAD format, then dimension, validate, and release from one controlled sheet.

How does revision locking work?expand_more

Once a revision is stamped, it becomes read-only. New work happens on the next revision, and every change is logged with author, timestamp, and reason.

Can we try it before committing?expand_more

Yes. Every tier includes a 30-day pilot with full features. Your data exports cleanly if you decide not to continue.
