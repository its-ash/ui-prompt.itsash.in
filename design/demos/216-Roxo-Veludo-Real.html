QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clarity on the essentials of Velvet Royale membership.

What is included in the membership fee? expand_more

Your membership fee grants access to our curated collections, lifestyle credits, concierge services, and invitations to exclusive events. Specific inclusions vary by tier—see our pricing page for detailed breakdowns.

How are lifestyle credits allocated and used? expand_more

Credits are deposited monthly into your account and can be applied toward experiences, purchases from our collections, or travel arrangements. Unused credits roll over for up to three months.

What is the cancellation policy? expand_more

You may cancel your membership at any time through your account settings or by contacting support. Access continues until the end of your billing cycle, with no prorated refunds for partial months.

Can I gift a membership to someone else? expand_more

Yes! Gift memberships are available for all tiers. Simply select the gift option during checkout and provide the recipient’s details. They’ll receive a personalized welcome package.

How do I access concierge services? expand_more

Your concierge is available via email, phone, or in-app messaging. Response times are within one hour during business hours (9 AM–6 PM EST, Monday–Friday).
