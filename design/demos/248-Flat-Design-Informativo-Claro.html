Frequently asked questions

Short answers, no runaround.

Is ClearPost really ad-free?chevron_down

Yes — on every plan, including the free Reader tier. No banners, no sponsored content, no trackers. ClearPost is funded entirely by subscriptions.

Where do your articles come from?chevron_down

We license and summarize reporting from 400+ vetted wire services, national and regional outlets, and specialist publications. Every summary links back to the original story.

Can I read offline?chevron_down

Pro and Newsroom plans sync briefings and saved articles for offline reading on mobile and e-ink devices. Your progress syncs back automatically.

How do you handle corrections?chevron_down

Corrections are appended to the original summary within 24 hours and listed in a public changelog — you always see what changed and why.

Can I cancel anytime?chevron_down

Yes. Plans renew monthly or yearly, and you keep access until the end of your billing period. No retention calls, no dark patterns.
