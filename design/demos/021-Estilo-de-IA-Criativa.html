Frequently asked questions

Do I own the art I create?expand_more

Yes. Everything you render on any plan is yours to use — including full commercial rights on paid tiers. Your work is never resold or reused by us.

Can Chromaflow match my existing style?expand_more

Upload a handful of reference pieces and Studio or Pro plans train a private style model in minutes — your look stays yours and is never shared publicly.

What can I upload?expand_more

Sketches, line art, and photos up to 25MP, in PNG, JPG, or PSD. Files are processed in-memory and are never used to train public models.
