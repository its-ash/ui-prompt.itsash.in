Questions from the dock

What harbormasters ask us before they sign on.

Do turbines harm marine life?expand_more
Our rotors turn at tidal speed, well below the threshold that harms fish or mammals. Every site runs a two-year ecological monitoring program, and results are published openly for the cooperative.
Who owns the equipment and the revenue?expand_more
The local cooperative does. Tidalpunk supplies hardware, training and maintenance under a service agreement, while power sales, kelp harvests and reef credits stay with the member towns.
What happens when a storm hits?expand_more
Platforms are built from salvaged hull steel rated for severe seas, and turbines feather automatically in rough water. Crews receive storm protocols and a 24-hour support line during their first year.
