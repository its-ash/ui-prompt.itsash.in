The Process

From Concept to Chase

  1. I

    Design & Punchcutting

    After consultation, our punchcutter refines the design, then engraves the master punch in softened steel before hardening and tempering.

  2. II

    Matrix & Casting

    The punch strikes a copper matrix, which is fixed to our hand-caster. Molten type metal is poured, yielding a fresh sort.

  3. III

    Composition & Presswork

    Sorts are selected, justified, and locked in the chase. The forme is rolled, pressed, and lifted to reveal the impression.
