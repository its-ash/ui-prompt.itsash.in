05

Asked at the edges

Do I need a braille display to read a field?expand_more

No. The embosser prints on paper — the raised, light-responsive dots are readable by any trained fingertip, no electronics required.

How does the field stay alive?expand_more

A photochromic layer softens and raises each dot with ambient light, producing a slow pulse that readers describe as breathing.

Is it usable by deafblind readers?expand_more

Yes. Fields are purely tactile, and every interface ships with a motion-free, high-contrast profile.

Can I try it before paying?expand_more

The Dot plan is free forever, and every paid tier adds a 30-day field trial — no card required.

Which languages and notations are supported?expand_more

140 languages, plus Nemeth math, music braille, and code notation packs on paid plans.
