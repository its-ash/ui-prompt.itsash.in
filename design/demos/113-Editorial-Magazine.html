Questions, answered

Frequently asked

Can I cancel at any time?expand_more

Yes — two clicks from your account, no phone calls and no retention theatre. Your access runs to the end of the period you have paid for.

Where does the print edition ship?expand_more

Worldwide, in a compostable sleeve, usually within five working days. Postage is included in Patron and Folio plans.

Can I give a subscription as a gift?expand_more

Gladly. Choose any plan and mark it as a gift at checkout; the recipient picks a start date and we enclose a letterpress card with your note.
