Inbox — 05

Spec-sheet questions

What does 20K/20K actually mean?

expand_more

The membrane holds back 20,000 mm of hydrostatic pressure — heavy-storm level — while moving 20,000 g of water vapor per m² per 24 hours, so you stay dry from the outside in and the inside out.

Is the DWR finish PFAS-free?

expand_more

Yes. Every shell ships with a fluorocarbon-free DWR, and re-proofing kits are included with lifetime repairs so the beading effect lasts seasons, not months.

How does sizing work for layering?

expand_more

Shells are patterned with room for a mid-layer. If you plan to wear a puffy underneath, size up once. Fit charts list chest measurements taken over a base layer.

What's the repair-for-life policy?

expand_more

Zipper blown in year eight? Torn on a branch? Mail it in and we fix it — or issue store credit if it's beyond saving. No shell goes to landfill on our watch.
