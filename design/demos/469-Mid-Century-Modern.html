Good Questions

Answers before you ask.

Can I change plans later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account settings at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing date, and we never charge cancellation fees.

Is my project data private?expand_more

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share it, and you can export or delete everything with one click from the account panel.

Does the free plan expire?expand_more

No. The Sputnik plan is free forever with no card required. It stays free as long as you like, and your projects carry over if you upgrade.
