Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the sensors? expand_more
TEMPER uses Sensirion SHT45 for temperature/humidity (±0.1°C, ±1.5% RH), Bosch BME688 for pressure/VOC (±0.5 hPa, ±15% IAQ), and Plantower PMS5003 for particulates (±10% @ 1μg/m³).
Does it require a subscription? expand_more
No. All features are available locally on device. Optional cloud backup for historical data is available via one-time purchase.
Where should I place it? expand_more
On a stable surface away from direct sunlight, vents, and heat sources. Ideal height is breathing level (3-5ft). Avoid placing near windows or doors.
