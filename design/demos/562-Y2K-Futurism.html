Questions from the launchpad

Everything else is one message away.

Is ChromeCloud really that fast?expand_more

Yes. Our gel sync layer pushes changes peer-to-peer first and to the cloud in parallel, so most edits land in under a second on a normal connection.

What happens if I cancel?expand_more

Your workspace stays readable for 90 days and you can export everything in open formats. No lock-in, no exit fees.

How secure is the vault?expand_more

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, with per-workspace keys. Galaxy plans add SSO, audit logs, and regional storage pinning.
