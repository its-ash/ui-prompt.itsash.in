How long does it take to get started?expand_more

Most teams open their first chapter within ten minutes. Connect a tool, invite your cast, and Nimbus starts writing the backstory for you.

Can I bring work from other tools?expand_more

Yes — Nimbus speaks fluent integrations. Import projects from the usual suspects and we keep every thread intact, so nothing is lost in translation.

Is my team's story private?expand_more

Completely. Your chapters are encrypted in transit and at rest, and you decide who gets a speaking role. We never narrate your data to anyone else.
