Common questions

Straight answers on rates, coverage and how fast you can start.

How is pricing calculated?expand_more

Rates are quoted per lane based on distance, cargo weight, equipment type and season. Contract plans lock those lane rates for 12 months, so your invoice never surprises you.

Which regions do you cover?expand_more

We operate road freight across North America and the EU, plus ocean and air forwarding to 40 countries through partner terminals at major ports.

Can I track shipments in real time?expand_more

Yes. Every load carries GPS tracking with automatic ETA recalculation. You can follow shipments in our dashboard or stream events into your own TMS via API.

Is my cargo insured?expand_more

Standard carrier liability is included on every load. Full-value cargo insurance is available on all plans and included by default on Dedicated contracts.

How long does onboarding take?expand_more

Most shippers are set up within five business days: credit check, lane rate card, and a test shipment. Dedicated fleet programs take three to four weeks.

Do you handle customs documentation?expand_more

Our in-house brokerage team prepares and files import and export declarations, and our bonded warehouses keep goods moving while paperwork clears.
