Common questions

How is pricing calculated?expand_more

Plans are priced on monthly shipment volume and lane distance. Fuel, tolls and standard insurance are included in the quoted rate, so the invoice matches the proposal.

Which regions do you cover?expand_more

We operate road freight across 40 countries in Europe, with bonded warehousing hubs in Budapest, Rotterdam and Milan. Overseas freight is handled through partner carriers.

Can I track shipments in real time?expand_more

Yes. Every vehicle reports GPS position every 60 seconds. Continental and Enterprise plans include the live dashboard with geofence alerts and automatic ETA updates.

Is my cargo insured?expand_more

All shipments carry CMR liability cover by default. Continental plans include full-value insurance up to €500,000 per load, and higher limits are available on request.

How long does onboarding take?expand_more

Most accounts go from signed contract to first dispatch in 7 to 14 days. Warehousing migrations typically take three to four weeks depending on inventory volume.
