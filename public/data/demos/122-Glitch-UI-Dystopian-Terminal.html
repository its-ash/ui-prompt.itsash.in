Decrypt common questions

Does NULLSEC make me invisible?expand_more

No. It makes you expensive. Traffic is scattered, re-timed and re-signed until tracking you costs more than you are worth. Cheap invisibility is a scam; profitable obscurity is engineering.

What happens if a relay is seized?expand_more

The relay knows nothing. Session keys live ninety seconds, routes dissolve mid-handshake, and the mesh re-routes before the door comes off its hinges. Seizures are scheduled maintenance.

Is “no logs” just a slogan?expand_more

It is an architecture. Nothing is written in the first place, so there is nothing to purge, subpoena or leak. Our warrant canary sings every morning at 06:00 UTC.
