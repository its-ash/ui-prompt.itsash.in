ASKED & ANSWERED

Do I need to know how to code?expand_more

Nope. If you can operate scissors, a photocopier and a stapler, you're overqualified. Nimbus is drag, drop, tape, done — and you can still crack open the raw HTML when you feel like showing off.

Can I leave whenever I want?expand_more

Yes, and we mean it. Export your whole site as plain HTML any time. No export ransom, no "please stay" emails written by a growth team.

Is this secretly some corporate trap?expand_more

Nimbus is four people, a risograph and a grudge against boring websites. Worker-owned, no VC in the pit, and the roadmap is voted on by actual users.
