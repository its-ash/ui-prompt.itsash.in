Practical matters

Questions, answered plainly.

When can I visit?add

Tuesday to Saturday, 11:00–18:00 in Basel and Lisbon. Guided tour every Thursday at 17:00 — no booking required.

How do I acquire a work?add

Reserve online or at the desk. We confirm availability, condition, and final price within one business day.

How are works shipped and insured?add

Museum-standard crating, door-to-door insurance, and tracked delivery to more than forty countries.

What is in the certificate?add

Signed authenticity, the full provenance chain, a condition report, and archive photographs of the work.

Can artists submit portfolios?add

Yes — three to eight works as images or a link, plus a short statement. We review submissions quarterly.

What if a work arrives damaged?add

Tell us within thirty days: full refund or replacement, and the insurer handles the rest.
