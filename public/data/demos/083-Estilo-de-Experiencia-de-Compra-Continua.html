Frequently asked questions

How does Nimbus know when I'm about to run out? expand_more

After your first two orders, Nimbus models your consumption cadence and drafts a reorder before stock hits zero. Optional smart-shelf sensors make it exact to the ounce.

Can I pause, skip, or cancel anytime? expand_more

Yes. Pause for up to 12 weeks, skip a single cycle, or cancel in two taps from the app. No fees, no retention scripts.

Where do autonomous deliveries operate? expand_more

We serve 240+ metro areas across three continents and add new hubs monthly. Check coverage for your address in the app before you subscribe.
