Common questions

How long does a palette take?add

Sample Pots ship in five business days. Studio Batches take two weeks including revisions, and Full Kiln projects run three to four weeks depending on proof rounds.

Do the colors work in dark mode?add

Yes. Every swatch ships with a dark-mode counterpart that keeps the same name and personality, adjusted for contrast on warm charcoal surfaces.

What if our team dislikes the first mix?add

Studio Batch and Full Kiln include structured revision rounds. If the direction is wrong, we remix from the brief rather than nudging hues you already rejected.
