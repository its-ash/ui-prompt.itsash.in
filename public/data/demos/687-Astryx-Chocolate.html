Good questions, honest answers

How is my chocolate shipped in warm weather?expand_more

From May through September we ship in insulated liners with compostable cold packs, and we hold orders over hot weekends so your bars never sit in a warm depot.

Where does your cacao come from?expand_more

Four partner farms in Ecuador, Peru, and Ghana. Each bar lists its farm and harvest year on the back of the wrapper, and farm-gate prices are published on our sourcing page.

Are your bars allergen friendly?expand_more

Our dark bars contain only cacao and cane sugar, but all bars are made in a workshop that handles milk, tree nuts, and gluten, so we cannot guarantee zero traces.
