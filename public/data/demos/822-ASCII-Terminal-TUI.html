Up and running in three commands

From a cold repo to a production deploy in under two minutes.

  1. step-01

    $ crank init

    Detects your stack, writes a 6-line config, and links the project to your account. Nothing is deployed yet.

  2. step-02

    $ crank deploy --dry-run

    Prints the full diff of what would ship: files added, removed, and changed. Pipe it to less if you like reading.

  3. step-03

    $ crank deploy --prod

    Builds, uploads, and cuts over atomically. The old deploy stays warm for instant rollback.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers. Longer ones live in the docs.

Does crankshaft work with my CI pipeline?expand_more

Yes. It is a plain binary, so it runs anywhere a shell runs: GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Buildkite, or a cron job on a forgotten VPS.

What happens if a deploy fails halfway?expand_more

Nothing changes for your users. Deploys are atomic, so a failed upload never touches live traffic. The previous version keeps serving.

Can I self-host the control plane?expand_more

Team tier includes a self-hosted option with the same CLI. Your artifacts and audit logs stay on your own hardware.

How do I delete my account and all data?expand_more

Run crank account delete and confirm. Everything is purged within 24 hours. Prefer a button?

Is there a GUI for the less terminal-inclined?expand_more

A read-only web view exists for sharing deploy status with stakeholders. All writes stay in the CLI, on purpose.
