$ man nimbus-faq

How does Nimbus handle rollbacks?
Each deployment creates an immutable snapshot. Rolling back simply switches the active snapshot, restoring the exact previous state in seconds.
Is my data encrypted in transit and at rest?
Yes. All traffic uses TLS 1.3, and stored artifacts are encrypted with AES‑256‑GCM using per‑project keys.
Can I use Nimbus with self‑hosted Kubernetes?
Absolutely. Provide your kubeconfig and Nimbus will apply Helm manifests or raw YAML files directly to your cluster.
