An editor that lives in the margins

Three quiet tools, each designed to help your sentences breathe rather than bury them under advice.

edit_note

Marginal notes

Suggestions arrive as gentle annotations beside your text, never as interruptions. Accept, adapt, or ignore each one with a keystroke.

record_voice_over

Voice memory

Quill & Ember studies your past work and learns your cadence, so every recommendation sounds like you on your best day.

auto_stories

Draft chapters

Long projects stay organized as chapters with their own tone goals, outlines, and revision history you can revisit anytime.

From blank page to finished draft

A calm three-step rhythm that respects the way real writing actually happens.

01

Bring your words

Paste a draft, import a document, or start fresh. There is no setup ritual and nothing to configure before you begin.

02

Read the margins

Review notes on rhythm, clarity, and structure. Each suggestion explains its reasoning in plain language.

03

Keep what rings true

Accept the edits that serve the piece and dismiss the rest. Your judgment stays final, always.
