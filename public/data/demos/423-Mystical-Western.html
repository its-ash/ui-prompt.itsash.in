Questions From the Campfire

Not at all. Every card and chart comes with a plain-language reading written for beginners, with deeper notes available when you want them.

How is my personal star chart calculated?expand_more

We use your birth date, time, and location to map the sky at your first breath, then update the chart nightly against current planetary positions.
