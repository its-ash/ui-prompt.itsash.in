Scribbled questions

Asked at the whiteboard

Do I need to be good at drawing?expand_more

Not at all. Nimbus is built for wobbly lines. The recognition engine straightens shapes and snaps connectors, so the roughest sketch still produces a clean, readable flow.

Can teammates edit a board at the same time?expand_more

Yes. On Workshop and Studio plans, anyone with the link can draw, move nodes, and leave sticky notes live. Every cursor shows a name, so you always know who is scribbling what.

What happens to my boards if I cancel?expand_more

Your boards stay readable forever and exportable for 90 days after cancellation. We never delete your work without warning, and PNG exports are always free.
