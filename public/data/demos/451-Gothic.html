Whispers & Doubts

Is my archive truly private?expand_more

Yes. Every volume is entombed with end-to-end encryption before it leaves your hands. We hold your trust, never your keys — not even our own acolytes can read what you seal.

What becomes of my records if I break the covenant?expand_more

You may sever the pact at any hour. Your volumes are returned in full, then erased from the crypt within thirty days — no echoes, no lingering shadows.

Can I retrieve what I have sealed away?expand_more

At any hour, from any chamber with a connection. The vigil never lapses, and no volume entrusted to us has ever been lost to the dark.
