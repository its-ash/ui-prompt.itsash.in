01 — Features

Typefaces with a point of view.

Every family starts as an argument about what letters should do — then we draw until the argument wins.

draw

Drawn by hand

No auto-tracing, no shortcuts. Each glyph begins as ink on paper, then gets argued over, tightened and cut at 2000 units.

tune

Built to bend

Weight and optical size ride on variable axes — one file, from whisper to poster, from footnote to façade.

verified

Licensed with clarity

Plain-language licenses for desktop, web and screens. Buy once, read once, ship without a lawyer on speed-dial.

02 — Process

From brief to broadsheet.

Three moves, no mystery — the same route whether you license a family or commission one.

  1. 01

    Send a brief

    Tell us the voice, the audience and the deadline. One page is plenty; a strong opinion is better.

  2. 02

    We draw and test

    Sketches become families. We stress-test every cut at 8pt and 8 metres until nothing snaps.

  3. 03

    You ship it loud

    Variable files, static cuts, web builds and a specimen site — delivered with kerning we obsess over.

05 — FAQ

Asked, answered.

Yes. Every family ships with a free trial cut — the full character set, lightly watermarked — cleared for pitch decks, coursework and mock-ups.

One domain with unlimited traffic, self-hosted WOFF2 files, and no tracking scripts. Subdomains count as one domain, because counting them felt rude.

Every family released since 2022 ships as a variable font with weight and optical-size axes, plus static cuts for apps that still fear the future.
