Deliberate absence

Revisions kept

How a draft is built

A short discipline, repeated. Most writers find the third step is where the work happens.

01

State the claim

One sentence, written plainly, before anything else is allowed on the page.

02

Order the reasons

Arrange the supporting points until each one follows from the last.

03

Remove the rest

Cut what does not serve the claim. The remainder is the draft.

Questions, answered plainly

Why so few formatting options?expand_more

Because formatting is a place to hide. When a paragraph cannot be made to look finished, it must be made to read finished. Bold and italic remain; the rest was removed on purpose.

Can I import documents from other tools?expand_more

Yes. Plain text and Markdown import cleanly. Formatting is stripped on arrival, which most writers find to be a relief rather than a loss.

Where is my work stored?expand_more

On your device first, and in an encrypted sync if you choose it. We cannot read your drafts, and we do not wish to.

Does it work without a connection?expand_more

Entirely. Thought does not require a network, and neither does Cartesian. Sync resumes when a connection returns.

May I cancel at any time?expand_more

At any time, in two clicks, with your drafts exported as plain text. Work made in Cartesian belongs to you in the most portable form we know.
