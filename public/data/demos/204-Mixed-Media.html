sticky notes

Questions, answered

Can I bring my own photos and scans?expand_more

Yes — drag in JPG, PNG, or TIFF files up to 40 MB. Nimbus treats every import like a fresh print, ready to tear, tape, or tint.

Do I need design experience?expand_more

None at all. Start from a blank page or a template; the tools are playful on purpose, and every action can be undone.

Can I export for print?expand_more

Studio and Atelier plans export at 300 dpi with bleed marks, so posters and zines come off the press looking exactly like your screen.
