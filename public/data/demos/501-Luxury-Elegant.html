Enquiries, Answered

How long does a typical commission take?expand_more

A single room runs eight to twelve weeks from first consultation to installation. Whole residences typically take six to nine months, depending on sourcing and any bespoke work.

Do you work outside the United Kingdom?expand_more

Yes. We regularly undertake commissions across Europe, North America, and the Gulf, with our own logistics partners handling transport, customs, and installation.

Can you work with pieces we already own?expand_more

Gladly. Many commissions begin with a client's existing collection. We assess, restore where needed, and compose new schemes around what you already value.
