Questions, answered

Everything you might wonder before your first batch arrives.

Where do you source your botanicals?expand_more

From certified-organic farms we visit each season — Oregon lavender, Andalusian olive oil, shea from a women-led co-op in Ghana. Origins are printed on every label.

Are your products really 100% plant-based?expand_more

Yes — no paraffin, palm oil, or synthetic fragrance. Just cold-pressed oils, essentials, and plant waxes, with full ingredients listed on each wrap.

How does plastic-free shipping work?expand_more

Orders ship in compostable paper mailers with paper tape and wood-wool padding, delivered carbon-neutral. Refill pouches cut packaging waste by 90%.

What is your returns policy?expand_more

Return anything within 60 days — even if it's opened — for a full refund or swap. Harvest members get free return pickup.

How do refills work?expand_more

Send empties back with the prepaid label included in every order; we sanitize and refill them, then credit your next batch.
