CONTINUE? — QUESTIONS

DO I OWN THE SPRITES I MAKE?expand_more

Yes. Every pixel you draw is 100% yours, including commercial use in shipped games. We claim zero rights over your art, and your exports stay yours even if you cancel.

CAN I EXPORT NES-READY TILES?expand_more

On Arcade and Legend plans, yes — export 2bpp CHR tiles and nametables that drop straight into popular homebrew toolchains. Starter exports clean PNGs and GIFs.

HOW DO I CANCEL MY PLAN?expand_more

Press quit anytime from your account screen. Plans are month-to-month, cancel takes two clicks, and nothing you made gets deleted. CONTINUE? is always on the table.
