Vol. 01 — Nº 04 · Help

Frequently asked questions

Is Broadside really bilingual?
Yes. Every edition can be published in two languages side-by-side with synchronized scrolling, independent typography controls, and translation tracking.
Do I need design experience?
Not at all. Broadside includes guided templates for common newspaper formats — broadsheet, tabloid, and magazine — with adjustable grids and rules.
Can I export to print?
Export to print-ready PDF with bleed and crop marks, or XML for professional typesetting systems. All exports honor your baseline grid and hairline rules.
