Interference, answered

What is Channel 47?expand_more

A cooperative relay network that rebuilds lost broadcasts over unused dead-band frequencies. Anyone can tune in; members can transmit.

Why does dark mode feel like night watch?expand_more

Because it is. Use the toggle in the header to switch between the day shift and the night shift; your choice is remembered on this device.

Does it work when the grid is down?expand_more

Yes. Static Uplink routes through battery-backed repeaters on the dead band, so your feed survives blackouts and carrier failures.

Can I cancel my relay?expand_more

Any time, from your console, in two clicks. Your feeds stay live until the end of the paid cycle, then drop back to the free Static tier.

Is my signal private?expand_more

Every transmission is encrypted end to end across the relay. We cannot listen in, and neither can anyone else on the band.
