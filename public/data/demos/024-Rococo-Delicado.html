Polite Inquiries

Questions, Gracefully Answered

How long does a commission take?expand_more

Petite pieces take two to three weeks; Grand Portraits, four to six. A firm timeline accompanies your sketch.

In what formats will I receive my artwork?expand_more

Print-ready PNG and PDF at 300 dpi, with source files on request. Gold-foil prints ship worldwide.

How many revisions are included?expand_more

Each tier includes the rounds listed; further rounds may be added à la carte, and politely.

May I use my artwork commercially?expand_more

Personal use accompanies every commission; full commercial licences ship with the Grand Portrait and above.

Do you design patterns for brands?expand_more

Gladly — pattern suites for packaging, fabric, and porcelain are a house specialty.
