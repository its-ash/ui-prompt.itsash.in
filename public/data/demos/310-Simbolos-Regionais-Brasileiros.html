Questions, Answered

Where do you ship, and how long does it take?expand_more

Worldwide. Brazilian addresses arrive in 3–6 business days; international parcels in 8–15 days — fully tracked and carbon-offset.

How are the artisans paid?expand_more

Fair trade, set by them: every maker receives at least 60% of the sale price, agreed with their cooperative — never by us. Their name and share are printed on each origin card.

Can I return a box or cancel?expand_more

Pause or cancel anytime before your monthly cut-off. Unopened artisan goods can be returned within 30 days, and we cover return shipping.
