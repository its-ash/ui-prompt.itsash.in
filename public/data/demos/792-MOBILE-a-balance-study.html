Questions, answered

The essentials, without the fine print.

What exactly is MOBILE?expand_more

MOBILE is a weekly planning tool modeled on kinetic sculpture. Your commitments hang on a visual beam, and the tool helps you keep that beam level as work shifts.

How does the balance method work?expand_more

You hang the week, weight each task honestly, then slide work along the beam until it rests level. The practice takes about ten minutes a day.

Does it respect my privacy?expand_more

Yes. Your plans stay on your device by default, sync is end-to-end encrypted, and we never sell or inspect your data. Dark mode is included in every tier.
