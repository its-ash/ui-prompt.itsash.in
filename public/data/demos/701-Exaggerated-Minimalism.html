Fair questions.

Does Nimbus replace our project tracker?expand_more

No. Nimbus sits on top of the tools you already use and turns their output into one daily plan. Your tracker stays the source of truth.

How long does setup take?expand_more

Most teams connect their calendar, chat, and tracker in under five minutes. The first ranked queue appears the same morning.

What happens to our data if we cancel?expand_more

You can export everything at any time. After cancellation we keep your data for 30 days, then delete it permanently.

Is there a free trial?expand_more

Yes. Every plan includes 14 days with full features and no credit card required. Downgrade or walk away with one click.

Can Nimbus work for a team of two?expand_more

Absolutely. The Solo plan covers individuals, and teams of any size can mix plans. Capacity planning works from two people upward.
