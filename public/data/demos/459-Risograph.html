How it works

From file to flat in three steps

  1. 01

    Send your separations

    Upload grayscale layers, one per ink. Our preflight checks trapping and coverage within the hour.

  2. 02

    We burn the masters

    Each color gets its own stencil. Expect a little drift between layers; that is the whole point.

  3. 03

    Collate and ship

    We fold, staple, trim, and pack by hand, then send your edition anywhere with tracking.

Common questions

How should I prepare my files?expand_more

Send one grayscale PDF per ink at 600 dpi, with 3 mm bleed. Darker areas mean heavier ink coverage. Our preflight guide covers the rest.

Will my colors match my screen?expand_more

No, and that is the charm. Spot inks are opaque and uncoated paper shifts them. We mail a free ink swatch chart so you can choose with your eyes.

Can I reorder an edition later?expand_more

Yes. We archive your masters for twelve months, so reprints cost less and ship faster. After that we reburn them for a small fee.
