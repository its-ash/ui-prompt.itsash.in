Common questions

Everything else you might want to know before your first flight.

Do viewers need to install anything?expand_more

No. Nimbus spaces run in any modern browser. Share a link and your audience joins instantly, on desktop or mobile, with the full layered experience intact.

Can I import my existing slide decks?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus reads PowerPoint, Keynote, and PDF files, then distributes each section across depth layers. You keep your content and gain the spatial structure on top of it.

What happens when my free trial ends?expand_more

Your spaces stay viewable forever. You keep editing on the Solo plan limits, and nothing is deleted. Upgrade any time and pick up exactly where you left off.
