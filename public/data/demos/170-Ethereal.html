The ritual

From petal to pulse, in three quiet acts.

  1. local_florist
    01

    Gather

    Petals, resins and citrus arrive from six small farms, cut at the hour their oil runs sweetest.

  2. tune
    02

    Compose

    Our perfumer blends by candlelight in batches of forty, weighing every note by hand and memory.

  3. bedtime
    03

    Rest

    The accord sleeps a lunar month in darkness, then is bottled, wax-sealed and numbered.
