Questions, answered

Everything teams usually ask before their first board goes live.

How long does setup actually take?expand_more

Most teams connect a source and see a live dashboard in under ten minutes. Nimbus auto-detects tables and suggests starting metrics, so there is no modeling layer to build first.

Which data sources do you support?expand_more

Postgres, MySQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, Stripe, Segment, and Google Analytics are first-class connectors, plus 120+ more through REST and a flexible CSV importer.

Can I change plans later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrades apply instantly and downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle, with every unused portion prorated automatically.

How is my data secured?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified, and row-level permissions keep sensitive metrics visible only to the right seats.

Do you offer startup or nonprofit discounts?expand_more

Teams under ten people and registered nonprofits get 50% off their first year. Write to hello@nimbus.app from your work email and we will apply it to your account.

What happens if I hit my plan limits?expand_more

We never drop data or pause pipelines. You get a notification at 80% usage and a seven-day grace period to upgrade while everything keeps flowing.
