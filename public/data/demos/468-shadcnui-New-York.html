Three steps to your first preview

Connect a repository and Relay handles builds, environments, and teardown automatically.

01

Connect your repo

Install the Relay GitHub or GitLab app. We detect your framework and build settings automatically.

02

Open a pull request

Relay builds the branch, seeds the database, and posts the preview URL as a PR comment.

03

Review and merge

Your team reviews the live preview. On merge, the environment is torn down and logs archived.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a preview take to build?expand_more

Most previews are live in under a minute. Relay caches your dependencies and build layers, so repeat builds on the same branch are nearly instant.

Can I use my own database seed?expand_more

Yes. You can upload a sanitized snapshot or point Relay at a seed script in your repo. Each preview gets an isolated copy that resets on every rebuild.

What happens to previews after a merge?expand_more

Environments are torn down automatically when the pull request closes. Build logs and review threads are archived and searchable for 90 days.
