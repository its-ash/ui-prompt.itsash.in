Questions, answered

The essentials before you clear your desk for good.

Which headsets work with Aether?expand_more

Aether runs on all major spatial headsets released since 2023, plus Aether Glasses. A companion mode also works on laptops and tablets with a depth camera.

Do my pinned windows stay in place between sessions?expand_more

Yes. Aether anchors windows to your physical room and restores them on your next session, even after you move furniture. You can save multiple room profiles.

Can I try it before paying?expand_more

Every plan starts with a 14-day trial with full features. No card is required up front, and you can export your layouts if you decide to leave.
