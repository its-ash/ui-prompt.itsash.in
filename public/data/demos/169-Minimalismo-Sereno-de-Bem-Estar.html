FAQ

Gentle answers to common questions

I've never meditated before — is Stillpoint for me?expand_more

Absolutely. Most sessions assume no experience at all, and our three-minute beginner path teaches the basics one breath at a time.

How much time do I need each day?expand_more

Three minutes is enough to begin. Many members build to ten or fifteen, but consistency matters far more than duration.

Is there a free trial?expand_more

Yes — 14 days of Serenity, free, with no credit card required. The Breathe plan also stays free forever.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes, in two taps from your settings. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Can I listen offline?expand_more

Serenity and Studio plans include offline mode, so calm travels with you — on flights, hikes, or quiet cabins.
