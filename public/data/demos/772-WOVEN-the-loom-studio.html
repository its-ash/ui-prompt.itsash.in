How It Works

01

Warp the Loom

Set up your loom with the foundation threads. We guide you through each step so the base is perfectly tensioned.

02

Weave the Pattern

Choose a design, select your colors, and watch your pattern emerge row by row. Endless possibilities.

03

Finish & Take Home

Trim, wash, and mount your textile. Walk out with a completed piece ready to hang or gift.
