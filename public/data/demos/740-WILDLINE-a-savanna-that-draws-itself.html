Questions from the trail

What exactly does WILDLINE export?expand_more

Every scene exports as resolution-independent SVG for print and web, or as a looping MP4 of the full draw-in. Explorer and Cartographer exports carry no watermark.

Do I need illustration skills?expand_more

No. You choose a horizon, a palette, and a mood. WILDLINE composes and draws the scene. If you do illustrate, you can import your own stroke styles on Cartographer.

Can I use the artwork commercially?expand_more

Yes. Paid tiers include a full commercial license for client work, products, and advertising. The free Wanderer tier is for personal projects.

How long does a scene take to draw?expand_more

A typical scene finishes in 20 to 60 seconds depending on wildlife density. You can scrub the timeline or export the still at any point.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes. Plans are month to month, and annual plans refund the unused months. Your exported files remain yours forever.
