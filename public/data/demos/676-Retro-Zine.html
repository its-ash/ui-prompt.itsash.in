Frequently Asked

How does the riso engine work?

It separates your artwork into spot‑color layers, simulates ink overlap, and outputs a print‑ready PDF with registration marks.

Can I use my own fonts?

Yes. Upload OTF/TTF files; they’ll embed in the export PDF for offline printing.

Is there a trial?

All plans include a 7‑day free trial. No credit card required to start.
