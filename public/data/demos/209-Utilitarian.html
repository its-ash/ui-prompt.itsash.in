SEC/06 // FAQ
Q-01Does Nimbus ship an agent?

Yes. One static binary per node, roughly 8 MB, no runtime dependencies. It opens a single outbound connection to your region endpoint.

Q-02Where is my data stored?

In the region you select at sign-up: EU-WEST or US-EAST. Records are append-only and exportable as plain text at any time.

Q-03What happens during an outage?

The status record is the source of truth. Each node keeps a local buffer and back-fills the record when connectivity returns.

Q-04Can I export everything?

Yes. Logs, metrics, and audit trails export as line-based text or JSON. No proprietary formats, no lock-in.
