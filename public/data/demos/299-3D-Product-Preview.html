Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you support? expand_more
We support GLB and glTF 2.0 formats. Other formats like OBJ, FBX, or STL can be converted using our free online tool.
How does this affect my site's performance? expand_more
Our viewer loads asynchronously and uses progressive enhancement. Initial load is under 500KB on average, with intelligent LOD for complex models.
Which devices support AR placement? expand_more
AR works on iOS Safari (AR Quick Look), Android Chrome (WebXR), and Microsoft Edge (WebXR). Older devices fall back to 3D viewer.
Is there a limit to model complexity? expand_more
We recommend models under 5MB for optimal performance. Our optimization pipeline automatically simplifies geometry and compresses textures.
How do I handle pricing and inventory updates? expand_more
Orbitly syncs with your product catalog via webhooks or scheduled imports. Changes reflect in previews within minutes.
Is the platform accessible and WCAG compliant? expand_more
Yes. Keyboard navigation, screen reader support, sufficient color contrast, and reduced motion preferences are all implemented.
