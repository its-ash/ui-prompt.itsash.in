Frequently Asked Questions

What browsers support the audio engine?
All modern browsers that support the Web Audio API (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). The gracefully degrades to visual‑only feedback if audio is unavailable.
Can I disable the sounds?
Yes. Use the floating mute button or the theme toggle’s audio preference. Your choice is saved in localStorage.
Is the page accessible?
Absolutely. All interactions have visual equivalents, sufficient contrast in both themes, full keyboard navigation, and ARIA labels.
