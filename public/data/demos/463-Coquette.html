Frequently Asked, Softly Answered

Can I include a handwritten love note?expand_more

Yes — every parcel carries a note penned on cotton paper. Add your message at checkout and our scribes will write it in flowing script.

How do I care for pressed florals?expand_more

Keep them out of direct sun and away from humidity. Each bloom is sealed under a glass-clear film to hold its blush for years.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?expand_more

Anytime, with no hard feelings. Pause from your account page or cancel with one gentle click before your next billing date.
