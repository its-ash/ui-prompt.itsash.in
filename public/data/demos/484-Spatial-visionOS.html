Common Questions

What hardware is required? expand_more
Aether is optimized for the latest spatial headsets and high-end AR glasses. It requires a device with depth-sensing cameras.
Is my spatial data private? expand_more
Yes. All room meshes are processed locally on your device. Only app coordinates are synced to the cloud with end-to-end encryption.
Can I use my existing 2D apps? expand_more
Absolutely. Aether provides a "Glass Window" wrapper that allows any standard 2D application to float in your 3D environment.
