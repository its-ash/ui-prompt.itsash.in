FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my bank data safe?expand_more

Yes. FinSecure encrypts everything with 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest, connects to your banks read-only, and requires two-factor authentication. We never store your banking passwords.

Do you sell my data?expand_more

Never. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers. FinSecure is funded entirely by subscriptions.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes — cancel in two clicks from Settings. You keep full access until the end of your billing period and can export your data at any time.
