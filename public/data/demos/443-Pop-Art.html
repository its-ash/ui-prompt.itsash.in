Is the free plan really free?expand_more

Yes. Solo costs nothing forever, with 3 boards and 5 teammates. No card required, no trial countdown.

Can I switch plans later?expand_more

Anytime. Upgrades apply instantly and downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle. Your boards stay intact either way.

Absolutely. Marketing, events, and operations teams use the same boards. Templates cover launches, campaigns, and editorial calendars out of the box.
