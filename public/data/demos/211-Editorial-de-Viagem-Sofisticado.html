Before You Pack

What exactly is Nimbus?expand_more

An editorial journal and a membership for discerning travellers: original essays and field notes, plus journey blueprints our hosts can turn into real itineraries.

Can I gift a membership?expand_more

Yes. Every tier arrives as a letterpress card with a handwritten note, posted anywhere in the world within two days.

Do you organise the journeys you write about?expand_more

We compose and support them for Voyager and Connoisseur members, working through our own hosts and local correspondents in each region.

Can I cancel my membership?expand_more

Anytime, in two clicks from your account — no calls, no questions. Access continues to the end of the paid year.

Is the print edition included?expand_more

Voyager and Connoisseur members receive the printed journal twice a year, posted worldwide at no extra cost.
