Still curious? Send us a transmission.

Will Silence ever hide an urgent signal?expand_more

No. Urgency thresholds are yours to set, and anything crossing them bypasses filtration instantly — with a clear escalation trail, never a quiet drop.

How long until the field goes quiet?expand_more

Most teams connect their first channel in under ten minutes. The filters need about a week of listening before the noise settles into stillness.

Can I export what the array captures?expand_more

Always. Every raw transmission is archived and exportable as JSON or CSV. Your signals remain yours — silence is only a lens.
