Frequently asked questions

Still curious? Write to sales@nimbus.example.

How does Nimbus keep our data secure?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are SOC 2 Type II certified, support SSO and SCIM provisioning, and every action is written to an immutable audit log you can export anytime.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrades apply instantly and are prorated automatically. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your billing cycle — you can trigger them yourself from Settings → Billing, with no retention calls.

Which tools does Nimbus integrate with?expand_more

More than 120 native integrations, including Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, and NetSuite. Anything not covered can be wired through our documented REST API and webhooks.
