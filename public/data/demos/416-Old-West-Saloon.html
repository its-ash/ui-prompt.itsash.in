Questions Posed at the Bar

Do I need to know any bookkeeping?expand_more

None at all. If you can write a price on a scrap of paper, you can post an invoice — the Ledger handles the totaling, numbering, and filing for you.

How do my clients pay me?expand_more

Every invoice carries a payment link. Clients settle by card or bank transfer, the money lands in your account, and the bill marks itself paid. No double entry, ever.

Can I ride off whenever I like?expand_more

Any time, no questions asked. There are no contracts and no cancellation fee, and you can export every record in one click before you go.
