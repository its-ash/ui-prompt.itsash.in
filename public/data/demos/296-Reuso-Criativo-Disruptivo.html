FAQ

Sticky questions, straight answers

What materials can I trade?expand_more

Wood offcuts, glass jars, textiles, pallets, clean e-waste, and most things dry, clean, and legal. Every listing carries a safety tag, and our no-go list keeps the scary stuff out of the loop.

Do I need DIY experience to join?expand_more

Nope. Guides are rated Rookie to Rad, and swap meets always have a veteran who has already made your mistake for you. Start with a bottle lamp and climb from there.

How do pickup and delivery work?expand_more

Donors drop materials at neighborhood hubs and makers collect locally, usually within a bike ride. Maker and Studio plans add scheduled pickup for big loads within 30 miles.
