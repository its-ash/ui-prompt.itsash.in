Frequently asked questions

Can I try Nimbus for free?expand_more

Yes — every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with full features. No credit card required, and you can downgrade to Free at any time.

How does annual billing work?expand_more

Annual plans are billed once a year at a 20% discount per user. You can switch between monthly and annual whenever you like, and we prorate the difference.

Can I migrate from Jira or Trello?expand_more

Our built-in importer moves projects, issues, and attachments across in minutes, with guided field mapping and a preview before anything is committed.

Is my data secure?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and offers SSO/SAML plus granular permissions on every plan.
