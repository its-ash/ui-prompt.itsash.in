Questions

Before you commission.

How long will my piece take? expand_more
Collection pieces are made to order in 8–12 weeks, depending on wood availability; full commissions take 14–20 weeks. You receive atelier photographs at every stage — drying, joinery, upholstery, and finishing.
Which woods and leathers do you use, and how do I care for them? expand_more
Only certified native species — freijó, cumaru, and reclaimed jacarandá — paired with vegetable-tanned saddle leather. Keep pieces out of direct sun, dust with a dry cloth, and apply the natural wax from your care kit once or twice a year.
How do authenticity, delivery, and restoration work? expand_more
Each piece is numbered and signed by its author and ships with a certificate of authorship. Delivery is white-glove and fully insured across Brazil and abroad, and our atelier restores any Robusto piece for as long as it exists — owners pay only materials and travel.
