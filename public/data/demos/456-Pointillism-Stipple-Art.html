Questions, answered dot by dot

What exactly is stippling?expand_more

Stippling builds an image from thousands of individual dots. Tone comes from density: more dots for shadow, fewer for light. Stipple automates that placement while keeping the hand-made character.

Which file formats can I upload?expand_more

JPG, PNG, and WebP up to 40 megapixels. Exports are available as PNG, SVG, and print-ready TIFF on paid plans.

Can I use the artwork commercially?expand_more

Yes. Studio and Atelier plans include a full commercial license for client work, merchandise, and print runs of any size.

Does it run on older computers?expand_more

The engine uses your GPU when available and falls back gracefully otherwise. A 12-megapixel photo dotifies in a few seconds on most laptops from the last six years.

What is the refund policy?expand_more

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. After that, cancel within 30 days of any charge for a full refund, no questions asked.

Is there a dark mode?expand_more

Yes, both this site and the editor follow your system preference, and you can switch anytime with the toggle in the header.
