FAQ

Questions about heatmap analytics

How do I configure a custom color scale?expand_more

Open the scale editor on any board and choose sequential (cool→hot) or divergent (±) mode. Drag breakpoints, lock domain bounds, and preview both light and dark themes — every palette is checked against WCAG AA before you can publish.

How does HeatGrid perform on large datasets?expand_more

Cells are GPU-rendered in tiled canvases, so a 1M-cell board draws at 60 fps in the browser. Server-side rollups and incremental streaming keep updates under 200 ms without shipping raw rows to the client.

Can I export or embed my boards?expand_more

Yes — export a PNG or SVG snapshot, download the underlying cells as CSV, or embed an interactive board via a signed iframe snippet. Scheduled email digests ship on Pro and above.
