Frequently asked

Straight answers, no coded language.

No honest system claims to be unbreakable. ENIGMA uses three layered AES-256 rotations with per-message key advancement, which means a compromised key exposes exactly one message, never the archive. Our threat model and third-party audit reports are published in full.

ENIGMA is zero-knowledge: we never see or store your keys, so we cannot recover them. During setup you receive a printed recovery rotor sheet. Store it somewhere physical and safe. If both are lost, the messages stay sealed permanently.
