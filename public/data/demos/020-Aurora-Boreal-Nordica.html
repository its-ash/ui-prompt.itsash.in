helpFAQ

Questions from the ice

How accurate are the forecasts?

expand_more

During active geomagnetic periods Nimbus predicts visible aurora with roughly 88% precision on a 3-hour horizon. In quiet-sun weeks confidence drops — and we show that confidence on every forecast card.

Does it work below the Arctic Circle?

expand_more

Yes. During strong storms (Kp 5+) the auroral oval pushes south, and Nimbus will alert you whenever your latitude has a realistic chance — even at 50°N.

Is there a free tier?

expand_more

Explorer is free forever: hourly forecasts and three saved locations. Push alerts and the hyperlocal cloud model live in Voyager.
