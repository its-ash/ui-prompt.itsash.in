Frequently Asked

Your questions, answered

Quick answers to help you glide through.

How do I choose my size?

We recommend measuring your bust, waist, and hips and comparing to our size chart. If you’re between sizes, size up for a relaxed drape or down for a more sculpted fit.

How should I care for tulle and satin pieces?

Hand wash cold with gentle detergent, lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing or direct sunlight to preserve the delicate fibers and color.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. You can pause or cancel anytime from your account page. Changes take effect at the end of the billing cycle.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping fees and delivery times vary by destination; see our shipping policy for details.

What is your exchange policy?

Unworn items with original tags can be exchanged within 14 days of delivery. Please contact us to initiate an exchange.
