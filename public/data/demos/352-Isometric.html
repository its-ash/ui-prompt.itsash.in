FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is Diorama only for software teams?expand_more

Not at all. Anything with parts and dependencies maps well — customers plan games, marketing campaigns, facility moves, even film productions. If you can draw a block for it, Diorama can track it.

Can I import my existing tasks?expand_more

Yes. CSV, Jira, Linear, and Trello imports take minutes, and every item lands on the grid already extruded, with owners and due dates preserved.

Does it work offline?expand_more

Scenes cache locally, so you can keep editing on a plane. When you reconnect, changes merge block by block — conflicts glow red and are never silently overwritten.

What counts as an editor?expand_more

Anyone who creates, moves, or edits blocks. Viewers — stakeholders, clients, execs — are free and unlimited on every plan, and you can swap editors any time mid-cycle.
