Before you ask

The questions we hear most often at the counter.

Do I need to install anything?expand_more

Nope. Nimbus runs right in your browser — open a tab, grab a board, and start scribbling. Nothing to download, nothing to configure.

Can I invite my whole team?expand_more

Any plan can host guests for a look-around. Counter Seat covers one regular, Corner Booth seats a small crew, and Whole Café throws the doors open with unlimited teammates and SSO.

Is my work safe if I spill coffee on my laptop?expand_more

Every stroke saves to the cloud the moment you make it, and you can export boards anytime. Your ideas will survive the spill — we can’t promise the laptop will.
