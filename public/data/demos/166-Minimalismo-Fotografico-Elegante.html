Questions

Before you write

How soon can we schedule a session?expand_more

We usually book four to six weeks ahead. Autumn and December fill fastest; weekday sittings are quieter, and easier to place at short notice.

When will the photographs be delivered?expand_more

Your private gallery arrives within fourteen days of the session, with a suggested sequence and print sizes. Rush delivery is possible for a small additional fee.

May I print and share the images?expand_more

Yes. Every commission includes personal printing and sharing rights. For commercial use we license images individually, always on plain terms.
