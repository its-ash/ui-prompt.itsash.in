FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does setup take?expand_more
Most teams connect their tools and launch a first workspace in under ten minutes. Guided importers map your existing projects automatically.
Can I migrate data from my current tools?expand_more
Yes — one-click importers cover the 40 most common ops tools, and a CSV mapper handles the rest. Nimbus mirrors first and cuts over only when you are ready.
Is my data secure with Nimbus?expand_more
Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, with encryption in transit and at rest. Choose EU or US data residency on every paid plan.
