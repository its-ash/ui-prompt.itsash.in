FAQ

Questions we get before the first call

How does a project usually run?expand_more

Direction locks in week one, an animatic lands by week two, and final frames roll straight after sign-off. You review everything on live render calls — no surprise endings.

How many revisions are included?expand_more

Every tier includes at least two structured rounds, and Studio adds a third. Extra rounds are billed at a flat day rate, quoted up front.

What formats do you deliver?expand_more

Masters in ProRes 4444 and 4K H.265, plus vertical and square social cuts. EXR sequences ship on request, and usage rights are baked into every deliverable.
