Quick answers

Is Nimbus just another project tool?expand_more

Nope — it's a project tool with a sound system. Serious muscle underneath (timelines, permissions, integrations), but the interface refuses to bore you.

Can my whole team try it free?expand_more

Yes. Spark is free forever for up to two teammates, and every paid plan starts with a 14-day full-volume trial — no card required.

What happens if I hit my plan limits?expand_more

Nothing breaks. You get a friendly nudge and a one-click upgrade path, and your boards keep working exactly as they were.
