FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Do I need an account?

expand_more

For the free plan, no. Notes stay on your device. Sync plans need an email address, and that's all we ask for.

Can I get my notes out?

expand_more

Yes. Any note exports as plain text at any time, and your whole account can be downloaded as a single file.

How do I cancel?

expand_more

Press the cancel button in settings. The plan ends at the close of the month. No phone call, no survey.

Start a note.

Put in your email and we'll send a link. That's the whole process.
