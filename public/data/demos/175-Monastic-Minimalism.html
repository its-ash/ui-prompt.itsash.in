Method

How a session runs

  1. 01

    Arrive

    Open one file. Name the session in a single line, then let everything else wait.

  2. 02

    Remain

    Write in a full-screen room. The only instrument on the wall is the clock, and it moves slowly.

  3. 03

    Close

    The hour seals the session by itself. Tomorrow, the page waits exactly where you left it.

Questions

Asked, answered

Where are my drafts stored?expand_more

On your machine, as plain text. There is no cloud, no account, and no second copy of your words anywhere else.

Does it work offline?expand_more

Entirely. Stillpoint runs locally, and even updates wait until a session has closed before they install.

Can I leave the Cloister plan?expand_more

Anytime, from settings. Your files stay yours, so there is nothing to cancel, recover, or regret.
