Questions, Answered

The practical side of planning a cultural journey in Brazil.

How large are the groups?expand_more

Small by design: never more than 10 travelers per guide, so dinners stay around one table and conversations stay personal.

Which languages do the guides speak?expand_more

Every guide speaks fluent Portuguese and English; many add Spanish, French, German or Japanese. Tell us your preference when booking and we will match accordingly.

Is Brazil safe for cultural travel?expand_more

We follow Ministry of Tourism guidance, use licensed drivers only, and every itinerary includes a 24/7 local support line you can call day or night.

Can I cancel or reschedule?expand_more

Yes — free date changes up to 21 days before departure, and a full refund up to 30 days out. Festival-ticketed events follow the venue's policy.

Are the experiences accessible?expand_more

Many are. Share your mobility, hearing or dietary needs when booking and we will confirm accessible routes, seating and kitchens before you pay.
