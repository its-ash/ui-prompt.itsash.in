The Method

Admission, in three movements.

  1. 01

    Write Your Letter

    Tell us, in one paragraph, the book that ruined you for ordinary reading. There are no wrong answers — only dull ones.

  2. 02

    Receive Your Syllabus

    Within a week, a fellow curates a reading list for your temperament: three texts a season, annotated to your pace.

  3. 03

    Take Your Seat

    Join your circle each week, attend the Sunday lecture, and let the syllabus do what syllabi do — change you slowly.

Enquiries

Questions, answered at leisure.

Must I be a scholar to apply?expand_more

No. We admit on the strength of your letter, not your credentials. Our fellows include a plumber, two accountants, and at least one lapsed lawyer.

How much time does membership ask?expand_more

One evening a week for your circle, and an hour on Sunday for the lecture. The reading itself is paced to a season, not a semester.

Are the texts available offline?expand_more

Yes. Every edition can be downloaded in full, so your library travels with you — countryside, coast, or anywhere the signal fails.

May I gift a membership?expand_more

Gladly. A gift membership arrives as a sealed letter with the recipient's name in gold ink — or its digital equivalent, which is considerably less dramatic.

What if I wish to withdraw?expand_more

Send one line to the registrar and it is done. Dues are refunded pro rata, and your place is held for a year, should you miss the lamplight.

Final Call

The lamp is lit. Take your seat.

Leave your address and we will send the season's reading list — three texts chosen for you — and an invitation to the next circle.
