Vehicle Systems

Engineered for repeat flight

Every subsystem is built to fly again within days, not months. Three core systems carry every mission.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should we book a launch?add

Dedicated payload missions typically book nine to twelve months out. Constellation customers on multi-launch agreements can hold rolling windows with ninety days of notice.

What orbits can your vehicles reach?add

Our current fleet serves low Earth orbit, sun-synchronous orbit, and geostationary transfer orbit. Lunar injection profiles are in certification and open to early manifest partners.

How many times can a booster be reflown?add

Each first stage is rated for twenty flights between major refurbishments. Our fleet leaders have passed fifteen recoveries with turnaround times under ten days.

What happens if a launch is delayed by weather?add

Your slot rolls to the next available window at no cost. Payloads remain in our climate-controlled integration facility, and your mission manager confirms a revised trajectory within 24 hours.

Do you support rideshare payloads?add

Yes. Quarterly rideshare missions accept payloads from 50 kg upward, with standardized dispenser ports and published separation schedules so small operators can plan with confidence.

Is crewed flight open to private astronauts?add

Private crew missions are available through the Crewed plan. Participants complete a twelve-week training program covering vehicle systems, emergency procedures, and microgravity operations.
