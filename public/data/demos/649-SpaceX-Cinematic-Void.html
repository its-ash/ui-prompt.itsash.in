COMMON QUESTIONS

MISSION SUPPORT

Standard lead time is 8-12 weeks for the Essential tier. Premium tier offers 4-6 weeks with priority scheduling. Enterprise tier timelines vary based on vehicle customization requirements.
We support inclinations from 0° to polar (90°) including sun-synchronous orbits. Custom inclinations require Enterprise tier engagement for trajectory analysis and vehicle adjustments.
Integration occurs at our certified facility in Texas. We provide mechanical, electrical, and environmental testing including vibration, acoustic, and thermal vacuum tests as required by your mission specifications.
We maintain a 10-day launch window reservation. Weather delays within this window are accommodated at no additional cost. Extensions beyond the window may require rescheduling fees depending on tier and vehicle availability.
Nimbus provides standard launch liability coverage up to $500M. Additional coverage can be arranged through third-party providers. We recommend customers obtain their own payload insurance for full value protection.
