Questions, Answered

Everything about origins, shipping and how artisans get paid.

How do you verify a product's origin?expand_more

Each partner files a provenance dossier — workshop or farm, technique and maker — which our regional curators verify in person and by video before listing.

Which regions of Brazil do you cover?expand_more

All five: North, Northeast, Central-West, Southeast and South — from Pará's rivers to the pampas of Rio Grande do Sul.

How long does shipping take?expand_more

Orders leave São Paulo within 48 hours; tracked delivery reaches most countries in 5–12 business days.

Can I return food items?expand_more

Yes — unopened food items can be returned within 30 days, and anything damaged or off is replaced or refunded immediately.

How much do artisans earn?expand_more

Artisans and cooperatives keep 72% of every sale, and we publish our revenue-share report every six months.

Can I visit the cooperatives?expand_more

Collector members receive an annual invitation to visit partner cooperatives — small trips hosted by the artisans themselves.
