Squishy questions

Is Puff really free?expand_more

Yes. The Free plan — 3 boards, nudges, and a week of history — stays free forever, with no credit card required. Upgrade only when you want unlimited squish.

Will dark mode hurt my eyes at 3 a.m.?expand_more

Never. Puff’s dark theme is recalibrated by hand — shadows are retuned, not inverted — so every card stays just as puffy at midnight. Try the toggle in the header.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Anytime, with one press of a (very soft) button in Settings. You keep Pro until your billing period ends, and you can export all your data as JSON.
