Common questions

Everything else you might want to know before your first dive.

Do I need any equipment to use Pelagic?expand_more

No. Pelagic runs entirely in the browser on any laptop, tablet, or phone. All dives are virtual and guided.

Is the content scientifically accurate?expand_more

Yes. Every lesson is reviewed by working marine biologists, and species data is sourced from peer-reviewed research.

What ages is Pelagic designed for?expand_more

Core lessons suit ages 8 and up. The Abyss plan adds classroom tools aimed at middle and high school science programs.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?expand_more

Yes. Cancel in two clicks from your account page. You keep access until the end of your billing period.

Does Pelagic work offline?expand_more

Downloaded lessons and your dive log work offline. Live dive sessions and new content require a connection.
