Frequently asked questions

The practical details clients ask about before signing a brief.

How long does a typical project take?expand_more

A Starter animation ships in about three weeks. Studio films run five to seven weeks, and Enterprise campaigns are scheduled per deliverable with a shared production calendar.

How many revision rounds are included?expand_more

Starter includes two rounds, Studio three, and Enterprise unlimited within the agreed scope. Revisions are consolidated through one point of contact to keep feedback actionable.

What formats do you deliver?expand_more

ProRes and H.264 masters in 4K or 8K, plus aspect-ratio versions for 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16. Cinema DCP and broadcast-safe masters are included at the Enterprise tier.

How is pricing structured?expand_more

Each package is a fixed-scope, fixed-price engagement. Fifty percent is due at kickoff and the balance on final delivery. Out-of-scope requests are quoted before any work begins.

Who owns the final work?expand_more

You receive full worldwide usage rights for the delivered masters in perpetuity. Framelix retains the right to show the work in its portfolio unless an NDA states otherwise.
