Ask the void

Do I need a VR headset?expand_more

No. AETHERTRIBE renders in any modern browser and shines on a plain screen. A headset deepens immersion for shared rites, but every tool — visuals, pattern studio, live spaces — works with zero extra hardware.

What does the free Initiate tier include?expand_more

Three audio-reactive presets, the pattern studio with ten saved masks, and solo ritual rooms. No time limit, no credit card. Upgrade only when you want to open the circle to others.

Can I export my generative patterns?expand_more

Yes. Shaman and Elder tiers export stills as PNG, loops as GIF, and full sessions with audio as WebM. The work is yours — print it, project it, sell it.

What kind of audio does the engine react to?expand_more

Any signal your device can hear: microphones, audio files, live streams, or MIDI. The engine tracks tempo, transients, and texture, so drum circles and modular synths both map naturally to motion.

Can I run rituals with people in other countries?expand_more

That is the point. Shared spaces host 64 initiates on Shaman and 500 on Elder, with beat sync relayed across continents in under 100 milliseconds.
