Common questions

How contained are the nanites during a build?expand_more

All assembly happens inside a triple-sealed chamber with electrostatic containment and a solvent purge cycle. Nanites are chemically keyed to the feedstock and inert outside the chamber, so nothing self-replicates or escapes.

What materials can the platform fabricate?expand_more

The standard library covers titanium and aluminum lattice alloys, conductive copper meshes, and two families of phase-change polymers. Lab and Foundry tiers add shape-memory alloys and biocompatible ceramics for implant work.

How does build certification work?expand_more

Each layer is hashed with its sensor readings as it completes. The final part ships with a signed record you can verify against our public ledger, giving regulators and customers a complete, tamper-proof manufacturing history.
