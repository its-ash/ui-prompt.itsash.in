Frequently asked questions

How does Nimbus collect evidence without exposing our data?expand_more

Every integration is read-only and scoped, all traffic is TLS 1.3 encrypted, and credentials live in a hardware-backed vault. Nimbus staff cannot view your data.

Which frameworks and standards do you support?expand_more

SOC 2 Type I and II, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS — mapped automatically from more than 300 shared controls, so one control update satisfies multiple audits.

Can we change or cancel our plan later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time from the billing console. Annual plans are refunded pro rata during the first 60 days.
