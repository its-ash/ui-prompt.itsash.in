Frequently drilled questions

The basics, answered. Anything deeper lives in the docs.

How deep can I drill?expand_more

All the way. Every chart is query-backed, so a drill is just a narrower query — from company totals to a single row, with no exports.

Does drilling change my filters?expand_more

Never. Your date range and filters carry through every level and stay visible in the breadcrumb, so any drill is one Back click away.

Does Nimbus work with my warehouse?expand_more

Nimbus connects read-only to Snowflake, BigQuery, Postgres, and Redshift. Your data never leaves your warehouse.

Is there a free plan?expand_more

Yes — Explorer is free forever and includes unlimited drilling across three dashboards.

Can I change plans later?expand_more

Any time. Upgrades apply instantly; downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle. No sales calls required.
