Pressing Questions

Answers from the curatorial desk, dusted weekly.

Is it really safe to press the buttons?expand_more
Perfectly safe. Every specimen is rated for ten million presses and inspected monthly by our conservators. The only recorded injury was a case of mild euphoria in 2023.
What happens if I press a button twice?expand_more
The counter goes up by two. Our buttons are debounced, dignified, and entirely unbothered by enthusiasm.
Is the museum accessible?expand_more
Yes. Every exhibit is fully keyboard operable, screen-reader friendly, and respects reduced-motion preferences. The museum believes a button you cannot reach is just wall decor.
Do the exhibits work in the dark?expand_more
Beautifully. Flip the toggle in the navigation and the whole gallery switches to its after-hours lighting. The Neon Touch wing insists it looks better this way.
Can I get a refund on a press?expand_more
All presses are final. However, if a press fails to spark joy, the admissions desk will issue one complimentary replacement press, no questions asked.
