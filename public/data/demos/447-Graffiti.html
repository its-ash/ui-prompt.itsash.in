Straight Answers

No fine print, no gallery politics.

Do I need to be a pro artist to join?expand_more

Not at all. Toys, kings and total beginners share the same walls. The tutorials and practice yard get you bombing with confidence in an afternoon.

Who owns the art I make on Spraywall?expand_more

You do, always. Every piece you paint is yours to export, print, sell or delete. We never claim rights to your work.

Can I use Spraywall on a tablet?expand_more

Yes. The wall is fully pressure-sensitive on iPad and Android tablets with stylus support, and it runs in any modern browser.

How do crew battles work?expand_more

Crews of 3 to 8 artists get a theme and 72 hours to paint a shared wall. The community votes on style, technique and concept. Winners take the homepage spot.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?expand_more

One click, no questions. Your walls stay live on the free tier and your exports remain yours forever.
