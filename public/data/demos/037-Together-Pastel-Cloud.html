FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which models can I deploy on Together?expand_more

Any weights you own or license: open-weight models like Llama, Mistral, and Qwen, plus your own fine-tunes. Bring safetensors, a container, or a Hugging Face repo — we handle the rest.

How does billing work?expand_more

You pay a monthly rate for reserved capacity and per-request pricing for serverless inference. Annual plans save 20%, and unused serverless credits roll over for 90 days.

Is my data private?expand_more

Yes. Prompts and completions are never used for training, are encrypted in transit and at rest, and can be pinned to single-region processing for compliance workloads.
