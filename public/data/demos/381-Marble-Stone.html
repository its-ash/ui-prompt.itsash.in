Questions

Before you commission

How long does a typical commission take?expand_more

Six to twelve weeks from slab selection to final polish. Reserve-block quarrying for rare statuary can extend to sixteen — and we will say so at the first meeting.

Is marble practical for a working kitchen?expand_more

Yes, with the right finish. We recommend honed surfaces for kitchens: they forgive acid etching and wear like a patina rather than a scar. Every commission includes a care manual and the first sealing visit.

Do you install outside your region?expand_more

We crate, insure, and ship worldwide, and our own fitters travel with the stone. We never use third-party installers — the hands that cut it set it.

Can you match stone already in my home?expand_more

Send photographs taken in raking light and we will search our archived quarry lots. We hold records of every block since 1987, so matches are found more often than you might expect.

What does the aftercare programme include?expand_more

Annual inspection, resealing on schedule, and priority restoration — for the life of the stone. It is written into every contract at no additional cost.
