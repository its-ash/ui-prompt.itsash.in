Questions, answered

The essentials before you lay your first tile.

What is a tile wall?

A tile wall is a shared modular canvas built from azulejo-sized tiles. Each tile carries a pattern — walls can be private, collaborative, or open to the public gallery.

Can I collaborate in real time?

Yes. Artista plans include live co-op for up to 20 artists with instant sync — cursors, edits, and chat on one shared wall.

Do I own my creations?

You keep full ownership of everything you paint. Tessela claims no rights over your art, and every export is watermark-free.
