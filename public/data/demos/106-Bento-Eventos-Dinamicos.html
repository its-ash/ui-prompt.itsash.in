Frequently asked

The quick answers, before you ask.

How does Bento pick events for me?expand_more

Your grid is built from categories you follow, cities you pick, and events your friends save. The more you browse, the sharper it gets — no noisy feeds.

Are tickets refunded if an event cancels?expand_more

Yes. Every purchase includes automatic refunds to your original payment method within 5 business days if a show is canceled or rescheduled beyond a 48-hour window.

Can I transfer a ticket to a friend?expand_more

Tap any ticket in your wallet, hit "Transfer," and send it by link or SMS. It re-registers to their account instantly and is valid at the door.

Does Bento work in my city?expand_more

We're live in 140+ cities across North America, Europe, and Latin America, with new cities added monthly. Free users can browse anywhere, anytime.

What's the difference between Pro and VIP?expand_more

Pro removes ticket fees and unlocks presales. VIP adds front-row seat alerts and a human concierge who can source tickets to sold-out shows for you.
