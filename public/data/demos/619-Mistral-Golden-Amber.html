Questions, answered

The essentials before you commit.

Where is my data processed?add
All inference and fine-tuning run in EU data centers by default. Enterprise plans can pin workloads to a specific country or to your own VPC.
Can I keep my fine-tuned models?add
Yes. Adapters you train are yours. Export them at any time and run them on open weights wherever you like, with no export fees.
What happens when I exceed my token quota?add
Nothing breaks. Requests continue at metered overage rates and you receive an alert at 80% usage, so there are no surprise invoices.
