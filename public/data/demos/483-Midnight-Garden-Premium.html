Questions, answered

The details people ask about before their first bottle arrives.

Is the ritual suitable for sensitive skin?expand_more

Yes. Every formula is fragrance-free by default and patch-tested on reactive skin panels. If your skin is highly reactive, start with the serum every other night for the first two weeks.

How does the returnable glass program work?expand_more

Each order includes a prepaid return label. Send back three or more empties and we credit $8 to your account. Returned bottles are sanitized and refilled in the next small batch.

When will I see results?expand_more

Most people notice softer morning skin within the first week. Barrier repair and tone evening typically take four to six weeks of consistent nightly use.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?expand_more

Anytime, from your account page, in two clicks. Pausing holds your refill pricing and batch access for up to six months.

Where do you ship?expand_more

We ship across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the EU. Orders over $40 ship free; smaller orders carry a flat $5 rate.
