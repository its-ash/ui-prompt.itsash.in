Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

What is Nimbus?

Nimbus is an API-first fintech platform that enables marketplaces and SaaS companies to embed payments, compliance, and payouts without building infrastructure from scratch.

How do payouts work?

Payouts are processed through our real-time settlement engine. Funds arrive in recipient accounts within seconds, with automatic tax reporting and reconciliation logs.

Is Nimbus PCI compliant?

Yes. Nimbus is PCI DSS Level 1 certified. All card data is tokenized at ingestion, and we maintain strict segregation of payment environments.
