How it works

1

Check the tide window

Open the app and see the exact low-tide window for your chosen coastline. Plan your walk before the water returns.

2

Chart your pool walk

Use the interactive map to plot your route, mark entry points, and note estimated exposure time based on tide height.

3

Log what you find

After your walk, log species, photos, and observations. Contribute to a growing community knowledge base.
