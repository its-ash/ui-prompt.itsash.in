FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does setup take?expand_more

Most teams connect their first tools and see a live flow map in under 15 minutes. Guided setup handles OAuth and credentials, and ready-made templates cover the most common stacks.

Does Nimbus work with my existing stack?expand_more

Nimbus ships with 60+ native integrations plus a REST API and webhooks for anything custom. If a service can send an event, it can be a node on your schematic.

Can I change plans later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from workspace settings — billing is prorated automatically, and downgrades never delete your flows or history.
