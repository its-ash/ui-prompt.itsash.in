Questions, Answered

Everything worth knowing before the first toast.

How quickly can I host my first event?expand_more

Most hosts publish their first event within a single afternoon. The guided setup walks you through venue, guests, and schedule in order.

Can I change tiers later?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account page. Changes take effect on your next billing date with no penalty.

Is my guest data kept private?expand_more

Absolutely. Guest lists are encrypted at rest, never sold, and exportable or deletable whenever you wish.

What does the concierge line cover?expand_more

Gilded and Grand members reach a live specialist for planning advice, vendor coordination, and urgent day-of changes.

Do you offer a trial?expand_more

Every tier begins with fourteen days at no charge. No card is required until you decide to stay.
