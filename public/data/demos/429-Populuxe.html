Frequently Asked Questions

How does the theme toggle work?

Clicking the toggle in the navigation switches between light and dark modes, persisting your choice via localStorage.

Is the page accessible?

Yes. We use semantic HTML, proper contrast, focus outlines, and ARIA labels to meet WCAG AA in both themes.

Can I customize the colors?

The design relies on CSS variables; swapping the values in :root re‑themes the entire page.
