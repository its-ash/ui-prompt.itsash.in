Questions, answered

How is Nimbus different from a traditional cloud platform?expand_more

Traditional clouds hand you raw parts and a manual. Nimbus is declarative: you describe the outcome, and the Neural Engine provisions, routes, and heals the infrastructure continuously — no pipelines to babysit.

Can I bring my existing stack?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus scans your repository and maps containers, databases, and domains exactly as they are. Most teams migrate a production service in under an hour, with zero code changes.

What happens if a whole region goes down?expand_more

The mesh detects the failure in milliseconds and reroutes traffic to healthy nodes before your users notice. Because every deploy is an atomic snapshot, a full-region rollback completes in under 400ms.
