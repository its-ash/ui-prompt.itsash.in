Squishy questions

The things folks always poke us about.

Do I need to know how to draw or animate?

expand_more

Not at all. If you can squish playdough, you can make a Claymates short. Every tool is a big friendly button, and undo forgives everything.

Can I use Claymates offline?

expand_more

Yes — the whole clay box lives on your device. Your films sync to the shelf whenever you wander back online.

Is it safe for kids and classrooms?

expand_more

Built side-by-side with teachers and parents: no ads, no strangers' comments, and a locked-down gallery mode made for classrooms.
