Pressed questions, answered

What counts as a specimen plate?expand_more

A plate is one cataloged item: a scan or photograph plus its metadata. Pressed plants, seeds, moss packets, and field slides all count as single plates.

How accurate is the taxon matching?expand_more

Suggestions are ranked by confidence and always shown as suggestions, never auto-applied. A curator confirms every identification before it enters the record.

Can I export my collection if I leave?expand_more

Yes. Full export of images, metadata, and annotations is available on every plan, including Seedling, in open formats (CSV, JSON, BibTeX).

Does Herbarium work offline in the field?expand_more

The mobile capture mode queues plates locally and syncs when you reconnect, with conflict detection for annotations made on two devices.

Who owns the data I upload?expand_more

You do, or your institution does. Herbarium claims no rights over plates; public sharing is opt-in per plate.

Can I migrate an existing herbarium database?expand_more

Institution plans include assisted migration from Specify, Symbiota, and flat CSV archives, with folio numbers preserved where possible.
