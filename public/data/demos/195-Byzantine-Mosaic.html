Questions

From the Scriptorium

Are the tesserae historically sourced?expand_more

Our smalti comes from Murano furnaces whose lineage reaches the fifth century, and every batch ships with a sealed provenance record.

Can a panel survive outdoors?expand_more

Each work is bedded in lime mortar and sealed beneath borosilicate glass — rated, like the Ravenna facades, for six centuries of weather.

Do you restore existing mosaics?expand_more

Yes. Our conservators document, stabilize, and restore historic panels to fourth-century standards before any new commission is accepted.
