Inquiries

Questions from the Curious

Do your engines truly run on steam alone?expand_more

Indeed. Each machine is fired by clean coal and a touch of ambition, though several of our grander models accept strong tea in emergencies.

How long does a bespoke commission take?expand_more

Between a fortnight and a season, depending on the ambition of the design and the temper of the weather. We keep you informed by telegram at every stage.

Is the workshop insured against boiler mishaps?expand_more

Thrice over. Our foundry carries policies with three separate underwriters, none of whom have ever yet had cause to frown upon us.

May I visit the foundry in person?expand_more

By appointment only, and do bring goggles. The machinery is friendly, but it knows no manners.
