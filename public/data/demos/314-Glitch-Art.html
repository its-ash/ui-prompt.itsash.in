// DEBUG LOG

Frequently asked errors

What is datamoshing, exactly?expand_more

Codecs store most frames as changes from the previous frame. Datamoshing deletes the “true” frames so those changes get applied to the wrong image — motion smears, melts and bleeds between shots. DATAMOSH automates the whole process on a visual timeline.

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?expand_more

No. DATAMOSH runs entirely in your browser via WebCodecs and WASM. Your footage never leaves your machine, and rendering works offline once the app is loaded.

Can I sell art I make here?expand_more

Yes — on every tier, including GLITCH. We claim zero rights over your corrupted output. Commercial, print or on-chain, whatever your transmission requires.
