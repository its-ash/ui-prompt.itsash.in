What is included in a monthly kit?

Each kit contains a curated herb blend, a charged crystal, a ritual candle, a parchment guide, and a surprise bonus item aligned with the current moon phase.

How do I know which ritual is right for me?

We provide a short quiz on the product page that matches your intentions (love, protection, abundance, etc.) to the appropriate kit. You can also contact our mystics for personalized advice.

Are the ingredients ethically sourced?

Yes. All herbs are wild‑crafted or organically farmed, crystals are conflict‑free, and candles are made from soy wax with cotton wicks.

Can I skip a month or cancel?

Absolutely. You can pause or cancel anytime from your account dashboard. No hidden fees.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship worldwide. Shipping rates and delivery times vary by location; see our shipping policy for details.
