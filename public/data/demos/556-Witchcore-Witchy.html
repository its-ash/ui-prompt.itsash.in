How the ritual unfolds

Three small steps each evening. No overwhelm, only rhythm.

I

Set tonight's intention

Open Nimbus at dusk and name one focus. The planner checks it against the current moon phase.

II

Pull and record

Draw your card, log the reading, and tag the herbs or crystals you worked with.

III

Review the pattern

Each full moon, Nimbus weaves your entries into a reflection of what grew and what released.

Questions from the threshold

Answers for those standing at the edge of the circle.

Do I need experience with tarot or lunar work?expand_more

Not at all. Nimbus includes gentle guidance for every spread and phase, written for beginners and depth for seasoned practitioners.

Is my journal private?expand_more

Yes. Entries are encrypted and never shared. Coven circles in the High Priestess tier only reveal what you explicitly post.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?expand_more

Whenever you wish. Your grimoire stays readable on the Apprentice tier after cancellation, and exports are always available.

Does Nimbus work offline?expand_more

The lunar calendar and your journal work fully offline. Entries sync the next time you reconnect.

Which traditions does the herb index cover?expand_more

Western herbalism, folk magic, and cottage craft, with clear safety cautions. We cite sources and avoid closed practices.
