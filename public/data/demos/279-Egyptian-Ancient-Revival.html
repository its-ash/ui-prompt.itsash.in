For the Curious

Questions for the Keepers

Are the artefacts authentic?expand_more

Every piece was either excavated under licence or accessioned before 1970; later revival works are clearly labelled as such, in the honest tradition of the 1920s exhibitions.

May I photograph the galleries?expand_more

Non-flash photography is welcome throughout. The mask chamber permits lamplight exposures only — our attendants will gladly steady a lantern for you.

Is the museum accessible?expand_more

All halls are step-free, with lifts to every gallery, wheelchairs on loan at the gate, and weekly quiet hours with dimmed soundscapes.

Can schools book a guided rite?expand_more

Yes. Scribe-led school visits run each weekday morning and include a cartouche-carving workshop; write to the registrars at least a fortnight ahead.

How do I cancel my membership?expand_more

Cancel at any time from your ledger page; your privileges endure until the end of the current billing cycle, as the old contracts promise.
