Frequently asked questions

What level of autonomy does the VK-1 have?

expand_more

The VK-1 is rated SAE Level 5 within validated zones, which cover 96% of urban and highway miles in our 12 launch cities. Outside those zones it operates as a Level 3 system with driver supervision.

How safe is autonomous driving?

expand_more

The stack has logged 40 million test miles with triple-redundant braking, steering and power. In validation testing it avoided 92% of collision scenarios that experienced human drivers missed.

How long does charging take?

expand_more

On a 350 kW fast charger the VK-1 goes from 10% to 80% in 18 minutes — roughly 240 miles of range in the time it takes to drink a coffee.

What happens to my data?

expand_more

Driving decisions are processed entirely onboard. Trip data is only uploaded with your explicit opt-in, is encrypted at rest, and is never sold to third parties.

When and where can I get one?

expand_more

Deliveries begin Q3 2026 across 12 launch cities in North America and Europe, expanding to 40 cities through 2027. Reservations open in waves by region.
