Sweet questions, honest answers

Is the free plan really free forever?expand_more
Yes. Sprout includes three habits, daily stickers, and reminders with no time limit and no card required.
What happens if I miss a day?expand_more
Nothing scary. Your streak pauses kindly, and a once-a-month mulligan sticker lets you patch a missed day.
Can I use Mochi on my phone and computer?expand_more
Mochi syncs across iOS, Android, and the web, so your garden looks the same everywhere you check in.
Can I cancel a paid plan anytime?expand_more
Of course. Cancel in two taps and you keep every sticker and plant you have already earned.
Is my journal private?expand_more
Completely. Entries are encrypted, never sold, and only you can read them. Shared gardens show plants, not words.
