What we cultivate

Landscape architecture

We shape outdoor environments that balance ecology, structure, and human use across residential and civic sites.

Botanical curation

Plant palettes selected for climate, soil, and seasonal rhythm, ensuring gardens mature with grace over decades.

Interior greenery

Living installations for homes and workplaces that improve air, light, and daily wellbeing without fuss.

How it works

01

Site reading

We walk the land, study light, soil, and water, then map what the place already wants to become.

02

Design and planting

A layered plan of structure, seasonality, and care, drawn to scale and built with trusted growers.

03

Stewardship

We stay through the first seasons, adjusting and teaching until the garden holds its own.

Questions

How long does a project take?

Most residential gardens run eight to sixteen weeks from first visit to planting, depending on season and scope.

Do you work outside your region?

Yes. We travel for projects that fit our practice and climate expertise, with travel costs agreed upfront.

What happens after planting?

We offer stewardship visits through the first two seasons to adjust care and ensure the garden establishes.
