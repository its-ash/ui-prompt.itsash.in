Frequently Asked

Do you carry hazardous materials?

We accept most cargo types, but items requiring special containment (radioactive, biological) need prior approval and additional insurance.

How do I change my shipment's destination mid‑flight?

Contact our control tower with the new coordinates; a reroute fee applies depending on remaining fuel and hazard windows.

What happens if a delivery is delayed?

Delays are logged in your tracker; compensation is issued per our service‑level agreement, typically a credit toward the next cycle.
