what's in the box

Tools that behave like real materials

Every brush, scrap and strip of tape follows the physics of the studio table, not the spreadsheet grid.

content_cut

Tear, don't crop

Rip any image or paper swatch along a freehand edge. Fibers, roughness and shadow are generated automatically, so no two tears look alike.

layers

Stacked like a desk

Drag scraps over each other, nudge rotation, and pin pieces down. Layer order works the way a pile of paper does: what you touch comes to the top.

palette

Paint that stays wet

Gouache, ink and marker swatches blend into whatever sits beneath them until you flatten the piece. Recolor a stroke hours later without starting over.

from blank page to wall

Three steps, no instructions needed

01 upload

Gather your scraps

Drop in photos, scan old paper, or pick from 400 included textures: kraft, newsprint, linen, graph and more.

02 gesture

Arrange and glue

Tear, rotate and overlap pieces on the canvas. Add tape strips, pins and handwritten captions with one click.

03 ios_share

Export or print

Save print-ready files up to 600 DPI, animated process videos, or layered source files you can reopen anytime.
