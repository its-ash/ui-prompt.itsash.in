Questions, answered softly

How long does a candle stay lit?expand_more

A Single Flame burns for seven days. Vigil Keeper candles are tended for a full year and may be renewed. An Eternal Light is kept burning for as long as Vigil exists, with stewardship passed to your family.

Who can see the memorial page?expand_more

Only people you share the private link with. Memorial pages are never listed publicly, never indexed by search engines, and never shown to anyone you have not invited.

Can guests add memories without paying?expand_more

Yes. Anyone with your invitation link may leave a memory, a photograph, or light a small candle of their own at no cost. Only the keeper of the memorial needs a plan.
