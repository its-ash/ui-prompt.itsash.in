Questions, Answered Gently

From the Atelier Ledger

How does a commission begin?expand_more

With a conversation. Write describing your subject, occasion, and mood; we reply within two days with scope, timeline, and a formal quotation.

What timelines should I expect?expand_more

A Sketch ripens in two to three weeks, The Atelier in five to seven, and Grand Commissions are scheduled across a full season of studio time.

How many revisions are included?expand_more

Each tier carries its stated revisions — one, three, or five — applied at the sketch stage, where changes bloom most naturally. Further rounds are invoiced modestly.

May I license the artwork?expand_more

Originals are yours outright. Reproduction and product licensing may be written into the contract for a negotiated royalty or buyout.

Do you ship originals internationally?expand_more

Yes — works travel in archival tissue within a rigid portfolio case, fully insured and tracked to your door, anywhere in the world.

When do atelier workshops open?expand_more

Cohorts are gathered each season — spring botanicals, autumn ornament — and announced first to our mailing list.
