FAQ

Questions and answers

Can I migrate from another platform?

Yes. Vanta supports importing from most major platforms. Our migration guide walks you through it step by step, and our team can assist with larger migrations.

What frameworks are supported?

Vanta auto-detects Node.js, Python, Go, Rust, and Ruby applications. Static sites and Docker containers are also fully supported.

Is there a free tier?

The Starter plan is free forever with 3 services and 100 GB of bandwidth. No credit card required to get started.
