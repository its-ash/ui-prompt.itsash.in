Questions, answered

The essentials before you start cutting.

Do I need to know how to draw?

No. Facet is built on cutting and composing, not rendering. If you can arrange torn paper on a table, you already have the core skill — Facet adds precision, layers, and an undo history.

Can I use my own photos and papers?

Yes. Drag any JPG or PNG onto the canvas and slice it like any other plane. Your uploads stay yours — we never use them for training, and Gallery studios can share papers privately.

What can I export, and at what quality?

Every plan exports crisp PNG. Atelier and Gallery add layered PSD files and print-ready PDF at up to 300 DPI, so your collage survives the gallery wall — or the laser cutter.
