Client Care

Questions, Answered

How quickly will my order arrive?addremove

Most pieces are delivered within two business days in signature packaging. Your stylist shares tracking privately, and delivery windows can be arranged around your schedule.

Can I return or exchange a piece?addremove

Unworn pieces may be returned within thirty days — we collect them from your address at no charge. Tailored garments are exchanged or re-fitted, never simply discarded.

Do you offer private appointments?addremove

Certainly. Book a consultation and a stylist will meet you at our showroom — or wherever in the world you happen to be.
