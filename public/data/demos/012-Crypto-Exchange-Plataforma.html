Frequently Asked Questions

Everything traders ask us before their first deposit.

Is my crypto safe on Nimbus?expand_more

Yes. 96% of client assets sit in geographically distributed cold-storage vaults secured with multi-signature approval. We publish proof-of-reserves monthly and maintain a self-insurance fund covering hot-wallet events.

What fees does Nimbus charge?expand_more

Maker fees start at 0.02% and taker fees at 0.05%, scaling down with your 30-day volume. Deposits are always free, and your first fiat withdrawal each month is on us.

How long do withdrawals take?expand_more

Crypto withdrawals are auto-processed in about two minutes once security checks pass. Fiat withdrawals typically reach your bank within one business day — with 24/7 support if anything stalls.
