/// INTEL

Questions, answered

Every feed is encrypted with AES-256 in transit and at rest, and paid tiers render inside your own private cluster. Role-based access, per-frame audit logs and customer-managed keys are standard on Operator and Commander.

HoloGrid ships native connectors for Postgres, MySQL, Kafka, Snowflake, BigQuery, S3 and any REST or GraphQL endpoint. Schemas are auto-mapped on first sync, and our SDK covers Python, Go and Node for everything else.

From the moment a record lands in the pipeline to the moment it moves inside your hologram, median latency is 40ms on standard broadband. At full 12B daily volume, the 99th percentile stays under 120ms.
