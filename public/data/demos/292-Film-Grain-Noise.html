Frequently asked

Will the grain survive social-media compression?expand_more

Yes. Grainhaus renders grain at your export resolution, and Compression Guard mode sizes grain and halation so they stay visible after JPEG and video re-encoding.

Does Grainhaus touch my original files?expand_more

Never. Every edit is non-destructive and stored as a recipe — your originals stay untouched, and you can peel every effect back at any time.

Can I sell work processed with Grainhaus?expand_more

Yes. All paid plans include a full commercial license covering client work, prints and advertising. The free tier is for personal projects only.
