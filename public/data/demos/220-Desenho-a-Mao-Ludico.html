Questions Grown-Ups Ask

The honest answers, in plain storybook English.

Are the weekly stories really free?expand_more

Yes! The Doodle Diary will always be free to read. Paid plans simply add extra coloring pages, audio read-alongs and printable storybooks for folks who want more.

What ages are the stories written for?expand_more

Mostly ages 3 to 8, though we have caught grandads giggling too. Each story lists a gentle age guide and a silliness rating before you begin.

Can I use the coloring pages in my classroom?expand_more

Please do! The Library Hero plan includes a classroom licence, and the free sheets are fine for home use too — just keep the little artist credit scribbled in the corner.
