The Meridian Method

01

Commission slowly

We ask a small circle of writers one question per issue, then give them months, not deadlines measured in days.

02

Edit on paper

Every draft is printed, marked by hand, and read aloud. If a sentence cannot survive being spoken, it goes.

03

Print to keep

Thread-sewn binding, uncoated stock, and a run small enough that each copy is numbered before it ships.
