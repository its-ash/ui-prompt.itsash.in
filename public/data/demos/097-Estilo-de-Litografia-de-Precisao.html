05 / FAQ

Engineering questions, straight answers.

What wavelength does the NX-1 expose at?expand_more

13.5 nm extreme ultraviolet. A 30 kW CO₂ drive laser vaporizes tin droplets to ignite the plasma, and the entire path from source to wafer runs in high vacuum with all-reflective optics.

What does installation require?expand_more

A vibration-isolated bay of roughly 45 m × 10 m, about 1 MW of conditioned power, and ultra-high-purity nitrogen. NIMBUS crews handle rigging, hookup, and qualification.

How fast can we reach production wafers?expand_more

Most fabs expose first production wafers within 12 weeks of chassis set, supported by a resident engineer through ramp and a 12-hour critical-spares program.
