Questions from the floor

How long does installation take?expand_more

Most workshops reach full steam within one afternoon. The couplings are standard, the dials are labeled, and our Gazette guides every turn of the wrench.

Which tools does the machinery connect to?expand_more

Over two hundred couplings ship standard: ledgers, shipping manifests, payroll drums, inventory reels, and every common telegraph protocol.

Is the machinery aether-safety compliant?expand_more

Every unit carries the Board of Aetheric Trade seal and passes independent inspection twice yearly. Certificates are available on the Master Artisan tier.

What is the Foundry Guarantee?expand_more

If the machinery does not earn its keep within sixty days, we refund every penny and collect the engine at our expense. That is the whole of it.

When is support available?expand_more

Our fitters answer the telegraph from dawn to midnight, seven days a week. Journeyman and Master Artisan stations receive priority wires.
