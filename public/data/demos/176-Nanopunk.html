Questions we get asked

Safety first — the goo questions included.

Can a Nimbus swarm go grey-goo?

expand_more

No. Every nanite is metabolic-capped and lease-locked to a sealed substrate volume. Replication beyond the signed budget trips a lattice-wide kill switch in under 50 microseconds and the swarm self-dissolves. Independent auditors hold the halt keys — not us.

What materials can I fabricate?

expand_more

Anything in the certified library: lattice alloys, gradient aerogels, programmable gels, and self-healing polymers. Custom element maps are available on the Foundry plan after a containment review by our safety engineers.

Do you lock in my blueprints?

expand_more

Never. Blueprints export as open .mol files and standard nanite bytecode. Dissolve your swarm, take your files, and recompile on any compatible forge. Your audit history exports with them.
