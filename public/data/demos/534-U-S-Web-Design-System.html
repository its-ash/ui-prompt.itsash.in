How it works

Three steps from a new request to a documented release. No special training required.

1

Intake the request

Log a request by form, email, or phone note. Nimbus assigns a tracking number and sets the legal clock.

2

Collect and review

Gather responsive files, apply redactions side by side, and route the package for sign-off in one queue.

3

Release and archive

Send the response, publish to your reading room if required, and file everything under its retention schedule.

Common questions

Plain answers, written the way we would say them on the phone.

How long does setup take?expand_more

Most offices are live within two weeks. We import your existing request log, set your retention schedules, and walk staff through a one-hour training session.

Does Nimbus meet federal accessibility requirements?expand_more

Yes. The product is built to Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 AA standards, including full keyboard use, screen reader support, and tested color contrast.

Where is our data stored?expand_more

All records are stored in U.S.-based data centers with encryption at rest and in transit. You can export everything you own at any time, in open formats.

Can we keep our current request intake form?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus accepts requests from your existing web form, a shared inbox, or manual entry. Each source feeds the same tracking queue.

What happens if we go over our monthly request limit?expand_more

Nothing stops working. We notify you, and you can move to the next plan at the start of the following month. We never hold records hostage.

Is there a contract or can we cancel anytime?expand_more

Plans are annual, which matches most procurement cycles. If you leave, you receive a complete export of your records and audit logs at no charge.
