// Diagnostics

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does Flow do?expand_more

Flow is a real-time data-pipeline platform: it ingests events from your sources, routes and transforms them in flight, and monitors throughput, lag and cost across every stream — at sub-8ms p99 latency.

How is my data secured?expand_more

Every event is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Pipelines support field-level masking, role-based access and immutable audit logs, and Flow is aligned with SOC 2 Type II controls.

What are the free-tier limits?expand_more

Initiate includes 2 active pipelines, 1M events per month and 7-day retention — free forever, no credit card required. Upgrade to Flow Pro anytime to lift the limits without re-building a thing.
