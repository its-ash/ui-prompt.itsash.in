Ask the ink room

Frequently pressed questions

Why only two or three inks? add

Because riso drums are physical. Fewer layers print cleaner, dry faster, and force bolder design. We build the same discipline into Nimbus.

Can I export print-ready files? add

Yes — CMYK pass-through with registration marks, bleed, and ink separations for every layer, at up to 1200 dpi.

Does Nimbus support smooth gradients? add

No. Next question. (If you need a fade, we offer honest halftones instead.)

Will my pages look right in dark mode? add

Yes. Inks stay saturated on dark stock, and every color pair is checked for WCAG contrast in both themes.

What happens to my pages if I cancel? add

You keep every file. Exports stay yours forever; we just stop the presses on anything new.

One zine a month. Ink swatches, halftone tricks, zero spam — we can’t afford the paper.
