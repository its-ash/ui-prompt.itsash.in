Questions, answered

Across customer benchmarks, Nimbus averages 94% accuracy on 30 day revenue forecasts. Every published forecast is backtested against held-out history, and the accuracy report for your own data is visible in the dashboard at all times.

The shaded band around a forecast shows the range where the actual value is expected to land 80% of the time, based on your data's historical volatility. A wider band means more uncertainty, so you can plan for the range instead of a single number.

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified, supports SSO and role-based access, and never trains shared models on your private data. Enterprise plans can deploy inside your own VPC.

Native connectors cover Snowflake, BigQuery, Postgres, Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, and Shopify, plus a REST API and CSV import for anything else. Alerts and insights deliver to Slack, email, Teams, and webhooks.
