Quick Answers

Can I import boards from other tools?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus imports from Miro, FigJam, and plain CSV outlines. Sticky notes become cards, and your column order becomes the timeline.

Does the free plan expire?expand_more

No. The Solo plan is free forever with three active boards. You only pay when you need unlimited boards, replay, or team features.

How does session replay work?expand_more

Every edit is versioned automatically. Open the history panel and scrub a slider to watch the board evolve, then restore any earlier state with one click.
