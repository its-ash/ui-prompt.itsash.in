Deliberations

Questions for the Scribes

Do I need any Egyptian to begin?expand_more

None at all. The tutor opens with the uniliteral alphabet and reads alongside you; by the tenth lesson you will parse a simple offering formula unaided.

Which phases of the language are covered?expand_more

Old, Middle, and Late Egyptian, plus a growing Demotic shelf. Every text is tagged by period, provenance, and reading difficulty.

Can I cite the facsimiles in academic work?expand_more

Yes. Each plate carries its publication history, museum number, and licence, and exports as a print-ready scholarly figure with bibliography attached.

What happens to my work if I cancel?expand_more

Your annotations, streaks, and exported plates remain yours. Everything is downloadable for ninety days after any cancellation.
