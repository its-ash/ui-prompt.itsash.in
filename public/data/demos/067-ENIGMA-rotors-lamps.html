Manual

Operator questions.

How does rotor-based key derivation work?expand_more

Each session starts from a fresh rotor manifest. Keys are never stored — they are stepped into existence per message and dissolve when the board resets, so there is no secret to leak in the first place.

What happens if a delivery lamp never lights?expand_more

The board treats a dark lamp as a failed exchange: it re-steps the rotors, retries once, and files the miss in your audit manifest with a full timestamp. You see every dropped transmission, not just the successful ones.

Can we run ENIGMA inside our own perimeter?expand_more

Yes. The rack deploys on-prem or in your VPC. Rotors, plugboards, and manifests never leave your perimeter — we ship the machinery, not your traffic.
