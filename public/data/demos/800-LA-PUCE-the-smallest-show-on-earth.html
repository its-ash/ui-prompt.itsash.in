Questions From the Crowd

Everything the curious giant asks before the lights go down.

Are the fleas real, and are they treated well?expand_more

Real, pampered, and unionized in spirit. Our performers work four-minute shows, dine on premium fare, and retire to a heated terrarium with full honors.

How long is the show?expand_more

Forty minutes of acts plus one encore. Flea attention spans are short, so every second on stage is pure headline material.

Will I actually be able to see anything?expand_more

Yes. Every seat includes a calibrated magnifier, and the stage is lit by a focused gold spotlight. Ringside guests report gasping at things smaller than a crumb.

Can children attend?expand_more

Absolutely. Children under ten receive a junior ringmaster hat and are invited to count the cannon launch down from three.

What happens if a performer hops off stage?expand_more

Our trained wranglers recover any runaway within seconds using a silk catch-cloth. The show pauses, the audience applauds the improvisation, and the act resumes.
