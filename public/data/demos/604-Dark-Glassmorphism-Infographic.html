Common questions

How does Nimbus access my cloud accounts?expand_more
Through a read-only IAM role you create with our scoped policy template. Nimbus can never modify, start, or stop any resource. You can revoke access at any time from your own console.
How long until I see real findings?expand_more
The first cost map renders in under five minutes. Waste findings appear within the hour, and anomaly detection reaches full accuracy after about two weeks of baseline learning.
Does Nimbus work with Kubernetes spend?expand_more
Yes. Nimbus breaks cluster costs down by namespace, workload, and label on EKS, AKS, and GKE, so shared clusters stop being a black box on the invoice.
Can I export data to my own tools?expand_more
Every view exports to CSV, and the full API is available on Growth and Enterprise plans. Native integrations cover Slack, PagerDuty, Jira, and Terraform Cloud.
