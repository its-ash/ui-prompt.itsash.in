Common questions

What if a plant arrives damaged?expand_more

Send us a photo within seven days and we ship a replacement free of charge. Roughly one in two hundred boxes arrives with damage, and every one is covered.

Are the plants safe around pets?expand_more

Yes, if you tell us about your pets during setup. We only match you with non-toxic varieties like calatheas, parlour palms, and peperomias.

Can I pause my subscription while traveling?expand_more

Anytime, for up to three months per year. Your care plan and plant history stay saved, and reminders resume when you do.
