Common questions

The things families ask before trusting us with the shoebox.

Are my original photos safe in the mail?

Yes. Every box is tracked, insured, and padded. Originals are never cut, glued, or altered, and they travel back to you in the same box as your finished albums.

What if a photo is badly damaged?

Tears, mold spots, and heavy fading are quoted before any work begins. If a photo cannot be safely restored, we tell you honestly and return it untouched at no charge.

Do I keep the digital files?

Always. Your full-resolution scans are yours on USB and as a private download link that stays active for one year. We never reuse or share your family photos.
