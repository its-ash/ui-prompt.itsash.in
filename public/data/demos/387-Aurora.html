FAQ

Questions from the cosmos

Do I need any art or design skills?expand_more

None at all. If you can describe a mood — "a quiet city under green rain" — you can make art with Aurora. The canvas handles the craft; you bring the dream.

Who owns the art I create?expand_more

You do. Everything you generate on any plan is yours to keep, post, and print. We never train on private skies without your explicit say-so.

Can I use Aurora for commercial work?expand_more

Yes. Stardust covers personal projects, while Nebula and Supernova include full commercial licenses for client work, games, and products.
