Questions, answered

The practical details, so the morning stays magical.

What happens if the wind is too strong?expand_more

Flights only launch in calm conditions. If your morning is cancelled, we rebook you on the next available dawn or refund the flight portion in full, your choice.

Can I get a refund if my plans change?expand_more

Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the festival. After that, passes are transferable to another guest at no cost through your confirmation email.

Is the festival accessible?expand_more

Yes. Viewing areas have step-free paths and reserved platforms, shuttles are wheelchair accessible, and two balloons are fitted with door baskets for seated boarding.
