The Process

Three sittings to a finished wardrobe

  1. 01

    The Intake

    A 30-minute call and a photo survey of your closet. We learn your proportions, palette, and weekly rhythm.

  2. 02

    The Edit

    Your stylist drafts look boards and a gap list. You annotate everything before a single purchase is suggested.

  3. 03

    The Fitting

    New pieces arrive for a guided try-on over video. Keep what works, return what does not, no pressure.
