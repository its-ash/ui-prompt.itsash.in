Frequently Asked Questions

How does the agitation reminder work?

The app uses gentle haptic pulses (if supported) and on‑screen flashes at the intervals you set.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. All timing and logging functions work without an internet connection.

Is my data private?

All data stays on your device unless you choose to export or sync.

What platforms are supported?

Progressive Web App works on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux.

How do I export my logs?

From the Logs screen tap the share button and choose CSV or PDF.
