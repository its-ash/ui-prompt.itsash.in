FAQ

Asked, answered.

The details our clients consider before reserving.

How long does a Nimbus take to craft? expand_more

Each object is assembled to order in Basel. Standard configurations leave the atelier within six weeks; bespoke commissions take up to twelve.

What does the lifetime care charter include? expand_more

Annual inspection, refinishing, recalibration, and priority restoration — for as long as the piece remains registered in your name.

Can I reserve before choosing a finish? expand_more

Yes. A fully refundable reservation secures your place in the atelier queue, and your configuration can be finalized up to two weeks before assembly begins.
