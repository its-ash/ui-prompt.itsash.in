Frequently Asked Questions

What data sources does DRIFT integrate?

DRIFT accepts NetCDF, GRIB, GeoJSON, and CSV formats from NOAA, ECMWF, AIS, GPS tracks, and citizen science platforms via our open connector framework.

Is my data private and secure?

All processing occurs in your browser or private cloud tier. We never store raw inputs; only anonymized aggregates improve public models.

Can I export my flow visualizations?

Yes—export as interactive WebGL embeds, high-resolution PNG/SVG, or layered GeoTIFF for GIS software. Animation sequences available as MP4/WebM.

What devices support DRIFT?

Any modern browser with WebGL2 support. Optimized for touch interfaces on tablets and fieldwork laptops down to 7-inch screens.

How does DRIFT differ from traditional GIS?

We model fluid dynamics as continuous fields rather than discrete vectors, revealing emergent patterns like vortices and shear layers that point analyses miss.
