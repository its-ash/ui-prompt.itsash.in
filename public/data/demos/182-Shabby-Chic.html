Curiosities

Questions we're often asked over tea

Everything we sell is genuinely old — most pieces date from 1900 to 1970. We photograph every chip and fading patch honestly, so what you see is precisely what arrives on your doorstep.

One-off finds ship within 3–5 working days. Bespoke commissions — such as re-upholstering a bergère chair — take four to six weeks, and we write to you weekly with progress notes.

Wrap it back in its tissue and tell us within 14 days. We collect, refund or restore it again free of charge, and we cover postage both ways — no quibbles, ever.

Any time, from your account or with a polite email. Skip a month, pause for winter, or cancel with a click — your loyalty lavender sprigs never expire.

We ship across Europe and to Canada, the US and Australia. International parcels travel with extra padding; delivery takes 7–12 days and duties are arranged at checkout.
