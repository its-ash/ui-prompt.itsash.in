05 / FAQ

ASK THE GRID.

HOW DOES THE GRID SCORE WORK? expand_more
The grid score combines brand rule compliance (typography, spacing, color) and attention prediction models into a 0–100 rating. Higher scores mean better alignment with goals and user focus.
DO I HAVE TO CHANGE MY DESIGN TOOLS? expand_more
No. GRIDPULSE works with your existing tools via browser extension, Figma plugin, or API. It observes rendered output, so your workflow stays unchanged.
IS THE FREE PLAN REALLY FREE? expand_more
Yes. The Solo plan is free forever with 1 active project, live scoring, and basic history. No credit card required.
CAN I EXPORT REPORTS FOR STAKEHOLDERS? expand_more
Export PDF/PNG reports are available on Team and Scale plans. Schedule automatic exports or generate on demand for presentations.
DO YOU STORE OUR SCREENS? expand_more
We store only anonymized metadata and scores. Rendered screenshots are encrypted, temporary, and deleted after processing. You can delete data instantly.
