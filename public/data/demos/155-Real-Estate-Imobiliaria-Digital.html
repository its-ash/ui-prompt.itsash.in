Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you begin.

How are listings verified?expand_more

Every property passes a 21-point inspection covering structural condition, legal title, energy rating and documentation before publication. Listings that fail any check never appear on the platform.

Do you charge buyers a commission?expand_more

No. Buyers on our membership plans pay a fixed monthly fee only — never a percentage of purchase price. Seller commissions are agreed transparently in the listing mandate.

Can I schedule a viewing online?expand_more

Yes. Request a viewing from any listing and your agent confirms a private tour within 48 hours, seven days a week, including virtual walkthroughs for international clients.

Which areas do you cover?expand_more

Lisbon, Cascais, Estoril, Sintra, Comporta and the Algarve, with selected opportunities in Porto and the Douro Valley through our partner network.

What happens after I request a viewing?expand_more

Your agent contacts you to confirm timing, prepares a property dossier with comparables and survey notes, and accompanies you personally through every viewing.
