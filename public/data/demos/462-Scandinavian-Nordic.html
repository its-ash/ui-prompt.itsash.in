The practical details, plainly stated.

We ship to all EU countries, Norway, Switzerland, and the UK. Shipping is free on every plan, and each delivery is carbon-neutral through our logistics partner.

FSC-certified wood, undyed or naturally dyed linen and wool, lead-free stoneware, and recycled glass. Every item lists its full material breakdown before it ships.

Yes. You have 30 days to return any item free of charge, no questions asked. Returned pieces are refurbished and offered in our seconds shop rather than discarded.

Boxes are made from recycled cardboard with paper-based cushioning and soy inks. No plastic, no foam. The box itself is designed to be reused for storage.
