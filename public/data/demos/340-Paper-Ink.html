The process

Three movements, from draft to shelf

  1. 01

    Draft

    Write in a quiet room. Focus mode keeps the margins empty; soft deadlines keep the muse respected.

  2. 02

    Refine

    Exchange chapters with your editor. Every note arrives as a letter — argued, humane, and honest about the prose.

  3. 03

    Publish

    Approve the proofs. We typeset, print, and register your edition; the rights, and the book, remain yours.

Questions

Frequently asked, carefully answered

Who holds the rights to my manuscript?expand_more

You do — entirely and in perpetuity. Folio is a studio, not a publisher of record. We sharpen the pen; you keep it, along with every right it writes.

Can I change tiers or pause my subscription?expand_more

Yes. Upgrades, downgrades, and pauses take effect at the next turning of the month, and nothing you have written is ever lost in the move.

How private are my drafts?expand_more

Drafts are private by default and encrypted at rest. No excerpt leaves your desk without your written consent — not for marketing, not ever.
