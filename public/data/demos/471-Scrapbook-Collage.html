Questions, answered

Do I need to know design to make a nice page? expand_more

Nope. Every book opens on a pre-ruled spread that already looks good — tilts and tape are optional spices, not homework.

Can I print my scrapbook? expand_more

Yes. Export a zine-quality PDF anytime, or order a bound copy with real kraft covers and taped spines.

What happens to my photos if I leave? expand_more

Nothing dramatic. Download everything at full resolution — we keep no hostages, only a little tape residue.
