Good Questions

Questions, Answered

Where does my donation go?expand_more

84 cents of every dollar funds field programs: planting, patrols, and community partnerships. The rest covers reporting and payment costs. We publish a transparency report every quarter.

How is reforestation monitored?expand_more

Every hectare is GPS-mapped and photographed from the ground. Independent auditors verify survival rates yearly, and your reports pair satellite imagery with field photos.

Can I change or cancel my plan?expand_more

Yes — anytime, in two clicks from any email we send you. No calls, no forms, and every tree you have already funded keeps growing.
