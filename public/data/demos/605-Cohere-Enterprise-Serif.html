How it works

Live in your cloud in three steps

Most teams ship a grounded assistant to their first department in under two weeks.

  1. 01

    Connect sources

    Point Calder at SharePoint, Confluence, S3 and your data warehouse. Permissions sync automatically.

    calder sources add sharepoint \ --sync-acl

  2. 02

    Deploy the model

    One Terraform module provisions models, retrieval and audit logging in your account.

    module "calder" { region = "eu-west-1" }

  3. 03

    Roll out to teams

    Ship the assistant in Slack, Teams or your own apps through one API with per-team policies.

    POST /v1/answer { "team": "finance" }

FAQ

What security teams ask us

Does any data leave our cloud account?add

No. Inference, retrieval, logs and analytics all run in your VPC. Calder only receives anonymous license telemetry, which you can disable.

How do you prevent made-up answers?add

Answers are generated only from retrieved passages, each sentence is linked to its source, and the assistant declines when sources are missing or conflict.

Which compliance standards do you meet?add

SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and GDPR, with HIPAA and FedRAMP Moderate deployment patterns available on Enterprise.

Can we use our own models?add

Yes. Bring an open-weight model or a fine-tuned variant; Calder handles retrieval, grounding and policy around it.
