Frequently asked questions

Everything else you were about to email us about.

How is Nimbus different from other workspace tools?expand_more

Most tools force your process into rigid boards. Nimbus adapts: views, fields, and automations are modular, and the whole app is engineered to respond in under 100 ms, even in very large projects.

Can I try Nimbus before paying?expand_more

Yes. Starter is free forever, and every paid plan begins with a 14-day trial — no credit card required. If you cancel during the trial, you are never charged.

How does data migration from other tools work?expand_more

Our importer maps projects from the tools you already use and pulls in files, comments, and history — usually in minutes. Scale customers get a hands-on migration engineer.

Do you offer discounts for education or nonprofits?expand_more

We do — verified schools and registered nonprofits receive 50% off any paid plan. Write to us from your organization email and we will set it up within a day.

What happens to my data if I cancel?expand_more

You keep read access for 30 days and can export everything to CSV or a portable archive at any time. After that window, data is permanently and automatically deleted.
