Questions, answered

Is Civitas an official government project?expand_more
No. Civitas is an independent education platform. All symbol descriptions follow the official federal specifications, and every lesson cites its sources.
Can I use images of the flag and coat of arms in class?expand_more
Yes. National symbols are public heritage. Each dossier includes print-ready files plus clear guidelines for respectful, law-abiding use.
Does the free Cidadão plan ever expire?expand_more
Never. The full symbol library and one quiz set per symbol are free forever — no credit card required.
Are the materials available in Portuguese?expand_more
Yes. Every dossier ships in Brazilian Portuguese and English, and educators can toggle languages inside any lesson.
How do I request an institutional quote?expand_more
Choose "Talk to us" on the Institutional plan or use a contact link in the footer. Our team replies within two business days.
