FREQUENTLY ASKED QUERIES

Does Nimbus work offline?

Yes — the deck caches your queue locally and commits to tape the moment the signal returns. Nothing is lost to a dropped line.

Can I export my archive?

Every reel exports as plain-text logs or standard JSON straight from the console. Your tape stays portable, forever.

What happens when the tape is full?

END OF TAPE simply triggers auto-rewind: the oldest runs compress to cold storage. Nothing is deleted without your explicit command.
