Questions from the bar

Everything guests usually ask before the first round.

How far in advance should I book?expand_more
Two weeks is comfortable, but we have staged full luaus with four days notice. Weekends in summer go first, so reach out early.
Do you serve non-alcoholic options?expand_more
Always. Every tier includes a zero-proof menu with the same house syrups, fresh juices, and over-the-top garnishes.
What happens if it rains on my outdoor event?expand_more
We bring canvas thatch canopies rated for a tropical downpour, or we move the whole setup indoors at no extra charge.
