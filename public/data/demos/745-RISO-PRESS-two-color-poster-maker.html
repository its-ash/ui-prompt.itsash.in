Common questions

Do I need a real risograph printer?expand_more

No. Exports work with any print shop, and the web-resolution files look great on screen. If you do own a riso, the separated plates drop straight into your workflow.

Why only two colors?expand_more

Two-color printing is the heart of the riso look. Where the inks overlap they create a third tone, so two plates give you a surprisingly rich palette.

Can I use posters commercially?expand_more

Yes, on every plan including the free one. Everything you make is yours to sell, exhibit, or give away.
