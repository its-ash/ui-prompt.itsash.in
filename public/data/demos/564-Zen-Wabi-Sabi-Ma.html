What the practice holds

Three simple instruments. Nothing more than you will use.

ink_pen

Morning pages

A plain page each morning, timed by tea rather than by clock. Write until the water cools, then stop.

potted_plant

One shelf

A weekly review of the objects you keep. Each thing earns its place again, or it leaves with thanks.

dark_mode

Evening quiet

A short guided pause at dusk. Ten minutes of sitting, breathing, and letting the day settle.

How it works

A gentle sequence. Repeat it until it becomes ordinary.

  1. 一

    Arrive

    Choose one corner of your home. Clear it of everything but a mat and a cup.

  2. 二

    Practice

    Follow one short ritual each day. Small repetitions, kept without strain.

  3. 三

    Keep

    After a season, notice what stayed. That remainder is your practice.

A few questions

Do I need experience with meditation?expand_more

None at all. Each ritual is short and plainly described. Most members begin with the ten-minute evening quiet and grow from there.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?expand_more

Yes, at any time, from your account page. Pausing keeps your notes and shelf history. There are no fees and no questions.

Is Kanso Studio a religious practice?expand_more

No. The rituals borrow the shape of tea ceremony and monastic routine, but they are secular and open to anyone who wants a quieter day.
