Questions & answers

Are the prints archival?addremove

Every edition is pigment-printed on 310gsm acid-free cotton rag, rated beyond a century behind glass, and ships with a numbered certificate signed on the verso.

How fast is shipping?addremove

Unframed prints ship flat between rigid boards within five working days, worldwide. Originals travel in custom crates by insured courier; framing is available at cost.

Can I commission or license a composition?addremove

Yes — commissions include three studies and personal display rights. Commercial licensing for covers, textiles and interiors is quoted per surface and print run.
