Plate II — Features

Curated as a Collector Would Keep Them

Every specimen in the archive is treated as a folio in itself: unbound, examined, and returned to the press with its provenance intact.

science

Archival Restoration

Each original plate is scanned at 600 DPI before a conservator lifts foxing, ink bleed, and old coffee rings — leaf by leaf, never painted over.

Scholarly Annotation

Every plate carries its Latin binomial, its first description, and its collector's provenance, set in the typographic fashion of the original folio.

Plate VI — Appendix

Questions from the Correspondence

May I use the plates in commercial work?expand_more

Single plates carry a personal licence. The Collector's Folio and Atelier Patron tiers add a commercial licence for print, textile, and editorial use, with no per-impression fees.

In what formats are the plates delivered?expand_more

Every plate arrives as a 600 DPI, colour-profiled TIFF and a web-ready JPG. Giclée pressings on 310gsm cotton rag ship flat in glassine within a fortnight, worldwide.

What if a plate disappoints on arrival?expand_more

Return any pressing within thirty days for a full refund, no correspondence required. Digital plates are refunded within fourteen days provided they have not been printed.
