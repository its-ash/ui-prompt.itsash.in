Features

Tools reduced to essentials

Every feature exists to keep your composition honest: shape, color, position. Nothing else gets in the way.

category

Primitive Library

Squares, circles, crosses and planes with locked proportions. Drag them onto the canvas and combine freely.

palette

Four Color Discipline

A fixed palette of black, red, blue and ochre keeps every poster, page and identity system coherent by default.

open_with

Tilt Physics

Rotate any plane by exact degrees and let tension do the work. Snap guides hold your composition in balance.

Three moves to a composition

Suprema replaces menus and panels with a short, deliberate sequence.

1

Place a form

Choose one primitive and set it on the ground. One strong form beats ten weak ones.

2

Add tension

Introduce a second shape at an angle. The space between forms is where the energy lives.

3

Commit color

Assign red, blue or ochre with intent. Export to SVG, PNG or a live web component.
