Frequently Asked Questions

What is a variable font?

A variable font contains multiple styles within a single file, allowing continuous adjustment of weight, width, slant, and other axes.

Can I use the fonts in commercial projects?

Yes. All licenses include commercial usage for print, web, broadcast, and merchandise.

Do you offer refunds?

Due to the digital nature of the product, all sales are final. We provide previews and support to ensure satisfaction.
