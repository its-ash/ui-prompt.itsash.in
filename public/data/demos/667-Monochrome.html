MARGINALIA · A QUIET WRITING STUDIO

Write for the page, not the feed.

Marginalia is a distraction‑free writing studio with archival versioning and literary export — typography that respects your words and gets out of their way.

✓ NO ADS ✓ NO FEEDS ✓ NO ALGORITHMS

FEATURES

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Three tools, kept deliberately small, so the sentence stays the largest thing on the screen.

01

Focus mode

A full‑screen editor with no toolbars, no streaks, and no counters you didn't ask for. Just you and the sentence.

02

Archival versioning

Every draft is kept the way libraries keep books — dated, shelved, and retrievable. Restore any version in a single keystroke.

03

Literary export

Publish to PDF or EPUB with real book typography: proper margins, hanging punctuation, and old‑style figures.

PROCESS

Three steps, in order.

01

Draft

Open a blank page and write in a room with the noise removed.

02

Refine

Compare drafts side by side, keep marginal notes, and restore anything you regret cutting.

03

Publish

Export a typeset PDF or EPUB, or share a clean reading link — no platform chrome attached.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.
