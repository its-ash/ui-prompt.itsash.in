Frequently asked questions

How is Volt different from a row-oriented database?expand_more

Volt stores data by column, not by row. Analytical queries read only the columns they need, so scans touch a fraction of the data and compress far better. The result is orders of magnitude faster aggregation on large tables.

Can I migrate from my current warehouse?expand_more

Yes. Volt speaks standard SQL and ingests Parquet, CSV, and JSON directly from object storage. Most teams replay historical data in hours and validate query parity within a week.

What consistency guarantees does Volt provide?expand_more

Inserts are atomic per block and replicated synchronously within a region. Reads are linearizable on the primary replica, with optional bounded staleness on read replicas for higher throughput.
