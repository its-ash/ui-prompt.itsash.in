FAQ

Before you flip the power switch

Do the knobs work with a mouse or trackpad?expand_more

Drag vertically to turn, double-click to reset, hold Shift for fine steps. Every control is also keyboard-focusable with arrow-key steps.

Does Nimbus process my audio locally?expand_more

Recording and effects run entirely on your machine. Episodes leave it only when you export or publish.

Will my old USB interface work?expand_more

Any class-compliant USB or Thunderbolt interface works, plus most XLR desks made after 2004. MIDI controllers map automatically.
