Frequently Asked Questions

How is Nimbus funded?expand_more

Solely by readers. We accept no advertising, sponsorship, or political money of any kind, and we publish a full budget breakdown once a year so you can see exactly where your subscription goes.

Can I see the sources behind a story?expand_more

Yes. Every investigation ships with an evidence locker: original documents with redactions noted, the datasets behind each figure, and the complete methodology explained in plain language.

What happens when you get something wrong?expand_more

The correction runs at the top of the story, is entered in a public corrections register, and stays there permanently. Accuracy is a process we show you, not a claim we ask you to accept.
