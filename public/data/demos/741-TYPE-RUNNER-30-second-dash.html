Quick answers

One dash is exactly 30 seconds. Words appear in a stream; type them in order. Mistyped characters count against accuracy but never stop the clock.

WPM is correct characters divided by five, normalized to one minute. Your board score multiplies WPM by accuracy, so sloppy speed does not pay.

Boards reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Your best verified run of the week is the one that ranks. Anti-cheat checks keystroke timing on every submission.

Any physical keyboard on any modern browser. QWERTY, Dvorak, and Colemak layouts are auto-detected. Touch keyboards work but are ranked separately.

Street Runner gives unlimited dashes and board access. Ghost replays, extra wordsets, and telemetry history are locked behind Dash Pro.
