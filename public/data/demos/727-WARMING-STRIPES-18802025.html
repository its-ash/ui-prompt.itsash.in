Frequently asked questions

What data sources do you use?
We combine NASA GISTEMP, NOAA GlobalTemp and HadCRUT5, all publicly available.
Why use stripes instead of a line chart?
Stripes encode temperature as color, making trends instantly perceptible without axes.
What is the 1951‑1980 baseline?
Anomalies are shown relative to the average temperature from 1951‑1980, a standard climatology period.
Can I reuse the graphics?
Yes, under CC BY 4.0—just credit “Warming Stripes / Ed Hawkins”.
What export formats are available?
PNG, SVG and PDF are supported; higher‑resolution exports require a paid plan.
