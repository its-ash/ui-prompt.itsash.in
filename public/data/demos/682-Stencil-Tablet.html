Sec. 01, Features

What the Kit Carries

Three components, each specified to archival standard and tested in rain, dust, and baggage holds.

Fig. 01, Tablet

menu_book

Archival Tablet

One hundred sheets of 120 gsm acid-free paper, thread-sewn and bound in waxed canvas. It lies flat, takes ink without bleed, and outlives the expedition.

Fig. 02, Stencils

grid_on

Steel Stencil Set

Twelve laser-cut plates in brushed spring steel: letterforms, arrows, measures, and map symbols. Each plate nests into the next and shrugs off field abuse.

Fig. 03, Case

backpack

Field Case

A riveted canvas roll with brass hardware that holds tablet, stencils, and four pencils. Printed to last. Built to travel.

Sec. 02, Procedure

How It Works

No batteries, no signal, no setup. The kit is ready the moment the case unrolls.

01 unfold_more

Unroll the Case

Open the canvas roll on any flat surface. Tablet on the left, stencil plates fanned on the right, pencils in the spine loops.

02 draw

Mark the Record

Lay a plate on the sheet and trace. Registration notches keep every line square, so field notes read like printed plates.

03 inventory_2

File and Forget

Tear along the micro-perforation and file the sheet. The paper meets ISO 9706 permanence, so the record outlives us all.
