Frequently asked questions

Anything else, our team can answer at hello@flowmetric.io.

How does FlowMetric handle data security?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We're SOC 2 Type II certified, support SSO/SAML on Enterprise, and never train AI models on your workspace data.

Can I migrate from Jira or Asana?expand_more

Yes — the importer maps projects, tasks, attachments, and comments automatically, and our team assists free with migrations above 1,000 tasks on Professional and Enterprise plans.

What happens when my trial ends?expand_more

Your workspace stays intact. On the free Starter plan you keep up to 5 users and 3 active projects; upgrade any time to unlock unlimited projects and analytics.
