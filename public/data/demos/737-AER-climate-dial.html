Common questions

Everything households usually ask before their first turn of the dial.

Will AER work with my existing heating system?expand_more
AER supports most 24V systems, including gas, electric, heat pump, and radiant setups. The app runs a compatibility check before you order, and every plan includes free returns if it does not fit.
Do I need a subscription for basic control?expand_more
No. Manual dial control, scheduling, and app access work forever without a plan. Subscriptions add adaptive learning, zoning, and detailed energy reporting.
What happens to my data?expand_more
Climate and schedule data stays on the dial by default and syncs encrypted only when you enable cloud features. You can export or erase everything from the app at any time.
