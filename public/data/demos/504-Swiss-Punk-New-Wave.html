Straight answers

Do I need to know Swiss typography to use GRIDBREAK?add

No. The templates enforce the underlying grid for you. You learn the rules by breaking them, and the app explains each move as you make it.

Can I export work for commercial print?add

Yes, on the New Wave and Atelier plans. Exports are press-ready PDFs with bleed, crop marks, and embedded fonts included.

What happens to my files if I cancel?add

Your projects stay readable forever on the free Photocopy plan. You can export everything before you leave, no lock-in, no ransom.
