Questions, answered

Where does my data go?expand_more

Your content stays in your region, encrypted at rest and in transit. Nimbus never trains shared models on your workspace data.

Which tools does Nimbus connect to?expand_more

Docs, wikis, chat, issue trackers, and cloud drives. New connectors ship monthly, and the API covers anything custom.

How accurate are the answers?expand_more

Every answer links to its sources. If Nimbus cannot find a grounded answer, it says so instead of guessing.

Can I control what actions it takes?expand_more

Yes. Actions start in approval mode. You promote individual action types to automatic only when you trust them.

What happens when I cancel?expand_more

Your workspace keeps working until the end of the billing period, and you can export your full index and history at any time.
