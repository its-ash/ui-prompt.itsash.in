Frequently Asked Questions

What wavelengths does Nimbus capture?

Nimbus sensors cover visible (400‑700 nm), near‑infrared (700‑1000 nm), and long‑wave infrared (8‑14 µm) bands, providing full‑spectrum thermal imaging.

Is the platform compliant with data privacy regulations?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we offer GDPR‑ and CCPA‑ready configurations with regional data residency options.

Can I integrate Nimbus with my existing SCADA system?

Absolutely. We provide REST APIs, MQTT brokers, and pre‑built connectors for common industrial protocols like Modbus and OPC‑UA.
