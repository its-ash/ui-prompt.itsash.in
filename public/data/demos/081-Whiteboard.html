Quick answers, marker-style

Still stuck? The board's always open.

Do I need an account to scribble?expand_more

Nope — open a board and start drawing immediately. Create an account only when you want to save, share or return later.

Can I export boards into real tasks?expand_more

Yes — highlight any cluster and push it to your tracker as tickets, complete with owners, due dates and source links.

Is my whiteboard data private?expand_more

Boards are private by default and encrypted in transit and at rest. Sharing is invite-only, and guests always need an explicit link.
