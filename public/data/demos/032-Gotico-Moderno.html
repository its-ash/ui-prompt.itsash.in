Questions from the Dark

✦

Answers to what the house is most often asked.

How are bespoke fittings arranged?expand_more

Every commission opens with a private consultation — in the atelier, or by candlelit video call. Up to twelve fittings follow, evenings included, until the piece feels inevitable rather than merely made.

Do you ship worldwide, and what about returns?expand_more

We ship to over forty countries in linen-lined coffers, tracked and insured. Ready-to-wear may be returned unworn within thirty nights. Bespoke commissions are final — they are cut for one body alone.

How do I care for my pieces, and can I cancel my covenant?expand_more

Store each piece in its cotton shroud, aired and away from direct sun; brush the wools and never wring the silks. Membership may be paused or cancelled at any moment from your account — no questions asked, no penance owed.
