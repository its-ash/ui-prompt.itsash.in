Questions

Before the lacquer

Is a kintsugi repair food-safe?add

Fully cured urushi is inert and safe for tea and dry foods. We advise resting the seam from boiling liquids for the first month while it hardens completely.

How long does a restoration take?add

Most vessels take six to eight weeks; urushi cures only in warm, humid stillness, so we never rush the join. Ancestral commissions may take up to four months.

Will the repair hide the break?add

Never — that would undo the point. Kintsugi honors the accident: the gold seam makes the vessel's history its finest feature rather than its shame.
