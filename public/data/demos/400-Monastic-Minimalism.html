The method

Three movements, repeated daily. The practice is the product.

  1. 01

    Sit

    Open Stillpoint at your chosen hour. The page is blank and the room is quiet. Take one breath before the first word.

  2. 02

    Write

    Answer the day's prompt in your own words, for your own eyes. Ten minutes is enough. Stopping mid-sentence is allowed.

  3. 03

    Return

    Once a week, Stillpoint lays your entries side by side. Read them slowly. Notice what repeats. That is the whole review.

Questions

Is my writing really private?expand_more
Yes. Entries are encrypted on your device with a key only you hold. We cannot read them, and neither can anyone else. Export or delete everything at any time.
What happens if I miss a day?expand_more
Nothing. There are no streaks to break and no badges to lose. The page waits for you, unchanged, whenever you return.
Can I use Stillpoint offline?expand_more
Yes. Writing works fully offline. Entries sync the next time a connection is available, and conflicts are resolved by keeping both versions.
How do I leave, and what do I take with me?expand_more
Cancel in two clicks from settings. Before you go, export your complete archive as plain text or PDF. Your data is deleted from our servers within thirty days.
