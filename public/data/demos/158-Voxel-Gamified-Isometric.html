Frequently Asked Quests

Quick answers before you hit start.

Do I need design or 3D skills to play?expand_more

Nope. If you can drag a block, you're qualified. Every board starts from a ready-made template, and the isometric magic renders automatically.

Can I switch plans mid-quest?expand_more

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from your profile. Your boards, XP, and streaks carry over, so nothing ever resets.

Is my party's data safe?expand_more

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, backed up hourly, and fully exportable. We're GDPR compliant, and you can wipe your save data whenever you like.
