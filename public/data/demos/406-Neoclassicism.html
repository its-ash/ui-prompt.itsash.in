Common Inquiries

Must I abandon my present books to begin?expand_more

No. The establishment act imports spreadsheets, exports from prior software, and even photographed paper ledgers. Every figure is carried over and reconciled before your first live entry.

How is the record kept unalterable?expand_more

Corrections are made by reversal entry, never by erasure. The original inscription remains, joined by its amendment, so the full history is always available to you and your auditors.

Does the system meet statutory filing requirements?expand_more

Yes. Filings for the United Kingdom, the European Union, and the United States are prepared from the same record and submitted on the schedule your tier provides.

May I withdraw my data if I depart?expand_more

At any hour, without fee or negotiation. Your complete archive exports in open formats, and we certify its destruction from our halls within thirty days.

Is counsel available when questions arise?expand_more

Every tier includes written counsel from trained accountants. Ionic receives priority reply within one working day; Corinthian retains a dedicated steward who knows your books by name.
