Can we run Nimbus inside our own cloud accounts?expand_more

Yes. Nimbus deploys into your VPC on AWS, GCP, and Azure. Your data never leaves accounts you control.

Everything keeps running. Nimbus is non-intrusive by design — export your full configuration and audit history at any time.
