Questions, answered badly but honestly

Five things people ask before they stop asking.

Is the broken look actually accessible?expand_more

Yes. The chaos is decoration only. Contrast, focus order, keyboard support, and semantic structure are enforced and audited on every export.

Can I keep my existing content?expand_more

Yes. GRIDRUIN re-skins structure, it does not rewrite copy. Your words stay exactly where you put them.

What if my client hates it?expand_more

Lower the ruin level to ten percent. They get a slightly crooked, memorable site and never find out how far it could have gone.

Does it work on mobile?expand_more

Yes. Tilts and overlaps flatten into a clean single column under 768px. Nothing scrolls sideways, ever.
