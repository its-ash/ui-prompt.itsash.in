Questions, answered

The things teams ask before they switch.

How does the free trial work?expand_more

You get 14 days of the full Pro plan with no credit card. At the end, choose a paid plan or drop to Starter, and your boards stay intact.

Can I cancel or change plans anytime?expand_more

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from workspace settings. Changes apply at the next billing cycle and we prorate upgrades automatically.

Is our data secure?expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Paid plans include SSO, role-based access, and optional EU data residency with annual third-party audits.
