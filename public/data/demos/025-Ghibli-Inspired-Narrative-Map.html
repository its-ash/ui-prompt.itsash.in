Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to draw?expand_more

Not at all. Sketch a few words and memories — StoryTrail's brushes paint the washes, borders, and lettering for you.

Can I print my maps?expand_more

Yes. Explorer and Storyteller plans include print-ready exports sized for postcards, posters, and textured paper stock.

Can friends add to my map?expand_more

Storyteller maps are co-authorable — invite companions to drop their own pins, notes, and photos along the trail.

Does StoryTrail work offline?expand_more

Your atlas syncs when you're back online, and Storyteller plans can export full maps for offline wandering.

What happens to my maps if I cancel?expand_more

Your maps stay yours forever. Download everything at any time — we'd never fold away your memories.
