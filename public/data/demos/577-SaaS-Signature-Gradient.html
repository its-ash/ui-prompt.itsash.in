Common questions

Everything teams ask before connecting their first repo.

Does ForgePilot train on my code?expand_more

No. Your code is processed in isolated environments and is never used to train shared models. Enterprise plans can run entirely inside your own infrastructure.

Which languages and platforms are supported?expand_more

We support GitHub and GitLab, with deep analysis for TypeScript, Python, Go, Rust, Java, and Ruby, plus solid coverage for twenty more languages.

How long does setup take?expand_more

Most teams see their first reviewed pull request within ten minutes of installing the app. No configuration files or prompt engineering required.

Can I customize the review rules?expand_more

Yes. Pro and Enterprise plans let you define team conventions, severity thresholds, and paths that require human sign-off.

What happens after the free trial?expand_more

You keep read-only access to past reviews. Upgrade any time to resume analysis; nothing is deleted for 90 days.
