01Features

Small dials, loud results

bolt

Instant spark

Canvas, preview and export all run GPU-snapped, so an A3 poster at 300 dpi moves at the speed you drag.

palette

Retro presets

120+ halftone, risograph and overprint recipes tuned by working print designers. Twirl the dials, or break the grid on purpose.

hub

Live collab

Share one link and jam on the same canvas. Comments, cursors and version history keep the loud ideas on record.

02Process

From spark to poster in three moves

  1. 01 draw

    Start from a spark

    Open the lab, pick a preset or drop in a phone sketch. WATTAGE snaps your idea onto a print-ready grid in one click.

  2. 02 tune

    Crank the dials

    Push hue, grain and overprint past sensible. Every value reads out in mono, so experiments stay reversible.

  3. 03 rocket_launch

    Ship it loud

    Export CMYK PDFs, 300 dpi PNGs or a live link. Print preflight runs before your file ever leaves the lab.
