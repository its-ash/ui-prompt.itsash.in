Common questions

Before you begin

Do my notebooks work offline?add

Yes. Quire runs entirely on your device; every stroke syncs quietly the next time you’re online.

Can I import from other note apps?add

Markdown, plain text, and Evernote exports import in a few taps — with tabs suggested automatically.

What happens to my pages if I cancel?add

Nothing is locked away. Volumes export to PDF or Markdown whenever you like — they’re your words, bound or loose.
