Common questions

How long does a visit take? expand_more

Most visitors spend two to three hours inside the cavern, including one resonance session.

Is the cavern accessible?

All main chambers are step-free. The spiral ramp has handrails and rest points, and wheelchairs are available on request.

Can I take photographs?

Photography is welcome throughout the cavern. Tripods are permitted in the main chamber only.
