Common questions

Quick answers about palettes, exports, and how ChromaFlow fits your stack.

Does ChromaFlow work with my existing design tokens?expand_more

Yes. Import tokens as JSON or CSS variables and ChromaFlow maps them into a palette you can edit visually, then export back in the same format.

How does the contrast guard handle dark mode?expand_more

Every palette generates paired light and dark variants. The guard scores both against WCAG AA and suggests text-safe alternates when a hue is too light or too dark.

Can I try Studio before paying?expand_more

Every new workspace gets a 14-day Studio trial with full sync features. No credit card required, and you keep your palettes if you downgrade.
