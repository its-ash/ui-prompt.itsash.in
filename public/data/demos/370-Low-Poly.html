From brief to build

Three passes. No filler geometry, no surprise invoices.

  1. 01

    Brief & moodboard

    Send references and your tri budget. We lock a five-tone palette before modeling a single facet.

  2. 02

    Blockout & facet pass

    Silhouette first, detail second. You review a flat-shaded blockout within four working days.

  3. 03

    Polish & handoff

    Palette bake, LOD chain, atlas packing — delivered as glTF, FBX and BLEND with a changelog.

Questions, answered

Do you follow an existing art direction?expand_more

Yes. We start inside your style guide — palette hexes, tri budgets, camera distance — and extend it in low poly rather than replacing it.

What file formats do you deliver?expand_more

glTF 2.0, FBX and OBJ with a packed PNG texture atlas. BLEND source files are included on Forge and Atelier plans.

Can you match our engine's triangle budget?expand_more

Every prop is modeled to a ceiling you set. The facet pass is counted per mesh, not estimated after the fact.

New facets, first inbox

One email a month: fresh prop drops, palette studies, and process notes. No pixel spam.
