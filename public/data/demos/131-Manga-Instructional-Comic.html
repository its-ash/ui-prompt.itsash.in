Chapter 06 — Sensei Answers

Before You Ask

Is this beginner-friendly?expand_more

Yes. Chapter 01 assumes you've never held a fineliner. We start from one confident line.

What materials do I need?expand_more

A pencil, ink pens, and paper. Every drill also has a digital twin, so any drawing app works.

Traditional or digital?expand_more

Both. Lessons are medium-agnostic — paper demos and screen demos, side by side.

How does sensei feedback work?expand_more

Upload pages to your dojo dashboard. Annotated ink comes back within one week.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more

Yes — two clicks, no cliffhanger. Everything you've already drawn stays yours.
