Fair questions, honest answers

Where do you ship, and how fast?expand_more
We ship across the EU, UK, US and Canada. Crates leave our coastal depot twice a week and typically arrive within 3–6 days, tracked from our harbor to your door.
How do I know the harvest is fresh?expand_more
Every label carries its grove name and pressing date. Nothing ships older than ninety days, and every oil is pressed within 24 hours of picking.
Can I pause or cancel my plan?expand_more
Anytime, from your account — no phone calls, no fine print. Pause for a season or cancel outright; whatever is already in the crate is yours to keep.
