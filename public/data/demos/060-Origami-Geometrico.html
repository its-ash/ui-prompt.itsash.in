01 — Features

Built on the grid, not on guesswork

Three tools that keep every angle, crease and cut mathematically honest.

grid_on

Grid-locked geometry

Snap points, angles and facets to a strict 8-point grid. Guides are drawn as live data — never as decoration.

content_cut

Crease-aware cutting

Mountain, valley and cut lines travel with the file, so the plotter always knows what to score and what to slice.

print

Print-perfect unfolds

Unfold any faceted form into flat patterns with tabs, glue margins and registration marks computed to the tenth.

02 — Process

From flat sketch to folded sheet in three moves

  1. 1

    Draft

    Block your form on the grid. Every facet stays a live, editable polygon — no flattened paths.

  2. 2

    Fold

    Assign mountain and valley creases. The fold engine simulates the paper in real time and flags impossible geometry.

  3. 3

    Export

    Ship cut-ready SVG, PDF or DXF with crease layers, tabs and tolerances intact.

05 — FAQ

Creases, answered

Do I need origami experience to use Foldform?expand_more

None at all. The grid keeps early work structurally sound, and the fold simulator flags impossible geometry before it ever reaches a cutter.

What can I export?expand_more

SVG, PDF and DXF with separate layers for cuts, creases and registration marks — production-ready for plotters, laser cutters and print shops.

Does Foldform run in the browser?expand_more

Yes. The editor is fully browser-based, and every project also opens offline in the desktop app for macOS and Windows.

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?expand_more

Yes — plans are month-to-month, upgrades apply instantly and downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period. Cancellation takes two clicks, no forms.

Is there an education discount?expand_more

Students and teachers get 50% off Studio with a valid academic email, and classroom licenses start at ten seats.

What if Foldform isn't for me?expand_more

Write to us within 30 days of purchase and we'll refund the full amount — no questions, no conditions, no paperwork.
