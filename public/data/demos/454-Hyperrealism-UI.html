From mic to master in three moves

Ferric removes the setup ritual. Plug in, sound check, and record.

1

Patch In

Connect any USB or XLR interface. Ferric detects your inputs and labels each channel strip automatically.

2

Set Your Gain

Speak at your normal level and turn the gain knob until the LCD reads green. The limiter catches anything louder.

3

Roll Tape

Press the record switch. When you stop, a finished, leveled master is waiting in your archive, ready to publish.
