Questions From the Doorway

The ones people ask in a lowered voice, hat in hand.

How long does a case take?

Simple traces wrap inside 48 hours. Street work runs a week or two. The long ones — the ones worth doing — take exactly as long as the truth takes to crack.

Is my information confidential?

Completely. Names burned, files sealed, mouths shut. Your secret stays off the books, and so do ours.

What happens if you find nothing?

You get a signed report that says so, and the meter stops there. We don't pad hours, and we don't sell hope.
